# Kurtulmak's Chosen — The Underground Ascension

> **STATUS (locked 2026-05-15):** Parallel thread in the breathing world. Players may or may not engage. The plot progresses on its own timeline even if untouched.

## Premise

A **Chosen of Kurtulmak** — a kobold (or kobold leader) divinely touched by the kobold god — is slowly accumulating wealth, magical power, and military strength in a hidden underground city. The Chosen's long-term goal is **transformation** — to become *something more than a kobold*. The exact nature of that "something more" is the campaign's slow-burn mystery: a draconic ascension, an avatar of Kurtulmak, a new kind of being, a god-touched apotheosis.

This is a reframing of the original Plot Point #1 (the kobold undercity led by a dragon). **The dragon-king framing is dropped.** The Chosen is a kobold, not a dragon, and the divine connection is the driver — not a hoard-sitting wyrm.

## Why this serves the campaign

- **Avoids dragon-glut.** With Claugiyliamatar locked in and the Two Black Brothers as a side plot, adding a third dragon was bloat. A divinely-elevated kobold is *more interesting* and *more original* than another dragon.
- **Pure breathing-world thread.** This story progresses regardless of player engagement. Even if the party never goes underground, the Chosen rises.
- **Long fuse, big payoff.** Tier 1 = whispers and disappearances. Tier 2 = first real evidence (kobold raids with unusual coordination, a captured kobold who speaks in tongues, magical anomalies). Tier 3 = the Chosen's transformation completes — and now there is a new power on the Sword Coast that the players either fight, ally with, or have to live alongside.

## Default trajectory (if players ignore it)

| In-game timeframe | What's happening underground |
|---|---|
| Campaign start (1525 DR) | Underground city ~500 kobolds, well-funded; the Chosen is anointed but not yet powerful; small surface raids for resources |
| ~6 in-game months | Population growing, wealth accumulating from intercepted High Road and Sword Mountains trade; recruiting unusual minions (drow exiles, deep gnomes turned cultists, derro mercenaries) |
| ~1 in-game year | First powerful magical artifact acquired; ritual to begin transformation discovered |
| ~2 in-game years | Transformation ritual in active preparation; raids on the surface intensify and become more brazen |
| ~3 in-game years | The Chosen completes the ritual. Becomes *something*. Reveal happens. Sword Coast feels the shift. |

If players never engage, this is what happens. The world changes. NPCs react.

## Plot threads that hook into this if players do engage

- **Tier 1 hooks (level 1-5):** Unusual kobold raids on caravans — well-coordinated, well-armed, organized far beyond typical kobold behavior. Disappearances of dwarven artificers, drow weaponsmiths, and underdark traders along the High Road. A captured kobold who refuses to speak — or speaks only in Old Draconic phrases that scholars find unsettling.
- **Tier 2 hooks (level 5-10):** Discovery of an entrance to the undercity. First contact with the kobold society — they are *not* the chaotic vermin of typical D&D; they are organized, religious, building something. An emissary from the Chosen may attempt to negotiate with surface powers (trade? non-aggression?).
- **Tier 3 hooks (level 10-20):** The ritual approaches. The Chosen is now visibly transforming — partly draconic, partly something else. The party can stop it, accelerate it, redirect it, or witness it. The Chosen may even ally with the party against a *larger* threat (Claugiyliamatar's son? The Sharran cult? Luskan?) if the moral framing is interesting.

## Possible intersections with other locked threads

- **Claugiyliamatar:** She would scry on this with extreme interest. A divinely-elevated kobold transforming into "something more than a kobold" is uncomfortably close to her own ambition (a dragon transforming into "something more than a dragon"). She may try to learn from it, sabotage it, or steal the ritual. Strong narrative parallel.
- **Two Black Brothers:** They might trade with the undercity in exchange for tribute, or compete for territory. A black dragon and a kobold undercity sharing the Mere/underdark border region is rich.
- **Shar cult:** Shar specializes in transformation and the unmaking/remaking of identity. A Sharran ritualist might be the one who supplies the ritual to the Chosen — or, conversely, might be commissioned by the Chosen to engineer the transformation. Either way, a clean thread crossover.
- **Luskan / Arcane Brotherhood:** If the Chosen has hired Brotherhood mages to engineer the ritual, that's a Bad-faction tie. The Brotherhood likely sees the Chosen as a useful tool — and miscalculates.

## Open questions to develop later

- **Who is the Chosen, specifically?** A name, a backstory, a moment of divine touch. Lore-bound (one of Kurtulmak's classic Chosen archetypes) or original?
- **What is the transformation goal?** Picks include:
  - Draconic ascension (becomes a kobold-dragon hybrid)
  - Avatar of Kurtulmak (becomes an embodiment of the god)
  - The first of a new kind (a "Kobold-King" species progenitor)
  - A lich-equivalent specific to kobold-kind (the "Bone Kobold"?)
- **Where is the underground city?** Below the Crags? Connected to the Underdark proper? Beneath the Mere? Beneath Neverwinter itself? Each option has different campaign implications.
- **What does Kurtulmak want?** Is the god directly involved (Sharran-style cult relationship), or is the Chosen acting on perceived divine guidance that the god is barely aware of?

## Tier hooks to write later (when player engagement is desired)

- **The Captured Kobold** — Tier 1 hook. A kobold survivor of a raid speaks in old Draconic, references "the Chosen," seems unafraid of dying.
- **The Missing Artificers** — Tier 1-2 hook. Dwarven and gnomish craftsmen disappear from Sword Coast trade routes; someone is collecting expertise underground.
- **The Sword Mountains Anomaly** — Tier 2 hook. Druidic / Emerald Enclave reports of magical aberrations in the deep tunnels.
- **The Chosen's Emissary** — Tier 2-3 hook. Direct contact attempt with Neverwinter or another surface power. Diplomatic, articulate, unsettling.
- **The Ascension Ritual** — Tier 3 climax option. Party can intervene at the moment of transformation.

## DM notes

- This thread is **load-bearing for the "breathing world" feel.** Even if players never engage, occasional rumors, news, and side encounters should reference what's happening. Caravan losses to "kobold raids." Lost dwarven scholars. Strange tremors. The world is alive.
- **Do not over-emphasize this in early sessions.** Tier 1 should feel mostly like a Neverwinter intrigue campaign with kobolds as ambient threat. The Chosen reveal grows slowly.
- **Resist making this the BBEG.** Claugiyliamatar is the primary villain. The Chosen is a *parallel* power, not a competing one. The Chosen could even become an ally in a complicated tier-3 arrangement.
