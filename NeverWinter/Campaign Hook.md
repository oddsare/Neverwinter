## The Crisis

### What's Happening?

The [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|Docks District]] is experiencing unprecedented disruptions:

#### Ships
- Vanish beneath **moonless skies**
- Simply disappear without a trace
- No wreckage, no survivors, no explanations

#### Cargo
- Disappears from **harbor warehouses**
- Vanishes overnight from secured locations
- Guards report nothing unusual

#### Caravans
- Report **strange encounters** on overland routes
- Some disappear entirely
- Survivors tell conflicting stories

### The Impact

- **Trade partners** are growing concerned
- **Merchants** are losing money
- [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter's]] hard-won **stability** is threatened
- [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]] is under pressure

---

## Your Mission

If you choose to accept this call:

1. **Meet with** [[NPCs/Soren Ironwake|Guard Captain Soren Ironwake]] in the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|Docks District]]
2. **Investigate** the mysterious disappearances
3. **Uncover the truth** behind the shadowy force
4. **Stop the threat** to Neverwinter's trade routes

### Promised Rewards

- **Riches** - Payment for solving the crisis
- **Glory** - Recognition as heroes of Neverwinter
- **Status** - Potential advancement and influence in the city
