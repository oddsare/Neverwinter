## Strong start (pick one)
💡 All of these happen at the docks, so the four strangers are already in the same place for their own reasons, and each works with any hook route.

- **A. The empty warehouse at dawn.** A warehouse is opened and found **empty**, with locks intact and guards who saw nothing. A crowd gathers, tempers rise, someone blames a rival merchant or "Shar's people," and a scuffle breaks out. Soren arrives and notices who kept their heads.
- **B. The ship that didn't come in.** After a moonless night, families and merchants wait at the harbor for a ship due at dawn. It never arrives. Grief turns to anger, and the characters are standing in the crowd when it turns ugly.
- **C. The Swamp Docks at dusk.** The construction crews rebuilding the rotting [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|Swamp Docks]] are attacked from the water (the district note already has sahuagin under the docks). The characters are nearby and the guards are too far away. Straight into a fight.
- **D. The poster line.** Soren's poster has drawn a line of chancers, cranks and real talent outside the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|Guard Captain's Office]]. He doesn't have time to interview them all, so he gives the line a test.

## Fights
💡 For four level-1 characters. Pick one or two; stat blocks to choose at the table.
- **Dock thugs** paid to scare off anyone asking questions (a few bandits and one thug). Easy to drop into any scene.
- **Sahuagin from under the Swamp Docks** (two is plenty at level 1). A real threat, and maybe a red herring: the city might blame them for the missing ships.
- **Cargo thieves caught in the act** at night. Who they work for is the question; they may not know themselves.
