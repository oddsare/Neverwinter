## Moments

### Start (pick one)
💡 All at the docks, so the four strangers are already in the same place for their own reasons, and each works with any hook route.
- **A. The empty warehouse at dawn.** A warehouse is opened and found **empty**, with locks intact and guards who saw nothing. A crowd gathers, tempers rise, someone blames a rival merchant or "Shar's people," and a scuffle breaks out. Soren arrives and notices who kept their heads.
- **B. The ship that didn't come in.** After a moonless night, families and merchants wait at the harbor for a ship due at dawn. It never arrives. Grief turns to anger, and the characters are standing in the crowd when it turns ugly.
- **C. The Swamp Docks at dusk.** The crews rebuilding the rotting Swamp Docks are attacked from the water (the district note has sahuagin under the docks). The characters are nearby and the guards are too far away. Straight into a fight.
- **D. The poster line.** Soren's poster has drawn a line of chancers, cranks and real talent outside the Guard Captain's Office. He doesn't have time to interview them all, so he gives the line a test.

### Moment: Meeting Soren
💡 What he offers (pay, an advance, guard backing), what he's under pressure from (Lord Renaer needs results before trade partners pull out), and what he can't say out loud.
- **Clue:** ✅ it always happens on **moonless nights**.
- **Clue:** ✅ the emptied warehouses show **no forced entry**; the guards saw nothing.

### Moment: The investigation
💡 The party picks where to look: the warehouses, the harbor, the witnesses, the Swamp Docks.
- **Clue:** ✅ **the caravans:** survivors tell conflicting stories, always at night.
- **Clue:** ✅ **the White Wyrm red herring.** A sailor swears [[NPCs/Arveiaturace - The White Wyrm|the White Wyrm]] took his mate. He's telling the truth, but she takes **people, not ships or cargo**. So the missing ships are something else.
- **Clue:** ✅ **Shar gets the blame.** People say Shar's followers are behind it. 💡 A Sharran sign left at a scene would feed that.
- **Rumor:** 💡 a Luskan delegation is coming for the anniversary of the peace. (Only a rumor; the event comes later.)

> [!danger] Combat (pick one or two)
> 💡 For four level-1 characters; stat blocks to choose at the table.
> - **Dock thugs** paid to scare off anyone asking questions: a few bandits and one thug. Drops into any moment.
> - **Sahuagin from under the Swamp Docks:** two is plenty at level 1. A real threat, and maybe a red herring if the city blames them for the ships.
> - **Cargo thieves caught in the act** at night. Who they work for is the question; they may not know themselves.

### Stock cliffhanger (pick one)
💡
- **A ship vanishes in sight of the harbor.** On the next moonless night the party watches a ship's lanterns go out one by one, then nothing.
- **A message on Soren's door.** By morning a Sharran symbol is painted on the Guard Captain's Office. The city blames Shar even louder.
- **Cutscene:** the players see someone the characters can't: a figure quietly leaving a Sharran sign at an emptied warehouse. The players learn the blame is being *placed*, without learning by whom.

## Location details

**The Docks** (from [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|Docks District]]):
- **The Ports:** busy day and night; the docks never sleep.
- **The Warehouses:** where cargo vanishes overnight.
- **The Swamp Docks:** sunken, rotting docks under reconstruction; criminals hide there; sahuagin under the water.
- **The Guard Captain's Office:** Soren's base for patrols and the investigation.
- **The Tarmalune Trade House:** a secretive warehouse complex dealing in unusual wares. *(Just a place to notice; nothing ties it to the Shadowy Force.)*

**Taverns** (canon, from [[Lore/Pre-1502 Supporting Cast]]):
- **The Beached Leviathan (Docks):** a grounded pirate ship turned inn, run by **Captain Harrag**, a gruff one-legged ex-pirate (he lost the leg to sahuagin). Staff and regulars include Andrella (lead barmaid), Feng (half-orc bouncer), Len-jes (the actual Harbormaster), Markul (sells secrets) and Korin (dragonborn bard). Regulars earn nautical nicknames. *1505 status: 12–15 years older; Brodie's call who's still there.*
- **The Driftwood Tavern (Docks):** upscale, almost a museum of a tavern; one of Neverwinter's oldest sites. Run in the 1490s by **Madame Rosene**, a fount of city history. *1505 status: she'd be about 85; maybe a successor.*

**Magic item shop** (from [[Items/Arcane Acquisitions Shop Table]]):
- **Arcane Acquisitions (Chasm District):** forges and braziers glowing purple, green and blue. Owner **Flinar Ralolar**, an eccentric half-elf wizard in robe and goggles, who talks telepathically with his goblin assistant **Gigz**. Items and prices: the d12 table in that note. *Fan-made source set in the early 1490s; Brodie's call whether he's still running it.*

## Plotlines
*What each villain and faction is doing at the start of the campaign, as if the party didn't exist. All from existing notes.*

- **The anti-god group** ([[Quests/The Shadowy Force]], [[Campaign Decisions]] §2): behind the attacks on ships, warehouses and caravans, using Shar's tactics openly so Shar takes the blame. *Name, goals and how far along they are: open.*
- **Renaer's Government** ([[Factions/Renaer's Government]]): two years in, facing pushback from influential people; pressing the Guard for results.
- **Luskan** ([[Factions/Luskan]], [[Quests/The Peace Anniversary]]): preparing a delegation for the peace's second anniversary, planning a political move against Renaer.
- **Claugiyliamatar** ([[Quests/The Foster King]], [[Campaign Decisions]] §14): in her new lair near Neverwinter, scrying Aldric every day; Aldric (28) is building a name in Neverwinter society. Throne first, ritual later.
- **Zundaerazylym and the Soft Claws** ([[Factions/The Soft Claws]]): steering evil dragons toward orc lands, gathering alchemy ingredients, spying on orcs.
- **The Two Black Brothers** ([[NPCs/Voaraghamanthar - Two Black Brothers]]): slow kidnappings continue in the Mere; feeding the Cult of the Dragon hope while it hunts Uth Tower.
- **Kurtulmak's Chosen** ([[Quests/Kurtulmak's Chosen - Underground Ascension]]): an underground city of about 500 kobolds, well funded; small surface raids for resources.
- **Thay** ([[Campaign Decisions]] §13): background war in the east. Valindra is in Neverwinter mainly for Dawnfire, and Thay smuggles Dawnfire east to its Dread Ring.
- **Shar** ([[Campaign Decisions]] §2): the Ugly for now: unpredictable, able to help or hurt the party, still evil.
