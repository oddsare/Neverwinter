# The Code Legal of Neverwinter

**Enforced By:** [[Factions/City Guard|Neverwinter City Guard]]
**Adjudicated At:** [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Hall of Justice]]
**Authority:** [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]]

---

## Original Text (As Written)

The Code Legal of Neverwinter

I. Crimes Against Lords, Officials, and Nobles
Assaulting or impersonating a Lord: death or exile up to 20 years
Assaulting or impersonating an official or noble: flogging, imprisonment up to 15 days, and fine up to 1,000 gp
Blackmailing an official: flogging and imprisonment up to 5 years
Using magic to influence a Lord without consent: imprisonment up to a year, and fine or damages up to 2,000 gp

II. Crimes Against the City
Defiling the River Never: imprisonment up to 3 months and damages up to 1,500 gp
Arson: death or hard labor up to 2 years, with fines and/or damages covering the cost of repairs plus 3,000 gp
Espionage: death or imprisonment for life
Poisoning a city well: death
Theft in the Market Square: flogging, hard labor up to a year, and a fine equal to double the value of stolen goods
Trespassing in the Hall of Justice or Castle Never: imprisonment up to a tenday and a fine of up to 500 gp

III. Crimes Against the Gods
Assaulting a priest or lay worshiper: imprisonment up to a tenday and damages up to 1,000 gp
Tomb-robbing in the Neverdeath District: imprisonment up to a tenday and damages covering the cost of repairs plus 1,000 gp
Disorderly conduct within the Shrine of Kelemvor: fine up to 50 gp
Public blasphemy against a god or church: public apology and fine up to 25 gp

IV. Crimes Against Citizens
Assaulting a citizen: imprisonment up to a tenday, flogging, and damages up to 2,000 gp
Blackmailing or intimidating a citizen: fine or damages up to 1,000 gp and edict
Slavery: flogging and hard labor up to 15 years
Robbery in the Bluelake District: imprisonment up to 3 months and damages equal to the value of the stolen goods plus 500 gp

V. Crimes Specific to Neverwinter's Districts
Unauthorized entry into the Sealed Chasm: imprisonment up to 6 months and fine of 500 gp
Smuggling through the Swamp Docks: imprisonment up to 1 year and forfeiture of goods
Magical disturbances in the Tower District: damages up to 1,500 gp, edict, and exile from the district for repeat offenses
Gambling fraud in the Gilded Arcade: confiscation of winnings, fine up to 1,000 gp, and public shame

Punishments
Punishment for crimes varies depending on severity, context, and location. Options include:
Death
Exile (for a number of years or summers)
Flogging (a set number of strokes)
Hard labor (for days, months, or years)
Imprisonment in Castle Never's dungeons, "The Holes"
Fines (payable to the city or injured parties)
Damages (payable to victims; inability to pay leads to hard labor or imprisonment)

## Punishments Explained

Punishment for crimes varies depending on **severity, context, and location**.

### Death
- Reserved for the most serious crimes
- Crimes against Lords, espionage, poisoning, arson (at discretion)
- Carried out at [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Hall of Justice]]

### Exile
- For a number of years or summers
- Can be alternative to death for some crimes
- Banishment from Neverwinter and surrounding lands

### Flogging
- A set number of strokes
- Public punishment
- Often combined with other penalties

### Hard Labor
- For days, months, or years
- Work on city projects, repairs, construction
- Alternative to imprisonment for some crimes

### Imprisonment
- Held in [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Castle Never's dungeons, "The Holes"]]
- Durations from a tenday to life
- Conditions vary based on crime severity

### Fines
- Payable to the city or injured parties
- Amounts vary from 25 gp to thousands
- Inability to pay leads to hard labor or imprisonment

### Damages
- Payable to victims
- Covers cost of repairs plus additional penalty
- **Inability to pay leads to hard labor or imprisonment**

### Edict
- Official decree or restriction
- May limit activities or require specific actions
- Violation can lead to harsher penalties

### Public Shame
- Public humiliation as punishment
- Often in [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Market Square]]
- Stocks, public announcement of crimes

## Justice System

### Adjudication
Trials are held at the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Hall of Justice]], temple to Tyr, god of law and justice.

### Enforcement
The [[Factions/City Guard|Neverwinter City Guard]] enforces the Code Legal throughout the city.

### Authority
[[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]] has ultimate authority over the law, though trials are conducted by magistrates in Tyr's name.

### Corruption Concerns
Given [[Lore/Neverwinter History|Neverwinter's turbulent political history]], corruption in the justice system may still exist despite Lord Renaer's reforms.

## Campaign Notes

**For Players:**
- Being arrested is a real possibility
- Fines can be substantial
- Hard labor can remove characters from play temporarily
- Some crimes carry death penalty
- The wealthy (Bluelake) get special protections
- District-specific crimes reflect local concerns

**For DMs:**
- Tool for consequences without killing PCs
- Opportunity for prison break scenarios
- Source of moral dilemmas
- Reflects city's values and priorities
- Can be used flexibly based on context
