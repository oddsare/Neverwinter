# Neverwinter Historical Timeline (-10 to 1489 DR)

> [!warning] Supplemental Material
> Lifted and paraphrased from a fan-made guide describing Neverwinter as it stood in the **early 1490s DR** — roughly **30+ years before the campaign's 1525 DR start.** Use as historical backdrop only. The "current era" closing entries below (1502+) are vault canon, added to bridge this historical timeline to the campaign start year.

> [!info] How to use this file
> Each entry pairs a date with 1–3 sentences of context, cited to the source PDF page. For the official vault chronology, see [[Lore/Neverwinter History]] and [[TIMELINE]].

---

## Foundation Era

### -10 DR — Halueth Never settles the region
The sun elf **Lord Halueth Never** conquers **Illusk** (whose ruins now lie beneath [[Locations/Luskan]]) and settles the region. Construction of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Castle Never]] begins on the cliffs at the river mouth. (p. 5, p. 20)

### 87 DR — Eigersstor founded
A settlement called **Eigersstor** grows up around Castle Never. A later Chondathan translation renames it **"Neverwinter."** (p. 5, p. 20)

## The Wizards' Era

### 457 DR — School of Wizardry founded
The **School of Wizardry** is founded in Neverwinter by four wizards collectively called **"the covenant"**: **Aganazzar, Ilyykur, Presper,** and **Grimwald**. (p. 20)

### 955 DR — The Orcgates Affair
**Red Wizards of Thay** aid orc hordes in escaping the wizards of Neverwinter. The incident ignites a **centuries-long feud between Thay and Neverwinter.** (p. 20)

### 1081 DR — School of Wizardry destroyed
Red Wizards of Thay slay **Aganazzar** and destroy the School of Wizardry. (p. 20)

## The Alagondar Era

### 1329 DR — Nasher Alagondar takes the throne
**Nasher Alagondar** becomes king of Neverwinter — beginning the dynasty whose legacy still drives much of the city's politics. See [[Lore/The Alagondar Dynasty]]. (p. 20)

### 1372 DR — The Wailing Death
A devastating illness — known as the **"Wailing Death"** — sweeps Neverwinter, killing most who contract it within ten days of onset. (p. 20)

### 1379 DR — Moonshadow Hall attacked
The **Shadovar** (Netherese of Shadowfell) assault **Moonshadow Hall** in the distant city of **Yhaunn** and magically tear one of its towers free, sending it to Shadowfell. The tower will reappear in Neverwinter a few years later as the floating **Shard of Night**. (p. 14, p. 20)

## The Spellplague & After

### 1385 DR — The Spellplague
The Spellplague strikes Toril. In and around Neverwinter it:
- Creates the **floating earthmotes** on which several city landmarks now sit ([[Locations/Neverwinter/The Moonstone Mask|Moonstone Mask]], Pirates' Skyhold, Fisher's Float).
- Sends the **Cloak Tower** to the Far Realm (it returns ~6 months later in a new location, empty of its wizards).
- Raises the undead of **Neverdeath graveyard**.
- Allows the **Netherese** to transport the **Shard of Night** to Neverwinter.
- Corrupts the **Underdark** directly beneath the city — the corruption that will later be exposed as the **Chasm**.
- **Corrupts the [[Lore/Neverwinter Sub-Locations|Neverneath]] ward.** The magical protective enchantment beneath Castle Never — originally built to maintain the castle's structural integrity *and* protect the buried corpse of Lord [[Lore/Ancient History|Halueth Never]] — is altered by Spellplague wild magic. It gains **malevolent sentience** and the ability to **reshape the corridors at will.** It now traps intruders in infinite magical loops filled with deadly traps and animated statues. The ward bides its time before springing; visitors may seem unharried at first. **Halueth Never's corpse remains undisturbed beneath the maze, still protected — by a guardian that now has its own agenda.**

(p. 20; Neverneath canon from user notes 2026-05-15)

## The Lost Generation

### 1429 DR — Dagult Neverember born
**Dagult Neverember** is born in Neverwinter, growing up with mercantile ambitions for the city. (p. 19)

### 1451 DR — "The Cataclysm" (Mount Hotenow erupts)
An adventuring party in **Gauntlgrym** sends the primordial **Maegera** into a fit of rage. Maegera lays waste to the surrounding area, causing **Mount Hotenow to erupt.** The eruption:
- Opens a massive **Chasm** in Neverwinter's southeastern quarter, exposing Spellplague-corrupted Underdark.
- Lays waste to much of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Castle Never]], killing **the entire known royal lineage** and most of the castle's inhabitants.
- Ruins much of the rest of the city; the Moonstone Mask's tethers snap and it drifts over the Sea of Swords for months.
- Devastates the **House of Knowledge**; Loremaster Atlavast seals himself in the inner sanctum to save the collection.

Commonly called simply **"the cataclysm."** (p. 6, p. 7, p. 10, p. 20)

See also [[Lore/The Three Cataclysms]].

## The New Neverwinter Movement

### 1467 DR — Mintarn mercenaries hired
Still reeling from the cataclysm, **Dagult Neverember** hires **Mintarn mercenaries** to restore order. He brands the campaign **"New Neverwinter."** (p. 20)

### 1474 DR — Sons of Alagondar formed
A nationalist resistance group, the **Sons of Alagondar**, forms in opposition to Neverember's rule. They split into two factions:
- **The Nashers** — more violent, willing to take direct action against Neverember.
- **The Graycloaks** — politics and subterfuge, secretly led by **Madame Rosene** of the Driftwood Tavern.

(p. 13, p. 20)

## The Tumult of 1479 DR

A genuinely chaotic year — three major events all hit in the same twelvemonth.

### 1479 DR — Seldra's false-crown plot
**Seldra Tylmarande** is given a **replica of the Crown of Neverwinter**. Disguised as a man, she uses the fake crown to claim the throne and try to depose Neverember. **The plot is defeated.** Neverember simultaneously claims to be a legitimate Alagondar heir (via Nasher's bastard son) but cannot produce the real crown to prove it. (p. 20)

### 1479 DR — The Shard cleansing
**[[NPCs/Selune and Shar - Sister Goddesses|Selûne]] intervenes** in mortal affairs, sending shards to Toril. **Lady Jasmine** and other Selûnite priestesses, aided by adventurers, raid the **Shard of Night** and purify it into the **Shard of the Moon** — restoring the tower to alabaster white and Selûnite custody. (p. 15, p. 20)

### 1479 DR — The Vargas / Ashmadai disaster
**Elden Vargas**, a Cormyrean War Wizard, uses his substantial psychic magic to send much of Neverwinter's populace into **madness** in a desperate plot to rescue his wife **Karis**, held in Helm's Hold by **Ashmadai cultists** trying to exploit him. The conflict spills onto the **Beached Leviathan**, where Ashmadai cultists and demons kill one of General Sabine's top advisors. Vargas leaves for Helm's Hold and is ultimately captured or killed there. His Bluelake townhouse remains magically sealed to this day. (p. 13, p. 20, p. 28)

## Closing the Wounds

### 1486 DR — The Chasm sealed
The **Chasm is finally sealed** with powerful magic — at **great expense** to the city. The leveled Chasm District is opened to free settlement by anyone willing to claim it. (p. 18, p. 20)

### 1489 DR — Neverember ousted from Waterdeep
The **Masked Lords of Waterdeep** oust Dagult Neverember as **Open Lord**. Freed from Waterdeep duties, he turns his full attention to Neverwinter. (p. 19, p. 20)

## Bridging to the Campaign (Vault Canon)

> The entries below are **not** in the source PDF; they are vault canon used to connect this historical timeline to the campaign start year.

### 1502 DR — Dagult Neverember disappears
Dagult Neverember vanishes. Cause and fate disputed. His Neverwinter restoration program loses its driver. *(Vault canon — not in PDF.)*

### 1502–1520 DR — The Dance of the Revolving Lords
A protracted instability follows Neverember's disappearance — a succession of would-be rulers, mercenary captains, and noble claimants cycle in and out of effective control over the city. Building projects stall and resume; factional balances shift; old Neverember loyalists and old Alagondar sympathizers re-align repeatedly. *(Vault canon — not in PDF.)*

### 1520 DR — Renaer ascends
[[NPCs/Renaer Neverember]] takes power in Neverwinter, ending the Dance of the Revolving Lords. *(Vault canon — not in PDF.)*

### 1525 DR — Campaign Start
Five years into Renaer's rule. See [[Campaign Home]], [[Lore/Current Era]], [[TIMELINE]].
