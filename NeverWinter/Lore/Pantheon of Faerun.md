## The Gods of Faerûn

### Bane - Lord Bane
**Domain:** Tyranny, Strife, Hatred
**Alignment:** Lawful Evil
**Symbol:** Black hand
**Worshipers:** Tyrants, conquerors, those seeking power through fear

**In the Campaign:**
- Opposed by followers of Tyr and good deities
- May have secret followers among [[Lore/Neverwinter History|the dissenters]] in Neverwinter

---

### Bhaal - Lord of Murder
**Domain:** Murder, Death, Assassination
**Alignment:** Neutral Evil
**Symbol:** Skull surrounded by blood
**Worshipers:** Assassins, murderers, those who kill for pleasure or profit

**In the Campaign:**
- Forbidden worship in civilized areas
- Cultists may operate in secret
- Connection to [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Chasm District|Thieves Cove]]?

---

### Chauntea - Earthmother
**Domain:** Agriculture, Nature, Life
**Alignment:** Neutral Good
**Symbol:** Blooming rose
**Worshipers:** Farmers, gardeners, those who tend the land

**In the Campaign:**
- [[Lore/Creation Myth|Born when the twin goddesses formed the earth]]
- Worshiped by common folk
- Her blessings ensure Neverwinter's food supply

---

### Corellon - Protector of the Elves
**Domain:** Elves, Magic, Arts, Crafts
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good
**Symbol:** Crescent moon
**Worshipers:** Elves, half-elves, artists, archers

**In the Campaign:**
- Elven population in Neverwinter may worship here
- Connection to [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Tower District|magical academies]]

---

### Cyric - Prince of Lies
**Domain:** Lies, Deception, Strife, Murder
**Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
**Symbol:** White jawless skull on black/purple sunburst
**Worshipers:** Liars, madmen, nihilists

**In the Campaign:**
- Dangerous cult
- May be involved in [[Lore/Neverwinter History|political intrigue]]
- Opposed by followers of truth and justice

---

### Gruumsh - He Who Never Sleeps
**Domain:** Orcs, War, Conquest, Survival
**Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
**Symbol:** Unblinking eye
**Worshipers:** Orcs, half-orcs, warmongers

**In the Campaign:**
- Orcish tribes outside Neverwinter
- Potential threat to trade routes
- Connection to [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|caravan attacks]]?

---

### Helm - The Great Guard
**Domain:** Protection, Vigilance, Guardians
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
**Symbol:** Staring eye on upright war gauntlet
**Worshipers:** Guards, protectors, those who stand watch

**In the Campaign:**
- Worshiped by [[Factions/City Guard|City Guard]]
- Protectors of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Castle Never]]
- Vigilance against threats

---

### Ilmater - The One Who Endures
**Domain:** Suffering, Endurance, Perseverance
**Alignment:** Lawful Good
**Symbol:** Hands bound at the wrist with red cord
**Worshipers:** The oppressed, monks, the suffering

**In the Campaign:**
- Healers and helpers of the poor
- May have shrine near [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Neverdeath District|Pauper's Section]]
- Comfort to those who suffered during [[Lore/Neverwinter History|the revolving lords]]

---

### Kelemvor - Judge of the Damned
**Domain:** Death, The Dead, Judgment of Souls
**Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
**Symbol:** Upright skeletal arm holding balanced scales
**Worshipers:** The dying, grave-keepers, mourners

**In the Campaign:**
- Primary deity of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Neverdeath District|Neverdeath District]]
- [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Neverdeath District|Shrine of Kelemvor]]
- Inscription: "All must pass through death's gate, and in his judgment, find peace."
- Opposes undead and necromancy

---

### Mystra - Mother of Magic
**Domain:** Magic, Spells, The Weave
**Alignment:** Neutral Good
**Symbol:** Circle of seven stars / Blue-white star
**Worshipers:** Wizards, sorcerers, all who use magic

**In the Campaign:**
- [[Lore/Creation Myth|Born from clash between Selûne and Shar, embodiment of magic itself]]
- Keeper of the Weave
- Worshiped at [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Tower District|magical academies]]
- [[Lore/Current Era|Resurgence of magic]] may be her doing
- Rumors of [[Lore/Current Era|tenth-level spells]] concern her followers

---

### Oghma - Lord of Knowledge
**Domain:** Knowledge, Invention, Inspiration
**Alignment:** Neutral
**Symbol:** Blank scroll
**Worshipers:** Scholars, inventors, bards, artists

**In the Campaign:**
- Primary deity of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|The House of Knowledge]]
- Preservers of historical records
- Important for maintaining [[Lore/Neverwinter History|city's history]]

---

### Selûne - Moon Maiden
**Domain:** Moon, Stars, Navigation, Questers, Good Lycanthropes
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good
**Symbol:** Pair of eyes surrounded by seven stars
**Worshipers:** Navigators, sailors, seekers, those who work at night

**In the Campaign:**
- [[Lore/Creation Myth|The Lady of Silver, twin sister to Shar]]
- [[Lore/Creation Myth|One of the first two gods]]
- Eternal conflict with Shar
- Worshiped by sailors in [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|Docks District]]
- Ships vanishing beneath [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|moonless skies]] - absence of her protection?

---

### Shar - Mistress of the Night
**Domain:** Darkness, Night, Loss, Secrets
**Alignment:** Neutral Evil
**Symbol:** Black disk with deep purple border
**Worshipers:** Those who work in darkness, the bereaved, nihilists

**In the Campaign:**
- [[Lore/Creation Myth|The Mistress of Shadow, twin sister to Selûne]]
- [[Lore/Creation Myth|One of the first two gods]]
- Eternal conflict with Selûne
- May have secret followers
- [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|Shadowy force]] operating at night - her influence?
- Connection to [[Lore/Neverwinter History|dissenters in the shadows]]?

---

### Silvanus - Oak Father
**Domain:** Wild Nature, Druids
**Alignment:** Neutral
**Symbol:** Oak leaf
**Worshipers:** Druids, rangers, those who live in harmony with nature

**In the Campaign:**
- Protector of wilderness outside Neverwinter
- May oppose excessive expansion
- Rangers and druids serve him

---

### Sune - Lady Firehair
**Domain:** Beauty, Love, Passion
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good
**Symbol:** Face of a red-haired, ivory-skinned beautiful woman
**Worshipers:** Lovers, artists, those who value beauty

**In the Campaign:**
- Worshiped in [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Bluelake District|Bluelake District]]
- Patron of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Bluelake District|The Gilded Arcade]]
- Beauty and luxury

---

### Talos - The Stormlord
**Domain:** Storms, Destruction, Rebellion
**Alignment:** Chaotic Evil
**Symbol:** Three lightning bolts
**Worshipers:** Those who revel in destruction, sailors fearing storms

**In the Campaign:**
- Feared by sailors in [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|Docks District]]
- Ships lost at sea may be his doing
- Connection to [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|disappearing ships]]?

---

### Tempus - Lord of Battles
**Domain:** War, Battle, Warriors
**Alignment:** Neutral
**Symbol:** Flaming sword
**Worshipers:** Warriors, soldiers, mercenaries

**In the Campaign:**
- Worshiped by [[Factions/City Guard|City Guard]]
- Mercenaries and adventurers pray to him
- Battle prowess and honorable combat

---

### Torm - Torm the True
**Domain:** Duty, Loyalty, Obedience
**Alignment:** Lawful Good
**Symbol:** White right gauntlet
**Worshipers:** Paladins, those who serve a higher calling

**In the Campaign:**
- Paladins in service to [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer]]
- Devotion to duty and loyalty
- May oppose [[Lore/Neverwinter History|dissenters]]

---

### Tyr - God of Justice
**Domain:** Justice, Law, Fair Judgment
**Alignment:** Lawful Good
**Symbol:** Balanced scales resting on warhammer
**Worshipers:** Judges, paladins, those who seek justice

**In the Campaign:**
- Primary deity of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|The Hall of Justice]]
- [[Lore/Code Legal of Neverwinter|The Code Legal]] is administered in his name
- Symbol of law and order in Neverwinter
- Training ground for his followers at the Hall

---

### Tymora - Lady Luck
**Domain:** Good Fortune, Adventurers, Skill
**Alignment:** Chaotic Good
**Symbol:** Silver coin with Tymora's face
**Worshipers:** Adventurers, gamblers, those who take risks

**In the Campaign:**
- Mentioned in [[Campaign Hook|the call to adventure]]: "May Tymora be in your favor"
- Worshiped at [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Bluelake District|The Gilded Arcade]]
- Patron of gamblers and adventurers
- Her blessing sought before dangerous ventures

## Divine Conflicts

### The Eternal Struggle: Selûne vs Shar
[[Lore/Creation Myth|The twin goddesses]] remain locked in endless conflict between light and darkness, moon and shadow.

**Campaign Impact:**
- [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|Ships vanishing on moonless nights]] - when Selûne's light is absent, does Shar's darkness grow stronger?

### Good vs Evil
- **Good:** Tyr, Torm, Ilmater, Helm, Chauntea, Selûne, Sune, Tymora
- **Evil:** Bane, Bhaal, Cyric, Shar, Talos, Gruumsh

### Law vs Chaos
- **Lawful:** Tyr, Torm, Helm, Bane, Kelemvor
- **Chaotic:** Tymora, Sune, Selûne, Corellon, Cyric, Talos, Gruumsh
