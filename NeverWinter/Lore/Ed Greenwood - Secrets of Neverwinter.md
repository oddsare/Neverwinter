# Ed Greenwood — Secrets of Neverwinter

> Source: **Realms Lore** (YouTube), with **Ed Greenwood**. Transcript added 2026-10-19.
> Summarized from the video's auto-captions. Names Ed spelled out letter by letter are marked *(spelled)*. Names the captions garbled are marked *(uncertain)*; check them against the video.
> **Movie lore left out** (Brodie, 2026-10-18).

## Neverwinter from the outside
- A port city in the Sword Coast North that is **unusually warm for how far north it is**. The Neverwinter River, warmed by the volcano, flows from Mount Hotenow through the city to the sea. The dwarves call it the **Nethlur** (see [[Lore/Ancient History]]).
- **Castle Never** dominates the skyline: tall, gleaming stone towers right on the edge of the sea. It's the first landmark you see when arriving by ship.
- **No maps:** for a long time the kings of Neverwinter **forbade publishing any maps of the city**. The Chasm and the rebuilding afterward then changed the layout a great deal.
- **Known abroad for:**
  - **water clocks** (the big one);
  - **hanging indoor lamps** that are small sculptures with panes of multicolored glass;
  - **fine jewelry**;
  - **green plants and fresh flowers, even in the dead of winter**.
- **Politics as outsiders see it:**
  - Neverwinter is in the [[Factions/Lords' Alliance|Lords' Alliance]] and **consistently sides with [[Locations/Waterdeep|Waterdeep]]** there.
  - Like Waterdeep, it's a tolerant, open crossroads trading city.
  - **Lots of outsiders take an interest in it.**
- ***Neverwinter Nights*** is the name of a **broadsheet (newspaper) published in the city**. That comes from Ed's home campaign, before the video games used the name.

## Dagult Neverember (Ed's take)
See [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember]].
- He's the rare **successful politician, which in Ed's words means a very good con man**.
- He **believes in what he's doing and does good things**, but he thinks **the end justifies the means**. If you stand in his way, he may trample you.
- He is **not** a tyrant who slaughters people to build his perfect society. His flaw is that **he sees nothing wrong with getting rich by doing good**.
- He was Lord Protector of Neverwinter and Open Lord of Waterdeep; **his power is now limited to Neverwinter**. In Ed's words he is "still on the scene" and "remains a player."

## The Chasm
- **Nobody agrees how it came about.** Theories: devils or Asmodeus; the Abolethic Sovereignty; an evil wizard; or angry gods punishing Neverwinter.
- It split the city in two and was **briefly a smoking rift** that daring adventurers went down to explore. Later it was **fixed and patched up and went away**.
- The damage, and what followed, meant **the whole city was more or less rebuilt with a completely new layout**. Everyone knows the Chasm ran somewhere down the middle, but **no trace of it is visible now**.

## Neverwinter from the inside
- It's **a trade city that makes things**. Its crafters have long led the way in **new casting methods**, mass-producing beautiful and practical castings. They are the "skilled hands" in the nickname **City of Skilled Hands**.
- That makes it a natural center for **precision goods**, such as the **measuring scales merchants use** across the Realms.
- **Everyone does everything.** Elsewhere, dwarves dominate smithing, gnomes casting and fine work, and halflings weaving and handicrafts. In Neverwinter everyone takes part in all of it.
- The result is a **skilled, sophisticated, literate citizenry**. They're good with their hands, understand how things are made, and *think they know how the world works*.
- **The ordinary citizen** has a strong work ethic, is handy, has **personal hobbies**, and often has a **side hustle** making, selling or trading something other than their day job.

## Secret 1 — Hidden Neverember kin
**More than a dozen relatives of the Neverember family live quietly in Neverwinter** as crafters and traders. They keep out of Dagult's attention, and he may not know about them, **but every one of them knows their place in the family tree.** Three examples:

### Wylandra Blaythloak *(spelled)*
- Makes and sells **slab bread** (Blaythloak breads) at her shop on **Himmer Lane** *(spelled)*. The shop is at the eastern edge of the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Protector's Enclave]], where it meets the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Tower District|River (Towers) District]].
- Her husband **Belaerak** *(spelled)* died recently. Her **six grown children** live with her and work in the shop.

### Unthra Rorivan *(surname spelled; first name uncertain)*
- Ed called her the most usable of the three for DMs. She's an **adventurer and guide to the Savage North**.
- Tangle-haired, hard-drinking and full of energy. She **plays an uncouth bumpkin** but is one of the **shrewdest and best-informed judges of people** you'll meet, and she notices everything.
- She has a **knack for staying alive in dangerous situations**, rumored to come from **inherited family magic items**.
- She's more interested in **being part of exciting events** than in coin or real power.
- **Probably in her 30s**, though she acts younger and more energetic.

### Rundrigo Blackserpent *(uncertain; "Blackserpent" is one word)*
- A **trader, investor and master caster**. He is working on **portable, wearable water clocks**, so far without success.
- **Rich.** At least **six Blackserpent-owned merchant ships** sail the Sword Coast. He keeps the Blackserpent name off the ships and off the cargo contracts their captains sign.

## Secret 2 — Murthra "Manytongues" Alaerin, a brass dragon
- **Murthra Alaerin** *(spelled)*, nicknamed **Manytongues**, runs the **House of Tongues** on **Wandaer Street** *(spelled)* in the River District.
- **What she sells:**
  - translations of written languages and dictation, for modest fees;
  - **"wristlists"**: lists clamped to the wrist with handy words and phrases in Goblin, Orc, Elvish, Dwarvish and many other languages.
- **She is secretly a kindly brass dragon.** Ed spelled her true name letter by letter; the captions caught it as **M-U-R-T-H-I-I A-L-Y-X A-N-T-R-T-H-R-O P-R-A-R-A** *(uncertain)*. She goes by Murthra so people won't know what she is.
- **What she wants:**
  - She has had **enough of powerful magic being used on Neverwinter and its people**. She **works against anyone trying more of it**, naming **Red Wizards, aboleths and servants of Asmodeus**.
  - She sees Neverwinter as **a new hope for cooperation and progress, with all races living in harmony**, as the city was first meant to be back in the **late 200s DR**. *(The captions garble who visited then.)*
  - She **isn't interested in piling up wealth**. Her command of many languages is her daily business.
- **How she lives:** in human form, revealing herself as a dragon **only in emergencies**.
- **Appearance:**
  - a strikingly beautiful older woman with a **white streak in styled silver-gray hair** and **piercing brown eyes**;
  - middling height, buxom build;
  - dresses conservatively in dark ankle-length gowns, black boots and stomachers.
- **Her only adornment** is dangly earrings made by Neverwinter crafters. **Her favorite pair is two *iron bands of binding*,** just in case.

## Secret 3 — Sentient magic items
- **How many sentient magic items are hidden in the city**, from spellbooks to swords? **How aware are they of each other, do they communicate, and do they share any agenda?**
- In any large Realms city there are **local powers that like the way things are and may act to defend it** when big change threatens. That's as true of Neverwinter as of Waterdeep or Baldur's Gate.
- Ed's suggestion for DMs: decide **which intelligent items are in the city and how they act**, both in peacetime and when a threat arrives.

## Pronunciation
- **Mount Hotenow** sounds like "**hotel-now**", not "hot-enough".

## Notes vs. the vault (Brodie decides)
- Ed treats Dagult as **still active** in the video's present. In [[TIMELINE]], **Dagult vanishes in 1493 DR** and [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer]] rules from 1503.
- Ed describes the hidden kin in relation to **Dagult**. With Dagult gone in the vault's timeline, how they relate to **Renaer** isn't settled.
- Ed says the Chasm was **patched and no trace remains** after the rebuild. The vault still has a [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Chasm District|Chasm District]] described as dangerous and partly destroyed.
- Unthra's age ("probably in her 30s") is given for the video's present, with no year stated.
