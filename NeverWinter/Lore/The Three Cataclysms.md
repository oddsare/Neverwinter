# The Three Cataclysms of Neverwinter

For centuries, [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] enjoyed unbroken prosperity and peace. But in the span of less than 80 years (1372-1451 DR), three catastrophic events shook the city to its core, ultimately leading to its near-total destruction.

---

## First Cataclysm: The Wailing Death (1372 DR)

**Year:** 1372 DR - Year of Wild Magic
**Duration:** Several months
**Death Toll:** Thousands
**Type:** Magical plague

### The Disease

A terrible disease known as the **Wailing Death** swept through Neverwinter, laying low most of the city's inhabitants.

**Symptoms:**
- Victims would begin to wail uncontrollably
- Progressive weakness and fever
- Eventually fatal without treatment
- Highly contagious
- No natural cure discovered

### The Hero of Neverwinter

According to historical records, an adventurer (whose name is lost to history or deliberately kept secret) discovered that the plague was:
- **Magical in nature**, not natural
- **Created deliberately** by conspirators
- Connected to mysterious creatures from beneath the city

The hero managed to:
1. Trace the disease to its source
2. Defeat those responsible
3. Obtain a cure
4. Save the city from complete devastation

### Aftermath

- [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Nasher Alagondar]] was praised for his leadership during the crisis
- The city recovered, but was weakened
- Increased paranoia about magical threats
- Investigation into what lay beneath Neverwinter's streets
- Questions about the city's vulnerability to magical attack

**Note for DM:** This event is from the video game *Neverwinter Nights* (2002). The "hero" could potentially be a retired NPC in your campaign if you wish.

## Second Cataclysm: The Spellplague (1385 DR)

**Year:** 1385 DR - Year of Blue Fire
**Scope:** Worldwide (all of [[Lore/Faerun|Faerûn]])
**Duration:** Effects lasted decades
**Type:** Metaphysical catastrophe

### The Blue Fire

In 1385 DR, the goddess **Mystra** (deity of magic) was murdered. Her death caused the **Weave** (the fabric of magic itself) to unravel catastrophically.

**What Happened:**
- Blue flames swept across the world
- Magic became wild and unpredictable
- Entire regions were transported to other planes
- Landmasses shifted and continents collided
- Thousands of mages died instantly
- Many magical items stopped working or became dangerous

### Effects on Neverwinter

Neverwinter suffered multiple Spellplague-related disasters:

**Immediate:**
- Blue flames consumed parts of the city
- Many of Neverwinter's [[Factions/The Harpers|wizards and mages]] died
- Magical defenses failed
- Water-clocks and magical lamps (the city's famous exports) stopped working
- Chaos in the streets

**Long-term:**
- Magic became unstable and dangerous
- Parts of the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Chasm District|city]] were affected by reality distortions
- The weakening made Neverwinter vulnerable to invasion
- Economic devastation as magical trade collapsed

### The Century of Weakness

The period between the Spellplague (1385 DR) and the eruption (1451 DR) saw:
- Neverwinter struggling to maintain its status
- **Netherese loyalists** infiltrating the power structure
- Secret Shadow Empire of Netheril attempting to control the weakened city
- [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|The Alagondar Dynasty]] fighting to keep the city independent
- Increased threats from [[Locations/Luskan|Luskan]] and orc tribes
- Gradual recovery as magic stabilized

## Third Cataclysm: Mount Hotenow Eruption (1451 DR)

**Year:** 1451 DR - Year of Knowledge Unearthed
**Type:** Volcanic catastrophe
**Devastation:** Near-total destruction
**Death Toll:** Tens of thousands

### The Eruption

[[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Mount Hotenow]], the dormant volcano that had warmed Neverwinter's river for centuries, suddenly and violently erupted.

**The Catastrophe:**
- Massive volcanic explosion heard for hundreds of miles
- Lava flows reached the city
- Earthquakes destroyed most buildings
- Ash cloud blotted out the sun for weeks
- The river ran molten for days
- Fires consumed what the lava didn't destroy

### The Great Rupture

The eruption created the **Chasm** - a massive tear in the earth that split the city:
- Hundreds of feet deep
- Unstable magic at its heart
- Reality seems warped near its edges
- Became known as the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Chasm District|Chasm District]]
- Source of ongoing dangers (monsters, planar creatures, madness)

### Death of the Alagondar Dynasty

The eruption killed the ruling [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Alagondar family]]:
- King and Queen perished
- All known heirs died
- Royal palace destroyed
- End of centuries of Alagondar rule

*Campaign Note: In our timeline, rumors persist that at least one Alagondar might have survived, but official records list the entire family as deceased.*

### The Refugee Crisis

- Population dropped from 23,000 to fewer than 5,000
- Survivors fled to:
  - Port Llast
  - Waterdeep
  - Lusk an
  - Inland settlements
- The city was abandoned by most
- Only the most stubborn remained in the ruins

## The Aftermath Period (1451-1467 DR)

**Duration:** ~16 years of abandonment
**Status:** City in ruins, mostly uninhabited

### What Remained

**Survived:**
- Some outer walls
- Portions of the harbor district
- The Hall of Justice (partially)
- Underground structures
- City's foundations

**Destroyed:**
- Castle Never (royal palace) - obliterated
- Most residential districts
- Gardens (the source of "Neverwinter's" name)
- Magical infrastructure
- The city's famous water-clock workshops

### Dangers in the Ruins

During the 16 years of abandonment:
- **Monsters** moved into ruined buildings
- **Bandits** preyed on scavengers
- **Aberrations** emerged from the Chasm
- **Undead** rose in [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Neverdeath District|Neverdeath District]]
- **Competing factions** fought over the ruins
- **Planar creatures** crossed through weakened reality

### The Mysterious Presence

Unknown to most, an ancient evil stirred beneath the ruins:
- The **Abolethic Sovereignty** (mind flayers and aboleths)
- Had manipulated events to cause the eruption
- Sought to use the Chasm for their purposes
- Pulled strings from below

*DM Note: This faction may or may not be active in your campaign.*

## The Long-Term Impact

These three disasters fundamentally changed Neverwinter:

### Before the Cataclysms (Pre-1372 DR)
- Population: 23,000+
- Status: Jewel of the North
- Economy: Thriving trade and crafts
- Reputation: Unshakeable peace and prosperity

### After the Cataclysms (1451-1525 DR)
- Population: Reduced, slowly recovering
- Status: Struggling to rebuild
- Economy: Dependent on external support
- Reputation: Resilient but scarred

---

## The Path to Recovery

### Initial Attempts (1451-1467 DR)
Various groups tried to rebuild or claim the city:
- Scavengers and treasure hunters
- Surviving Neverwintans
- Merchants seeing opportunity
- Netherese agents
- Thayan enclaves
- Luskan spies

All failed due to:
- Lack of resources
- Monster attacks
- Competing factions
- Instability from the Chasm

### The Arrival of Lord Dagult Neverember (~1467-1468 DR)
*See [[Lore/Neverwinter History]] for details on the rebuilding efforts*

Around 1467-1468 DR, **Lord Dagult Neverember**, Open Lord of Waterdeep, arrived with:
- Significant financial resources
- Mintarn mercenaries for protection
- Workers and craftspeople
- A claim to be an Alagondar descendant (disputed)
- Vision to rebuild Neverwinter

This marked the beginning of serious reconstruction, though challenges remained.

### The Mystery of Dagult's Disappearance (~1502 DR)
*See [[Lore/Neverwinter History|Neverwinter - Recent History]] for full account*

In our timeline, Dagult Neverember mysteriously disappeared around 1502 DR, triggering another period of instability known as the "[[Lore/Neverwinter History|Dance of the Revolving Lords]]."

## Living Memory (1525 DR)

**Current Campaign Year:** 1525 DR

### Who Remembers?

**The Wailing Death (1372 DR) - 153 years ago:**
- No living witnesses in the general population
- Perhaps a few ancient elves or dwarves
- Historical records in libraries
- Old stories and legends

**The Spellplague (1385 DR) - 140 years ago:**
- A very few elderly elves might remember
- Most knowledge is historical
- Effects still visible in reality distortions
- Magic users study it as history

**Mount Hotenow Eruption (1451 DR) - 74 years ago:**
- **Elderly humans** (70-80+) were children during the eruption
- **Many dwarves and elves** remember it clearly
- **Survivors** with firsthand accounts
- The physical scars are everywhere
- This is LIVING MEMORY for many NPCs

### Current Situation

As of 1525 DR:
- The Chasm still splits the city
- Reconstruction continues under [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]]
- Some areas remain abandoned and dangerous
- The city has ~20,000 inhabitants (approaching pre-eruption numbers)
- Mount Hotenow is closely watched for signs of activity

## Plot Hooks & Campaign Integration

### Mysteries
- What really caused Mount Hotenow to erupt? (Abolethic Sovereignty? Something else?)
- Are there Alagondar survivors hidden away?
- What lies beneath the Chasm?
- Connection between past disasters and [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|current threats]]?

### Opportunities
- Ruins still hold treasures from pre-cataclysm era
- Ancient secrets buried under rebuilt districts
- Survivors with knowledge of the old city
- Factions still competing for control
- Unstable magic from the Spellplague
- The Chasm as a source of adventure

### NPCs
- Elderly survivors with trauma from 1451 DR
- Scavengers who've explored the deep ruins
- Descendants of Neverember's original workers
- Refugees who returned after decades away
- Those who profited from the disasters
- Those who lost everything
