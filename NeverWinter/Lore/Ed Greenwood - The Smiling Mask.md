# The Smiling Mask (Ed Greenwood)

A secret club in **Waterdeep**. Canon reference, summarized in my own words. Names come from auto-captions; spellings may be off. Ed's "now" in the video is **1501 DR, the Year of the Shining Mythal**. **Campaign connections are open threads.**

## Getting in
- **Where:** Castle Ward, on the west side of the **Street of the Sword**, a little east of the north end of Mount Waterdeep and south of the market. Two doors south of a long-standing silverware shop, five doors north of a side street.
- **The building:** a solid four-story brick building with a tiled roof. No signboard, just a tiny door plaque: **"Taratha's."** Officially all monthly rental suites.
- **The front business:** at the back of the ground floor, down a long side passage, is **Taratha's Tender Remedies**.
  - Taratha is a **motherly old woman** who knits, paints terrible portraits for fun, and mixes **on-the-spot potions** for stomach, bladder and bowel upsets, rashes and drunkenness.
  - Customers drink the remedy in her parlor and **rest there for hours** (a cot, shabby comfortable chairs, her privy) while she adjusts the treatment.
  - **The business is real and good.** Elminster recommends her and her kind manner, and she's a **great source of gossip**.
- **The secret door:** her "privy" is a passage with **six cubicles**. The farthest is a working privy with a **secret door** in its side wall, leading to narrow, steep, dark stairs, then a landing with a **doorman**, then a long, gentle ramp into the club.
- **The club itself** lies in old cellars **under the silverware shop's deepest cellar level**, stretching east under the street and north.
- **Other exits:**
  - A **long tunnel** out (not a way in) that surfaces through a **magically hidden door in a fat old oak** in a stand of trees on Mount Waterdeep's northernmost spur.
  - A **one-way gate** in an alcove beside the doorman, leading down into **Undermountain**, near Skullport.
- **Air and waste:** vent shafts climb the outsides of several buildings to the roofs, and a pulsing **teleport** brings in fresh sea air from Mount Waterdeep. Waste is teleported out (Ed suspects to the Rat Hills). Enchantments **muffle all sound**, so the cellars are never deafening and nothing carries upstairs.

## The rooms
- **Seven low, dark, rectangular cellar rooms**, old fieldstone walls, recently re-leveled flagstone floors. Damp, but not slick.
- Lit only by **blue glowstone** sconces and table lights.
- **Half-round black tables** against the walls, three chairs each. **Feet up on the middle chair** means "don't join us."
- **Service:** **merfolk** waitstaff glide between tables and refill vessels in place (usually flaring metal flagons).
- **Drink:** everything from absinthe to cider, but the house specialty is a **thick, jet-black, licorice-tasting semi-sweet wine** that nearly all drow love. They find it filling, so they rarely get past tipsy.
- **Food:** small seafood bites (prawns, snails, oysters, mussels) on rusks and crackers with whipped spreads. No meals.
- **No gambling, no dancing** (no room). Cards for fun only. Drinking, talk and laughter; kissing and caressing; **drow massage for a few coins**; and **minor healing** (bandages, ointments, setting limbs). It's one of the few places drow can trust on or near the surface. **Far safer than Skullport.**

## The hostess
- The club is run by **the Smiling Mask** herself: a **floating black harlequin mask** with no head or body, trailing a **fine black chain veil** about as long again. The veil **curves when she smiles** and hangs straight when she isn't amused.
- Pleasant contralto voice; warm and good-natured, but **arch or biting** when threatened or around bullies and sneerers. **She never gives her name** ("your hostess," "the ruling lady of this house").
- **The secret (Elminster knows):** she's the only moving part of a **lich too decayed to leave the stone casket** she used as a bed, hidden somewhere **inside Mount Waterdeep**.
  - In life she was a **Netherese wizard** who opposed where Netheril was heading (flying cities, archwizards exalting themselves, conquest). She **faked her death in -2212 DR** and worked toward lichdom as a recluse living simply among elves in the forest.
  - Her mask has its own **mythal**. She can hurl spells like a **30th-level wizard**, but usually just **counters** any magic she disapproves of in the club, or **reflects it at full force** back at its caster or item. The mythal can handle at least **six spells** not her own active at once.
- **Why she founded it:** she was **lonely** and wanted conversation with fellow powerful spellcasters.

## How it became the drow's club
- Over time the regulars were the **misfits and loners** among Waterdeep's wizards and sorcerers. One, a sorceress (now dead), became close friends and later lovers with a **drow adventurer**, and brought her in.
- The drow loved the place and the hostess's warm welcome, and came back with her **sisters**. The hostess loved talking with them because their lives were fresh to her, and when they shyly asked if more drow could come, she said **yes, enthusiastically**.
- People uneasy around drow stopped coming. Patrons now: **drow**, people who do **business** with drow, a few young casters **fascinated** by them, and old regulars who don't mind.
- **By 1501 DR:** the place drow meet in Waterdeep, and **acknowledged neutral ground for all drow on the Sword Coast**, whether they follow **Lolth, Eilistraee, Vhaeraun**, another god, or a trading family, cabal or city.
- **House rule:** leave all hatred and violence at the threshold. There are still arguments, fights, breakups, the odd brawl and even assassination attempts, but they're **personal**, not about faith or orders from the Spider Queen.
- Drow patrons stay watchful (they always must, near the surface) but come to **relax with friends**. Some tense up when someone like **Mirt** or **Elminster** walks in, until the newcomer buys drinks and chats.

## The doorman
- A gravel-voiced, wildly **sarcastic** figure who looks like a **slate-gray, bald orc or half-orc** with tusks, wearing a **butler's tailcoat with lace ruffles**. Answers to **Guldroth** *(spelling uncertain)*.
- **Truly:** an **archhag** (she), who keeps her real name to herself. She has all her powers, is the hostess's **firm friend** and employee, and has found a safe way to stay close to **Waterdeep, the city she loves**.

## Tales (true and false)
- **True:** someone was **banned for bringing a pet beholder**. Some lonely, aging Waterdhavian noblewomen have come looking for handsome young drow.
- **False:** six wizards dueling and ending up as ducks and chickens.
- Elminster: **still the best place in Waterdeep to hire a drow.**
