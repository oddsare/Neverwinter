# Lolth's Power and the Demonweb (MrRhexx, part 2)

Lolth's stats, divine powers, avatars and realm. Summarized in my own words. Stats are Rhexx's adaptation of older (3e) numbers to 5e; he says the real thing would be somewhat stronger. Part 1: [[Lore/MrRhexx - Lolth and the Drow]]. **Campaign connections are open threads.**

## How strong is a god? (Rhexx's scale)
- **Challenge rating** is a rough measure of a monster's power.
- **A player character is roughly CR = two-thirds of their level**, so a level 20 wizard is about **CR 12–14**: the range of efreet, full vampires, storm giants and several adult dragons.
- **Above CR 16–17:** beyond any single character, however strong (planetars, balors, pit fiends).
- **Above CR 20:** always needs a full party of the strongest adventurers (ancient dragons, demon lords, archdevils, liches, krakens). Many demigods fit here.
- **CR 30 and up:** **actual gods** and beings with divine power. The **tarrasque (CR 30)** is one of the few things that can kill gods. Tiamat appeared in 5e adventures around CR 30 (heavily weakened so she could be fought).

## Divine protection and divine rank
- **Mortals can't damage a deity at all**, no matter the magic weapon or spell slot. Only a god of roughly equal **divine rank** can.
- To act on the Material Plane, gods usually send **avatars**, which **don't share most divine protections** and **can be destroyed**. That's what adventurers face.
- **Divine ranks:**
  - **Tiamat** is roughly **rank 10** (not strictly Realms canon). Dragons don't really worship their gods; they blame them for the ancient chromatic–metallic war, so she gets little power from them.
  - **Lolth** is **rank 15**, on the edge of greater deity. She's a **world-conquering god**, moving from world to world forcing peoples to worship her.
  - The strongest Realms gods, **Moradin, Corellon and Chauntea**, are **rank 19**.

## Lolth's stats (Rhexx's adaptation)
- **About 820 HP**, speed **60**.
- **Str 24, Dex 29, Con 22, Int 41, Wis 41, Cha 32.**
- **Expertise** in alchemy, deception, weaving, disguise, stealth, arcana, history, religion, perception and insight.
- **Gods of her rank don't roll checks**; they're treated as rolling a **20**. Even using only a CR 13-level proficiency bonus, her Stealth is a flat **47**, Perception **53**, passive Perception **37**. Probably higher in truth.
- **Spider bite:** about **+19 to hit**, 1d6+10 piercing plus **poison**: a huge Con save or **lose 3d6 Strength**, and again one minute later on another failed save. **Strength 0 means death.** One bite is very likely fatal for any adventurer.
- **Whip** (drow form): 1d4+12, **+2d6 against lawful** and **+2d6 against good**, stacking, so devastating against lawful good. Strikes into the **Ethereal Plane**.
- **Smite up to 15 times a day:** +20 damage on a hit.
- Old weapons were +5; modern rules cap at +3.
- Rhexx estimates she'd be at least **CR 35–40**.

## Immunities
- Immune to **poison, acid, lightning and cold**. Her ability scores **can't be reduced**. Divinely immune to effects that let you save against her power.
- **Can't be disintegrated, banished or imprisoned.** Always has ***mind blank***. Immune to **paralysis and stun**, and effectively to **transmutation** that would change her shape.
- **Immortal:** no need to eat or sleep, doesn't age, immune to disease.

## Divine powers
- **Alter Reality:** gods rarely need *wish*. She thinks of something and **makes it so**, as an action, followed by **rest** depending on the effect's size.
  - **Reshape land:** 1 round per 10-foot cube, then **a day of rest per 10-foot cube**.
  - **Make any magical effect permanent:** rest 10 minutes per level of the effect per subject (more for powerful subjects).
  - **Create objects:** any non-magical object for **15 days**, any magical one for **15 hours**.
- **Call Creatures:** like *gate*, but up to **15 vermin** at once of up to 15 Hit Dice (for example, 15 driders), never more than 15 at a time.
- **Take over drow:** any **drow she can see**, she can **dominate completely as an action for up to 25 days**. Up to **15 a day, 150 at once**. Once linked, she needs no line of sight and can control them **across planes and through any barrier**. Rhexx: this is what having **drow in her portfolio** means.
- **Divine Blast:** a ray of **raw divinity**, **26d12** divine damage, which most magic damage reduction can't lessen. **Destroys walls of force and every layer of a prismatic wall.** **14 times a day.** She can **split it into up to 75 rays**, each doing full damage, at targets **up to 15 miles apart**.
- **Divine Shield:** absorbs **150 damage** per activation, **9 times a day**.
- **Hand of Death** (Lolth and her avatars): if she's present with an unbroken line to you and **points at you**, you make an enormous save or **die instantly and permanently**. Only a **higher-ranked god, personally** (not through a cleric), could restore you.
- **Spellcasting:** separate slots for **cleric** spells (for example, seven 9th-level and seven 8th-level) and **wizard** spells (for example, five 5th-level and six 4th-level). The god herself barely needs them because of Alter Reality; mostly they matter for avatars.
- **Demonic webbing** (spider form): toxic. Touching it means a Con save or **lose 1d6 Strength**, and again a minute later.
- **Making magic items:** permanent items for herself or her worshippers (light or medium armor, any weapon, many wondrous items) **tied to her portfolio**: **darkness, spiders, poisons, disguises**, and **divination** items her clergy are known for. No power limit as long as the **creation cost is under 200,000 gp**, but it comes from **her own wealth**, and she barely cares about the drow, so she rarely does.

## Divine senses
- She can **see, hear, smell and even feel by touch** up to **15 miles** away.
- As an action, she can extend her senses **15 miles around any worshipper, holy site or holy object**. That's how gods **hear prayers**: they can choose to see and hear you if you're a worshipper, and they can sense consecrated places.
- **She can extend her senses to any place where one of her names or titles was spoken in the last hour.** If you're working against her or her drow, **never say her name**.
- She can **block the divine senses of lower-ranked gods** (Rhexx's examples: Eilistraee, Bahamut), stopping them from seeing their own followers.
- **Portfolio sense:** she knows **anything that affects drow as a whole** the instant it happens, and remembers it for **150 days**: mainly big events, like a whole drow city destroyed. She also knows when **drow make war or commit violence against each other**.
- **Greater gods** (like Moradin and Corellon) sense portfolio events in the **future** too. Lolth would need one or two more ranks for that.

## Avatars
- An avatar is the god's **alter ego**, letting her be in several places at once. She **senses everything the avatar senses**, and vice versa.
- Making or replacing one takes **one year**, in her **own realm**, with no special effort.
- **Lolth can run up to 10 avatars** at once, but since she meddles in many worlds, you'd rarely face more than one in a given world.
- **Avatars are much weaker:**
  - Call Creatures only **8**, and weaker creatures.
  - **No Divine Blast, no drow domination, no Alter Reality.**
  - **Divine Shield** absorbs **80** instead of 150, still 9 a day.
  - **They keep Hand of Death.**
  - They must use **normal spell slots**.
- **Killing an avatar** doesn't permanently harm the god, only slows her plans for a year, and **puts you at the top of her kill list**.

## The Abyss and the gods' politics
- Lolth is a demon, and much more than that: **a deity**. Even **Demogorgon** is a fly in her web.
- **Many gods live in the Abyss:** something like a beholder god on layer 5, a god of yuan-ti and bullywugs on layer 74, a god of undead on layer 142, the troglodytes' god on layer 181, a lycanthrope god on layer 183, and some eastern gods around layer 300. **Lolth is the strongest**, but even she couldn't dominate the whole Abyss.
- **Gods make alliances for protection.** Cross an evil gnome god and you may face ten good gnome gods. **Umberlee** (rank 12), who would stand no chance against Lolth, has a **protection pact with Talos** (rank 16): she gives him part of her divine essence, and he protects her.
- **Greater gods** can see into the near future and make magic items **without limit**, so many lesser gods ally with them to get items for their clergy. That's why **pantheons** matter, and why Lolth can't just kill everyone. Her attempts to seize parts of the Abyss caused the events of *Out of the Abyss*.

## The Demonweb Pits (layer 66)
- Practically **unmappable**. A vast network of overlapping **20-foot-wide walkways of polished stone**, about 3 inches thick. The stone's patterns **slowly shift into the twisted bodies and faces of the damned**.
- **Curved walls of sticky webbing** enclose the paths like tubes; the webbing is only tacky enough to feel strange, not to hold you.
- Doors and openings along the paths are **portals to places Lolth has conquered** with her drow and demon armies, **sometimes whole worlds**, bound into her web and plunged into the Abyss. **Countless enslaved worlds** hang off "layer 66."
- **No personal teleportation** (her will forbids it). Servants travel through **geometric teleport hubs** that need the right **key**.
- **Hazards:** drow patrols, demons, arachnid horrors. The **unmarked metal doors** to other worlds can only be explored by trial and error without native help.
- **Residents:** mostly **drow clerics** of Lolth, powerful and arrogant, who defend the realm and drive her plans in the linked worlds. Her inner circle sees itself as major players in planar politics. She favors her own demons, the **yochlols**, who outrank even **balors and nalfeshnees** bound to her, much to their annoyance.
- **Cocooned in the web:** ancient **pyramids** wrapped in webbing, **demigods** trapped in sticky prisons, and the **husks of gods and primordials** killed in the Dawn War. The most chaotic and dangerous creatures live inside those dead shells, and Lolth **discards prisoners and defeated enemies** there, smiling, because living there is worse than death.
- **Break through the walls or floor** and you find an endless gray void of putrid cloud, then the Abyss's darkness: **oblivion**, described as a fate worse than death. Possibly where she throws the most desolate worlds, worst enemies, or defeated gods.
- **Portals:** the Demonweb has countless planar portals, **rivaling Sigil**, some leading to lost realms deep in the Astral Sea, others to **forgotten chambers under the gods' own citadels**.
