# Tenth-Level Spells of Netheril (MrRhexx)

What 10th-level spells were and what they did. Summarized in my own words. Brodie: **not gospel, ideas to draw on.** Spell names checked against the Forgotten Realms wiki (the captions garbled them). Series: this note, [[Lore/MrRhexx - How Mystra's Ban Works]], [[Lore/MrRhexx - Epic Magic and High Magic]].

## Netheril in brief
- A human empire where the **Anauroch desert** is now, back when it was green. **Floating cities** (enclaves), mages casting **10th- and 11th-level spells**, and **every human could cast cantrips**, most could cast 1st-level spells.
- Two things ended it:
  - **The phaerimm** (captions say "Ferrum"): underground creatures drawn to Netherese magic, which **drain magic and life**. The wars exhausted the empire, and their draining helped turn the land into desert.
  - **Karsus** cast the only **12th-level spell** ever, and it backfired: magic failed worldwide for a short time.
- **Skullport** (Undermountain, level 3) was built by Netherese wizards. When magic failed, its cave-in wards failed, **two-thirds collapsed**, and the dead wizards became the **floating green skulls** seen there today.
- **Netherese arcanists invented most modern wizard spells:** *magic missile* and *shield* were made together (why *shield* blocks *magic missile*), plus *fireball*, *wall of fire*, *fire shield* and the classic charm spells. Sorcerers and devils could throw fire before that, but **shaping the Weave into a spell** was Netherese.

## The mythallar
- Netheril's greatest creation: an **orb that holds concentration for you**, and **draws raw magic from the Weave** to store for spells and devices. It was the engine of their cities; they made many.
- Made by **Ioulaum**, "father of Netheril," who also built the first floating city.

## The 10th-level spells
| Spell | What it did | Cost / components |
|---|---|---|
| ***Lefeber's weave mythal*** | A permanent **force sphere** (300 ft radius per caster level, 3-day casting) that **no one can pass without the activation key**: a gesture, a password, the right alignment or motive, a written mark, a few bars of a song, or a keyed item. It could also be set to **harm intruders**. | **Caster loses a level** every casting. (The wiki lists a later 4th-level wizard version too.) |
| ***Mavin's create volcano*** | Range a mile per caster level. A small earthquake, ash and steam within a week, a **full erupting volcano within a month**. **Never cast:** it was considered to end the orc and goblin threat, but the danger to dwarves, elves and other underground cities was too great. | A mantle of sun diamonds (double-strength fire resistance), a *helm of brilliance*, and a **mythallar placed at the site**. |
| ***Mavin's earthfast*** | Makes tunnels **immune to cave-ins** (a mile per caster level, lasts a year per caster level). Used for **dangerous mining**, and by cities to **stop streets and buildings from weathering**. | A stone of controlling earth elementals, six chunks enchanted with *transmute mud to rock*, and an earth elemental, all consumed. |
| ***Proctiv's move mountain*** | How enclaves were made: sever a mountain, leaving a flat mesa, **flip it flat side up, and make it float permanently**. Then build a city on it. | A **mythallar** set in the new city within two weeks, a flying or levitation device, and a crystal ball. |
| ***Tolodine's killing wind*** | A killing cloud (300 ft per caster level, 1 hour per level, 1 round to cast). One save or die. | **Drains 2 levels** from the caster. Sacrificing more makes the save harder (–1 per 2 levels, max –10), but each extra level has a **5% chance the caster's essence is pulled into the negative energy plane forever** (alive: dead and beyond resurrection; undead: trapped there). Rhexx: that plane's role is now the **Shadowfell**. |
| ***Valdick's spheresail*** | Spelljamming: travel between worlds' crystal spheres. Rhexx saved it for another video. | — |
| ***Ioulaum's longevity*** | Huge damage in an area (range 12,000 ft); **every living creature it kills adds a year to the caster's life**. Constructs and undead are damaged but add nothing. | Full details lost. |

- **Mavin** also invented *meteor swarm*, *flesh to stone* and *wall of iron*, and an 11th-level spell.
- **Sacrificing levels to cast** was common. Before the ban, the only limit was skill, and **life force could make up for missing skill**. In theory a skilled enough caster could become a demigod.
- **Ioulaum's fate:** the phaerimm drained the magic keeping him alive, so he **became a lich, abandoned his enclave and went into seclusion on the Sword Coast**. Never seen again; fate unknown.
- **Upper and lower Netheril:** nobles lived in the floating cities, commoners on the ground.

## Ideas Brodie asked for (2026-10-20; not decided)
- **The cost is the hook:** 10th-level spells drain the caster. **Dawnfire** could pay that price instead, which would explain why every enclave wants Neverwinter.
- **The Xinlenal mythallar as the engine:** whoever finishes or steals it has a battery for 10th-level casting; mythallar plus Dawnfire could be the full recipe.
- **Weave mythal:** a house seals its estate or district, or Renaer wants one over the city after the Dance.
- **Earthfast:** the mining house's dream for Dawnfire tunnels; or the 1486 Chasm seal as an earthfast-style effect whose year-per-level timer is running out.
- **Create volcano:** Mount Hotenow, where Dawnfire comes from.
- **Move mountain:** the **Earthmote** under the Moonstone Mask as a failed or test casting; a house wanting a floating estate gives a literal upper and lower city.
- **Killing wind:** the Shadowfell risk fits Shar, or the anti-god group using her tactics.
- **Ioulaum's longevity:** a villain motive for an aging house head; Ioulaum himself might be nearby.
