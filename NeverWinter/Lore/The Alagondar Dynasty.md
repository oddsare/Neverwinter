# The Alagondar Dynasty

The **Alagondar Dynasty** ruled [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] for over six decades, from the late 14th century DR until their tragic end in the [[Lore/The Three Cataclysms|Mount Hotenow eruption]] of 1451 DR.

They were beloved rulers who kept Neverwinter stable and prosperous through incredibly turbulent times, including the [[Lore/The Three Cataclysms|Spellplague]] itself.

---

## Founder: Lord Nasher Alagondar

**Birth:** Unknown (early 1300s DR estimated)
**Reign as Lord:** Unknown start - 1385 DR
**Reign as King:** 1385 DR - ~1400 DR
**Death:** ~1400 DR (old age)
**Title:** Lord of Neverwinter (later King)

### The Reluctant King

**Lord Nasher Alagondar** was an aging veteran adventurer and devout worshiper of [[Lore/Pantheon of Faerun|Tyr]] who ruled Neverwinter as "Lord" rather than "King" for most of his reign.

**As Lord (Pre-1385 DR):**
- Ruled fairly and justly
- Maintained Neverwinter's peace and prosperity
- Defended against [[Locations/Luskan|Luskan]] aggression
- Supported the [[Factions/The Harpers|Many-Starred Cloak]] (order of good wizards)
- Promoted justice and fairness (Tyrran faith)
- Frowned upon greed
- Kept the city well-defended both physically and magically

**Rejection of Kingship:**
> Lord Nasher rejected the position of King of Neverwinter for much of his life, preferring the simpler title of "Lord." Only in old age did he accept the crown by public acclamation.

### Leadership During the Wailing Death (1372 DR)

When the magical plague struck Neverwinter:
- Lord Nasher maintained order during chaos
- Coordinated the search for a cure
- Provided resources to healers and mages
- Kept the city from descending into panic
- Was praised for his steady leadership

### Accepting the Crown (1385 DR)

After surviving the [[Lore/The Three Cataclysms|Spellplague]] and seeing Neverwinter through its darkest hour:
- The people demanded he become King
- Nasher finally accepted out of duty
- **King Nasher Alagondar** ruled briefly as monarch
- United Neverwinter in the face of magical catastrophe
- Died peacefully of old age around 1400 DR

### Legacy

- Founded the Alagondar royal line
- Set the standard for just rulership
- Kept Neverwinter independent and prosperous
- Protected the city from both mundane and magical threats
- Remembered as "The Just King" or "The Reluctant King"

## King Bann Alagondar

**Reign:** ~1400 DR - 1451 DR
**Relationship:** Son of Nasher Alagondar
**Death:** 1451 DR (Mount Hotenow eruption)
**Duration:** ~51 years

### The Second King

**King Bann Alagondar** was Nasher's son and the second ruler of the Alagondar Dynasty.

**Challenges of His Reign:**
- Inherited a kingdom recovering from the Spellplague
- Magic remained unstable and dangerous
- Economic hardship from collapsed magical trade
- Pressure from Netherese loyalists trying to infiltrate the government
- Threats from Luskan and orc tribes
- Rebuilding what the Spellplague destroyed

### Achievements

Despite the difficult circumstances:
- **Kept Neverwinter stable** through the dark post-Spellplague period
- **Maintained independence** from Netheril's shadow empire
- **Preserved Alagondar traditions** of justice and fairness
- **Rebuilt the economy** slowly but steadily
- **Defended the city** from external threats
- **Ruled for over 50 years** - a remarkable achievement given the era

### The Royal Family (1451 DR)

By 1451 DR, King Bann had:
- A Queen (name lost to history or unrecorded)
- Multiple heirs (exact number unknown)
- Extended family members
- A stable line of succession

The family was beloved by the people and seen as guarantors of Neverwinter's continued stability.

## The Eruption and the End (1451 DR)

**Year of Knowledge Unearthed, 1451 DR**

When [[Lore/The Three Cataclysms|Mount Hotenow erupted]]:

### That Terrible Day

- King Bann Alagondar was in Castle Never (the royal palace)
- The Queen and heirs were also present
- Castle Never was at the epicenter of the destruction
- The palace was completely obliterated
- **All members of the Alagondar family were killed**

### Official Record

According to historical records:
- The entire Alagondar royal family perished
- No heirs survived
- No distant relatives came forward
- The dynasty ended after just over 60 years
- The royal bloodline was extinct

## The Mystery of Survivors

**Official Position:** No Alagondars survived.

**Rumors and Speculation (1525 DR):**

However, over the 74 years since the eruption, various rumors have persisted:

### The Hidden Heir Theory
- Some believe a distant Alagondar relative survived
- Perhaps a bastard child or secret marriage
- Could have been away from the city during the eruption
- Might not even know their heritage

### The Neverember Claim — CANONICALLY FRAUDULENT

[[NPCs/Dagult Neverember|Lord Dagult Neverember]] publicly claimed descent from Nasher Alagondar through an alleged bastard son, **Vers Never**.

**Dagult's public story:**
- Vers Never was supposedly Nasher's bastard son.
- Vers left Neverwinter for Waterdeep in the 14th century DR.
- Vers married **Mirtria Ember** there, founding the **Neverember** family line.
- Therefore Dagult is a cadet-branch Alagondar heir.

**What canon actually says:**
- **The claim is provably false.** Canonical Waterdhavian aristocrat [[NPCs/Indrina Lamsensettle|Indrina Lamsensettle]] commissioned genealogical research and found "enough genealogical data left intact to prove that Dagult's claim of lineage was completely fabricated." She found **no blood ties between Dagult and Nasher Alagondar.**
- Dagult **knows** (or at minimum suspects) — he actively suppressed dissent:
  - Paid scribes to find or forge legitimizing books
  - Burned books that cast doubt
  - **Sought the death of rival Alagondar claimants** to clear the field
- Vers Never's existence is itself flagged in canon as "conflicting records / unconfirmed rumors" — he may never have existed at all.

**Implications:**
- Dagult is **not Alagondar.** He cannot open Alagondar bloodline-locked vaults. He cannot wear the [[Lore/The Lost Crown and Hidden Alagondar Vaults|Lost Crown of Neverwinter]] (or it kills him with cold/heat burst).
- [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer Neverember]] has the same lineage problem — he is Dagult's son, biologically a Neverember, not an Alagondar.
- The fact that Renaer styles himself "**Lord**" rather than "King" is doubly significant in canon: not just modesty, but acknowledgment that he cannot legitimately claim the Alagondar throne.

### Why It Matters

If a legitimate Alagondar heir exists:
- Could challenge Renaer's rule
- Might unite factions currently divided
- Could be manipulated by enemies of Neverwinter
- Represents hope for "the old Neverwinter"

## The Alagondar Legacy (1525 DR)

### What They're Remembered For

**Justice and Fairness:**
- The Alagondars were known for even-handed rule
- No corruption in their courts
- Protected the common people
- Resisted pressure from wealthy merchants

**Stability:**
- 60+ years of continuous, just rule
- Survived the Wailing Death
- Survived the Spellplague
- Only ended by natural disaster, not internal strife

**Independence:**
- Kept Neverwinter free from Luskan
- Resisted Netherese manipulation
- Maintained membership in the Lords' Alliance
- Never became anyone's puppet

### Modern Sentiment

**Among the People:**
- Elderly who remember the Alagondars speak of them fondly
- "The Golden Days" before the eruption
- Symbol of Neverwinter's former glory
- Some wish for an Alagondar restoration

**Among the Nobility:**
- Mixed feelings - some supported, some opposed
- [[Lore/Code Legal of Neverwinter|The Code Legal]] still bears their stamp
- Some noble families claim marriage ties to Alagondars
- Used as political football in modern disputes

**Political Implications:**
- The [[Factions/The Harpers|Sons of Alagondar]] resistance movement (mostly disbanded)
- Used by those opposing current government
- Questions about Renaer's legitimacy tied to Alagondar claims

## Alagondar Artifacts and Relics

> **CANON (updated 2026-05-15):** After the destruction of Neverwinter in 1451 DR, **survivors hid the treasures of the Alagondar family in secret rooms and vaults across the city.** These caches are **magically protected** and will **only open to a true heir of the Alagondar bloodline.** Among the hidden treasures is the **fabled Lost Crown of Neverwinter.**

This single piece of canon is one of the most plot-load-bearing details in the campaign. See dedicated lore file: [[Lore/The Lost Crown and Hidden Alagondar Vaults]].

### The Lost Crown of Neverwinter (the Alagondar Crown)
- **Status:** Lost since the 1451 Cataclysm. Hidden by survivors. Never recovered.
- **Significance:** Proof of royal authority. The artifact a true Alagondar heir would use to claim the throne.
- **Location:** In one of the hidden Alagondar vaults across Neverwinter (specific location unknown — possibly beneath Castle Never, possibly in a Neverdeath crypt, possibly somewhere ordinary made extraordinary by warding).
- **Mechanism:** vault is magically sealed; only a true Alagondar bloodline-bearer can open it.

### The Seal of Neverwinter
- The royal seal used to authenticate official documents.
- Likely also hidden by survivors. Same bloodline-lock mechanism presumed.
- Possession plus the Crown = uncontested royal claim.

### Royal Records & other treasures
- Genealogical archives, regalia, signet rings, private royal correspondence.
- Some likely in additional bloodline-locked vaults.
- Could prove or disprove specific Alagondar descent claims — politically explosive in any direction.

## DM Notes

### Using Alagondar History

**Respect the Legacy:**
The Alagondars were genuinely good rulers. They weren't perfect, but they were just and beloved. Don't make them secretly evil.

**The Succession Question:**
Whether any Alagondars survived is up to you:
- **Yes:** Creates succession drama
- **No:** Maintains the tragedy
- **Unknown:** Keeps mystery alive

**Renaer's Claim:**
Consider whether:
- He truly believes he has Alagondar blood
- He knows it's false but thinks he's doing good
- It's a complete fabrication for power

**Political Factions:**
Different groups have different views:
- Alagondar loyalists (want restoration)
- Neverember supporters (want stability)
- Opportunists (want chaos for profit)
