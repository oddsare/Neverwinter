## 1. Before history: gods vs. primordials
- The world, **Abeir-Toril**, begins this story **locked in an ice age**. Whatever came before is myth.
- Every culture's myth agrees on one thing: a great **war between the gods and the primordials**.
  - **Gods:** powers formed from the hopes, fears and wishes of living beings.
  - **Primordials:** raw elemental forces that grew minds of their own.
- When the ice retreated it revealed **one supercontinent**, **Merrouroboros**.

## 2. Days of Thunder (~ -35,000 to -30,000 DR)
**The creator races** were the first thinking peoples:
- **Sarrukh:** reptilian serpent-folk
- **Batrachi:** amphibians
- **Aearee:** bird-folk, little known
- **Early humans:** still primitive

### The sarrukh empire
- A mysterious god, **the World Serpent**, gave the sarrukh **magic and smithing** in exchange for **obedience and blood sacrifice**.
- They built **the greatest empire the planet ever saw**, believed to cover **the whole world**.
- **The Nether Scrolls:** at their peak, the sarrukh gathered **every scrap of magical knowledge in the world** into a set of scrolls. The text **adapts to each reader**, so a scroll can always teach you something new. They're the most powerful mortal-made magic item in the Realms. **Most magic cast today traces back to them.** They were lost and scattered when the empire fell.
- **The fall:**
  - **A crisis of faith.** Every race in the empire was forced to worship the World Serpent, but each wanted different things from it (tyranny for the sarrukh, freedom for their slaves). The god **split into warring aspects**, which deepened the civil strife.
  - **Decadence.** The sarrukh made the **yuan-ti** (and nagas) as a servant race, handed them everything (war, diplomacy, trade, labor, guarding), and **sank into hedonism**. Over thousands of years the empire crumbled, and the yuan-ti took over many of its cities.

### The batrachi and the Tearfall
- The **batrachi** rose next, and fought the **titans and giants of the north** (whose god is **Annam the All-Father**).
- **Losing**, and without divine help, the batrachi **performed rituals that freed imprisoned primordials**. Gods came down to fight them, and the war reignited.
- A draconic primordial, **Asgorath**, **hurled a moon or asteroid** into the world: **the Tearfall**. It shattered the land and created **the Sea of Fallen Stars**.
- **The batrachi were destroyed.** Some fled to other planes, some went under the sea and **devolved into amphibious monster races**, and most died.

### The split
- **Ao, the Overgod**, above all gods and primordials, usually only watches. The Tearfall forced him to act.
- He **split the world in two**: **Abeir** for the primordials and **Toril** for the gods, ending the war.
- The supercontinent broke into the continents of today. **Faerûn** is the one almost all D&D takes place on.

## 3. The Dawn Age (-30,000 to -24,000 DR)
- **Dragons appear.** Many myths say Asgorath's moon was **full of dragon eggs** that fell like meteors across the world.
- Within about a thousand years they were adults and ancients. **The yuan-ti retreated**, and the **aearee fell**, since they could no longer hide in the sky.
- Only the **titans and giants** (their kingdom **Ostoria**) could match dragons.

### Dragons vs. giants
- They fought for **4,000–6,000 years**. **Giants and dragons still hate each other in their blood.**
- Meanwhile other races got room to grow:
  - **The first elves** came through portals from the **Feywild**.
  - **The first orcs** came through a portal in the northern mountains, opened by one of the creator races.
  - **Dwarves** grew in the deep places, undisturbed for millennia.
  - **Nagas** (created by the sarrukh), **halflings** (always in others' shadows), goblinoids and ogres.

### The dragons break themselves
- The giants' gods follow one leader, **Annam**. The dragons' gods had **no single leader**, and dragons are individualists. They were **deeply religious**; the giant war itself was really their gods' feud.
- **The Draconic holy wars** split them by faith: what Asgorath was, old gods vs. new ones, and above all **Bahamut vs. Tiamat**.
- The result: **chromatics** (black, blue, white, red, green) against **metallics** (gold, silver, bronze, copper, brass).
- The dragons crippled the giants and pushed them north, but **weakened themselves**. They eventually **abandoned their gods**, too late.

### How magic works: the Weave
- **The Weave** is an invisible fabric of magic through everything.
- **A normal spell** is like scooping river water in your fist and carrying it to a flower: fast, easy and crude, and some spills.
- **Elven high magic** is like **digging a channel** from the river to the flower:
  - It takes years or centuries to learn, many casters working together, and a long casting.
  - If you don't finish, it's wasted.
  - **Once done, it's permanent** and hard to undo. Done badly, it **floods or drains** parts of the world.

### The Dracorage Mythal
- The elves wove a **high magic curse tied to the King-Killer Star**, a red comet that appears every few decades or centuries. **Whenever it's in the sky, every dragon goes mad**: they **eat their own young, destroy their own lairs and cities, and turn on their allies**, for tendays at a time. It affects anything with dragon blood.
- **Dragons can never hold cities or a civilization again.** The **Avariel** (winged elves) were nearly wiped out defending the ritual site.
- **The dragons struck back:** a desperate spell to destroy the comet. **They missed and hit the moon, Selûne.** The chunks became the **Tears of Selûne**, the trail of asteroids that still follows the moon. Their failed attempt also left rings of standing stones (the **Hills of the Seven Lost Gods**).

## 4. The First Flowering (-24,000 DR onward)
Elves spread across the continent. About **7,000 years of relative peace**, which is about nine elven lifetimes.

**Major elven kingdoms and their rough modern locations:**
- **Aryvandaar** (gold elves): the **High Forest**
- **Miyeritar** (green and moon elves): later the **High Moor**
- **Ilythiir** (dark elves): the **Shaar**, the forest of Amtar
- **Syorpiir**, **Orishaar**, **Thearnytaar**, **Eiellûr** *(spellings uncertain)*
- **Shantel Othreier**
- **Illefarn**: around **Ardeep and Kryptgarden**, the Dessarin valley, **later the site of Waterdeep**
- **Keltormir**: today's **Tethyr, Amn and Calimshan**

### The Sundering (~-17,600 DR)
- Hundreds of high mages gathered. **Thirteen** cast the spell: **twelve channeling, one directing**. **Dark elves weren't invited**, and many opposed it.
- **Huge pieces of the continent were torn up and flung into the sea** to create **Evermeet**, the elves' holy island. A **piece of Arvandor**, the elven heaven, was attached. **The elven gods (the Seldarine) approved** and **helped finish the ritual** when it nearly failed.
- **The magic rippled through time.** Reality was rewritten so the island **had always existed**, possibly affecting the Tearfall in the past, and later the **Second Sundering in the 1480s**.
- **The cost:** thousands of elves dead, settlements destroyed, and **regions starved of magic**. This is believed to be **why wild magic and dead magic zones** exist.

## 5. The Crown Wars (~-12,000 to -9,000 DR, about 3,000 years)
- **First Crown War:** **Aryvandaar** (gold elves, arrogant and elitist) tried to annex **Miyeritar**, which had become the center of elven art and high magic. Pressure turned into trade war, then **invasion and occupation**. Aryvandaar also threatened Shantel Othreier.
- **Second Crown War:** the **dark elves of Ilythiir** declared war on the gold elves and anyone who sided with them.
  - They conquered **Orishaar** and burned **Syorpiir** with **magical fire**, which was taboo, burning out whole borderlands.
  - Thearnytaar and Eiellûr attacked Ilythiir and lost more than half their forces to **"corrupt magic."**
  - They used **enslaved monsters, undead, and mind-controlled dragons**.
  - **Many of today's missing forests were burned in this war.**
- **During this time:** some elves fled to the sea and became **sea elves** (the Moonshaes, underwater). **The first mind flayers** arrived from the planet **Glyth** and built the underground city **Oryndoll**.
- **Third Crown War:** Aryvandaar invaded and conquered **Shantel Othreier**.
- **The Dark Disaster:** **killing storms** of black and olive mist and icy rain raged over **Miyeritar for months**. When they cleared, the forest kingdom was a **poisoned, blasted plain**: today's **High Moor**. **Nobody knows who cast it.** The theories are the gold elves (on purpose, or a test that backfired) or resistance fighters whose spell went wrong. Ninety dark elf wizards and three high mages went into the storm to stop it and were never seen again.
- **Fourth Crown War (~-10,450 DR):**
  - The dark elves' **"corrupt magic" was revealed**: they'd been drawing power from **forbidden gods**. Mainly **Lolth**, Corellon's former wife, who had tried to kill him and was cast out, now a demon lord, plus **Ghaunadaur**.
  - They summoned **demons**.
  - Over time everyone forgot the gold elves started the whole thing, and **blamed the dark elves**.
- **The Descent of the Drow:**
  - In answer to mass prayers, **Corellon cursed every dark elf**, guilty or innocent, soldiers and children alike.
  - **Physically:** the sun now pains them, so they had to flee into **the Underdark**.
  - **Spiritually:** they were cut off from the other elves. **Their trance shows only silence and darkness**, with no glimpses of Arvandor.
  - They were named **"dhaerow"** (traitor), which became **drow**.
- **Fifth Crown War:** the elven gods ruled that **Aryvandaar started it all**. The smaller kingdoms (plus revolts) brought down the gold elves. **~-9,000 DR: the Crown Wars end.**

## 6. The Founding Time (-9,000 to -3,000 DR)

### Dwarves
- **Bhaerynden**, the first great dwarf kingdom under the Shaar, was **crushed** when the fleeing drow poured into the Underdark. Later **the cavern roofs collapsed** for unknown reasons, killing many drow. **Gold dwarves** later resettled it as **the Great Rift**, still **the largest concentration of dwarves in the world**.
- **Shanatar:** founded earlier by **Taark Shanat the Crusader** under today's **Amn, Tethyr and Calimshan**.
  - Survivors fled there and became **shield dwarves**, more defensive and warlike.
  - Others went to Chult (wild dwarves) and the far north (arctic dwarves).
  - **Eight kingdoms**, one per son of Taark Shanat, each following a different dwarf god, so they **fought among themselves**.
- **Wars of Shanatar:**
  - **The Spawn Wars:** **deepspawn** (monsters that create perfect copies of anything they eat) were used by dwarf factions to **clone armies**. It became a dwarven **clone war**.
  - **The Spider Wars:** **drow invasions** united the dwarves. Three wars, ending in stalemate.
  - **The Mindstalker Wars** against the mind flayers of Oryndoll. Afterward, the kingdom **Barakuir** was found **empty**: the mind flayers had taken **Clan Duergar** and spent millennia **breeding and twisting them** into the **duergar**, joyless and prideless. It took them **4,000 years** to escape. They **hate other dwarves** for abandoning them.
- **Offshoot kingdoms:** **Delzoun** (the North and the Silver Marches; **Mithral Hall** and many fortresses are its remnants), **Ammarindar** (Graypeak Mountains, long-lasting), Haunghdannar and others.
- **Peak dwarven power: ~-5,600 DR.**

### Imaskar: the first human empire (~-8,350 to -2,488 DR)
- On the **Raurin Plateau**, far east. They were **masters of dimensional magic**, maybe learned from batrachi relics.
- **Portal gates** moved whole armies across the empire.
- **Setbacks:**
  - A sudden **kraken attack** destroyed the capital and split the empire in two.
  - Later, **the Silent Death**, a plague with crop blight, killed 15–20% even in the lightest-hit areas. **The people turned on their gods** and killed or exiled their priests.
- **The atrocity:** the ruler opened **portals to Earth** and **kidnapped tens of thousands of Egyptians and Mesopotamians** as slaves, then **sealed the planes** so their gods couldn't follow. The slaves later mixed with locals into the **Mulan**.
- **The shadow plane was discovered** in this era. The archwizard **Halaster** stopped a rival who misused it; later he hid in stasis and became **Halaster Blackcloak**, creator of **Undermountain** under Waterdeep.
- **The end (-2,488 DR):** the **Egyptian and Mesopotamian gods**, after millennia of searching, sent **avatars** through the void, raised a slave rebellion, and **sacked the empire's cities within ten days**. **Horus** killed the last ruler.

### Calimshan
- **Keltormir** (elves) was broken by a **flight of red dragons** whose fire destroyed clans, giant settlements and thousands of square miles of forest. It split into the forests of Mir and Tethyr and others.
- **Calim**, a powerful **djinni**, came with genie armies and **human and halfling slaves**, founded **Calimport**, and annihilated the red dragons.
- **Memnon**, an **efreeti**, settled to the north. They warred for **400 years** and created **the Calim Desert**. **The elves imprisoned both** with high magic: Calim bound into the sky, Memnon into the earth.
- **Shanatar's dwarves plus freed slaves** defeated the remaining genies. The humans took the south, the dwarves the north.
- Later, **human tomb robbers**, including **the human heir**, were killed in sacred dwarf tombs. That started a **3,000-year war**. **Calimshan won around -2,600 DR** and the dwarves **sealed off Deep Shanatar**.
- In the east, the psionic human kingdom **Jhaamdath** blocked Calimshan's expansion.

## 7. The Age of Humanity (-3,000 to 1,000 DR)

### Netheril (founded ~-3,500 DR)
- **Origin:** fishing villages around the **Narrow Sea**, in today's **Anauroch** desert, which was fertile then. They allied with the elves of **Eaerlann** against orcs, and **the elves taught them magic**. The shaman-king was **Nether**.
- **Two big events:**
  - **The Terraseer**, a mysterious lich who knew the past, present and future, **advised Netheril** at key moments. He was really an **ancient sarrukh lich**. His goals are unknown.
  - **They found the Nether Scrolls** in the ruins of **Aryvandaar** (High Forest, forbidden to settle) and looted them. The Terraseer helped translate them. They **abandoned elven magic for the scrolls'**.
- **Example spells:** *awaken* (gives a beast or plant intelligence) and ***alter beast*** (rewrites any creature's nature, and the changes **breed true**). The sarrukh made the yuan-ti and nagas with it, and it's said to be behind most of the world's species.
- **Ioulaum** created the first **mythallar**, a **glowing crystal sphere of near-infinite magic**. Casters didn't need rituals, components or concentration, and Netherese mages built **10th- and 11th-level spells**.
- **Floating cities:** Ioulaum used 11th-level magic to **shear off a mountain, flip it upside down and float it**, building on the flat base to escape orc raids. The elite followed, and archmages each ruled their own floating **enclave**.
- **Everyday magic everywhere:** cold boxes, street lights, magic purses. **All of it ran on the mythallars.**
- **The dark side:**
  - An anything-goes magocracy: **cruel experiments**, **damage to the Weave**, portals to dangerous places, even an attempt to **invade the Outlands**.
  - **They enslaved the gnomes for about a thousand years.** Elves and dwarves ran a secret escape route (the **Trail of Mists**).
  - **A gnome who escaped stole a Nether Scroll of illusion magic.** That's said to be why **gnomes are naturally good at illusion**. The gnomes hid in an **illusion-cloaked city** (*Songfarla*, spelling uncertain).
- **The phaerimm:** **magic-draining monsters** from below drained the land and enclaves, **beginning the Anauroch desert**. Ioulaum couldn't stop them and **abandoned his people**.
- **Karsus's Folly (-339 DR):**
  - The archwizard **Karsus** cast the **only 12th-level spell ever cast**, *Karsus's Avatar*, to **become a god** and wipe out the phaerimm.
  - **The Weave began to collapse.** The goddess of magic, **Mystryl, sacrificed herself** to shut it off and restart it.
  - Karsus was **turned to stone and died**. **Every mythallar failed**, and **nearly every floating city fell**. Netheril collapsed almost overnight.
  - The new goddess, **Mystra**, is stricter: **no spells of 10th level or higher**.
- **Where the Netherese went:**
  - the Dales and Cormanthyr
  - through Delzoun to the **Sword Coast North**
  - by flying ship to found **Halruaa**
  - **one whole enclave shifted into the Shadowfell**: the **Shades**, who return about 1,700 years later
- **The scrolls scattered:** some to elves, some to gnomes, some hidden under the Grandfather Tree in the High Forest, some given to Mystra. *Candlekeep Mysteries* reportedly has one hidden in an adventure.
- **Ioulaum** later merged himself with an **undead elder brain** he built from mind flayer pupils (the **alhoon**). He still exists as a **lich elder brain oracle**.
- **The sharn:** dark elf high mages lost in the **Dark Disaster** had become **the sharn**, blob-like beings asleep in the land who hate life-draining magic. They woke, fought the phaerimm for **600 years**, and sealed them behind **the Sharn Wall**, which **stopped Anauroch from spreading**. (Rhexx notes nearly every desert in Faerûn was made by magic.)
- **Fallen Netherese cities** are scattered everywhere, as far as the Sword Coast and Icewind Dale. Pure-blooded Netherese survive in a few isolated groups.
- **The Terraseer** appeared one last time to warn that Karsus would change magic forever, and was never seen again.

### Chult
- **Ubtao** tricked and **imprisoned the primordial Dendar the Night Serpent** (possibly the World Serpent) under a volcano. In return the gods gave him **Chult**, to guard the prison.
- His servants (**couatls**) fought the yuan-ti. He called **human tribes from an unknown western continent** (the Tabaxi, Eshowe and Thinguth). They were the original "Tabaxi"; the cat-folk were named after them.
- A **tarrasque** that served those tribes' god turned on everything and woke every thousand years, until **Ubtao's Chosen killed it with one punch**.
- **Mezro**, the holy city. Ubtao later **withdrew** into animal spirits. A **civil war (~-137 DR)** was ended by a Chosen who **raised undead armies**, and **those undead, dinosaurs included, still roam Chult**. Batiri goblins boomed afterward.

### Dwarves decline
- **The duergar** built **Gracklstugh** (the City of Blades). Under **Queen Deep Duerra**, they **destroyed Deep Shanatar (~-1,800 DR)**, and she **became a goddess**. Then the duergar slowly stagnated.
- **Delzoun** traded with Netheril, but the **phaerimm destroyed it** after Netheril fell. Its fortresses still stand.
- **Ammarindar** held out against orcs until **882 DR**, the longest-lasting shield dwarf kingdom.
- **Today:** gold dwarves of the **Great Rift**, halflings of **Luiren** (a halfling kingdom), and gnomes in their hidden city (about 12,000) and in Lantan. Gnomes are rare.

### Calimshan's wild history (short version)
Beholder invasions, more than once with a **beholder on the throne**; a **red dragon (Ilvarsalisar)** ruling from the throne for a century; plagues, great fires, five tidal waves, succession wars; and **the Night Wars with the drow**, which lasted generations, with about **75,000 humans enslaved and 150,000 killed**. **Calimport has a drow-held underside to this day.** It ended in a secret deal with the drow matrons. **Amn** and **Tethyr** split off around **500 DR**.

### Mulhorand and Unther
- After Imaskar fell, the Egyptian god-kings founded **Mulhorand** and the Mesopotamian god-kings founded **Unther**. They ruled for millennia.
- **Mulhorand invented the modern sailing ship (~-1,500 DR).** The **star elves** of the Yuirwood withdrew into a demiplane, and the elves left behind mixed with humans: **the Yuirwood is the half-elf heartland**.

### The Orcgate Wars (~-1,076 DR)
- **Thayd**, a renegade wizard, opened an **Imaskari-style portal** to a world of **religious orcs**. He died, but the gate stayed open **five years**.
- **Two kinds of orcs, per Rhexx:**
  - **Mountain orcs** (the old ones, from the north): brutal raiders who swarm out of the Spine of the World. **Most western half-orcs come from their raids.**
  - **Gray orcs** (from the gate): smarter and **deeply religious**, with powerful clerics.
- **The Battle of the Gods:** gray orc clerics summoned **avatars of Gruumsh and his pantheon**. **Ra fell to Gruumsh** and passed his power to Horus (Horus-Re) and into his soldiers' weapons, which became **orc-slaying**. Many Untheric god-kings died. **Tiamat** joined (Mesopotamian worship had drawn her back), and **Marduk sacrificed himself to kill her**. The orcs were driven back. Surviving gray orcs are small nomad tribes.

### Narfell and Raumathar
- **Mercenaries** from the Orcgate Wars founded **Narfell** (demon-worshipping, under a sorcerer devoted to **Orcus**) and **Raumathar** (Imaskari lore, portals, golems).
- **A 500-year war:** Narfell summoned frost giants and the demon lords **Kostchtchie** and **Eltab**. Raumathar called on white dragons and finally an **avatar of Kossuth**, the fire lord: **the Great Conflagration**. **Both kingdoms burned.**
  - Raumathar became **the Endless Wastes**.
  - **The Witches of Rashemen** restored order and **imprisoned Eltab**.
  - Narfell fell into barbarism. Many **tieflings** came from its demon pacts.
- **Thay:** the **Red Wizards**, named for Thayd's red-robed followers, **rebelled against Mulhorand** with **Eltab's help**, seceded, and **imprisoned Eltab beneath their new capital**.

### Jhaamdath and the elves
- **Jhaamdath** cut timber from the elven forest of **Nikerymath** and slaughtered elves who resisted, killing **nine in ten**. The elves answered with **high magic**: **great waves from the Sea of Fallen Stars** destroyed Jhaamdath (about 60,000 dead) and **permanently flooded the Vilhon Reach**.
- **Many green elves withdrew** from elven culture into deep forests and became the **wild elves**.
- **Illefarn** didn't fight the spreading humans. It **erased its own cities** with high magic and **left for Evermeet (~-1,100 DR)**. **Waterdeep** was later founded on its old capital, and **some of that residual high magic can still be felt in Waterdeep.**
- **Rystall Wood** fought the drow for 2,000 years (with Cormanthyr and **Eilistraee's drow**), then **fell to orcs**.
- **Eaerlann** lasted until **882 DR**, then fell to **demon-allied orcs**.

### Cormanthyr and the Dales Compact (1 DR)
- **Cormanthyr** (the elven court) chose **friendship with humans**. The **Dales Compact** let humans settle around it **on unforested, unclaimed land**, as long as they **never cut elven forests**. A **standing stone** marks it.
- **The Dale Reckoning calendar starts that year: 1 DR.** It's the first true union of friendship between races, the root of today's mixed cities like Waterdeep and Mirabar, and **the birth of half-elves** as a major people.
- **Later:** the visionary leader died, his heir was assassinated by hardliners, and after centuries of strife the elves began the **Retreat to Evermeet**. **Cormyr** formed just south.
- **Other notes:**
  - **The gnomes' original kingdom** in the eastern Shaar was **wiped out by orcs in ten days**.
  - **Vastar** was a true **orc kingdom** that lasted about a thousand years and raided Cormanthyr and Narfell. It spawned many eastern mountain orcs, until **humans, elves and dwarves** (with dwarf-made orc-slaying weapons) defeated it.

## 8. The present era (1,000 DR onward)
An age of **magical cataclysms and shifting worlds**: **dragonborn appear**, **gods walk the world again**, and a new divine order with **Ao** making himself known. Most published adventures are set **1485–1492 DR**.

## Threads that touch this campaign (open, not decided)
- **Maegera and bound primordials:** primordials imprisoned by gods and mortals are a pattern throughout this history (Dendar under Chult, and the batrachi freeing them).
- **Gauntlgrym** was a **Delzoun** city. This note covers Delzoun's rise and fall.
- **Illefarn** was the elven kingdom of this region (Ardeep, Kryptgarden, the Dessarin), and **its residual high magic lingers in Waterdeep**.
- **The Dracorage Mythal and the King-Killer Star:** whenever the comet appears, **every dragon goes mad**, including your dragons (Gnawbone, the Black Brothers, Arveiaturace, Zundaerazylym). Possibly kobolds too, since they have dragon blood.
- **Netheril and the Nether Scrolls:** **10th-level spells** were real, and Mystra banned them. That fits your [[Lore/Current Era]] rumor of enclaves near 10th-level magic. Netherese refugees came to the **Sword Coast North**. See [[Lore/Ed Greenwood - Netheril]].
- **Halruaa**, founded by fleeing Netherese, is where Zundaerazylym's **Never Tokens** came from.
- **Your race rules** ([[Lore/Races and Alignment]]):
  - **the drow's curse** came from Corellon, and their god Lolth
  - **the duergar** were twisted by mind flayers
  - **mountain orcs vs. gray orcs**: the raids behind half-orcs; the religious orcs of Gruumsh
  - **tieflings from demon pacts** (Narfell)
- **Thay and Eltab:** the Red Wizards' origin, and a demon lord imprisoned under their capital. See [[Lore/Ed Greenwood - Thay Today]].
- **Wild magic and dead magic zones** come from high magic draining the Weave.
