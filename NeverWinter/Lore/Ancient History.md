# Ancient History of Neverwinter

The land that would become [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] has a history stretching back thousands of years, predating human settlement by millennia.

---

## The Elven Kingdom of Illefarn

**Period:** After the [[Lore/Creation Myth|Crown Wars]]

The first major civilization in the [[Lore/Faerun|Savage Frontier]] was the elven kingdom of **Illefarn**, a bustling and prosperous nation that flourished in the lands of northwest Faerûn.

### Geography
Illefarn was vast, encompassing much of what is now:
- The Sword Coast North
- The region around modern Neverwinter
- Lands extending south toward present-day Waterdeep

### Culture
- Advanced elven civilization
- Centers of magic and learning
- Beautiful architecture in harmony with nature
- Thriving trade and culture

---

## Division into Three Nations

Eventually, the kingdom of Illefarn was divided into three separate elven nations:

1. **Iliyanbruen** (Most Prominent)
2. **Rilithar**
3. **Synnoria**

---

## Iliyanbruen - The Direct Predecessor

**Status:** Weakened successor state of Illefarn

**Iliyanbruen** was the most prominent of the three successor kingdoms and directly controlled the land where Neverwinter now stands.

### The Orc Invasions

The elven realm was gradually weakened by repeated invasions from:
- **Orc tribes** from the mountains
- **Goblinoid forces** from the hinterlands
- **Other hostile creatures**

These relentless attacks over centuries wore down Iliyanbruen's strength and population, setting the stage for human settlement.

---

## Eigersstor - The First Multi-Racial Settlement

**Founded:** Unknown exact date (centuries before Neverwinter's formal founding)
**Original Name:** Eigersstor
**Significance:** First multi-racial settlement in the region

### The Transition

As Iliyanbruen weakened from orc invasions, a new settlement emerged:
- **Eigersstor** was established as a multi-racial community
- Humans, elves, dwarves, and halflings lived together
- Built on the ruins and remnants of elven settlements
- Located in the ideal position - sheltered bay, warm winters, fertile lands

### Why This Location?

The site had natural advantages:
- **Geothermal Heat:** Warmth from [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Mount Hotenow]] heated the Neverwinter River
- **Natural Harbor:** Protected bay ideal for trade
- **Warm Winters:** Gardens could bloom year-round
- **Defensible Position:** Rivers and terrain provided natural barriers
- **Rich Resources:** Forest, fishing, and fertile soil

---

## From Eigersstor to Neverwinter

**The Name Change**

Eigersstor gradually became known as **Neverwinter** due to its most famous characteristic:

> The city's gardens bloomed throughout winter, warmed by the volcanic heat channeled through the river. Visitors marveled that winter never truly came to this northern jewel.

The name "Neverwinter" stuck, and the settlement grew into a proper city.

---

## The Founding of Civilized Neverwinter

**Estimated Period:** Several centuries before 1357 DR
**Status:** Became a center of civilization, peace, and culture

Once Neverwinter was formally established as a city:
- Developed reputation for skilled craftspeople ("City of Skilled Hands")
- Became known as the "Jewel of the North"
- Famous for:
  - Multi-colored glass lamps
  - Precision water-clocks
  - Exquisite jewelry
  - Year-round gardens

### Unbroken Prosperity

From its founding, Neverwinter enjoyed seemingly unbroken prosperity and peace for centuries. The city became:
- A marvel to visitors
- A center of trade and culture
- A beacon of civilization in the wild north
- A refuge from the chaos elsewhere in Faerûn

This golden age lasted for centuries until the [[Lore/Current Era|troubles of the late 14th and early 15th centuries DR]].

---

## The Elven Legacy

**Modern Era (1525 DR):**

Though Iliyanbruen fell long ago, traces of the elven presence remain:
- Ancient ruins in the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter Wood]]
- Occasional elven artifacts discovered during construction
- Some elven families claim descent from Iliyanbruen nobility
- Mysterious elven magic still lingers in certain locations

### Potential Plot Hooks
- Ancient elven vaults beneath the city
- Artifacts from Illefarn's heyday
- Descendants of Iliyanbruen seeking to reclaim ancestral lands
- Lingering elven magic affecting current events
- Connection between ancient elven sites and [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|current mysteries]]
