# The Blackstaff

**Canon reference.** Source: Forgotten Realms Wiki pages *Blackstaff*, *Blackstaff (staff)*, *Blackstaff Tower* and *Blackstaff Academy* (checked 2026-10-18).

## What it is
- **The Blackstaff** is a **title**: the **Archmage of Waterdeep**.
- Whoever holds it is master of **the staff** (also called the Blackstaff), **Blackstaff Tower**, and the **Blackstaff Academy**.
- It started as a nickname for **Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun**, after his staff. When he died, it became the title his successors use.

## The Blackstaffs, in order
1. **Khelben Arunsun:** Chosen of Mystra, built the tower, nearly a thousand years old. Died in **1374 DR** raising the hidden city of Rhymanthiin. Considered the greatest.
2. **Tsarra Chaadren:** half-elf sorceress. Disguised herself as Khelben in public so people thought he was still alive. Died in **1399 DR** fighting vampire wizards.
3. **Kyriani Agrivar:** half-drow, a Moonstar. Carried the staff home after the 1399 battle. Began the tradition of a **public proclamation from the tower top** when a new Blackstaff takes office.
4. **Krehlan Arunsun:** son of Khelben and **Laeral Silverhand**. Blackstaff from **1400 DR**. Later killed.
5. **Ashemmon:** half-elf sorcerer of Rhymanthiin, Krehlan's friend. Blackstaff from **1424 DR**.
6. **Samark Dhanzscul:** carried the *Duskstaff*. Secretly murdered in **1479 DR**.
7. **Vajra Safahr:** took the title in **1479 DR**, the **youngest Blackstaff ever** (born 1455). Lawful neutral, 18th-level wizard.

## The Blackstaff's Heir
- Each Blackstaff chooses an **heir**.
- **Vajra's Heir is [[NPCs/Laraelra Harsard|Laraelra Harsard]]**, chosen over Eiruk Weskur in 1479.
- The Heir must **touch the Blackstaff** to settle into their new powers.
- **Only the Blackstaff or the Heir can safely carry and wield the staff.**
- **While the Heir touches the staff, the Blackstaff's own power can be channeled through it.**
- When someone becomes Blackstaff **or Heir**, a copy of their mind and knowledge becomes part of the tower.
- **If the Blackstaff dies, the Heir must go to the tower immediately.** At least twice, a Blackstaff died away from the tower and the Heir **went insane** until they got back.

## The staff
- A series of staves, not just one. Khelben's was black, about 7 feet long, topped with a claw.
- Vajra's "true blackstaff" is runed duskwood with silver veins, topped with an axe head shaped like a snarling wolf with glowing green eyes.
- **Powers:** everything a *staff of power* does, plus a permanent *blackstaff* spell that can:
  - boost melee attacks
  - dispel magic on touch
  - drain a spellcaster's spells or power
  - channel mind-affecting spells

## Blackstaff Tower
- In Waterdeep's **Castle Ward**. Looks like a windowless three-story black tower; inside there are **a dozen or more levels**, some reachable only by magic.
- **Repairs itself**, warded against scrying and magical intrusion. Tunnels link it to the palace and other spots in the city. One library door opens **one way to Candlekeep**.
- **The Blackstaff controls the tower:** opens any door by thought, hears any conversation inside, and knows every magic item in it.
- **Advisors in the walls:** since **1415 DR**, glowing green copies of every former Blackstaff live in the tower. They can't leave, but the Blackstaff can consult them. (They're copies, not true spirits.)

## Blackstaff Academy
- An informal elite school of about **50 apprentices**, wizards and sorcerers, run out of the tower's hidden levels.
- Founded after **Laeral Silverhand** moved in with Khelben in **1357 DR**.
- **Vajra's goal:** make it **the center of magical study in Faerûn**. She roams it in magically silent boots and sometimes lets top students add spells to her own spellbook.
- **Dues:** 10 gp a month and up. Any new spell a student invents goes into the tower library.
