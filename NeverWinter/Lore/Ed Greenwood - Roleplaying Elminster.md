# Roleplaying Elminster (Ed Greenwood)

Canon reference, summarized in my own words. Doubles as a guide for playing **very powerful NPCs**. **Campaign connections are open threads.**

## Elminster isn't Ed
Ed portrayed Elminster at Gen Cons and public appearances for years and now looks a bit like him, so people assume Elminster is his self-insert. But Ed created him **at age five**, as a shy kid. Elminster is also **much ruder** than Ed would ever be comfortable being. Ed is, however, the world's greatest expert on him.

## How to play him
- **Accent:** don't worry about it. Say **"ye"** now and then and talk normally the rest of the time.
- **Truly ancient:** older than dirt, has seen it all, has outlived everyone from his childhood **and the kingdoms of that time**.
- **Gruff, cantankerous, but kind.** A shrewd judge of every creature, so **rarely surprised**.
- **Always a step ahead:** player characters should feel he **already knows their secrets** and has **plans B, C, D and E** if things go wrong or they turn on him.
- **Mysterious, and above all whimsical:** zags when everyone expects a zig, within limits.
- **Spreads magic:** Mystra wants magic spread through the world, so he **hands out minor magic items** (glowstones and the like), teaches spellcasting, and warns of pitfalls (for example, that a caster's fireball will pull to the left). **Broke young wizards may find a purse of coins** has appeared; he was an accomplished **thief** in his youth.
- **Teases, but never to the point of real upset.**
- **A very good actor.** **Can't be embarrassed:** naked, covered in filth or shown up, it doesn't faze him.
- **Doesn't get angry, gets even.** Plays the long game, arranging things that pay off **years** later.
- **Doesn't need sleep** (drawing on the Weave). **Can't run out of spells:** he can call on the Weave directly to counter or shape magic even without the spell.
- **Rarely throws magic around.** He believes **mastery of magic is knowing when not to use it**. Showy spells are for young wizards who still need to impress, unless he's truly exasperated.
- **Short version:** if you meet him, be nice. He's a much better friend than enemy. And if you need a spell or scroll right now, **just ask**.

## His abilities (Ed's version, summarized)
- **Arcane Recovery (once a day):** after a short rest, regains all spell slots of 5th level and lower.
- **Elminster's Contingency:** if he's about to die or have his soul trapped, he's **teleported to a demiplane safehold** (with a full spell library) and **healed**. Needs **10 days** before it works again.
- **Kiss of the Weave (three times a day):** turns a **failed save into a success**. Recharges instantly, but only where the Weave reaches (Realmspace).
- **Magic Resistance:** advantage on saves against spells and magical effects.
- **Spell Mastery:** can cast any wizard spell he knows, has prepared, or carries in books or scrolls **as a ritual**.
- **Weave Mastery:** as a free action, can twist the Weave to create **any magical effect, spell or item effect he knows or has seen**, with **no somatic or material components** (like a bonus-action spell). Costs **one level of exhaustion**, which a ring he wears can undo. Realmspace only.
- **Multiattack:** casts a spell, then up to two melee weapon attacks; up to three attack actions a turn (two if he used psionics).
- **Psionics:** an **18th-level psionic talent** (d12), works like a sorcerer but spends **hit points** instead of sorcery points to power mind over body. Uses psionic talent, psychic strike, mind over body, psychic aura and tower of iron will, **mostly defensively**.
- **Silver Fire (three times a day, 1 minute):** resistance to cold and fire, immunity to magic and psionics that affect the mind, can breathe underwater, and as an action fires a **bolt of silver fire** 5 feet wide and 60 feet long (Dex save DC 19, **10d8 radiant**, half on a success).
- **Unerring Direction:** always knows north on any world or plane that has one, and always knows up and down underground or indoors.
- **Sleepless:** can stay awake indefinitely in Realmspace without losing alertness, but becomes fatigued, so he only does it in emergencies.
- **Loremaster:** double proficiency on Arcana, History, Nature and Religion checks; initiative can be an Intelligence or Dexterity check.
- **Spell shaping:** can swap a spell's damage type (acid, cold, fire, force, lightning, necrotic, radiant, thunder) for another on that list; once per short or long rest, can change a spell's saving throw ability; can **disguise how his spells look**.
- **Alchemical Casting:** spend an extra slot to boost a spell: 1st-level adds **2d10 force** damage (on the casting turn); 2nd-level boosts range (30 feet or more becomes **1 mile**; touch becomes 20 feet); 3rd-level raises the save DC by **2**.
- **Prepared-spell swap:** as a bonus action, replace one prepared spell with another from his spellbook, once per short or long rest.
- **Master of Magic (once per long rest):** as a bonus action, gain the ability to cast **any spell from any class's list** (of a level he has slots for, no material components, counts as a wizard spell). This lets him exceed the usual one-spell-per-turn limit.
- **Gear:** a huge arsenal: several daggers, **three ever-smoking pipes**, the mighty **Staff of Mystra's Herald**, and **small spell-storing trinkets** sewn into his garments that release a stored spell when snapped (he can toss one to someone or break it himself). (The bit about his underwear is the hosts joking.)

## Campaign connections (open threads)
- **Abundant magic** ([[Campaign Decisions]] §3): Elminster handing out magic for Mystra is part of *why* magic is everywhere.
- **Mystra and her Chosen:** Elminster is her Chosen. The open reminder about **what Mystra and her Chosen do about the 10th-level push** ([[Campaign Decisions]] §3) involves people like him.
- **The powder (Dawnfire)** boosts all non-divine magic ([[Lore/The Powder]]). Whether it affects someone who draws on the Weave directly, and what he'd think of it, is open.
- **Playing your level 10–15 house heads:** a step ahead, backup plans, gets even, long game, rarely shows full power.
