# Ed Greenwood — Lolth's Dark Queen

> Source: **Realms Lore** (YouTube), Ed Greenwood. Transcript added 2026-10-19. Summarized in my own words.
> Ed's "present" is the **late 1490s DR**; the campaign is in 1505.

## The rumor
- **The drow** are the evil dark elves of the Underdark who raid the surface to pillage, kill, and kidnap people into short-lived slavery. **Lolth**, the Demon Queen of Spiders, wants to rule all drow, and through them the whole world.
- **Merchants who trade at Skullport** and other Upperdark trade meets say Lolth has anointed a **"Dark Queen"**: a **war leader** commanding a strike force now on the move in the Underdark.
- **Her targets** are the caravans, patrols and sentries of **drow settlements that don't bow to Lolth**.
- **Her message:** give absolute loyalty to Lolth, or be destroyed. She spares a few survivors, usually **after cutting off their hands**, to spread the word. Sometimes she adds: **"We're coming for you."**

### Who's telling it
**Rorgo Illith'vir**, a scarred veteran merchant.
- He wears a **necklace of eyes**: an enchanted collar of eye-like gems that **grow into death tyrants** (undead beholders) under the wearer's mental control.
- He has spent a long career trading in **fungus-derived drugs and fiery wines**, as well as more legal goods.

## Who she is: Ryelahavdra Faentalach
- **A "Favored One":** a **female draegloth**. She looks like an **eight-foot-tall, muscular, beautiful drow woman with two pairs of arms** and long-taloned fingers.
- **Appearance:** **thigh-length white hair**, blazing **crimson eyes**. She wears a **leather war harness** like many veteran drow soldiers: a tight weave of buckled straps with small armor plates slid into leather pouches, and **at least a dozen dagger sheaths** sewn all over it.
- **Character:** a wily, cunning war leader.
- **Bodyguard:** about **forty heavily armed fighting priestesses of Lolth**. They're **fanatically loyal**, because Lolth has ordered it and made clear they'll **rise in her favor, or fall, with the Dark Queen**.

### Her army and tactics
- **Officers:** ambitious, ruthless **priestesses of Lolth**, riding steeds they **mind-control** to keep them loyal and eager.
- **Soldiers:** veteran **male drow** troops.
- **Tactics:** always on the move, using **hit-and-run raids** on non-Lolthite drow everywhere in the Underdark, rather than sieges or long battles.
- **The plan for now:** **wear down** drow who don't worship Lolth. Conquering cities and territory comes later.

### Game details
- **Chaotic evil draegloth:** fighter 4 / sorcerer 4 / **priestess of Lolth 16**.
- **Summon a yochlol:** she can, without fail, **summon a yochlol once every six hours** to fight for her as an ally.
- **Belt of spider scarabs:** she throws a scarab, and it **expands in mid-air into a giant wolf spider** under her mental control, which fights for her until it dies.
- **Innate abilities**, on top of everything a 16th-level priestess of Lolth can do:
  - ***Spider climb* at will**, with no spell needed. She can stand on a wall or ceiling with her hands free, or cling with her hands and keep her feet free.
  - **Passweb at will:** she moves freely through **webs of any kind**, including vines and knotted rope "webbing" like a ship's rigging.
- **Rare or unique spells** Lolth has gifted her, including *sharpweb* (below).

### Spell: *Sharpweb*
*3rd-level conjuration*

- **Casting time:** 1 action
- **Range:** 60 feet
- **Target:** a point or creature of your choice within range
- **Components:** V, S, M (a strand of spiderweb)
- **Duration:** up to 1 hour (until it expires, or you will it out of existence)
- **Classes:** cleric (of Lolth), sorcerer, wizard
- **Effect:** a straight line of thick, sticky webbing shoots from just in front of your hand toward your target.
  - If it hits an **immobile object**, the line hangs **taut in the air** between that object and the point in mid-air where it started.
  - The strand bristles with **knife-sharp cilia** sticking out a foot in every direction along its whole length, slashing anyone it hits, brushes or lashes.
- **Damage:** any creature that **starts its turn touching the line, or touches it during its turn,** makes a **Dexterity saving throw**.
  - **Success:** 1d4 + a small bonus slashing damage *(the exact bonus is garbled in the captions)*.
  - **Failure:** 2d4 + 2 slashing damage.
  - This repeats every turn until the creature gets free of the line.
- **Fire:** the line is **flammable**.
  - The caster can **mentally ignite it** at any time during the spell. It gives off acrid smoke, **burns away completely in 1 minute**, and deals an extra **2d4 fire damage**.
  - **Anyone else** can set it alight the same way with a torch, burning oil or any open flame, whatever the caster wants.
- **A stronger version:** Elminster believes the Dark Queen knows a **higher-level version** that hurls a **constricting sphere of sticky webbing** at a creature, but he doesn't know the details yet.

## What she's queen of: nowhere, yet
- **Lolth has promised her a surface kingdom of her own**, if she can found it.
- Elminster still has friends among the drow. They pass on what other drow guess Lolth intends:
  1. **Conquer weakened non-Lolthite drow settlements** outright, so drow loyal to Lolth can take them over.
  2. Once **about six Upperdark settlements** have fallen, their new inhabitants, guided by her army, will **raid non-Lolthite drow** in the Underdark. They're **forbidden to raid the surface**.
  3. **Then her army begins forays to the surface.** It will start with **ruins and caverns in thinly settled wilderness within reach of the Sword Coast and Heartlands trade routes**.
  4. Once **drow bases** are established there and enough strength has moved in:
     - they'll **coerce surface folk into spying** for them;
     - they'll **pick out weakened surface settlements** open to a sudden attack. **Scornubel** and **Priapurl** are already on the list;
     - and they'll prepare to strike at the right moment.
  5. **Storm those places and take them**, then **ambush anyone** who rushes to help or tries to retake them.
- **The goal:** make the World Above **fear and worship Lolth properly**, and give the Dark Queen her promised kingdom, with Lolth's **yochlol spies** watching everywhere in it.

## How this fits the vault (Brodie decides)
- **Maybe (Brodie, 2026-10-19):** the Dark Queen **might be used** in the campaign; not decided. See [[Campaign Decisions]] §10.
- **The Sword Coast is the target region.** Her surface stage aims at wild ruins and caverns near the **Sword Coast and Heartlands trade routes**. The Neverwinter region has plenty of those: Neverwinter Wood, the Crags, and Gauntlgrym, where the 4e Campaign Guide already has **drow plotting** (see [[Lore/Campaign_Guide/Chapter_3_Factions_and_Foes]]).
- **Timing:** this is Ed's late-1490s present. **Whether her surface forays have begun by 1505**, and whether they reach Neverwinter, is open.
- **Skullport** is where the rumor comes from, and it's Laeral's secret haunt as "Irusyl Eraneth" (see [[NPCs/Laeral Silverhand]]).
- **Scornubel** keeps coming up: Bakur's Chalice ([[Lore/Ed Greenwood - Fanes of Shar]]) and Ed's spell-ink mini-campaign ([[Lore/Ed Greenwood - The Monster Man]]).
- **Death tyrants:** Rorgo's necklace makes the undead beholders that Hammardratha may have created (see [[Lore/Ed Greenwood - Netheril]]).
- **Other drow in the vault:** Bregan D'aerthe is behind the Ships of Luskan (see [[Locations/Luskan]]). They're drow mercenaries, not necessarily Lolth's.
- **Another evil power:** in the vault's Three-Faction Rule, Shar starts as the Ugly and turns Bad after the "first evil threat" (see [[Campaign Decisions]] §2). What that first threat is hasn't been decided.
- **No clashes** with the vault, and no movie lore.
