# Ed Greenwood — Fanes of Shar

> Source: **Realms Lore** (YouTube), with **Ed Greenwood**. Transcript added 2026-10-19.
> Summarized from the video's auto-captions. Many names and places were garbled and are marked *(uncertain)*. Part of the video is missing where a sponsor segment was cut.
> Ed's "present" is the **late 1490s DR**; the campaign is later (1505).

## What a fane is
- Gods good and evil have **holy places where their power is augmented** for their clergy, and for any mortal who wanders in. These are called **fanes**.
- Faerûn has **a score or more fanes of Shar**, where the **Shadow Weave is stronger** than elsewhere. Most are spots **Shar has visited more than once** and spent some of her divine power on to strengthen the Shadow Weave locally.
- They're **rare**. Shar tries to keep them known only to her clergy and **often fails**.
- **Many serve as secret refuges.** Where it can, the Church of Shar builds shrines at them, because the stronger Shadow Weave makes its rituals and spells more powerful.
- **Most are remote dells or ravines.**

## The seven fanes Ed names

### 1. Bakur's Chalice — Scornubel (Western Heartlands)
- **Where:** an old warehouse still in use in **northeastern Northbank, Scornubel**. It has no signboard, and it sits far enough from the river to have cellars.
- **Layout:**
  - The stone-lined cellars **reek of mildew**, which makes them useless for cargo.
  - Walls of rotting crates of long-spoiled fabric (mostly brocade and samite) form a **maze through five progressively smaller rooms** to a well chamber.
  - That chamber's back wall is a stack of rotten kegs. **Duck around the right-hand (east) end** to find a rough tunnel sloping west and down in a long ramp to a **large, low-ceilinged chamber under the cellars**.
- **History:**
  - It was **founded by Chalice Bakur**, a handsome, bold, merry, insolent man who made many enemies. Only his name survives.
  - It began as a **brothel-tavern** run by a long-vanished trading coster to support its Scornubel end of the routes to the Sea of Fallen Stars. It became **notorious for murdering clients** and closed.
  - The deep chamber then stored **smuggled weapons and goods**, until the armies of the realms being smuggled into wiped out the stores.
  - Locals used it to **dump murder victims**, so carrion-eating monsters moved in and everyone else stayed away.
- **Shar's temple:** Shar met local worshippers there several times through the **1300s and early 1400s DR** to give instructions. It became **her local temple**.
  - It's **guarded at all times by clergy** who live in the warehouse above and in nearby homes.
  - It's led by the **Dark Hand of Despair Malakhera Yoran** *(uncertain)*: a neutral evil Chessentan human high priestess. Dark beauty, purring sarcasm, and she always gets more than even.
- **How the cell makes money:** **poisons, potions, smuggled weapons, kidnappings for hire and stolen goods.** The goods are stored elsewhere in Scornubel, not in the Chalice.
- **Power:** the Shadow Weave is so strong there that the air feels like chilled thunder. Sharran spells **roughly double** in duration, area, power and damage.

### 2. The Delve — Archendale (the Dales) *(region uncertain)*
- **Where:** a small, deep ravine not far south of the northern edge of the **westernmost Archwood**.
- **History:**
  - Local Sharrans long used it as a meeting place. It became the resting place of **Hela Hallwinter**, a Sharran priestess who had often met there.
  - She was **fatally wounded** in a dispute with **Sembian merchants and their bodyguards** in a nearby town *(name garbled)*. Knowing she was dying, and not wanting her body robbed of a **sacred obsidian knife and bowl**, she struggled to the Delve and died praying to Shar.
  - Her prayers **brought the goddess**, who praised and thanked her as she died, then **used the priestess's skeleton to weave a fence of dark thorns** around the ravine.
- **Today:**
  - The ravine is **always gloomy**, with a rainwater pool, many spiders, toadstools, and a narrow, mossy, mostly level floor about **60 ft long**.
  - The high, thick thorn wall **parts if "Shar" is spoken aloud within 10 ft** of it, and reseals a minute later.
  - The air is **warm whatever the weather** and crackles with currents of energy.
- **Power:** lightning-based Shadow Weave spells last or cover **6× the usual duration or area** and deal **3× the power or damage**. Other Shadow Weave magic is boosted in various ways.
- **Use:** Sharrans traveling to and from **Cormyr, Sembia and the Dales** meet here secretly in **groups of seven or fewer**. The locals hate them, as they hate any outsider who does anything that smacks of assembly, authority or wielding magic.

### 3. *(Name lost in the cut — "Dusgrill"?)* — north of the Spiderhaunt Woods
- **Where:** due north of the northernmost arm of the **Spiderhaunt Woods**, near **Daggerdale** *(uncertain)*. Several old **gnome and dwarf tombs** with shallow passages run back from natural caves, northwest into the foothills of the **Desertsmouth Mountains**.
- **Use:** Sharrans have turned them into a camp for short stays. Food, tools, footwear, cloaks, other clothing and cots are stored there, and there are usually **about six Sharran clergy** present.
- **Power:** Shar has greatly strengthened the Shadow Weave here, but **the boost rises and falls seemingly at random**. Elminster thinks **ancient gnome and dwarf enchantments on the tombs are drawing on the Weave**: the Shadow Weave is weaker when their drain is strong and stronger when it's slight.
- **Who:** two Sharran priests who travel the **Dales, Hillsfar and refounded Yûlash** *(uncertain)* often work major magic here and lead gatherings to **plan operations in Sembia, the Dales and the Moonsea**:
  - **Havalo Dunsten** *(uncertain)*: neutral human male priest, Battle Hand of Despair *(uncertain)*.
  - **Rartrial Gandallahan** *(uncertain)*: neutral evil human male priest 6, Hound of Shadows.

### 4. Galander Farm — Dragon Coast *(region uncertain)*
- **Where:** an overgrown, abandoned farm on a peninsula between a haunted manor and the ruins of an abandoned village *(names garbled)*. The old stump-and-stone field boundaries can still be seen. Springs rise in the westernmost part of a forest above and flow down through the fields to the sea.
- **The fane:** in a hollow along the biggest stream, out of sight of **Firedrake Road** *(uncertain)*, is a **Shar-strengthened, ever-present gloom**: an area of dark mist that wind can't clear.
- **Use:** Sharrans operating in **Altumbel and Teziir** *(uncertain)* gather here to confer, swap supplies and work magic boosted by the Shadow Weave.
- **Who:** no one alive remembers who Galander was. A Sharran priestess who often leads rituals here has taken the name as a nickname. She is **Fandra "Galander" Shandar** *(uncertain)*, a neutral human female priestess 9, and Sharrans know she's **Shar's local favorite**.

### 5. Kant's Tomb — near Berdusk (Western Heartlands)
- **Where:** an ancient barrow on the northern edge of the **easternmost Snakewood**, due south of the **Troll Mountains**, southeast of **Berdusk** and the **Green Fields**.
- **Trolls:** the area is **troll-ridden**. Sharran-enchanted tokens carried by clergy, or spells with the same effect, keep the trolls back, so they threaten anyone approaching except Sharrans.
- **No clergy live there.** There's no food (thanks to the trolls) and no easy water.
- **Inside:** a cramped, cold, stone-lined tomb with a long, wide passage, empty burial alcoves on both sides, and a **stone block altar** at the end. It was looted long ago by necromancers, who animated the dead and marched them out to serve them.
- **Power:** **Shar listens, and often speaks, from the altar.** A chilly magical gloom surrounds it. Her power fills the whole tomb but is strongest when touching the altar. It has **greatly boosted some Sharran magic and only mildly boosted others**; Sharrans don't understand why and call it Shar's whim.
- **Kant** didn't build the tomb. He was a **dwarf adventurer of the late 1200s DR** who found it and used it as a cache until the trolls got too bad.

### 6. The Secret Shrine — Mhessen, Sembia *(uncertain)*
- **Where:** a natural cavern under a house cellar on the northern edge of **Mhessen** *(uncertain)*, a Sembian town where the **Beluk Road meets the Way of the Manticore**, north of **Saerloon** *(uncertain)*.
- **Secrecy:** people know it exists, but not how to get in or anything about its importance, size or features.
- **Cover:** the house is a small cottage no grander than its neighbors. Comings and goings are hidden because it and several cottages around it are **rented out to travelers**. That began in **1312 DR**, after a local inn burned down and a plague killed or drove off the cottage owners.
- **History:**
  - A local merchant **Balan M—** *(surname garbled)* bought most of the neighborhood, rented to travelers, and turned his bakery into an eatery.
  - He was **murdered by a Sharran priest, Jazan Condre** *(uncertain)*, who wanted the income.
  - When Condre went to enlarge the cottage cellars, he **found the cavern** and prayed excitedly to Shar. **She appeared to him** and was so pleased with the **strategic location of the large, many-armed cavern** that she worked a mighty magic to strengthen the Shadow Weave there, **consuming Condre's life to do it**.
- **Today:** it's led by **Vin Oland** *(uncertain)*, a neutral evil human female priest 7 and Hand Mistress of Despair, who **lives in the cottage above**. **The way down is hidden in the closet of the smallest back bedroom.**

### 7. Veil of Shadows — Nether Mountains (the North)
- **Where:** a dell between two foothills on the **northern flank of the Nether Mountains**, directly south of the **eastern end of the Arn Forest**. A small spring trickles down into a pond that is a **sinkhole draining into the Upperdark**.
- **Use:** too remote, and its winters too fierce, for anyone to live there. But the Shadow Weave is **very strong**, so Sharrans travel great distances to camp, meet and **work powerful magic**.
  - They once **magically assembled an undead colossus to attack Sundabar**. Adventurers destroyed it before it reached the city.
- **Shar dances here:** the Church of Shar correctly believes this is **where Shar dances**. Her dances aren't entertainment; they are **magical weavings of great power**.
  - She dances **rarely, and more rarely these days**; Shar seems to be **guarding her power**.
  - She **often appears to Sharrans who pray** here.

## How this fits the vault
- **Decided (Brodie, 2026-10-19):** there is a **fane of Shar in the Chasm** in Neverwinter. What it's like isn't decided yet. See [[Campaign Decisions]] §2 and [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Chasm District]].
- **Shar's role:** she starts as the **Ugly** in the [[Lore/3 Faction Rule|Three-Faction Rule]] and turns **Bad** after the first evil threat is defeated (Campaign Decisions §2).
- **Possible tension:** Ed says Shar dances **more and more rarely** and seems to be guarding her power. [[Lore/Current Era]] says the gods have become **more active**. Ed's version is from the 1490s and the campaign is in 1505, so both can be true.
- **Name echo:** **Hela Hallwinter** shares her surname with the Hallwinters, a family of knights known along the Sword Coast in canon.
- None of Ed's seven fanes is near Neverwinter. The closest is the **Veil of Shadows** in the Silver Marches.
