# Records of Time - The Creation Myth

**Also Known As:** The Tale of the Twin Goddesses

---

## Original Text (As Written)

The lore of Faerûn is vast—far more than any scholar could learn in a single lifetime. Yet one story is told and retold, preserved in the records of creation itself. It is the tale of a being of immense power who formed the first two gods: twin sisters, one of light and one of darkness. Pure opposites of one another. Creating the heavens, the sea between worlds and all the planets set before your eyes. When they formed the ground beneath our feet they formed another god, The Earthmother: Chauntea. These two came to be known as the Lady of Silver and the Mistress of Shadow, forever bound together in endless conflicts.  During one particularly fierce confrontation, their clashing powers unexpectedly birthed a new entity: Mystryl, the embodiment of magic itself, who would become keeper of the Weave and guardian of all arcane forces in the realms. This delicate balance of powers—light and shadow, earth and magic—forms the cornerstone of Faerûn's existence, a testament to the wisdom of those first beings who shaped our world. Their legacy lives on in every spell cast, every sunrise witnessed, and every seed that takes root in the fertile earth.

## The Beginning

The lore of [[Faerun|Faerûn]] is vast—far more than any scholar could learn in a single lifetime. Yet one story is told and retold, preserved in the records of creation itself.

It is the tale of a being of immense power who formed the first two gods: **twin sisters**, one of light and one of darkness. Pure opposites of one another.

---

## The Twin Goddesses

### The Lady of Silver
The goddess of light, one of the first two deities created by the original being of immense power.

*Known in common lore as:* **Selûne**

### The Mistress of Shadow
The goddess of darkness, twin to the Lady of Silver. Forever bound together in endless conflicts.

*Known in common lore as:* **Shar**

---

## The Creation of the World

These twin sisters created:
- **The heavens**
- **The sea between worlds**
- **All the planets** set before mortal eyes
- **The ground beneath our feet**

When they formed the earth, they birthed another deity:

### Chauntea - The Earthmother
The embodiment of the earth itself, agriculture, and nature. Born from the creation of the material world.

---

## The Birth of Magic

During one particularly fierce confrontation between the twin goddesses, their clashing powers unexpectedly birthed a new entity:

### Mystryl
The embodiment of **magic itself**, who would become:
- **Keeper of the Weave**
- **Guardian of all arcane forces** in the realms

---

## The Balance

This delicate balance of powers forms the cornerstone of [[Faerun|Faerûn's]] existence:

- **Light** (Selûne) and **Shadow** (Shar)
- **Earth** (Chauntea)
- **Magic** (Mystryl)

This is a testament to the wisdom of those first beings who shaped our world.

---

## Their Legacy

Their legacy lives on in:
- Every spell cast
- Every sunrise witnessed
- Every seed that takes root in the fertile earth
