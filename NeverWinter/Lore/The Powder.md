# The Powder

*Name to be decided.*

## Decided (Brodie, 2026-10-12)

### What it is
- **Brimstone (volcanic sulfur)** charged with the primordial's fire.
- Real gunpowder needs sulfur, and real volcanoes produce it as yellow crystals around vents where volcanic gas escapes. Here, that brimstone carries **Maegera's fire**.

### Two forms
- **Raw / ground brimstone → gunpowder** (the explosive).
- **Brimstone crystals → magic catalyst.**
- Crystals come two ways:
  - **Natural:** grow on their own. **Better than crafted, at least for now.**
  - **Crafted:** made from raw brimstone by skilled workers.
- **Crystals can be used up.** Details later.

### Where it comes from
- The source is **Maegera**, the fire primordial locked beneath **Mount Hotenow** (and Gauntlgrym).
- *Replaces the earlier idea of heated river water turning sand into powder.*

### What it does
- **Explosive:** it's gunpowder. **Guns and explosives now exist in the world.**
- **Magic catalyst:** it **magically increases all magic, except magic from a divine source.**
  - **Paladins and clerics do not benefit.**

## Canon background: Maegera

- A **fire primordial** (sometimes called the Dawn Titan), older than the gods' order.
- Bound deep beneath **Gauntlgrym**, the ancient dwarven city under the **Mount Hotenow** region, **northeast of Neverwinter**, roughly a day or two away. The nearest entrance is a cave in a ravine in the Crags just outside the city.
- The Delzoun dwarves chained it with elemental magic (water elementals hold it) and used its fire to power Gauntlgrym's **Great Forge**.
- **1451 DR:** the binding was disrupted, Maegera partly broke loose, **Mount Hotenow erupted** and Neverwinter was devastated (R.A. Salvatore's *Gauntlgrym*). The binding was restored afterward.
- Canon explains the city's name with the **warm Neverwinter River**, heated from deep underground. It keeps the city mild, the harbor ice-free, and the gardens blooming in winter.

### Related canon
- **Smokepowder** is the Realms' canon gunpowder: magically made, its secret long kept by **Lantan** and the **church of Gond**.
