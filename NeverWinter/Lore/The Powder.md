# The Powder

**Name:** Dawnfire
- The name comes from Maegera's title, the Dawn Titan. In canon, very few people know she is down there. So whoever named it Dawnfire may know more than most.

- **A catalyst for magic.**
  - Works on all magic **except magic from a divine source**. Paladins and clerics do not benefit.
- **Gunpowder**, or the D&D equivalent (smokepowder), **can be made from it.**
- **Produced and managed by Neverwinter.**
- **Unique to Neverwinter.** No other place can replicate it, so far.

## Origin
- **Left over from the 1451 DR eruption** of Mount Hotenow. Inside the city, the river burst into steam and lava.
- That lava **cooled in the riverbed and streets, still holding Maegera's fire.**
- **Looks like molten rock and glows slightly.**
- **Why only Neverwinter has it:** the city is where her fire hit.
