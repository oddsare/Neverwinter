# Kobolds (MrRhexx)

Kobold lore, especially the Forgotten Realms version. Summarized in my own words. Names come from auto-captions; spellings may be off. **Campaign connections are open threads**, and this ties closely to **Kurtulmak's Chosen** (`Quests/Kurtulmak's Chosen - Underground Ascension.md` in the vault root).

## Monster Manual basics
- Usually seen as filthy little reptiles, barely better than goblins, malicious but not much of a threat. **Being underestimated is exactly what they want.**
- **Physically frail**; they survive by numbers. A single solid hit kills one.
- **Tunnels full of clever traps.**
- Some hatch **with wings** (**urds**); ordinary kobolds are jealous and treat them worse.
- **Darkvision, sunlight sensitivity**, so they live underground. **Pack tactics:** weak alone, strong in numbers.
- **Small weapons** (daggers, slings). Too weak to draw a bow.
- **They hate gnomes and pranks.** The Monster Manual's story: Kurtulmak served Tiamat until the gnome god Garl Glittergold stole a trinket from her hoard; sent to get it back, Kurtulmak was tricked and trapped in an underground maze forever. **This is the generic version, not Realms canon.**

## Realms origin (from *Grand History of the Realms*)
- **Io**, creator of dragons, made the first **true dragons** as immortal lesser gods, each with a different aspect of his personality. They rarely agreed except in wanting **wealth**, and asked Io for help shaping creation.
- Io taught them to **create life by giving up a piece of themselves**, warning it would make them **mortal**. They accepted. Each **cut off a limb**: it **regrew**, and the severed part grew into an adult dragon, giving them **mates**.
- **Wherever their blood spilled, small creatures with alert red eyes rose from the ground**, already looking to their makers for guidance: **kobolds**, born as their makers' immortality slipped away.
- **Kurtulmak was the first kobold**, from the blood of the first dragon to perform the ritual, a **green dragon** *(name unclear in captions, roughly "Kasidikal")*. He was **far bigger** than the rest, so she commanded the kobolds through him, and he became their leader.
  - Told to mine precious metals, he **invented the pickaxe**.
  - Told to tile her lair with gold, he **minted the first draconic coin**.
  - Told to mine gems, he **taught himself sorcery** to find minerals.
- With her lair finished, she was **the wealthiest true dragon in creation**, and **released the kobolds**. Kurtulmak began a lair of his own, and every kobold he'd worked beside joined him unasked.
- **The gnome war:** searching for wealth, Kurtulmak's kobolds found a sanctum of special gems, and **gnomes** (who began as souls in gems, given life by **Garl Glittergold**), and **enslaved them**, the first people ever to enslave gnomes. During the dragons' wars, **Garl rescued the gnomes and their crystals and collapsed the entire kobold underground empire** in a cataclysm, killing most of them. **No dragon helped.**
- **Only Io pitied Kurtulmak and made him a true god**, a martyr the kobolds would remember forever. He never lets them forget what the gnomes did, which is **why kobolds hate gnomes** (it's in every kobold entry since 1e). **Kurtulmak's holy symbol is a gnome skull**, sometimes impaled on a weapon.
- **Why it matters:** kobolds carry **true dragon blood**; reverence for dragons is **historic and in their blood**; and their mining and wealth-finding are legendary even to gods.

## Body
- **2 to 2.5 feet tall** (smaller than halflings and gnomes). Legs naturally bent for sudden speed; **double-jointed**, and stretching adds up to a foot but is uncomfortable.
- **Scaly skin**, brown to reddish-black. Those with strong dragon blood can take on **their dragon's color**.
- **Crocodile-like face**; the jaw opens wide enough to hold a melon. Strong teeth that **fall out and regrow**, the whole set replaced over about three years, several times in a life. Kobolds wear **necklaces of their old teeth** to show their age.
- Eyes burnt ochre to red. A ridge of small horns above each brow sweeps back and grows larger toward the rear of the skull.
- **They speak Draconic, but sound like yapping dogs**, which with their doglike heads means **nobody takes them seriously**. They use that.
- **Live up to 120 years** thanks to dragon blood, longer than goblins or orcs. **Dragonwrought kobolds**, with especially strong dragon blood, can live **nearly 400 years**, are considered **sacred**, have wings and greater power, and are immune to sleep and paralysis magic. Like a half-dragon for their race, but they hatch naturally.
- **Cold-blooded** (ironic, since true dragons are warm-blooded):
  - Comfortable in warmth, they **soak up heat**. **24 hours above 40°F lets one go three more days without eating.**
  - Prolonged cold below 40°F for more than three days **triples** their food needs. A sudden cold blast doesn't hurt them more than normal.
  - **A desperate kobold can eat almost anything:** bark, bone, dirt, leather, shells. A hungry tribe feeds its youngest whatever they can eat.

## Culture
- **Everything revolves around mining.** About 99% of kobolds swing pickaxes all day, and they **love it**: canon says they live in a **steady state of contentment** despite setbacks. They take the long view and work for the future.
- **The tribe comes first.** A strong self-sacrificing instinct; they'll struggle against impossible odds to buy time for the tribe. They don't throw their lives away (they retreat when needed), but **the tribe's success is personal success**, and they **genuinely feel happy** when it grows.
- **Very clean:** they shed in patches and brush off old scales; a popular **bitterleaf oil** strengthens and shines their scales and delays shedding indefinitely. **They love baths**, their favorite pastime after mining, at any natural spring. They chew roots and bones to clean their teeth and keep their claws clean.
- **Rich:** a typical tribe is **wealthy**, with wealth spread among everyone. **Gem-encrusted** necklaces, bands, earrings, and especially **tail jewelry**.
- **Clothing:** no cotton underground, so **spider and worm silk**. Miners wear sleeveless leather tunics and short breeches. **No footwear, ever**: their tough feet handle gravel and stone.
- **Better miners than dwarves.** They make up for weakness with numbers, breed fast, and work like an **ant colony**, rarely bumping into each other, using their tails to avoid collisions. Competent and fast at every level, magic included.

## Religion and magic
- **Work is worship.** Every pickaxe swing is a prayer to Kurtulmak.
- **Clerics of Kurtulmak:** go where the workers are and **raise efficiency**; act as **front-line doctors**; lead rhythmic **work chants** to swing to; use **divination to find ore**; and sometimes cast enhancement magic on workers.
- **Sorcery** is a greater blessing than the priesthood. Kobolds with the spark are **guided, not left to discover it**, with more ceremony than anything else in their culture, and lifelong vows like a priest's.
  - **The draconic rite of passage:** a solitary rite of **nine days of fasting**, a **24-hour trance** (many fail to focus), sacrifice of a **100 gp gem**, and a permanent loss of some vigor. Then the kobold is a sorcerer.
  - **"Searching for the dragon":** every morning before mining, **the whole tribe gathers in a great hall** to sit in silent meditation for at least 15 minutes, embracing their kobold heritage. Sorcerers must do it to regain spells. In it, they **pledge their bodies to the dragon**.

## Leadership
- **The All-Watcher** leads the tribe and is its **accountant**: tracks accumulated wealth, divides it among groups, decides how to spend it to strengthen the tribe, weighs proposals from experts, priests, spellcasters and commanders, sets deadlines and assigns workers, approves "chosen one" bonds, and judges **betrayal and exile**. The challenge is making every kobold feel they benefit from, not just labor for, the tribe's wealth.
- **Rank by merit only.** No aristocrats or commoners; mostly experts and warriors, with adepts supervising the mines.

## How they deal with others
- They dislike most races. Tiny in a world of giants, and the other small folk (gnomes) already allied with the big ones, they're **social pariahs**.
- **The longest grudges of any race**, longer than dwarves'. A kobold will remember an insult all its life and plan revenge, but **petty revenge, not murder**.
- **Fairly docile if treated well** and allowed to mine and earn. **Never compare them to goblins.** (Rhexx's contrast: you may never befriend a true orc because of their murderous blood, and goblins are too cruel to roam a city freely.)
- **Gnomes are the exception:** hatred both ways. Civility is strained even at the best of times; part of every kobold is always looking for a dagger when a gnome is near.
- **Easily offended by misunderstandings.** With their own kind they're blissfully happy; with others they carry **a chip on the shoulder** and something to prove. Key for roleplaying them.
- **Dragons:** seen as older, wiser kin and cultural heroes. Kurtulmak may be their god, but **dragons are a glory they can touch**. **Around a dragon they become servile** and do anything. **A poor kobold tribe usually means a dragon overlord** has taken everything.
- **Half-dragons** can command whole tribes if charismatic enough. **A half-dragon, half-gnome is an abomination** to be destroyed. **Dragonborn** get suspicion and grudging respect, since most were "created" rather than descended.

## Family and numbers
- A fertilized female **lays an egg within two weeks** (10% chance of two). It **incubates 60 days**; the hatchling (a "wyrmling") walks and feeds within hours. The mother goes straight back to work.
- Eggs go to a **communal hatchery** tended by a Kurtulmak cleric and a few helpers. Eggs are **extremely hardy**: they can be moved and jostled, and even a broken egg late in incubation often survives.
- **Nobody knows whose child is whose**, so the whole tribe treats each other like cousins. Mating isn't taboo and females have several partners a week, so fathers are unknown. **Close relatives can smell each other** and use that only to avoid mating, since it never produces young.
- **No room for privacy:** everyone sleeps and changes together; nudity is normal and clothing is optional.
- **Romance is rare.** Bonds ("chosen ones") form from **mutual respect and working better together**. The pair swears to serve and care for each other; the **All-Watcher approves** and a **priest blesses** it. Not monogamous, and that causes no friction. Bonded pairs get private quarters **if their contributions earn it**, which they must dig themselves.
- Population growth is **celebrated**. When a warren overflows, **the tribe splits in half**, each half with all the key roles; one stays, one goes looking for a new mountain. **That's when they clash with other races.**
- **Kobolds can change sex** over time if the tribe runs short of males or females.

## War
- They rarely start wars, and **retreat when victory isn't certain**, since long fights aren't profitable.
- **When cornered** with nowhere to go, they're most dangerous: **wave after wave of bodies** hurled at defenses until they crack. Commanders **plan for heavy casualties**.

## Death and rebirth (their belief)
- **Die serving the tribe:** Kurtulmak sends you back as **the next egg laid**. When an important kobold dies, the next egg is set aside and the hatchling is groomed for their role and given a version of their name.
- **Die serving yourself:** reborn as the next pup in the **dire weasel** stables, a domestic animal that can't choose to serve.
- **Die betraying the tribe:** reborn as a **giant stag beetle**, which kobolds hunt for food and armor.
- **Sacrifice yourself for the tribe:** **welcomed into Kurtulmak's own realm**. In that heaven, **the most productive and loyal are eventually reborn as true dragons.**
- So they charge to their deaths smiling. **Kobolds can live 120 years but rarely pass 10.**

## Odds and ends
- **Merchants in disguise:** they must trade for what they can't get underground, but don't want anyone to know kobolds are selling, for fear their mines will be found. So they **use magic to pose as other races** in cities. The diamond you bought for a resurrection may have come from a disguised kobold.
- **Fear of bears**, which raid caves to hibernate; one great bear can kill dozens.
- **Executions by traps:** condemned kobolds are put through traps, which also tests them. **Survive three traps and you're free, and treated as a hero.**
- **Traps are an art form.** Master trapmakers obsess over details; creativity is allowed for a cunning trap. Kobolds keep **deadly traps in their own bedrooms**, as calmly as a person keeps a dangerous dog.

## Campaign connections (open threads)
- **Kurtulmak's Chosen:** a kobold **becoming something more than a kobold**. Canon gives a basis: **the most loyal kobolds are reborn as true dragons** in Kurtulmak's realm.
- **Your race rules** ([[Lore/Races and Alignment]]): kobolds are on the lawful evil list. Rhexx gives them a **different flavor of evil** from drow or orcs: petty, clannish, grudge-holding, docile if treated well, murderous only toward gnomes. **Kobolds revere their god; drow fear theirs** ([[Lore/MrRhexx - Lolth and the Drow]]).
- **The powder:** kobolds are **the best miners in the Realms** and **love heat**. A tribe drawn to Maegera's warmth, or to digging Dawnfire, could be the mining house's labor, rivals or smugglers ([[Lore/The Powder]]).
- **Dragons:** kobolds become servile to any dragon. **Gnawbone is a green dragon**, the same color as the dragon whose blood made Kurtulmak ([[NPCs/Claugiyliamatar - Old Gnawbone]]).
- **Disguised kobold traders** fit the masks theme.
- **Gnomes in Neverwinter** would be natural enemies of any kobolds near the city.
- **The King-Killer Star** ([[Lore/MrRhexx - History of the Realms]]) affects creatures with dragon blood; whether kobolds are touched is open.
