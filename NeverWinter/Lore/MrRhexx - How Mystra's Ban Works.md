# How Mystra's Ban Works (MrRhexx)

The secret rules behind the ban on 10th-level spells. Summarized in my own words. Rhexx reads from ***Secrets of the Magister*** (a 2e Realms sourcebook), so this is closer to real canon than most of his material. Brodie: **not gospel, ideas to draw on.** Series: [[Lore/MrRhexx - Tenth-Level Spells of Netheril]], this note, [[Lore/MrRhexx - Epic Magic and High Magic]].

## What everyone believes
- Right after Karsus's Folly, **every cleric of Mystra** got a clear warning: never again; nothing above 9th level.
- Any wizard who tries a known 10th-level spell, or invents a new one, **just fails** for no visible reason. So sages and priests believe it's impossible.

## What she actually did
- Made the **existing** 10th-level spells **fail and become unreadable**: Netherese casters found their own spellbook glyphs suddenly meant nothing.
- Made **studying** some high-magic symbols **injure the mind**.
- Anyone attempting a 10th-level spell **feels part of their life essence swallowed by the Weave**, plus other penalties.
- **It's a bluff.** *Secrets of the Magister* says history wrongly records that humans were changed or magic made unintelligible; that's what the gods **want** mortals to believe, to keep the waste of casters' lives small. **10th-level casting is possible, just buried under restrictions.**

## The secret rules
1. **Who can try:** only a master of the arcane who can already cast every other kind of spell (Rhexx: **level 20**). **Intellect isn't enough: high wisdom is required.** The Netherese had intellect and lacked wisdom; that's what produced Karsus.
2. **Never alone.** A solo attempt goes wrong. A **second master caster** must link **will, mind and life energy** to the main caster as an **anchor**. The two must act as one.
3. **The first attempt always fails.**
4. **9 out of 10 attempts**, success or failure, **drain every caster involved** (Rhexx: lose a level, regained only after a long time). So no back-to-back castings: at level 19 you fail automatically, and lose another level for trying.
5. **Only the Magister**, Mystra's chief mage in the world, knows these rules. Everyone else fails repeatedly, loses power, and concludes it's impossible.
6. **Mystra and Azuth notice every attempt.** If the casting is judged to be:
   - for purely personal ends,
   - aimed at killing a rival mage or destroying magic items, or
   - in support of **tyranny or oppressive authority**,
   then **every caster is feebleminded** and **the written spell vanishes**.
7. **Secrecy is enforced.** She shuts down anything that would make it public that 10th-level spells are still possible.

## Which Mystra?
- **Mystryl:** the first goddess of magic. No restrictions. Died stopping Karsus (-339 DR).
- **Mystra** (reborn from her): made the ban. **Killed by Helm** in the Time of Troubles for defying Ao.
- **Midnight:** a mortal wizard the old Mystra was grooming. Ao made her the new goddess, and she took the name Mystra. **Murdered by Cyric with Shar's help in 1385**, which collapsed the Weave: the **Spellplague** (blue fire; casters died, went mad or lost magic; it's also how dragonborn reached Faerûn).
- **Midnight returned** about 140 years later, and was whole again shortly before *Hoard of the Dragon Queen*.
- **Rhexx: nobody knows if she kept the rules.** She might keep the ban, or allow a new magical empire.

## Wiki check (2026-10-20): the Shadow Weave, Mystryl's Weave, raw magic
- **The Shadow Weave:** Shar made it after studying the Weave in ancient times; it's built in the **negative space between the Weave's threads**. **Shar can deny it to anyone, mortal or god.** Better for enchantment, illusion, necromancy and darkness; worse for evocation and transmutation. Known and widely used from about 1372. In 1385 Shar meant to seize the Weave itself; it collapsed so completely she **lost her Shadow Weave too**. It's back in play by *Baldur's Gate 3* (1492).
- **Shar is Mystryl's "mother."** Mystryl didn't hate her but didn't trust her. The Shadow Weave made Shar Midnight-Mystra's eternal enemy.
- **No canon about tapping Mystryl's Weave specifically**: the Weave passes from Mystryl to each successor. But: every caster and magic item **carries tiny amounts of its own original Weave**, used until exhausted.
- **Raw magic:** the Weave is a **gate between raw magic and the world** that also **shields casters from its harm**. Wild mages tap raw magic directly. Between the Spellplague and the Weave's full restoration in **1487**, every caster learned to use raw magic.

## Ideas Brodie asked for (2026-10-20; not decided)
- **The rumor itself is the problem:** the enclaves' 10th-level push is a **public rumor** ([[Lore/Current Era]]). Under these rules Mystra would act on that alone, unless Midnight changed them.
- **Your house heads (level 10–15) can't qualify**, so anyone in Neverwinter trying needs a cheat: **Dawnfire** for the missing levels or life cost, or the **Xinlenal mythallar** as the anchor.
- **Anchors as victims:** bad houses kidnap wizards to use as anchors; or two houses ally and one betrays the other at the casting.
- **The intent test:** tyranny and personal gain fail automatically, so the bad houses would need to hide their intent or use someone else to cast.
- **Evidence trail:** failed attempts leave wizards who suddenly lost power, feebleminded mages, spellbooks with blank pages.
- **The Magister** in 1505 is the only one who knows the rules. If they're in Neverwinter, or someone is selling the rules, that's the most valuable person in the city.
- **The Shar link:** Mystra would read anything that looks like Shar touching the Weave as a repeat of her murder.
- **Mystryl's Weave (Brodie's idea):** a relic made **before the ban** (like Xinlenal's mythallar) could still hold Mystryl-era Weave the ban never touched, letting someone skip the anchor, level loss and intent test.
