# Faerûn - The Forgotten Realms

**Current Year:** [[Current Era|1525 DR]] (Dale Reckoning)
**Primary Setting:** Sword Coast North
**Campaign Location:** [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]]

---

## Overview

Faerûn is a vast continent in the world of Toril, home to countless kingdoms, city-states, and untamed wilderness. Known as the **Forgotten Realms**, it is a land where magic and divine power shape the destinies of mortals.

---

## Creation & Cosmology

The origins of Faerûn are detailed in the [[Creation Myth|Records of Time]], which tells of:

- The original being of immense power
- The twin goddesses (**Selûne** and **Shar**)
- The birth of **Chauntea** (The Earthmother)
- The creation of **Mystryl** (embodiment of magic and keeper of the Weave)

---

## The Current Age

The year [[Current Era|1525 DR]] represents a unique time in Faerûn's history:

- **Recovery** from the Spellplague
- **Resurgence** of magical power
- **Increased divine activity**
- A sense of **impending change**

---

## The Sword Coast North

The Sword Coast is the western coastline of Faerûn, along the Sea of Swords. The northern portion includes major cities such as:

- **[[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]]** - The Jewel of the North
- **[[Locations/Luskan|Luskan]]** - The City of Sails (pirate haven to the north)
- **Waterdeep** - The City of Splendors (to the south)

---

## Major Regions

### The North
Wild, dangerous lands filled with monsters, ruins of ancient civilizations, and hardy frontier settlements.

### The Sword Coast
Civilized coastal region with major trading cities and busy sea routes.

### The Savage Frontier
Untamed wilderness beyond the major cities, home to barbarian tribes, monsters, and ancient secrets.

---

## The Weave

Magic in Faerûn flows through the **Weave**, a fundamental force maintained by the goddess of magic. The Weave was nearly destroyed during the Spellplague, but has been recovering and growing stronger in recent years.

The [[Current Era|current resurgence]] of magical power has led to rumors of **tenth-level spells** becoming possible once more.

---

## Calendar

Faerûn uses **Dale Reckoning (DR)** as its primary calendar system, counting years from the founding of the Dales civilization.

**Current Year:** 1525 DR
