# Alaundo's Prophecies (Ed Greenwood)

Canon reference, summarized in my own words from Ed's video. Names come from auto-captions; spellings may be off. **Campaign connections at the bottom are open threads, not decisions.**

## Prophecy in the Realms
- Local sayings and priests' promises exist, but everyone agrees the true source of prophecy is **Alaundo the Seer**, long dead.
- **Prophecies are not fixed destiny.** They always come true, but often only make sense **in hindsight**. Their real power is that they **change how gods and mortals act**.
- **Example:** a fulfilled prophecy foretold that in the time when gods walked the land (the Time of Troubles), **Bhaal would die** and his power would survive in **mortal children**. Bhaal, hearing it, **deliberately fathered huge numbers of children** so his essence couldn't be wiped out, which made it come true. (The modern recitation ends with "so saith the wise Alaundo.")

## The Endless Chant (Candlekeep)
- Candlekeep's monks, **the Avowed**, process through the monastery **three times a day chanting Alaundo's prophecies** in **Old Common**, on a fixed route, so visitors can choose to listen or close a door.
- Between processions, **novices and monks doing penance keep chanting in private**, so somewhere in Candlekeep the words are always being spoken.
- **Fulfilled prophecies are dropped**, so the chant is shrinking. About **90 remain**.
- Alaundo wrote them down **after vivid dreams** and never claimed to understand them.
- **Candlekeep doesn't publish interpretations or write the prophecies down.** To discuss one with a monk you must travel there, pay the usual price of entry (**a book**), and **take your own notes**. That's brought Candlekeep a steady stream of **"prophecy tourists."**

## Six unfulfilled prophecies (paraphrased), with one sage's guesses
Elminster and the Avowed refused to speculate. The proud young sage **Feldonar Orath of Akatla** did; Elminster stresses these are guesses, nothing more.

1. **A lone rider finds a buried god in ruins, takes up its scepter, and slays dragons in the sky.**
   *Guess:* a giant idol in a forgotten buried temple holding a scepter-like magic item whose ray is especially deadly to dragons.
2. **Beings "born of dreams" rule openly until "the brain-feeder" delivers the world from them, as towers fall and "the overlooked ones" rise.**
   *Guess:* **beholders** found a realm or seize one; **mind flayers** overthrow them and wreck it; someone overlooked takes what's left.
3. **"The bound one" wakes in fury and pulls a floating citadel from the sky. Its shattering frees three magics that roam many realms and push dragons to take other forms and seize crowns.**
   *Guess:* a **bound primordial** (or powerful devil or demon) imprisoned by **Netherese** wizards brings down a surviving flying Netherese city; the freed magic roams like living spells and changes dragons into new forms that then rule countries.
4. **A "dark queen" unlocks her throne's secrets and is transformed. "Gems that hold minds" master her; she fails to beware "the griffin," and dragons burn.**
   *Guess:* maybe Lolth or Shar, but more likely a mortal ruler, perhaps a **drow matriarch**. The gems' minds must still function to master her. The griffin could be the creature, a nickname, or an item. Elminster's comment: see how long the chain of "perhaps" has grown.
5. **"The purple dragon" returns, his descendants tremble, and new lords are traded for old.**
   *Guess:* either **King Azoun IV of Cormyr** (long called the Purple Dragon) or **Thauglor**, the actual ancient purple dragon (his remains were burned, dismembered and spell-treated to prevent a return). Either a shake-up of Cormyr's nobility and appointed lords, or strife among dragons toppling rulers elsewhere.

(Ed lists six; the captions only make five clear.)

## Campaign connections (open threads)
- **Candlekeep:** a possible starting point for the players. The Endless Chant is a ready-made opening scene, and the "bring your own notes" rule is a natural reason to travel there.
- **"The bound one"** fits **Maegera**, the bound fire primordial under Mount Hotenow. See [[Lore/Dawnfire]].
- **Dragons taking other forms and seizing crowns** echoes **Gnawbone's** goal and the **Lost Crown**. See [[NPCs/Claugiyliamatar - Old Gnawbone]], [[Lore/The Lost Crown and Hidden Alagondar Vaults]], [[NPCs/Zundaerazylym - The Laughing Wyrm]].
- **The dark queen** fits Lolth's Dark Queen (maybe, [[Campaign Decisions]] §10). The **"gems that hold minds"** echo the kiira in [[Lore/The Blackstaff]].
- **The purple dragon** ties to [[Factions/House Crownrood]] (Cormyr).
- **Bhaal's scattered children** mirror Szass Tam's hidden heirs ([[Campaign Decisions]] §9).
- **Beholders and mind flayers:** see [[Lore/MrRhexx - World-Breaking Artifacts]] (the Living Gate).
