# Ed Greenwood — Elves: Star Elves, a Rude Gesture, and Funerals

> Source: **Realms Lore** (YouTube), with **Ed Greenwood**. Transcript added 2026-10-19.
> Summarized in my own words. **The final segment is cut off**: it starts mid-topic ("the vines," never named) and ends mid-sentence.
> Ed's "present" is the **1490s DR**; the campaign is in 1505.

## Star elves in the Sword Coast lands
- **Still few and reclusive** in the Sword Coast lands in the 1490s. **Most live far to the east, in the Yuirwood in Aglarond.**
- **Where the western ones live:** mainly the **High Forest**, the southernmost **Moonwood**, the **Misty Forest** and the **Westwood**.
- **Who they deal with:** they have willing contact only with **sun elves, wood elves, wild (green) elves, moon elves and half-elves**. No one else.
- **What they think of others:**
  - They **detest everything about crowded cities, especially the smells**.
  - They **mistrust humans** as too quick to violence: you never know when a human will turn on you with a blade.
- **Mistaken identity:** at a chance meeting, many humans **mistake a star elf for a moon elf**.
- Some star elves **travel, explore and adventure** in the Sword Coast lands.

### Families and houses
- **Evemdrel** and **Saeyil**: **star elf merchant families**.
- **House Dahlmkess** (noble): believes that **all the civilized races merging into one civilization is inevitable**, and that it's **better managed than resisted**. Managed well, star elves could **control or influence humans' unfortunate belligerence**.
  - This sets Dahlmkess apart from most star elf nobility, notably the **conservative houses Lalaundura and Rannekho**.

### A brewing conflict
- Some **High Forest star elves are planning a military conquest of the Reaching Woods**, to make it a **new home** and **exterminate or drive out its gnolls**.
- **Others think it's unwise and overbold**: it would inevitably give star elves **too high a public profile**.

### Secret treetop holy communities
**Small and very secret.** Ed names these:
- **Shilmista**, at the southern end of the forest, just north of **Riatavin**.
- The **southernmost part of the western half of the Gulthmere Forest**, where it juts into a bight of the mountains along its southern edge.
- **The heart of the Reaching Woods**, about midway along the part east of the river, not far from the river.
- **The High Forest**, on the **east bank of the river that runs from the Star Mounts** east and south to join the **Delimbiyr** upriver of **Loudwater**. It's about a day's travel north from the forest's edge for experienced foresters.
- **Deep in the heart of the Yuirwood**, about midway east to west, in **Aglarond**.

There are many others, but they're smaller, or in parts of Faerûn that print hasn't covered much.

## The elven "finger"
Anyone who deals much with elves, or is one, will run into this rude gesture. It means much the same as raising the middle finger in our world.
- **Crook one middle finger** into a curve **pointing inward, toward yourself**, while bringing that arm **up across your body on a rising diagonal**.
- **For added insolence:** finish by **slowly licking the tip of the crooked finger** while **staring hard at your target**.

## Elven funeral rites
- **They vary widely** by elven subrace, region, community and family.
- **They always include a prayer of remembrance** spoken, often whispered, by the living.
  - When many mourners gather, they often **murmur responsive lines in unison** in reply to the person praying. To human ears it sounds like a **humming chant**.
- **The prayer is said while the remains are being treated**, which varies:

### Misty Forest (wood elves)
- They **"plant" bodies**: bury their dead **beneath seedlings**, or **plant new seedlings above the burial**.
- **Esteemed and powerful elves are entombed inside a living tree**, which is opened and then **resealed around the body by magic**.

### Ardeep Forest (past practice)
- Bodies **were seeded with particular mosses** that grow on decaying flesh.
- They were then laid on **small canoe-like barges**, taken to fast-flowing streams and rivers that run into the **Sea of Swords**, **set alight at night**, and sent to a grave **both fiery and watery**.

### Evermeet
- Certain trees, notably the **curving, grotesquely bowed Antharimm**, are cut open with **vertical cuts as long as the body is tall**.
- The body is **bonded half into the cut** with **Flaerarra**: a **dark brown syrup** made from a **secret recipe of natural ingredients** and **awakened with a simple spell**.
- The syrup **glues the remains to the tree's living innards**. The tree then **grows astonishingly fast**, **feeding on the remains until they're an empty husk**, expanding to **cover the body** and growing **new bark** to heal itself.

## Realms Speak: Ayesunder
- The **first name of a famous Truesilver noble of Cormyr**.
- Pronounced **"EYE-sun-dur."** The **old pronunciation**, from before the emigration from Tronadar to what became Cormyr *(place name uncertain)*, was **"AY-eye-sun-dur."**
- It's usually **shortened to "Aye"** in everyday speech. That got confusing, so it **went out of fashion as a first name**.
- It's still common as a **second or third name** in families with pretensions, who give babies four or five names. **So there are a lot of Ayesunders around.**

## How this fits the vault (Brodie decides)
- **Near Neverwinter:** the **High Forest**, the **Misty Forest** (near [[Locations/Daggerford]]), **Ardeep Forest** and the **Westwood** (near [[Locations/Waterdeep]]) are all in the Sword Coast North. [[Locations/Silverymoon]] has a large elven population.

- **No clashes** with the vault, and no movie lore.
