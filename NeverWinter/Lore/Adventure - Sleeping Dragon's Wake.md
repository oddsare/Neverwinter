# Adventure — Sleeping Dragon's Wake

**Publisher:** Wizards of the Coast (5e)
**Format:** Digital (D&D Beyond / Roll20 / Fantasy Grounds)
**Series:** One of the follow-up adventures to *Dragon of Icespire Peak* (D&D Essentials Kit)
**Status in this campaign:** Candidate — **might use.** Not committed.

---

## Why it's on our radar

It's the official 5e adventure that puts **Iniarv's Tower** on the table — the tower of the lich who flooded the Mere of Dead Men and built the Twin Crowns. That makes it a ready-made dungeon for the [[NPCs/Voaraghamanthar - Two Black Brothers|Two Black Brothers]] thread, close enough to Neverwinter to fit the "stay in or near the city" plan.

---

## What we know (from public listings — book not yet read for this note)

- Has a full **Iniarv's Tower** section: location overview, travel there, arrival.
- The tower sits **south of Leilon**, on the northern edge of the **Mere of Dead Men** (Leilon is the High Road town between Neverwinter and the Mere).
- In the adventure, a **Cult of Myrkul** has taken over the tower.
- The adventure's blurb ties its villains to **Myrkul** and the **Cult of the Dragon** rising out of the Mere.
- Older canon puts Iniarv's home on the **western slopes of the Sword Mountains** — consistent with foothills-meets-Mere south of Leilon.

**To do when prepping:** read the Iniarv's Tower chapter and note the map position, layout, the Myrkul cult's leaders/goals, and any mention of Iniarv, the Twin Crowns, Uth Tower, or the brothers.

---

## Campaign fit

- **Two cults in the Mere:** Cult of the Dragon (canon — working with the brothers, hunting sunken Uth Tower) and the Cult of Myrkul (squatting in Iniarv's Tower). Rivals? Unknowing allies? One a front for the other?
- **Path B hook (lich puppets):** cultists of the god of death sitting in a lich's tower while the lich's fate is unknown — perfect place for the party to learn Iniarv may still exist.
- **Level fit:** the Essentials Kit sequels are built as continuations of a low-level adventure — check the chapter's level and scale up/down as needed.
- **Distance:** a road trip south down the High Road, not a relocation — fits the "near the city until ~level 10" plan.
- **Breathing world:** if the party ignores it, the Myrkul cult keeps working — possibly finding Uth Tower or the second crown before the Cult of the Dragon does.
