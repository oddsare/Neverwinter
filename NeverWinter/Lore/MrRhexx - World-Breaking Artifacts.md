# World-Breaking Artifacts (MrRhexx)

Five artifacts that could spell doom or salvation if they got loose in the world. Summarized in my own words. **The captions garble several names**; I've used likely canon spellings where I'm fairly sure and flagged the rest. **Campaign connections are open threads.**

## 5. The Infinity Spindle
- A **crystalline shard** from the early multiverse. Its full powers aren't described, but it can turn **even the lowliest monster or humanoid into a demigod**.
- **Raxivort**, treasurer to the demon lord **Graz'zt**, spent centuries guarding the treasury, grew greedy, **stole** treasures including the spindle, fled to the Material Plane, and **became a minor god**, forging a realm (the Black Sewers) in **Pandemonium**.
- **Graz'zt didn't want it back**; he already had greater power. Instead he **spread word far and wide of what the spindle could do**, and owned by such a pathetic creature. Soon all kinds of enemies were hunting Raxivort for it.
- To survive, Raxivort created the **xvarts**, a whole race that **look like copies of him**, so **divination aimed at him finds them instead**. He's believed to still wander the planes, never staying anywhere long.

## 4. The god-killing dagger *(name unclear in captions; sounds like "Jotham's" or "Jhothamite")*
- From **Netheril**: a cult in that empire **hated the gods** and believed they could be killed by force with the right weapon.
- Through powerful arcane magic and the **sacrifice of 39 cult members**, they made a **+5 unholy dagger** that **pierces all protections a target has from being divine**, and has properties that **twist the fate of divine beings** through circumstance.
- **Bane**, while still **mortal**, wielded it to **kill a god** *(name unclear in captions)*, and that killing **made Bane a god**. It's been used only once, and it did exactly what it was made for.
- **The dagger is still stuck in the dead god's heart**, buried deep on a **secret island off the coast of Sembia**, at a site marked by a small monument. **Pull it out** and the heart pours **warm, muddy water** that in **30 days reforms the god**, unless someone with great strength **plugs the heart**. Supposedly only the Dead Three (**Bane, Bhaal, Myrkul**) know where it is.

## 3. The Scepter of Savras
- The strongest **god-binding** artifact. Made by **Azuth**, god of wizards, to fight **Savras**, god of divination, for dominion over the gods of magic, back when gods fought each other for portfolios (before **Ao** forbade it). Taking divination into his portfolio would have made Azuth stronger.
- A staff able to **hold a god's whole essence** without breaking, and tap its magic to empower the wielder.
- **Strike a god with it and the god is imprisoned inside.** It works on **mortals** too, and **anyone** can use it.
- **A trapped god loses touch with that world**: his clerics there **lose their powers**, and if he has no presence elsewhere he **starves and dies**, since gods need followers. If he has worshippers in other worlds, he survives and may use them to seek rescue.
- The scepter **absorbs the trapped god's power** and takes on his traits. Its crystal came to look like Savras's symbol (a crystal ball with eyes). From Savras, the greatest divination god, it lets the wielder **scry anywhere** (the Material Plane, the planes, the heavens) **safely**, watch whole pantheons, and **read the minds of anyone scried, gods included**, without them knowing. Some gods can block it; many can't.
- Azuth later **freed Savras**, and they're now colleagues, but the staff **permanently kept much of his power**.
- **It has vanished.** Only a **divine** being (or one with divine traits) can free a prisoner, by **speaking their true name three times while striking the ground** with the staff. **Only Azuth can destroy it.**

## 2. The Living Gate
- **The only known permanent opening to the Far Realm**, the realm of tentacled horrors beyond most gods, where **beholders, mind flayers and aboleths** come from. Anything could cross freely.
- Called "living" because the opening is **fleshy**, oozing a fluid that **warps the world around it into a nightmare**.
- **Two are known:**
  - One, more myth than fact, in the far **Astral Plane**, **destroyed by the gods** after a massive Far Realm invasion. Its fragments drift there and are used in **githyanki silver swords**, letting the githyanki **sense Far Realm breaches** and mind flayers.
  - The other is **still intact** in the **Feywild's Underdark (the Feydark)**, beneath a beautiful ruined eladrin city of crystal spires, in a secret section. It's **sealed**, guarded by an eladrin knight appointed by **Titania**, the Summer Queen. Its opening is **measured in eyelashes**.

## 1. The Nether Scrolls
- Made by the **sarrukh** empire (the serpent-folk who once ruled nearly the whole continent) to gather **all the world's magical knowledge** in one place.
- **50 scrolls, five chapters (ten scrolls each).** The content **shifts to suit the reader**: what they can best learn (for example, a halfling thief gets illusion, not necromancy).
- **The five chapters** *(Latin-like names garbled in captions)*:
  1. **Fundamentals of magic:** how magic works and flows; readers match or surpass true masters, with **spells up to 9th level**.
  2. **Making magic items.**
  3. **Making constructs** and complex magical objects, like **golems**.
  4. **The planes:** how they work and connect, how to travel them, and how to avoid harm there.
  5. **Making artifacts:** needs a special **key**; never unlocked at the time.
- **Rereading reveals new things.** Hidden secrets open when you **read with other senses**: for example, magically turning a scroll into raised script and **reading by touch**. Many miss this.
- **Netheril rose because of them**, all the way to **10th-, 11th- and 12th-level spells**. Most modern magical theory comes from them; without them, Rhexx says, wizards might still struggle with 5th-level spells.
- **The elves stole them** from Netheril and used high magic to turn them into a **golden beech tree** *(the Golden Grove of hidden knowledge; name garbled)*: metal leaves, a **treant's face** in the trunk, a small **silver bird** and a **snake** of gold, silver and electrum scales in the branches.
  - **Roots:** their shapes taught chapter 1.
  - **Leaf patterns:** chapter 2.
  - **The animals' sounds:** chapter 3, including secrets impossible to learn by reading, such as **self-sustaining magic fields**, **wild magic** and **dead magic**.
  - **Rustling leaves:** chapter 4.
  - **The whole grove contorting into symbols while leaves and animals sang:** chapter 5, artifacts, including a secret section on **creating new life forms and monsters**.
  - **When the bird and snake merged into a small golden dragon**, a **sixth chapter** appeared: the **upper levels of the Weave**, **10th- and 11th-level spells**, and **high magic rituals**.
- **The tree was destroyed** when **evil human shades** tore it out. It turned back into scrolls, which were recovered and **scattered** so they couldn't be found again.
