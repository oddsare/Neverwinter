# Calendar of Harptos & the Roll of Years

**Campaign year:** 1505 DR, the **Year of Ogres Marching**
**Covers:** 1492 DR (*Dragon Heist*) through 1530 DR (twenty-five years past the campaign start)

---

## How Years Are Named
- **The Roll of Years:** every year has a name. The prophet **Augathra the Mad** wrote thousands of year names, and the list is kept at **Candlekeep**. The names are used across Faerûn, and people often say the name instead of the number, as in "the Year of the Drawn Line."
- **The names come true:** tradition holds that it is rare for a year's events not to fit its name. The names work as prophecy that only makes sense afterwards.
- **Numbers:** **Dalereckoning (DR)** is the most common count. It starts in 1 DR, the Year of Sunrise, when the Standing Stone was raised in the Dales. Some regions count differently (Cormyr Reckoning, Northreckoning and others).
- **Where 1492's name comes from:** "Three Ships Sailing" is a real-world nod to Columbus's three ships in 1492.

## The Calendar of Harptos

The wizard **Harptos of Kaalinth** created this calendar, and it is used across most of Faerûn. A year has **12 months of 30 days**, with each month split into **three tendays**. Five festival days fall between months, giving **365 days**, or **366 in a Shieldmeet year**. The year begins with Hammer and ends with Nightal.

### Writing dates
- **Days of a tenday** have no names. Use numbers: "the fourth day of the first tenday of Flamerule" is **Flamerule 4**.
- **Common forms:** "15 Hammer," "Hammer 15" or "15th Hammer." In speech or poetry you might hear "the 15th of Deepwinter."
- **Full date example:** *Kythorn 20, 1505 DR, the Year of Ogres Marching*, which is the summer solstice of the campaign year.

### Shieldmeet
- **When:** the extra day after Midsummer, in every year divisible by four. The next one is **1508 DR**. The last one was 1504 DR.
- **Waterdeep:** the Masked Lords appear publicly at the **Shieldmeet Council**, where the Lords' Rule is reaffirmed (see [[Locations/Waterdeep]]).
- **Amphail:** one fan source says Amphail's Lord Warder changes at each Shieldmeet (see [[Locations/Amphail]]).
