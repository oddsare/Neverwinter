# Epic Magic and High Magic (MrRhexx)

How power above 9th level is still reached without true 10th-level spells. Summarized in my own words. Brodie: **not gospel, ideas to draw on.** Series: [[Lore/MrRhexx - Tenth-Level Spells of Netheril]], [[Lore/MrRhexx - How Mystra's Ban Works]], this note.

## Netheril's everyday magic
- Roughly 2,000 years ago, Netheril made magic **for the middle class**: cheap, useful items like magical cold-boxes, voice-activated lights, self-adjusting spectacles, shoes that stay clean.
- The **phaerimm**, magic-eating creatures highly resistant to spells, were their perfect enemy.

## Karsus's Folly
- **Karsus** spent a decade on a spell to **take a god's place for a time**, planning to become the goddess of magic and win the war.
- **What he didn't know:** Mystryl spent much of her mind **constantly repairing the Weave**. Heavy high-level casting **damages the Weave**; unrepaired, magic turns wild in some places and dies in others (wild-magic areas are where **wild sorcerers** are born).
- Once he had her power, he became all-knowing and saw that without her, magic would stop. **Mystryl killed herself, killing him.** Magic stopped, and the **floating cities fell**. The reborn goddess saved only a few.
- Karsus was left **petrified in his bloated god-form**. The spell, ***Karsus's avatar***, is the only 12th-level spell ever made or cast. It took a decade, millions of gold pieces, and **tarrasque blood**.

## The new Mystra's rules (Rhexx's account; shakier)
- Nothing above 9th level; spells that threaten the Weave can't be cast.
- **Only people with aptitude** can use magic (before, a farmer could cast a cantrip with the right gestures and words).
- **Preparing spells each morning** and **limited daily slots**, so single wizards can't shred the Weave casting dozens of spells an hour.
- **Longer casting times** and extra hurdles. Divine casters were restricted too.
- **The one upside:** wizards no longer had to specialize in one school to reach its high spells. She hoped wizards would help each other across all schools.
- Before, arcane casters were **arcanists**; afterward, the word **wizard**.
- **Making magic items became much harder** too.

## Epic magic
- The **legal workaround**: accept handicaps to reach above 9th level. Example: **several casters casting *wish* together** for a stronger result.
- **Who:** multiple casters who can each cast 9th-level spells, working as one casting that can take **months**.
- **Sacrifice:** substitute your life or parts of yourself for missing skill or components. Sometimes **some or all casters die** to empower it.
- **Components:** about **300,000 gp** for weaker effects, around **800,000 gp** for strong ones, the highest Rhexx has seen about **2 million gp**.
- **Spells are made up as you go**, not written down, so effects are technically unlimited.
- **Canon examples:** raising an island from the sea; a lich **splitting its phylactery into several**; **creating a new breeding species** (like the owlbear or manticore); **permanent immunity to 1st–9th-level spells**, reflecting them back; **rain that turns anyone it touches undead**.

## Elven high magic and mythals
- **Elven high magic is epic magic, elf-style.** The ancient elves found that a **caster sacrificing themselves** gives even more power, and considered it an honor.
- **Mythals** are the result: city- or region-sized fields with built-in effects. Examples: **no dragons can enter** (Rhexx compares Waterdeep's ward; canon calls Waterdeep's **Ahghairon's dragonward**, not a mythal), **no evocation** inside, **free flight** inside.
- **Elves stopped** because of the cost, the uncertain results, and losing a 9th-level caster every time. Now **only a few very old elves in inner circles** know the fundamentals. **Epic magic is as rare as it gets.**
- Humans had focused on true 10th- and 11th-level spells instead, so after the ban, they fell back.

## Ideas Brodie asked for (2026-10-20; not decided)
- **Epic magic forces coalitions:** house heads at 10–15 can't do it alone, so it takes several houses, or outside archmages. Then: who gets sacrificed? The elves gave themselves; bad houses would give other people.
- **Paying for it:** the Dragon Heist gold (about 500,000 gp in canon; Brodie floated "the vault" with Dagult's stolen money) could fund one weaker casting. Dawnfire could replace some components.
- **A mythal over Neverwinter:** a city ward like a dragonward would be a huge prize for Renaer, and a threat to dragons and their allies.
- **Villain projects:** undead rain (Neverdeath District); a new breeding species (Kurtulmak's Chosen, kobolds); a multi-phylactery lich.
- **An ancient elf who knows high magic** would be worth more to the houses than any vault: a kingmaker, or a kidnapping target.
- **Two routes past the ban:** secret 10th-level casting is quiet and judged by Mystra; epic magic is brute force, expensive, deadly and hard to hide. Different factions could choose different routes.
