# The Current Era - 1525 DR

**Year:** 1525 DR (Dale Reckoning)
**Era Status:** Period of relative peace and magical resurgence
**Tension Level:** High - the world stands on the precipice of great change

---

## Original Text (As Written)

The current year in Faerûn is 1525 DR (Dale Reckoning). This places us in a period of relative peace, though remnants of the Spellplague still linger in isolated Plaguelands, affecting those who venture within. The realm has largely recovered from past calamities, with magic and divine power flowing more strongly than ever through the land.

This resurgence of magical energy has led to unprecedented developments in arcane studies, with rumors circulating of powerful wizard enclaves on the verge of unlocking tenth-level spells. The gods, too, have become more active in mortal affairs, their influence more directly felt across Faerûn. There is a palpable tension in the air, as if the world stands on the precipice of another great change.

## The State of the Realms

This places [[Faerun|Faerûn]] in a period of **relative peace**, though the scars of past calamities remain visible.

### The Spellplague's Legacy

Remnants of the **Spellplague** still linger in isolated **Plaguelands**, affecting those who venture within. However, the realm has largely recovered from past calamities.

The [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Chasm District|Chasm District]] in [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] stands as a stark reminder of the Spellplague's destructive power.

---

## The Resurgence of Magic

Magic and divine power flow more strongly than ever through the land.

### Unprecedented Arcane Developments

This resurgence of magical energy has led to unprecedented developments in arcane studies:

**Rumors circulate** of powerful wizard enclaves on the verge of unlocking **tenth-level spells** - a feat thought impossible in modern times.

> *If true, this would represent the greatest advancement in arcane magic since the time of Netheril.*

---

## The Gods Walk Among Us

The gods have become **more active in mortal affairs**, their influence more directly felt across [[Faerun|Faerûn]].

- Divine interventions are more common
- Clerics report stronger connections to their deities
- Miracles and divine signs appear with increasing frequency

---

## An Age of Tension

There is a **palpable tension** in the air, as if the world stands on the precipice of another great change.

Scholars, seers, and priests alike sense that something momentous approaches. Whether this change will bring prosperity or catastrophe remains to be seen.
