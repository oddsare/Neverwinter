# Neverwinter Campaign

**Campaign Start Date:** 1505 DR
**Current Year:** 1505 DR (Dale Reckoning)
**Setting:** [[Neverwinter]], Sword Coast North, [[Lore/Faerun|Faerûn]]

---

## Campaign Overview

The [[Campaign Hook|Shadowy Force]] haunts the trade routes of Neverwinter. Ships vanish beneath moonless skies, cargo disappears from harbors, and caravans report strange encounters. The city guard has called upon brave souls to uncover the truth.

**Current Quest:** [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|Investigate the Disappearances]]
**Contact:** [[NPCs/Soren Ironwake|Guard Captain Soren Ironwake]] - [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|Docks District]]
