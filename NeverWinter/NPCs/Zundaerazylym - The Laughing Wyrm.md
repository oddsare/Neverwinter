# Zundaerazylym — "The Laughing Wyrm"

> **STATUS (added 2026-05-20):** Active. Canon-validated. Integrated with Ophala Cheldarstorn and the Moonstone Mask. She is Neverwinter's secret guardian — and Claugiyliamatar is moving into her territory.

## Canon profile (researched 2026-05-20)

**Sources:** *Wyrms of the North* (Dragon Magazine / Realmshelps.net), *Dragons of Faerûn*, Forgotten Realms Wiki, Soft Claws Wiki entry.
**Canon date:** 1374 DR. Campaign date: 1525 DR — **151 years later.** Ancient steel dragons are long-lived; she is almost certainly still alive.

## Personality

- **Loquacious and joyful.** Talks constantly in both forms. Amundra gossips warmly with everyone who brings laundry. Zundaerazylym lectures her enemies mid-fight — while laughing.
- **Philosophically driven.** Believes dragons and humanoids can live as partners and champions. Wants wyrms to see themselves as *guardians of a common treasure* rather than hoarders of a private one.
- **Pragmatic idealist.** Her grand plan is patient and indirect — not naïve altruism, but long-game manipulation toward a good end.
- **Wary of:** unfamiliar wizards, mercenary companies, secret organizations. She knows how easily any of these can become vectors for something worse.
- **Dislikes tyrants and bullies.** Has personally intervened — non-fatally — to humble them inside Neverwinter. The city has legends of calamities that befell certain cruel merchants and never got explained.
- **Not soft.** She has killed — fought an Arcane Brotherhood mage who raided her city, devoured a pair of wyverns in the Crags. She draws blood when Neverwinter needs it.

---

## Human Alias — Amundra Nelaerdra

In daily life, Zundaerazylym is **Amundra Nelaerdra**: a plump, jolly, gossiping laundress and seamstress. She makes stylish everyday gowns and cloaks for the ladies of Neverwinter and works out of the [[Locations/Neverwinter/The Moonstone Mask|Moonstone Mask]].

- **The cover is perfect.** A laundress hears everything. Nobles, merchants, and spies all talk around servants. Amundra has been collecting information for over a century.
- **She is warm and beloved.** Amundra is the kind of woman people hug on sight. She remembers everyone's children's names, everyone's troubles. Nobody suspects her.
- **Her hoard is hidden by Ophala.** Magically concealed somewhere in or near the Moonstone Mask. Only Ophala knows where.

## The Soft Claws

Zundaerazylym secretly leads a mercenary company called **the Soft Claws**, based out of the Moonstone Mask.

- Each member receives a magical token called a **Zundaerazylym's Nevertoken** — enchanted items tied to her directly.
- The Soft Claws serve as her eyes, hands, and sword arm in situations where a laundress — or a dragon — cannot act directly.
- In 1374 DR they were an active operation. In 1525 DR, after 151 years, the organization may have evolved, changed membership many times over, or taken on a life of its own. The nevertokens are the thread of continuity.
- **Open question:** Do current Soft Claws members know they serve a dragon? Or do they simply know Amundra as a well-connected, unusually wise employer?

## Her Grand Plan

Initiated circa 1344 DR — roughly 180 years before campaign start:

1. **Manipulate evil dragons** into establishing lairs in orc territories, so evil dragons and orc hordes destroy each other over time.
2. **Develop alchemical methods** that make it easier for good dragons to live alongside humanoids peacefully — eventually as champions and protectors rather than threats.

By 1525 DR, this plan has had **180 years to develop**. This raises real DM questions:
- Has any part of the plan succeeded? Which evil dragons have she redirected — and toward whom?
- Has she accidentally made anything worse? Orc hordes she weakened may have been replaced by something more organized.
- Does any part of her alchemy research exist as an item, a formula, a manuscript in her hoard?

This plan also puts her in **direct intersection** with Claugiyliamatar, whose own schemes on the Sword Coast overlap with exactly the kind of evil-dragon manipulation Zundaerazylym has been running for centuries.

## Relationship with Ophala Cheldarstorn

This is the most campaign-load-bearing connection.

- **Ophala is her closest friend and ally.** Canon states this explicitly.
- **Ophala keeps her hoard magically hidden.** A hidden dragon hoard inside or near the Moonstone Mask — protected by one of the most powerful covert mages in Neverwinter.
- Both have survived every disaster Neverwinter has faced since the 14th century: the Spellplague, the Earthmote event, the 1451 Cataclysm, the Dance of the Revolving Lords.
- They are likely the **single most durable partnership in the city**, hiding in plain sight.
- Ophala's canon ties to the Many-Starred Cloak (Silverymoon's mage fraternity) and her long watch for Dagult Neverember are almost certainly *known* to Zundaerazylym. The dragon is probably the reason Ophala has survived so long.

**For the party:** The Moonstone Mask visit almost certainly means meeting Amundra the laundress before they ever suspect the truth. The moment of reveal — if it comes — should be one of the campaign's great turns.

## Relationship with Claugiyliamatar

**This is the primary campaign tension.**

- Zundaerazylym regards Neverwinter as her territory. She has protected it for over a century.
- Claugiyliamatar is an ancient green dragon — evil, cunning, politically sophisticated — who is actively moving into Neverwinter with a criminal network and a long-term plan to become a human noblewoman.
- They are **the exact same tier of power** (both ancient dragons operating through human networks in the same city), pulling in opposite directions.
- Zundaerazylym almost certainly **knows** Claugiyliamatar is here, or is about to find out. The question is what she does about it.

**Possible stances:**
- She is already running a counter-operation through the Soft Claws — trying to expose Old Gnawbone's network before the party even gets involved.
- She is watching and waiting, gathering intelligence, preferring to act through others rather than confront an ancient green dragon directly.
- She has tried to confront Claugiyliamatar directly once, failed to find her, and is frustrated — she needs agents who can go where a laundress cannot.
- She does not yet know how deep the infiltration runs — and Old Gnawbone's network includes people Zundaerazylym trusts.

## Known Actions (Canon, 1374 DR)

- **Fought off a raiding Arcane Brotherhood mage** — took her true dragon form to do it. The Brotherhood knows *something* powerful protects Neverwinter. They may not know it is a dragon.
- **Devoured a pair of wyverns** in the Crags north of Neverwinter. No witnesses.
- Operated the Soft Claws for at least 30 years before the canon date.
- Began the grand evil-dragon / orc manipulation plan 30 years before canon (1344 DR).

---

## In 1525 DR — What Has Changed

151 years is a long time even for an ancient dragon. Consider:

- The Soft Claws are now a multigenerational organization. The current members were not recruited by her personally — or were they? (Magically extended lives among her most loyal?)
- Her alchemy research has had 151 years to develop. Does she have something close to a working formula?
- She has watched six failed lords rule Neverwinter. She helped where she could. She failed where she couldn't.
- She watched Dagult's disappearance in 1502 DR. Does she know anything? Does she and Ophala share that partial knowledge?
- She has been watching Claugiyliamatar's Neverwinter network develop for however long it has been active — possibly years.

## Stats Reference (for DM use)

**Steel dragon** — treat as Ancient Silver or Ancient Bronze (5e) for baseline, reskinned. Steel dragons are not in the 5e MM; use:
- **Ancient Silver Dragon** (MM, CR 23) as the mechanical base
- Add her personality traits: opens with Laugh Action (impose Frightened on failed Wis save, but *she* is immune to fear herself), prefers breath weapon and flight over grapple
- She laughs when reduced below half HP — unnerving rather than frightening

**Her Soft Claws nevertokens:** Homebrew item — a small steel token etched with a claw-mark. Bearer can send a one-way mental word to Zundaerazylym once per day (she hears it wherever she is). She can choose to respond or not.

## Open Questions (DM decisions)

- **Does she know about Kurtulmak's Chosen?** The kobold undercity beneath or near Neverwinter is exactly the kind of long-game underground operation she'd have sensors for. Possibly yes.
- **Has her grand plan touched Claugiyliamatar?** Did she ever try to redirect Old Gnawbone toward orc territory — and fail?
- **What is the current state of the Soft Claws?** Active? Dormant? Infiltrated by someone?
- **Does Renaer know about her?** Ophala may have hinted. Or kept it entirely secret.
- **What does she want from the party?** She needs agents who can operate in noble society (where Amundra cannot go) and in dragon politics (where she'd rather not reveal herself).
- **When does the reveal happen?** The moment the party learns Amundra is the dragon is a campaign milestone. Should be earned, not accidental.

## Quest Hooks

- **Tier 1 — The Friendly Laundress:** Party meets Amundra at the Moonstone Mask. She is warm, gossipy, helpful. She knows things. She gives them small nudges toward the right answers — seems like luck.
- **Tier 1-2 — The Nevertoken:** Party finds a steel token on a dead Soft Claws agent (or receives one from a mysterious benefactor). Investigating it leads toward Amundra.
- **Tier 2 — Old Gnawbone's Network:** Zundaerazylym approaches the party directly (still as Amundra, or through Ophala) and reveals she knows what Claugiyliamatar is. She needs them to go somewhere she cannot.
- **Tier 2-3 — The Reveal:** Party witnesses Amundra transform, or is shown the truth by Ophala in a moment of crisis. The question isn't whether they're shocked — it's whether they trust her.
- **Tier 3 — Dragon vs. Dragon:** Zundaerazylym and Claugiyliamatar's conflict comes to a head. The party chooses who to back — or tries to position themselves between two ancient dragons.

## DM Notes

- She is the **party's most powerful potential ally** and they will spend most of the campaign not knowing it.
- **Do not reveal her too early.** Amundra should feel like a recurring warm NPC — trustworthy, funny, a little too knowing — before the truth lands.
- **She is not a pushover morally.** Good dragon does not mean soft or passive. She has killed. She manipulates. She keeps secrets from people who love her (Ophala excepted). The party may disagree with some of her methods.
- **The Ophala connection is the key.** If the party gets close to Ophala, they get close to the truth. Ophala may reveal it if she trusts the party and thinks they need to know.
- **Her laugh is the tell.** In any encounter where things get dangerous — even as Amundra — she starts to find it funny. A good player will eventually connect the dots.
