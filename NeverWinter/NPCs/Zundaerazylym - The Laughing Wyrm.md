# Zundaerazylym — "The Laughing Wyrm"

**Public identity:** a seamstress in Neverwinter — "the Laughing Laundress"
**True identity:** an adult steel dragon living as a human
**Leads:** the Soft Claws, a secret guild of ~60 agents
**Base:** her seamstress shop + [[Locations/Neverwinter/The Moonstone Mask|The Moonstone Mask]] (secret meeting ground)

**Sources:** MrRhexx YouTube video on Zundaerazylym (transcript supplied 2026-10-11), which draws on Ed Greenwood's *Wyrms of the North* series in *Dragon* magazine; steel dragon canon via D&D Beyond.

## Steel dragons in brief

- Rare, and the **weakest dragon type** — weaker than whites and brasses.
- They **avoid ever being seen as dragons** and live in castles, towers, mansions and cities, not caves.
- Known for **"going human"**: truly living as a person — a job, neighbors, maybe a family — not just disguising themselves.
- Often mistaken for silver or copper dragons.

Her weakness is the reason her disguise and security matter so much: if she's exposed, she can be assassinated.

## Her life in Neverwinter

- Runs a normal **seamstress shop**, making gowns for the city's ladies.
- **Official seamstress of the Moonstone Mask** — she makes the courtesans' gowns.
- When a Mask courtesan is ill, she sometimes **shapeshifts into that courtesan and works her shift** as a favor to Ophala.
- **Human form:** plump, curvy, a little stooped, with white streaks in her hair. Looks like a kindly, harmless older woman.
- **Ophala** is one of the very few who know what she is. They're close friends and help each other whenever needed.

### The two nicknames
- **"The Laughing Wyrm"** — she cackles and hoots in combat; fighting thrills her.
- **"The Laughing Laundress of Neverwinter"** — her cheerful, always-laughing human self.
- The names were earned separately. **Nobody has connected them.**

## Her dream

She wants **dragons and humanoids to live together in cities**, as dwarves, halflings, gnomes and humans already do. Dragons should come to see **civilization itself as a shared hoard** worth protecting, instead of fighting over private hoards.

**Obstacles she's identified:**
1. **Dragon hunger.** Hundreds of dragons in a city would eat it bare.
2. **Evil dragons.** As long as dragons burn cities, people will fear all dragons.
3. **Orcs.** The North's worst threat in her eyes: mountain tribes overbreed, run short of food, and send hordes south in cycles.
4. **The cold of the Savage North** — which she can't do anything about.

## The Soft Claws

- A secret guild of **about 60 agents**, working fairly independently.
- **The Moonstone Mask is their hidden meeting ground.**
- She leads like a mother. Agents in mortal danger find she **"just happens" to arrive in time to save them** — one reason they're fiercely loyal.

### Operations
1. **Steering evil dragons (the big, dangerous one).** They lure evil dragons into lairing near **orc warrens or on horde routes** — planted treasure maps, rumors, even comfortable ready-made lairs.
   - Dragon kills the orcs → good. Orcs kill the dragon → good, and they spread word of the "unguarded hoard" to lure the next evil dragon in. Neither wins → both are kept busy.
   - **Risk:** dragons usually realize they were played — too late to move their hoard easily — and then **hunt whoever played them.**
2. **Gathering ingredients** for her alchemy.
3. **Spying on orcs** (and on nearby dragons).

## Her alchemy

### Ripplebark
The fungus orcs live on. Hardy, needs no light but loves it, spreads fast near heat, survives drought and cold, only needs to feed every ~8 years. Its spores pass harmlessly through humans, so travelers spread it.

### Longbite (her great discovery)
- Made by soaking ripplebark for **months** in an iridescent syrup: mostly water, plus **elixir of vitality**, **dissolved air spores**, oak or ash sap, dissolved pearls, powdered animal bone, and a dozen-plus ingredients **only she knows**.
- A meal about the size of three humans (~400–500 lb) **feeds nearly any dragon for months.**
- **The recipe is secret.** Evil dragons would kill for it. So would good dragons who live in disguise and hate breaking cover to hunt.
- Her agents bring ingredients but don't know the full list.

### Soft-Scale Soak (work in progress)
- A bath in a large vat, using a similar syrup.
- Already works to: keep scales supple, sharpen mind and senses, slow aging.
- **Not yet working:** the goal — **fast full regeneration of badly wounded dragons** (dragons heal slowly; a wounded dragon can't fight again for months).
- **Field test:** she has deliberately fought an orc horde until badly hurt, then had **Ophala teleport her out** to a **disused quarry near Conyberry**, where the bath waited. So far it only soothes.

## The Never Tokens

### The Halruaan inheritance
- **Halruaa** is a far-southern nation of wizards founded by Netherese mages who left Netheril before it fell, disgusted by its magical greed. Its noble houses carried magical **tokens** as badges of rank — made with lost high magic, some reportedly from spells **above 9th level**.
- Zundaerazylym spent **about 30 years protecting the last survivors of one Halruaan noble family.**
- When the last of them died, they left her **their fortune and every token they had** — **100+ tokens.**
- That inheritance is where her money and her agents' power come from.

### What they look like
Glossy, silvery-blue, four-pointed metal stars, about 4 inches across and an inch thick, lighter than they look, pierced in the center for a chain. Blunted edges. Nearly indestructible — only a forge hammer marks one. When broken, a token flares into harmless flame and crumbles to dust. Struck, it rings like four bells in a chord.

### How they work
- **Identify magic reveals nothing.** You can only use a power if you've been told it exists.
- Hold the token against bare skin and will the effect.
- **Each power once per day; only one power active at a time.**
- She tells agents the basics, and **more as she trusts them more.** She knows every power, including secret ones.
- She appears to **know where every token is** at all times (or can scry every holder).

### Known powers
- **Spells:** Drawmij's instant summons, feather fall, invisibility, mending, mind blank, pass without trace, sending, silence, teleport.
- **Elsewhere:** summons a single steel item (no bigger than a longsword) from an extradimensional store **for one round**, then it returns. Lets her lend a powerful weapon that can never be lost or stolen. Only she can change the stored item.
- **Never Return:** costs the user hit points; sends the token to a **secret cavern safe house under the Neverwinter Wood.** Used when an agent is about to die or be captured, so tokens never fall into enemy hands.
- **Sovereign Summons:** call any other token yours has touched. The holder feels the call and has **~10 seconds** to accept — and be teleported to you with all their gear — or refuse. Can chain (A summons B, B summons C...). Accepting usually means jumping into lethal danger with seconds to grab your weapons.
- **Trace:** gives the direction and distance to the nearest token.

## Personality

- **Warm, upbeat, motherly.** Constant laughter.
- **Likes adventurers.**
- **Wary of:** large mercenary companies, secretive organizations, unfamiliar wizards.
- **Hates bullies and tyrants** — from alley-bullies to conquering kings.
- **Her "lessons":** she poses as a flirty tavern wench, lures a **drunken brawling sailor or a cruel visiting mage** somewhere private, then becomes a dragon — jaws clashing, laughing wildly. Usually she lets them flee unhurt. But she has torn away a mage's staff, cloak and clothes to strip his magic, and broken a sailor's sword arm along with his sword.

## The danger she's in

- **Evil dragons** would kill or capture her for Longbite if they learned of it. Even rumors of a working Soak would do the same.
- For her dream to work she must **eventually give Longbite to all dragons** — ideally all at once so none gains an edge.
- Even then, the **Cult of the Dragon** (whose hold on dragons Longbite would undercut) or a powerful dragon would likely try to **seize the ripplebark supply.**
- **Certain Harpers, and probably some of the Chosen of Mystra, know about her work.** They could move against her or swoop in to take what she's made. **She only suspects this** — some Soft Claws know for sure.
- She senses her time may be short and is **seeking adventurers to carry her secrets to Candlekeep** and other keepers of lore. If she succeeds, Faerûn changes forever.

## Campaign hooks

- **The errand:** a kindly seamstress hires the party to fetch odd ingredients (pearls, sap, a crate of "bark"). Low-level, innocent — until it isn't.
- **The lesson:** a party member bullies the wrong "tavern wench."
- **The token:** the party finds a Never Token on a dead agent. It does nothing they can see. Then, at 2 a.m., it calls — ten seconds to decide.
- **The courier:** she asks the party to carry the Longbite recipe to Candlekeep.
- **The hunted:** an evil dragon she steered into orc country has figured it out and comes looking for "the seamstress."
- **The Harper move:** a Harper faction decides to seize her work. Ophala is caught between her friend and her network.
- **The Soft Claws as allies:** a ready-made friendly network the party can join or work alongside.
