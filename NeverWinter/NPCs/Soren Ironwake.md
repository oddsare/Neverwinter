## Overview

**Guard Captain Soren Ironwake** is a ranking officer of the [[Factions/City Guard|Neverwinter City Guard]], currently stationed in the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|Docks District]].

He is leading the investigation into [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|the mysterious disappearances]] plaguing Neverwinter's trade routes.

## Background

Soren Ironwake rose through the ranks of the [[Factions/City Guard|City Guard]] and now holds the position of Captain in the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|Docks District]].

His experience with the rough-and-tumble waterfront has made him both street-smart and politically savvy.

---

## Current Situation

Captain Ironwake is currently dealing with the crisis of [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|disappearing ships and cargo]]:

- Ships vanish beneath moonless skies
- Cargo disappears from harbor warehouses
- Caravans report strange encounters
- Trade partners are growing concerned
- [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Neverember]] is pressuring him for results

He has been authorized to recruit capable adventurers to investigate this matter.
