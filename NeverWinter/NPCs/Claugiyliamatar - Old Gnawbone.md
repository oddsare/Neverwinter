# Claugiyliamatar — "Old Gnawbone"

> **STATUS:** Candidate. User flagged for possible use. Not yet integrated. Must be stress-tested by the Council before promotion to active plot.

## Canon profile (researched 2026-05-14 — full canon)

**Sources:** Ed Greenwood's *Wyrms of the North* (Dragon Magazine), *Storm King's Thunder* (5e), *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*, MrRhexx YouTube series. URLs in research log.

### Stats / power
- **Ancient female green dragon.** 5e statblock = Monster Manual ancient green, **CR 22**.
- *Wyrms of the North* (3e) statted her at **CR 28** with **Rogue 4 / Druid 4** levels — i.e. she is canonically more *cunning rogue* than brute brawler. She thinks like a thief, not a warrior.
- Has held her lair since **1303 DR** — roughly **1,200 years** of established power on the Sword Coast.

### Lair / territory
- Lairs in **Deeping Cave**, deep in **Kryptgarden Forest** north of Waterdeep.
- Lair is phosphorescent with creeping lichen, giant toadstools, and a **gallery of stolen statues of imperious human/elven warrior-women** (one is reportedly an emerald golem). She *collects depictions of powerful noblewomen.*
- Her claimed dominion stretches across the **Neverwinter hinterland** — from River Mirar south past Waterdeep, west through Dead Horse Ford, northwest through the Evermoors, Nesmé, Longsaddle, Grunwald, south of Mirabar. She effectively claims the lands around Neverwinter as hers.

### Personality
- **Cunning, paranoid, vain, patient, cruel.** Considers herself intellectually superior to every other dragon — believes wit beats brute force.
- Cruel sense of humor. **Lectures rather than threatens.** Speech is imperious and mocking.
- Prefers psychological torment over swift kills.
- Punishes disloyal agents by nailing them to trees, drenching them in blood, leaving them for wolves.
- **Signature mannerism:** carries a half-eaten corpse dangling from her jaws and gnaws on it absently "as some humans suck on unlit pipes or chew cigars." Hence "Old Gnawbone."
- Actively **repels male green dragon suitors.** Wants no consort.
- Keeps a male humanoid servant who oils her scales daily with tree-sap and boiled forest-leaf compound — vain about her appearance.

### Motivations (CANON — directly relevant to user's hook)

> **CANON STATES:** Her **single greatest ambition is to rule a city as a hidden human noblewoman.** Waterdeep is the canon target. **Neverwinter is explicitly named as a city she already manipulates** through her criminal network.

> **CANON STATES:** She is actively **seeking a polymorph spell that lets her retain her breath weapon, magic, and dragon immunities in human form.** This is described as her single greatest obsession.

> **CANON STATES:** She fantasizes about being a "mysterious, alluring noblewoman whom all tongues dart with the news of her latest outrageous deeds."

This means **the user's hook is essentially canon**, not extrapolation. Pivoting her primary city of interest from Waterdeep to Neverwinter is a one-line setting tweak, fully justifiable by Renaer's politically open rebuilding period and the noble-society vacancy left by 18 years of chaos.

### Allies / network
- **Already operates a criminal gang inside Neverwinter** (canon). Humans and halflings. They stoke shortages, steal magic items, inflame noble-house rivalries.
- Surveillance grid of **multiple crystal balls** — she watches her targets for hours, especially noblewomen.
- **No cult.** Actively avoids the Cult of the Dragon. No hags, no druid orders.
- One personal servant in her lair (the scale-oiler).

### Enemies / rivals
- **Balagos** — red dragon rival who openly mocks her ("a twisted, crabbed thing given to petty silliness").
- **Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun** (Waterdeep) — major canonical enemy.
- **Mages of Silverymoon** — feed her false scrying images to misdirect her.
- Killed the rival green **Endracritar** by manipulating Waterdeep's forces into doing it for her.
- Killed the young red **Smergadas** by dropping **Lance Rock** on him.

### Notable canon schemes (useful as prior reputation)
- **The House Brokengulf con:** posed as a silver dragon, tricked noble Saerlin Brokengulf into hiring "her" to kill herself, extorted the family. **This is the textbook example of how she operates politically — false-flag identity, noble-house manipulation, slow extortion.** This is the playbook she will use in Neverwinter.
- Drops caravan wagons onto Waterdeep rooftops as trophies.
- Devours fields at Goldenfields and tears caravans on the High Road and Long Road.

### Hoard / magic items
- ~12,000 gp, seven 1,000-gp emeralds, nineteen art objects (~15,000 gp, **mostly female-warrior statues**).
- *Hand of the mage*, *ring of chameleon power*, *rod of spheres*, the haunted **scroll of the wizard Hyrix Greentree** (he tried to charm her with shapechange; she killed him; his ghost clings to the scroll).
- Multiple crystal balls (surveillance grid).

- **Emerald gemstone golem** — guardian in lair.

### Schemes & infiltration capability
- Crystal-ball network + *detect scrying* counter — extremely hard to scry on her, very easy for her to scry on others.
- *Alter self* / *improved invisibility* for infiltration when she has no other shape.
- Commands the **Gnawbones** — dozens of human and halfling agents across Waterdeep *and Neverwinter*.
- Obsessed with stealing or commissioning items that grant true permanent humanoid shape with retained power.

## MrRhexx video — specific takes (transcript captured 2026-05-15)

Source: MrRhexx, *"What They Don't Tell You About Claugiyliamatar – Dragons of D&D"* (YouTube, video ID Ttdbbl0frqE). Transcript provided by user from local download.

### Why she matters for *this* campaign — MrRhexx's framing
- He explicitly calls her **"an adventurer slayer."** Her lair is **a day's ride from Waterdeep** in Kryptgarden Forest — i.e. she is one of the most *accessible* powerful ancient dragons in the Realms. Adventurers don't need an excuse to find her; they need a reason to survive her.
- Quote: *"convincing your DM to let you walk to the literal closest forest from the city to challenge a green dragon — well, anyone can do that."* For a Neverwinter-based campaign, she's even closer — within her own claimed dominion.

### Her four crystal balls — each enchanted differently (USE THIS)
This is the single most plot-mechanically useful detail MrRhexx adds. **Each of her four crystal balls has its own unique enchantment**:

| # | Effect | What she sees |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Detect Magic** on scried locations | Magic items, magical auras, spellcasters present — and can even glean *what the most powerful spell level a wizard has access to* is. |
| 2 | **Detect Thoughts** on anyone scried | Reads minds at range. |
| 3 | **See Invisibility** on the scried scene | No invisible target can hide from her surveillance. |
| 4 | **Telepathy** to whoever she's scrying | She can speak directly into a target's head from across the Sword Coast. |

**Campaign use:** when the party reaches a tier where they realize they are being watched, the *specific* item that exposes them is one of these balls. Destroying a single crystal ball is a tier-2 milestone victory.

### The Alustriel Silverhand "decoy soap opera" (DM gold)
- She **specifically watches Alustriel Silverhand and the rulers of Silverymoon.**
- Silverymoon's mages **noticed she was scrying them** and designed an **illusion spell that activates only when Old Gnawbone is watching** — they feed her a **fabricated soap-opera of fake court intrigue**, including a fictional male character "who is secretly a green dragon in disguise seducing the powerful women of the court for political power."
- **She is utterly hooked on the show.** She has sent agents trying to contact the fictional character.
- **Campaign use:** the party could uncover this scheme in tier 2 and either help Silverymoon maintain the deception (good with mages, deepening intel) or expose it to her (would enrage her — escalates the campaign).

### Psychological profile — MrRhexx's reading
- **"Secret power" obsession:** she does not want force or threats. She wants power that *looks* effortless but required massive background scheming. She wants people to do what she wants because they choose to, not because she made them.
- **She is heavily neurotic** — uses her crystal balls to spy on her own servants and murders any who behave wrongly.
- **CRITICAL psychological insight from MrRhexx:** *"She is extremely physically imposing and powerful, but she deeply believes that she's not."* She avoids direct challenges out of belief that she is frail and weak — yet always wins when tested. This mentality is **why she's still alive** — other dragons got cocky and died.
  - Effectively: imposter syndrome / cowardice that paradoxically protects her. She is a coward who runs the long game because she does not trust her own strength.
- **She is too scared to walk in Waterdeep in person, even disguised.** The fear of getting killed is too great. So she sits in her lair and lives vicariously. *"Old Gnawbone sighs often as she stares into her crystal balls."* This is a sad, longing dragon. **Tragic register is canon-supported, not softening.**

### The servant ritual (signature scene)
- Her lair has **only male humanoid servants.**
- She makes them serve her **naked except for fake bindings** on legs and arms — bindings they could remove at any time, but choose not to.
- They oil her scales. When done, they may take as much gold as they can carry in one hand.
- **The point is not the bondage — it's the consent.** She wants servants who do degrading work *willingly* for reward. That is the "secret power" she craves: people choosing the indignity rather than being forced into it.
- She spies on them via crystal ball. Kills any who deviate.

### Schemes / known acts beyond what was already captured
- **Wolverine massacre:** ~200 years ago, single-handedly destroyed a burgeoning elven kingdom called Wolverine. Slaughtered everyone.
- **Killed thousands of orcs** over the centuries — many orc hordes have died in Kryptgarden Forest assuming they could handle her.
- **The Brokengulf con — full sequence:** appeared as a fake silver dragon → offered to hunt "herself" → scryed where the payment money was being assembled → killed those guards and stole the money → fabricated an illusory battle scene in the forest → returned as the silver dragon claiming victory → collected a second payment → vacated and trapped her lair → returned **as herself** to the Brokengulf manor demanding *double* the payment in revenge for the noble family "hiring her assassin" → toppled a tower, killed three of the patriarch's sisters, crippled him → forced him to pay, bankrupting his estate → waited until rival green **Endracritar** was in her forest → when Waterdeep's army came hunting her in revenge, they found Endracritar instead and killed *him*. **End result:** she stole a noble family's entire wealth, killed her rival, and was believed dead by Waterdeep. **This is her playbook.**

### Her long-form project — adopting wizard children (MAJOR PLOT HOOK)
**This is the most campaign-actionable plot hook in the transcript.** Because she cannot find a wizard she trusts to craft her permanent-human ritual, she has started a long-term plan:
- **Adopt** human children with strong magical potential.
- Raise them **benevolently** so they trust and love her over decades.
- Wait 40–70 years for them to become powerful mages.
- Have them craft the ritual for her out of loyalty.
- **Hedging in parallel:** she is considering doing this in *multiple* locations at once. If one child turns on her or dies, others remain. If multiple succeed, she has a personal army of loyal wizards.

**For a 1525 DR campaign, the project is recent — children would be young.** This is a beautiful campaign hook:
- One of the party's allies (or even a PC's younger sibling, ward, or apprentice) is a Claugiyliamatar foster-child without knowing it.
- The Sharran cult angle (if used) could be one of the children's "schools."
- The party investigates a series of magically-gifted orphans being mysteriously adopted across the Sword Coast — and the trail leads to her.

### Her offspring
- **Half-ettin half-dragon child** — recent. She mated with an ettin. (Ettins are short-lived, so this had to be in the last few centuries.)
- **Adult green dragon son** — 5e Adventurer's League canon. **He is in the Cult of the Dragon.** She apparently does not care or has disowned him.
- These are *both available as supporting villains or wildcards.* The cult-of-the-dragon son in particular is a potential tier-2 antagonist who works *for* the cult, not for his mother — and the party may have to choose sides.

### Magic items — MrRhexx specifies
- 4 crystal balls (detailed above).
- **Hand of the Mage.**
- **Ring of Chameleon Power** (worn).
- **Ring of Draconic Deception** (worn) — *NEW addition from MrRhexx's read; not in baseline canon.* Use as a flavor item that helps her maintain disguised forms longer.
- **Rod of Spheres** (worn).
- *Player note:* she actively wears Ring of Chameleon Power + Ring of Draconic Deception + Rod of Spheres simultaneously — those are her **attuned three.**

### Hoard contents — MrRhexx's read (slightly different from baseline canon)
- 12,000 gp coinage (a *fraction* of what she stole from House Brokengulf — most of her wealth is invested in her organizations).
- 7,000 gp gems.
- **15 tall busts of powerful human women** — looted from ancient tombs across the land, painstakingly moved to her lair because **they remind her of what she wishes she could be.**

### Lair — MrRhexx detail
- **Deeping Cave**, Kryptgarden Forest.
- **On a ravine at the foot of a large mountain.** The mountain is **riddled with abandoned dwarf-holes, ancient tombs, and monster-infested complexes** — i.e. her lair is the *gateway* to a much larger dungeon network if you want one.
- Lair guards: humans, halflings, **and recently hobgoblins** (new addition — recent recruitment).
- She knows **basic druidic magic** that can summon weak fey spirits.
- Her green dragon abilities let her dominate **beasts of the forest** as minions.
- Lair interior: phosphorescent and glowing mushrooms, very tall fungi, massive pile of coins at the back forming her bed where she lounges watching her crystal balls.

### CR — MrRhexx's number
- He uses **CR 28** (Wyrms of the North statline = Ancient Green + Rogue 4 / Druid 4), not the baseline 5e MM CR 22. **For a tier 3 finale, the CR 28 version is the right choice.** Plan accordingly for action economy at the table.

### Her known vulnerability (MrRhexx flags it)
- *"She probably wouldn't be able to survive Forrest Gray from Waterdeep hunting her down."* Even MrRhexx admits there are Waterdhavian heroes who could clearly kill her if they tried. Her safety is built on **misdirection and the belief that she is dead or elsewhere**, not on raw invulnerability.
- **For the party:** at high tier, gathering allies from Waterdeep (Khelben Blackstaff's apparatus, Force Gray) is a legitimate path to victory. She is not unkillable; she is *hidden*.

### MrRhexx's "supplemental material" pitch
- He spends ~2 minutes pitching his own Kickstarter, **"Sands of Doom"** — an unrelated 5e campaign about an undead army and a mummy king. **Not relevant to running Claugiyliamatar.** Noted here only because the user asked what he recommended.
- He does not specifically name other published Claugiyliamatar material in this video. His sources are implicitly Wyrms of the North (he quotes from it directly) plus standard 5e canon.

## Campaign-actionable takeaways from MrRhexx

1. **Tragic register is now confirmed by canon, not softening.** She is sad, longing, and frightened underneath her cruelty. Players who meet her in human guise will *want* to believe her.
2. **The crystal balls are individually destroyable assets.** Each one is a milestone in dismantling her power. Use this for pacing.
3. **The wizard-children project is your tier 1–2 plot.** Investigate adoptions of magically-gifted children. Find the network. Discover what she's trying to build. *Save or recruit one of the children.*
4. **The Silverymoon decoy is a tier 2 turning point** — the party finds out about it, must decide whether to maintain or expose it.
5. **Her son in the Cult of the Dragon** is a tier 2 mid-villain. The party may meet him before they ever meet her. He hates his mother. He may be useful to them — or use them.
6. **The tier-3 finale destination is Deeping Cave** in Kryptgarden Forest, accessed through ancient tombs and dwarf-holes that are themselves a dungeon. The travel itself is content.
7. **Use CR 28** (Wyrms of the North) for the final encounter, not the baseline 5e MM CR 22. Add Rogue and Druid abilities to the standard ancient green statblock.

## Register correction (push-back on motive softness)

The user said "loves human life and wants to be a noble." Canon-her **wants to be the most talked-about woman at the gala** — not because she loves people, but because she wants the *power and glamour* of mortal noblehood. She is **not sympathetic by default.** She is vain, cruel, patient, and would absolutely sacrifice innocents to get her ritual.

Recommend running her as **monstrous register dressed as tragic** — she will *say* she loves human life. She will *seem* sympathetic. Players will doubt her villainy until they see what she does behind closed doors. That tension is the campaign.

### Canon-supplied price tag
Canon-her would absolutely:
- **Nail disobedient agents to trees as a warning.**
- **Manipulate noble heirs into ruin** (proven in House Brokengulf precedent).
- **Sacrifice mages, scholars, or rivals** for the ritual components.
- **Erase witnesses** through her criminal network.
- **Forge an entirely fake noble lineage** via her surveillance network and Forge subterfuge.

No need for the user to invent a price tag — canon supplies plenty.

## Updated open questions

- **Will she abandon Deeping Cave?** Canon says no — she doesn't leave her lair lightly. **Better answer: she commutes.** She is *establishing a Neverwinter operation* with a human alias, while her dragon body still lairs in Kryptgarden. Tier 3 finale = the party tracks her back to Deeping Cave for the dragon-form fight after destroying her human-life infrastructure in Neverwinter.
- **What is her current human alias?** Not in canon. Must be invented. Should evoke "old foreign money, newly arrived in Neverwinter, fabulously wealthy, mysterious past."
- **Who in her Neverwinter criminal gang knows what she is?** Canon implies most don't. Pick a number — recommend 1-3 lieutenants who know, 50+ who don't.
- **Does the ritual exist yet or must it be invented?** Canon: she's been hunting it for centuries. The campaign can be **the centuries-long quest finally bearing fruit** — high stakes, narratively earned.

## User's proposed hook

> Claugiyliamatar moved into Neverwinter because of all the chaos. She is about to find someone to make her a way to be **human in form while retaining her dragon's might and powers**.

## Motive — DECIDED (2026-05-14)

**She loves human life and wants to be a noble.**

- Not boredom, not exile, not a curse. **Genuine fascination** with mortal existence after centuries of observing it from above.
- Her goal: *become* a noblewoman of Neverwinter, with a title, a house, a place at the court — not just visit human life, but live it permanently.
- Neverwinter is the *right* target because it's a **major trade hub** (Jewel of the North) that is *also* in the middle of a politically rebuilding period under Renaer — an unusually open noble society for an outsider with old wealth to enter. She didn't pick a backwater; she picked the most opportune major city on the Sword Coast.
- She brings the wealth of an ancient dragon's hoard, converted into "old foreign money." She is buying her way into the noble class — but the *ritual* to make it permanent is the other half.

### Sharpening questions still open (motive's edges)

- **Why this fascination, specifically?** A long-ago human she loved? A century watching one mortal lineage? A philosophical conversion? An interaction with a specific Neverwinter noble house in the past? Pick one — it gives her a *signature attachment* the players can discover.
- **What is she willing to do for this?** Without a price tag the motive is sympathetic without being villainous. Does she sacrifice innocents for the ritual? Manipulate political enemies into ruin? Erase rival noble lineages? The cost defines whether she is *tragic* or *monstrous*.
- **What does "be a noble" look like in detail?** Marry into a house (whose)? Be granted a title by Renaer (how)? Buy a defunct line and reactivate it? Forge a foreign ancestry? Each is a different campaign shape.
- **Tragic or monstrous register?** Council asked this and the new motive pushes hard toward *tragic*. If tragic, the campaign's question becomes: do you stop someone whose only crime is wanting to be one of you, when the cost is paid by people who don't deserve it?

This is gold for a political-intrigue spine. Notes:

- "Permanent human form with dragon power retained" is **not standard polymorph** — it's a high-magic ritual / artifact / divine boon. That's a **quest seed** in itself: she needs a wizard, a priest, an artifact, or all three. The party can encounter her *as the patron seeking the ritual* before they ever know she's a dragon.
- She is **already operating inside Neverwinter** — that means she's likely posing as a wealthy patron, a noblewoman, a merchant-princess, or a mysterious benefactor *right now*. The party meets her early, likes her, takes her gold. Then later: reveal.
- "Because of the chaos" is a great in-fiction motive: the 18 years of Neverember's collapse and Renaer's fragile rebuild = power vacuum she's exploiting.

## Why she fits the political-intrigue spine — strongly

- Green dragon = **political by nature**, not a combat-first villain. No genre drift.
- Operating inside the city = she **interacts with the factions directly**, not from a hidden lair. Every noble house, every guild, every temple is a potential pawn or partner.
- Her goal (human form + power retained) requires **service from Neverwinter's elite** — magic users, priests, scholars, smugglers of components. That ties her *into* the faction web, not above it.
- She's a **single recurring face** the party can grow to love or fear — a real character, not a hidden BBEG behind seven veils.

## Possible integration angles (NOT decided)

- **The Shadowy Force is her supply chain** — ships and caravans are bringing in the ritual components (rare spell reagents, dragonblooded scholars, ancient texts).
- **Her sponsor is a Neverwinter noble house** — they think they own her. They don't.
- **The Arcane Brotherhood of Luskan** is who she has commissioned to build the ritual. This connects her to Luskan's "Emperor of the Seas" thread.
- She could be a **sympathetic-then-monstrous** figure — the party works *for* her unknowingly in tier 1, suspects her in tier 2, opposes her in tier 3. Classic.

## Open questions

- **Why does she want to be human?** Power-retention plus humanity = she wants something only mortals can have: rulership? Marriage into a noble line? A position on the council? Children? Pick the answer and the politics write themselves.
- **Who in Neverwinter knows what she really is?** Zero? One? Three? Each answer is a different campaign.
- **What's the ritual cost?** Innocent lives? A god's favor? Another dragon's heart? (← this is where Voaraghamanthar might fit.)
- **Is the ritual completable?** Does she succeed at the end, or do the players stop her, or does she succeed *and the campaign continues from there*?
