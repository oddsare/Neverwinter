# Volothamp "Volo" Geddarm

> Source: **Realms Lore** (YouTube), Ed Greenwood with host Ivan, *Who Is Volo in D&D? (His Creator Tells All)*. Full transcript added 2026-10-19; names are spelled correctly in it.
> Ed's "present" is the **late 1490s DR**; the campaign is in 1505.

**Known as:** Volo
**Known for:** the *Volo's Guides* (*to Waterdeep*, *to All Things Magical*, *to Spirits and Specters*, *to the North*, and more)
**Really:** a powerful mage and a legendary storyteller, and an **unreliable narrator**

---

## Who he is
- **A teller of tall tales**, or as Ed puts it, a "fudger of the truth" known for spin. His guides **leave out truths you might need to stay alive**.
- **He has written a great many guidebooks.** Some were published, many were **suppressed**, and others were **private commissions**.
- **Three traits:**
  - a **boldness, or recklessness,** most people lack;
  - an **extremely hardy constitution**, so he can travel all over the Realms;
  - a **vaulting ambition** that feeds the boldness when prudence says it shouldn't.
- **His name:** there's a city in **Calimshan** called **Volothamp**. He's thought to have been conceived there and certainly spent time there as a baby, so he's named after it.

## Why Ed made him unreliable (real-world origins)
- The Volo's Guides were planned as in-world travel guides, like real-world Frommer's or Fodor's. Ed told **Jeff Grubb** from the start that he'd write them through **a really unreliable narrator**:
  - **Elminster knows too much.** He wouldn't lead readers astray, and he'd keep correcting local legends with the truth.
  - Ed needed someone **unlikable and not a magical powerhouse**, so that when places **ban him, the ban sticks**. Elminster would just blast the gates down.
  - **Bans let the writers leave things out** they couldn't fit in the word count, or had other reasons to skip:
    - **Silverymoon** is missing from *Volo's Guide to the North*;
    - the **Yawning Portal** is missing from *Volo's Guide to Waterdeep*, because separate licensing, computer game and adventure teams were planning to use it.
- **The name** was Jeff Grubb's, after the real **Volo Bog** in Lake County, Illinois, not far south of TSR's old headquarters in Lake Geneva. Ed expanded it to Volothamp Geddarm and created the character.
- **His look** began with the cover of *Volo's Guide to Waterdeep*: a portrait of TSR artist **Clyde Caldwell**, painted by TSR artist **Robh Ruppel**.
- Ed went on to voice Volo in game products, fiction, and a column series, *The New Adventures of Volo*.
- **Much of Volo's early life and career is still obscured by NDAs**, because there were plans to use him in many projects.

## Elminster and Volo
- Volo has at times **apprenticed under Elminster** and others, and he has **irked** Elminster and others.
- As punishment for great transgressions, Elminster has made him spend time as a **frog in Elminster's pool**, and at other times as a **stone ornament**, for **18, 20 or even 50 years**. It was that or blast him to kingdom come.

## Why he lives so long
- **Volo is an unwitting Weave anchor and Chosen of Mystra.**
  - Mystra knew she might be killed, so she **anchored the Weave** so it wouldn't collapse with her. Some anchors are things that don't move; others are mobile, such as humans and dragons.
  - The obvious anchors, like her Chosen, are the ones her **enemies, such as Shar,** know to target. So Mystra also picked **unlikely anchors like Volo**. Who would be crazy enough to vest power in him?
  - **Volo must never know.** He'd boast about it to everyone.
- **His guardian angels:** **Mystra, Azuth and their servants**, including the Magister and the Chosen, quietly **keep him alive when his stupidity should kill him**.

## What he's really like
- **Ambitious, or less politely, greedy.** He's involved in many money-making ventures, including **buying and selling city real estate up and down the Sword Coast**. That includes **Trollskull Manor**, as seen in *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*.
- **Recklessly indiscreet** in his spying and prying, often risking his neck for an exposé.
- His shady, undignified methods have made him an **infamous scoundrel and scapegoat**, blamed in tavern talk for far more than he actually does.
- **His secret money-maker:** he has made **far more money from racy, explicitly erotic chapbooks** than from his guides. He always carries a supply, and **pulls them from an inner pocket to fan out for buyers**.
  - He writes them under the **pen name "Valhalaeria the Vaunted"**, and **won't publicly admit** it's him.
- **Upcoming:** *Volo's Guide to Ormpur*, written after touring Ormpur with **Mirt the Moneylender** (not Elminster), is substantially finished but unpublished. Volo spends a lot of time in **Waterdeep**.

## Three secrets
Things Volo either doesn't know about himself, or would rather the Realms didn't know.

### 1. Sa'sani, the yuan-ti merchant
- In a **blood ritual**, the yuan-ti merchant **Sa'sani** gave him a lasting enchantment that **lessens the effects of alcohol**, so he can drink enormous amounts without getting drunk. She said she did it "to save him from himself."
  - He **pretends to be drunk** to hide it, and to lull rivals and opponents into behavior they'd otherwise avoid.
- **Unknown to Volo, she wove something else into the ritual.** A tiny part of her, linked to her own body, is in his blood. **If she's ever killed, she can come back**, slowly regenerating her body by carefully **draining him** over time.
- **Side effect:** she receives some of **his thoughts**, randomly and confusedly, fading in and out. She uses this to **keep track of where he is and what he's up to**.
- **Unintended side effect:** **she's falling for him.**
  - Everyone knows Volo is infatuated with her. She used to be cold and haughty toward him, though she showed him her dry humor now and then.
  - Now, despite knowing all his faults, she wouldn't mind sharing her life with him. That's why she hasn't killed him.

### 2. Nanthe' Ryddorn, undercover War Wizard
- During a shady misadventure in an **upper-room club in Suzail**, Volo crossed **smugglers from Sembia and Westgate**. In his wild escape, he **accidentally saved the life of an undercover War Wizard, Nanthe' Ryddorn**.
- She considers him a "loathsome worm" but **likes him anyway**, and finds his smell alluring. They **share tankards and sometimes beds**.
- He's in Suzail more and more, making it his **latest urban real-estate investment center** as a way to visit her.
- **Sa'sani sees Nanthe' as useful, not as a rival.**

### 3. The mind flayer's Stone of Thoughts
- During a **tavern brawl**, the mind flayer **Ixruu Thalarkul** choked Volo helpless with its tentacles and **burrowed one into his brain**. It wasn't feeding; it **implanted an enchanted gem**.
- This **"Stone of Thoughts" constantly collects random thoughts from Volo**. Ixruu uses him as an **information gatherer**, valuing what he learns in his travels.
- **Ixruu is active in Athkatla, in Amn,** hiding in back rooms in **magical disguises**. It **guides a growing network of merchant traders who manipulate the city's rulers**.
- It **intends to craft and implant more Stones of Thoughts** when it finds the right targets.

## Volo's spells
Ed's own spells for Volo. The video gives descriptions only; the **full 5e stat blocks** are linked from the video description through Ed's Patreon (free, per the host).

| Spell | Level | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| ***Volo's False Arcane Eye*** | cantrip | Creates a **solid-seeming, opaque fake eyeball** identical to your own, as a lure or distraction. You need at least one intact eye for it to work. |
| ***Volo's Flatus Nullifier*** | cantrip | **Cancels strong scents** (farts, perfume, skunk spray). It moves with the caster for a short time, and that's all it does. |
| ***Volo's Soft Landing*** | cantrip | A ***feather fall*** for a very short drop, about the length of your forearm, so dropped coins or keys **make no noise**. |
| ***Volo's Floating Page*** | 2nd | Captures a **perfect floating image of a page, scroll or inscription** on a flat surface you're looking at as you cast. It only captures what you see, not hidden writing. Others see a glowing swirl; **only you can read it**. Good for reading a scroll you don't dare carry, or copying a page from someone else's library. |
| ***Volo's Snatch*** | 1st | **Flings a loose object, fist-sized or smaller, to your hand**, as long as no one is holding it. It can't pull things off belts or break them open, and you still need a **Dexterity check** to catch it. |

> **Ed's anecdote:** Volo once used *Volo's Snatch* to grab clothing (frilly lingerie, as it happened) while fleeing naked through a shop after a husband came home early.

## How this fits the vault (Brodie decides)
- **Two mind flayers working through merchants:**
  - House Cyl's **Iscahill** works in Neverwinter (see [[Factions/House Cyl]]).
  - **Ixruu** works in **Athkatla**. High Lord Faralthos Cyl married into an **Athkatla banking family**, and Lurs Thult began as a spy for Athkatla's merchants (see [[NPCs/Unsung Heroes of the Realms]]).
  - Whether any of this connects is open.
- **Shar and Weave anchors:** Ed says Mystra's enemies, including Shar, would hunt Weave anchors, and Volo is a hidden one. Shar is the Ugly who turns Bad in this campaign (see [[Campaign Decisions]] §2).
- **Dragon Heist (1492):** Volo owned **Trollskull Manor**. [[Campaign Decisions]] §5 still asks who the 1492 party was.
- **Source caution:** the *Volo's Guides* are **deliberately unreliable**. Vault lore drawn from *Volo's Guide to the North* (or any Volo's Guide) may be **in-world spin**.
- **Neverwinter:** in the video on Neverwinter's secrets, the host quotes Volo calling Neverwinter the **most cosmopolitan and civilized city in Faerûn** (see [[Lore/Ed Greenwood - Secrets of Neverwinter]]).
- **No clashes** with the vault.
