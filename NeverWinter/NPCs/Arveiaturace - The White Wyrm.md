# Arveiaturace — "The White Wyrm" / "Iceclaws"

**Lair:** Ice Peak, a frozen island off the coast north of [[Locations/Luskan|Luskan]]
**Territory:** the sea lanes of the Sword Coast, from Ice Peak south to Lantan (she roams it freely)
**Prey:** ships

**Source:** MrRhexx, *"What They Don't Tell You About Arveiaturace — Dragons of D&D"* (YouTube, transcript supplied 2026-10-11), drawing on Ed Greenwood's *Wyrms of the North* series in *Dragon* magazine. Name spellings from auto-captions; "Meltharond" may be spelled differently in print.

---

## At a glance

- **Ancient white dragon, unusually smart.** Most whites are savage, bad at planning and worse at magic. She was educated for centuries by a wizard.
- **The best-known dragon in the North.** Ordinary sailors know where she lairs, her range, and what she can do.
- **Largest territory of any dragon in the North**, almost all of it over water.
- **"Iceclaws":** she swoops past a ship, snatches one person, and leaves.
- **Sailor slang:** "the White Wyrm came calling" now means any sailor's death.
- **Knows the Sword Coast's winds, weather and waves better than anyone alive**, and fights with them: against ships, wizards and other dragons.
- **Still a white at heart:** she likes to prepare, but tends to go berserk and go for the kill. Other dragons don't want to push her.

---

## Meltharond, her wizard

- A **17th-level wizard** based on Ice Peak. Little else is known about him.
- He **magically enslaved her as his mount**; enchantments stopped her from attacking him.
- She hated him at first, but he treated her as an equal: taught her history and the world, kept her company. They **raided together for magic and treasure**, and both loved it.
- Over centuries she came to **love him**, close to devotion.
- He died of **old age**, despite life-extending potions. She has been **lonely and melancholy** ever since.

### His three lasting enchantments on her

1. **Spell turning (permanent).** Advantage on saves against spells and a chance to **reflect a spell back on its caster**. Many wizards have died underestimating her.
2. **"Awaken from Afar" at will.** A unique 5th-level effect: as an action she can **activate any magic item she can see** — no attunement, no command word, no touching.
   - **Limits:** not an item someone is holding in hand (on a belt is fair game; worn clothing is unclear). Same plane. She must roughly understand what the item does. She must have **touched it at least once** in her life.
   - She **can't cast spells herself**, but through this she wields Meltharond's huge store of wands and staffs like an expert.
3. **His skeleton holds together.** Spells he cast on himself in old age (to stay in the saddle, keep brittle bones intact) **still work on his skeleton**. It stays securely mounted, and **its skull turns to look wherever she looks.**

---

## The "lich rider" illusion

She **flies with Meltharond's skeleton strapped to her back and talks to it.** She isn't mad — she knows he's dead — she just enjoys reliving the old days. Wands fire around her (sometimes ones the skeleton is "holding"), so witnesses believe **a lich rides an ancient white dragon.**

**Rival dragons are afraid to attack her because of it.** In truth she's doing everything herself.

---

## What she wants

- **Meltharond back.** Death by old age blocks ordinary resurrection, so she's **searching for a powerful wizard who could return him** — probably as a lich or other undead. No luck so far.
- **Company.** Her loneliness makes her **willing to talk**.
  - She's still savage and **kills without a thought when hungry**.
  - People who survive her usually did it by **offering conversation**.
  - Some sailors have been **carried to Ice Peak to keep her company for up to a month**, then flown home — as long as they stayed polite and didn't attack her or touch Meltharond.
  - **She is not kind.** She has a soft spot for talk.
- **She might answer a *sending* spell** and come to a meeting — rare for an ancient dragon.

---

## Relationships

- **Arauthator ("Old White Death")** — her **mate**, an ancient white who lairs near Ice Peak. He talks to her when she's lonely and doesn't want her territory; she doesn't want his. He's an **ardent Cult of the Dragon member and Tiamat worshiper**, and he's trying to convert her.
  - **DEAD (locked 2026-10-11, canon).** Killed by adventurers in *Rise of Tiamat* (~1489 DR). She didn't kill him. After his death she **took his territory**: the Reghed Glacier, the Sea of Moving Ice and Icewind Dale. By 1505 she is effectively **queen of the white dragons of the North**.
  - With him gone, the Cult of the Dragon has lost its inside voice and must court her directly.
- **Laeral Silverhand, Open Lord of Waterdeep** — mutual respect.
  - **The Candlekeep incident:** she heard from a captive that a Candlekeep sage had written badly of Meltharond. She **tore the roof off Candlekeep**, grabbed the writer, and set him straight.
  - Within a month **Laeral personally brought her a newly printed Candlekeep history praising Meltharond** and stayed over ten days talking with her. What they discussed is unknown.
  - Since then she **mostly ignores ships sailing near Waterdeep.**
- **Old Alden of Mintarn** (seafarer and shipwright) — mutual respect.
- **Candlekeep** — speaks of her with awe and fear.
- **Evermeet's elves** — constant aerial battles; her territory touches theirs. Young elves talk about killing her.
- **Cult of the Dragon** — considered **the greatest threat to her future**.

---

## Her famous battle

A **portal to another plane** opened too close to her lair. Out came a **flying ship carrying several wizards, escorted by 12 young adult black dragons.** She attacked, and in a fight lasting most of a day **destroyed the ship, the portal and all twelve dragons**, despite the wizards' spells. Witnesses included Khelben Arunsun, Alustriel of Silverymoon and senior Lords' Alliance mages.

## Lair & hoard

- **Ice Peak:** the soft snow-ice is unstable and her growing bulk keeps causing **collapses** she has to repair.
- **Guards:** powerful **golems**.
- **Treasure:** ~26,000 gp coin; ~70,500 gp gems, mostly **diamonds** — including her famous **floor-to-ceiling diamond wall**; ~9,000 gp masterwork arms and armor; ~22,000 gp of statues and tapestries, many **depicting elves being slaughtered**.
- **Magic:** +3 mithral shirt (silent), +3 longsword, **crystal ball** (she spies with it), flesh golem and stone golem manuals, a metamagic (empower) rod, ring of protection, and **many wands and staffs**.
- **Wand of endless unseen servants:** they keep the lair tidy, do fine work her claws can't, and **strap Meltharond's skeleton onto her back or lay it gently down.**

## Possible futures (canon)

- She heals fast and is in vigorous health; the sea won't kill her. A lost battle might — against the **Cult of the Dragon**, or a lucky band of **Evermeet elves**.
- **If someone brings Meltharond back** (alive or undead), she becomes the **scourge of the Sword Coast again**.
- **If Meltharond regained control of her** and wished it, she could become a **controlled messenger and fighting force** — far less dangerous to ordinary folk. No other great dragon of the North could be "tamed" like that.

## Campaign hooks

- **The Shadowy Force red herring:** some missing sailors really are her doing. But she snatches *people*, she doesn't make whole ships vanish without trace — a clue that something else is behind the rest.
- **Taken by the Wyrm:** an NPC (or PC's contact) is snatched from a ship. Alive? Maybe — if they're good company. A rescue that's really a negotiation.
- **The sending:** a desperate party sends her a message. She comes.
- **The resurrection offer:** whoever offers to bring Meltharond back wins her. **Luskan's Arcane Brotherhood** is the obvious bidder — a Brotherhood "Meltharond project" would put an ancient dragon on Luskan's side.
- **Cult of the Dragon courtship:** with Arauthator dead, Cult agents must press her toward Tiamat themselves — a risky job.
- **The skeleton:** stealing or damaging Meltharond's bones is the fastest way to earn her undying hatred — or, held hostage, the strongest leverage anyone could have over her.
- **High-level payoff:** a party that finds a way to restore Meltharond (or replace him) could gain or unleash the most dangerous creature on the coast.
