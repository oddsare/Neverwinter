# Laeral Silverhand — Open Lord of Waterdeep

> Source: **Realms Lore** (YouTube), Ed Greenwood with host Ivan, a **two-part series**. Part one covers her life and who she is now; part two covers her heart, her weak spot and her secret grief. Transcripts added 2026-10-19.
> Summarized in my own words. Ed's "present" is the **late 1490s DR**; the campaign is in 1505.
> Short profile in [[Locations/Waterdeep]]; this note is the full version.

## Names and titles
- **Born:** Anamanué Laeral Silverhand, in **765 DR** (the Year of the Cowl). Later known as **Laeral Silverhand Arunsun**, after Khelben Arunsun.
- **Friends call her** "Lear."
- **Her true name is Myroon**, for anyone who wants power over her.
- **Family:** the **fifth-born of the Seven Sisters**, and a **Chosen of Mystra**.
- **Titles over the centuries:**
  - **Open Lord of Waterdeep** now: spokeswoman for the Masked Lords who rule the city.
  - **Lady Mage of Waterdeep**, also called **"Lady Blackstaff."** That wasn't an official title, just what almost everyone in the city called her.
  - **Witch Queen of Stornanter** and **Witch Queen of the North**. In those days "witch queen" meant a queen powerful in arcane magic, said with respect by many, not only fear.

## Her life

### Raised by Elminster
- After their mother died, Laeral and her sisters **Dove and Storm** were **raised by Elminster** on Mystra's orders. (Ed's short story *Dark Talons Forbear*, in *The Best of the Realms II*, shows a glimpse of this.)
- Dove and Storm left when they came of age. **Laeral stayed on as Elminster's apprentice** until she felt capable and wise enough in magic to fend for herself.

### The Harpers
- She then joined the **Harpers**, but soon left. She found the life too violent and too full of fellow Harpers who loved acting mysterious and keeping secrets from each other, whatever it cost. She was also grieving several Harper friends lost in action.
- **Khelben watches:** without her knowing, she caught the eye of **Khelben "Blackstaff" Arunsun**, a fellow Chosen.
  - He watched her from a distance, and up close in many disguises.
  - He was **falling in love with her**, but resisted it, testing himself and her again and again to be sure it wasn't a passing infatuation. He really didn't want to get personally entangled with anyone.

### Queen of Stornanter (806–841 DR)
- She kept one Harper goal: **expanding safe, law-ruled, justly guarded territory** in the Sword Coast North.
- With like-minded adventurers from Waterdeep, Amn and Tethyr, she founded the **kingdom of Stornanter**. She built its capital, also called Stornanter, from the fishing village of Stornal, which is **present-day Port Llast**.
- She took the throne as its **first ruling queen in 806 DR** (the Year of the Warrior's Rest), aged 41, and reigned until **841 DR**.
- **Her plan:** refound ruined **Illusk** (present-day **Luskan**), creating a settled coastline along the trade road to **Mirabar** and linking Mirabar overland with Waterdeep.
- **Khelben in disguise:**
  - Without her knowing, Khelben served as her **court mage, "Malek Aldhanek"** *(spelling uncertain)*. The two became very close.
  - Then he **faked Malek's death** so he could leave and deal with a dark prophecy he believed was coming, from one of **Alaundo's** prophecies.
- **Grief:** shattered by Malek's "death," Laeral left her court and spent **25 years wandering Stornanter**, grieving, seeking purpose, adventuring and aiding her people.
- **The end of her reign:**
  - In the Year of the Hunted Elk, she fought her sister **Syluné**, who had come to rule the now queenless land that ambitious would-be nobles were fighting over.
  - **Mystra appeared mid-battle** to stop them destroying each other. She revealed that they were **sisters and her daughters**, and offered them the mantle of **Chosen**.
  - Both accepted. Mystra's servants, in mortal guise, **spread word that Laeral had fallen in battle**. Mystra and Azuth made clear that a Chosen's constant missions meant she couldn't keep ruling, and leaving would also protect her people from reprisals.

### Becoming Chosen
- **Mind-sharing:** becoming Chosen meant Mystra saw everything of her character and knowledge, **and altered it**, granting powers.
- **Laeral's gift: mastery over magic items.**
  - She understands how enchantment is infused into items and how materials withstand it.
  - **By touch she can identify an item's powers exactly without activating it**, and **hold curses and traps on it in check**.
  - She can **ground harmful item magic into the Weave**. If she touches something trapped with *explosive runes*, they go off, but she feels only a moment's pain and the energy dissipates.
  - She can **switch off item abilities, or make them go awry** into wild magic effects that she can aim.
- **Azuth taught her to make magic items.** She has made **hundreds** over the centuries, though relatively few since the Crown of Horns.
- **Training:** for about a year she and Syluné adventured under Azuth's guidance.
  - They learned **Mystra's great goal**: to make magic available to all thinking beings across Toril, curbing only those who use magic for tyranny or to deny it to others.
  - They also learned their new powers: **silver fire** and **Weave-work**.
  - Then Azuth "set them free" to live their own lives between missions, contacting each separately. They were told to **hide their Chosen status**, living successive ordinary mortal lives under different names and appearances.

### Centuries of adventure
- Laeral took to it, living as **a succession of adventurers**. Using the Weave to heal herself fast and purge poisons and infections, she became **fearless and enjoyed life**, drinking and lovemaking included.
- She fought monsters, bandits and robber barons all over Faerûn with many companions and explored the planes.
- She learned **Draconic, Elvish, Giant and Infernal**, and mastered spells beyond what Elminster had taught her.

### The Crown of Horns (1337–1357 DR)
- **Finding it:** in **1337 DR** (the Year of the Wandering Maiden) she led an adventuring band called **the Nine**, based near the **Unicorn Run** in the **High Forest**. There she found the **Crown of Horns**, an ancient Netherese artifact. **The Netherese lich Aumvor the Undying** had planted it for her, hoping to use it to make her **marry him**.
- **What it did to her:** she put it on intending to master its evil. Her **silver fire fought its power and she went mad**.
  - Aumvor couldn't control her, but the crown held the power of the god **Myrkul** and gradually took hold of her.
  - She became its **thrall**, acting for evil across the Sword Coast North as the **"Wild Woman"** and the **"Witch of the North."**
  - She made the Nine's stronghold her home, and **enslaved, drove off or killed the rest of the Nine**.
- **Khelben's rescue:**
  - Her destruction eventually forced **Khelben**, then Lord Mage of Waterdeep, to act. Mystra advised him how to beat Laeral without destroying her or tearing the Weave with wild and dead magic.
  - In **1357 DR** (the Year of the Prince) he lured her into the High Forest for a **spell duel** that scarred the land, while Mystra, Azuth and their servants invisibly dampened the worst of it.
  - Khelben won by **shattering the crown**, freeing her. He took her to **Blackstaff Tower** and nursed her back to health.

### Lady Mage of Waterdeep
- **Her life with Khelben:**
  - She became Khelben's partner and the **Lady Mage of Waterdeep**, living in Blackstaff Tower.
  - The city called her **Lady Arunsun**, **though she and Khelben never formally married**.
  - Some believed Khelben had magically enslaved her. **She never denied it**, because it made some of his enemies approach her, revealing themselves.
- **Running the Tower:** she was **chatelaine of Blackstaff Tower**, and ran the **Blackstaff Academy** she and Khelben founded to train would-be wizards.
- **Building trust in magic:** she spent much of her time socializing, **building friendships with the nobility, the guilds and independent merchants**, so magic would be trusted and the Blackstaff seen as an ally, not someone to fear.
- **Secret work:**
  - She met secretly with **Harper agents**, coordinating their work in Waterdeep and across the North.
  - After the Harper schism she **co-led the Moonstars** with Khelben.
  - She was a **covert diplomat for the Lords' Alliance** and a **spy for the Lords of Waterdeep**, using the persona **"Irusyl Eraneth"** in **Skullport**.
  - She also kept watch on apprentices after they left the Tower, and could **scry several people at once**.
- **Everything else:** she studied spells, networked at feasts and revels, and **shopped for clothes**. She had time because Azuth taught her to draw energy from the Weave and balance her body, so **she doesn't need to sleep**.
- **Her greatest contribution**, by Elminster's and many Waterdhavians' account, was her **influence over Khelben**. He was a law-and-order stickler with little patience for incompetence and none for corruption. She **calmed his rages and guided his diplomacy**; together they were far more effective than he was alone.
- **What came next:**
  - For their move to **Rhymanthiin** in the High Moor and **Khelben's fate**, Ed points to Steven Schend's novels ***Blackstaff*** and ***Blackstaff Tower***.
  - In Ed's novel ***The Herald***, Laeral and her sister **Alustriel** hid in disguise among monks *(order garbled in captions)* until **1487 DR**, when they fought the lich-lord **Larloch** there.

### Open Lord (from 1489 DR)
- **There's a new Blackstaff: Vajra Safahr**, the seventh to hold the title. She and Laeral have had their differences but **work together professionally** in Blackstaff Tower.
- **How she became Open Lord:**
  - The **Masked Lords asked Laeral in late 1488 DR** to take the post after they **ousted the corrupt Dagult Neverember**.
  - She accepted and moved into **Mirt's mansion** in **1489 DR** (the Year of the Warrior Princess). Mirt hadn't been seen since the Spellplague and was presumed dead.
- **Behind it:** **Mystra** had manipulated the Masked Lords into the offer. After **Luruar** (the Silver Marches alliance) failed, Mystra spoke to Laeral directly and asked her to move to Waterdeep and accept, to **keep the Sword Coast North from sliding into strife** and to **curb tyrannical uses of magic** that would turn people against all wizards.

## Laeral today (late 1490s)

### Manner
- **She has mellowed.** She stays calm and pleasant when most people wouldn't, has a lively sense of humor, and her eyes often dance with mirth.
- She can **trade barbs and witticisms** with any sharp noble or dandy, but **usually doesn't want to**. She prefers being relaxed and friendly, so most people soon think of her as a friend. They still **respect her** when she has to take stands or make judgments that might feel like betrayals of that friendship.
- **She's never haughty, and never servile.**

### Appearance
- **Tall, slender and good-looking**, with **long, unbound hair** that she can **move and even grip things with by silent will**, as all the Seven Sisters can.
- Her hair is **metallic silver**. Far more than most of her sisters, she magically disguises it, usually as **dark brown, black or very dark blue**.

### Power
- **Chaotic good, a 19th-level wizard**, and more formidable than that thanks to her **silver fire**, her **Weave mastery**, her **mastery of magic items**, and centuries of spellcasting under pressure.
- **Her measured level has been declining**, because she diverts more and more Weave energy to **maintaining her aging body**.
- **Mystra and Azuth visit and help her less often now**, as all the gods distance themselves from mortal affairs. That just returns her to the self-reliance of her wandering centuries, which is where she's most comfortable.

### Habits
- **Remarkable focus and memory:** she can hold one spell going while writing letters, carrying on conversations and recalling runes and glyphs from memory, all at once. **She can sing and dance, too.**
- **Very organized:** as Open Lord she gets more done than **any dozen clerks and diplomats**.
- **The "high sun nap":** she doesn't need sleep, but pretends to need a **noon nap**. That wins her solitude to:
  - sing, exercise and keep in touch magically with people far away;
  - or **slip through the passages under Mirt's mansion, Castle Waterdeep and Piergeiron's Palace to Skullport** to play **Irusyl Eraneth** again.

### What she does as Open Lord
- **Underground, in Skullport:** her years under the **Crown of Horns** gave her an understanding of evil natures. That serves her well **negotiating with rogues and sentient evil beings**, whom she often meets there.
- **In the city:**
  - She builds personal relationships with **guildmasters**, **rising would-be nobles**, **established nobles**, and especially the **Masked Lords**, so they're often **steered into agreeing with her without realizing it**.
  - She's building **a growing roster of Waterdhavian women, from Dock Ward trolls to noble matriarchs**, who eagerly work with her to influence others toward **better lives and friendlier relations** in the city.
- **Guarding the city:**
  - She thinks the **Masked Lords are too slow and too quarrelsome** to defend the city from invasion, open or stealthy, from **attempts to poison the water supply**, or from tampering with the **city's wards**. So she stays vigilant herself.
  - She guards constantly against **the Unseen** and others trying to infiltrate the Masked Lords. She makes it her business to know **exactly who every Masked Lord is and everything about them**: petty secrets, hobbies, circles of friends.
- **Beyond Waterdeep:**
  - She works patiently to bring **Luskan**, and eventually **Cormyr** and **Westgate**, into the **Lords' Alliance**.
  - She works against **Zhentarim and Red Wizard influence** everywhere.

### What angers her
- **Deceit.** That includes **doppelgangers and other shapeshifters** who use their gifts to prey on others, even if it's their nature.
- **Above all:** **spellcasters who use arcane magic to coerce, mind-control or deceive** others. She will oppose them tirelessly.
- **Oppression:** she believes all thinking beings should live free, and acts against **oppressors and slavers**.

### Family
- **Three children.** All are alive but out of contact with her; her relationships with them have only ever been loving.
  - **Maura:** conceived while Laeral was in thrall to the Crown of Horns. Her father is still under NDA. Khelben believed it was part of an attempt to use Laeral in a **breeding program by a dark, magically powerful entity**; Ed hints he knows who. **Maura is missing**, fate unknown, and her husband **Lamruil Moonflower** of Evermeet is searching for her.
  - **Twins by Khelben, born 1373 DR:** a son **Krehlan Arunsun** and a daughter **Kamaulra**. Both have **magically extended their lives**, are capable wizards, and have careers still under NDA.

### Possessions
- **An ever-growing wardrobe** of superb gowns and shoes for feasts, revels and formal appearances. **She doesn't care if they get damaged**, and if she has to run she'll **kick off her shoes and go barefoot**.
- **As Open Lord in public** she wears a **silver, rune-adorned over-robe**: a specially enhanced ***robe of the archmagi*** that, among other things, can **fly by itself** to and from places at her will.
- **Little frog, snail and toad ornaments:** she loves ones that fit in her palm. She owns a set of **jewels carved as frogs and snails that magically orbit** a favorite mushroom-shaped bedside table. She brought them to Mirt's mansion; she's said that wherever her "little whorl" circles, she's home.

### Who she is, really
- Her **true home is people**: folk living together, and the friendships and achievements among them.
- She isn't tired of life or of serving Mystra. She's working toward a **brighter future for Waterdeep, the Sword Coast North and all of Toril**.
- **Unless you're actively evil, a magical tyrant or a deceiver, she wants to be your friend.**

## Part two: her heart

### Elminster, Khelben and patience
- **Under Elminster** the sisters were squirmy, rebellious little girls. Centuries have changed all of them. Ed stresses that people who live that long **change in feelings, viewpoint and tolerance**, and have far more time to **grow patient** with each other.
- **Laeral is one of the most patient of the Seven Sisters.** She had to be: she put up with Khelben, and spent much of that time **curbing his temper** and calming him down.
- **The two men, as Ed plays them:**
  - **Khelben is like Captain Kirk.** He's still trying to **force the world into his image**: inflexible, like the paladin who won't let the thief stab the enemy in the back.
  - **Laeral's view of Elminster:** he's what **Khelben might become if he ever grows up**. Elminster has had enough plans blow up in his face to learn patience. He knows the world will never bend to his wants, and that it isn't about him; it's about **getting done what Mystra wants**. So he gives each mission his honest best, even ones he expects to fail.
  - Ed suspects **Mystra sometimes knows a plan won't work** and wants to see how far her servants get, to learn their abilities and limits.
- **Laeral increasingly values Elminster as the pragmatist.** She's reached the same conclusions he has, which is why she steers Khelben: just finish the mission, and worry about changing people's attitudes later.
  - With Elminster she never needs that talk. Their eyes meet, they nod, and **they work as a team**.

### Trust
- From her years as his apprentice, Laeral learned **she can trust Elminster**. Ed's moral: **someone you can trust is the most valuable treasure you'll ever find.**
  - Even when Elminster says and does seemingly crazy things, she knows **his heart is in the right place**.
- **She trusts Khelben differently:** he's **predictable**. She always knows what he'll do, and which brush fires she'll have to put out afterward.
  - With Elminster, her only worry is that he'll **show off and be clever**. Then he winks, and she relaxes.

### Her weak spot
- **Show up looking like Khelben.** A tiny part of her has always believed **he could cheat death**. If someone walked in wearing his face, she'd be shocked, hopeful ("my love, you managed it"), and **wide open to attack**.
- That is **her one big vulnerability** today. Ed knows of no other hidden weakness.

### Her hidden side
- **As an adventurer she shared everything with her companions**, drinking and bedding them regardless of gender or race.
- **That's at odds with her public persona as Open Lord.** 5e art shows her looking almost virginal, and she plays the proper spokesperson: the Masked Lords are wise, they mean well, trust me.
- **Younger Waterdhavians** who never knew the old Laeral would be flabbergasted. If someone confronted her with evidence of her wild past, she **wouldn't be appalled or annoyed**, just pleasantly amused.

### Her secret grief: Stornanter
- **The thing she never breathes a word about:** she had to **give up her kingdom**.
  - She built **Stornanter from nothing**.
  - Even after grief made her leave the throne, she stayed **a queen on the ground**: riding in, sleeping in people's stables, saving common folk from monsters and raids. **More of a queen than most monarchs**, because she wasn't separate from her people.
  - Then, as a Chosen, she had to leave entirely. **The moment she did, the kingdom declined and was soon gone.**
- **It keeps happening:** **Luruar** failed, and she watched **the Silver Marches fall apart under her sister Alustriel**.
- **The Seven's shared dream:** she, and many of her sisters, want to **build a kingdom that's simply good to live in**.
  - It would have peace and a strong army. In the North, its defenses would turn back orc hordes before anyone had to flee their homes.
  - Ordinary people could live full lives, not lives squeezed between wars.
  - When the sisters were tired, they could come home, take off their armor and soak in a hot bath, knowing they'd helped build a safe place. It's a **pet project in the back of all their minds**, if Mystra ever lets them stop.

### Why she stays in Waterdeep
- **She takes the Open Lordship seriously.** It's **the closest she'll get to that dream right now**, so she makes the best of it.
- **She's feeling her age for the first time:**
  - **all the gods are pulling back**, and the mortals who serve them have less power;
  - her **magic is dimming** and her level is **declining**, as more Weave energy goes to keeping her body working. Her knees and back hurt.
- **She stays out of faith in Mystra.** The gods of the Realms are fallible, and Mystra has died, returned and been replaced by mortals. Still, Laeral assumes Mystra sees farther than she does, so she'll do **the best job she can, out of self-respect**.
- **She isn't alone:** **Mirt** serves Mystra too, and so does **Elminster**, whom Mystra also told where to be. When Laeral needs to unwind:
  - she can walk down the hall in **Mirt's mansion**, knock on Mirt's door and announce she needs to get drunk;
  - and she can **summon Elminster through the Weave** from elsewhere in the city to join them.
  - They're two blunt old rogues who'll speak to her plainly, in private or in public.
- **What Ed is really exploring:** not debauchery, but **what it is to grow old and keep a direction in life**.
  - The Chosen **aren't what we'd call sane anymore**. The power changes them.
  - They've watched not only everyone they knew as children die, but **entire kingdoms from their youth vanish**.

### A full list of Mystra's Chosen?
Ed won't give one. Keeping it secret leaves DMs free to **reveal a Chosen nobody knew about**, the way Volo turned out to be one.

### Ed's lesson: corruption and the devil you know
- **Laeral, and Cormyr's Queen Filfaeril, are the power behind their thrones.** They put the brakes on powerful men (Khelben, and King Azoun) who could do great harm, making them more reasonable, patient rulers.
- **Ed's abiding lesson in the Realms:** of course leaders are corrupt; **they're human**. It isn't that they want to be, or are evil; it seems to be part of the beast.
  - The real questions are: how do you **curb your own impulses**? And what do you do when you catch someone with their **hand in the cookie jar**?
  - **Would you take the corrupt ruler's head and seize the throne?** Or decide that **civil war is far worse than the devil you know**? Will the next ruler really be any better?
- **Good campaigns are full of moral decisions** as well as tactical ones. Long-lived NPCs like Laeral aren't there to steal the player characters' thunder, but to let players **explore these choices at the table** before they ever face them in real life.

## Realms Speak: Reghed
- **Reghed** (the **Reghed Glacier** and the **Reghed folk**) **rhymes with "ledge."** Ed coined it about a decade before D&D.
- It's a glacier in the Sword Coast North that used to have plenty of glacier life and **lately seems far more lifeless**. Nobody's sure why.

## How this fits the vault (Brodie decides)
- **Her 1492 role:** Laeral is Open Lord during *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*, and lets the party keep the finder's fee (see [[Campaign Decisions]] §5).
- **Ed differs from some vault lines:**
  - **Not married:** the vault says she was **married** to Khelben ([[Locations/Waterdeep]], [[Factions/Lords' Alliance]]). Ed says they **never formally wed**; the city just called her Lady Arunsun.
  - **Krehlan's fate:** Ed says Laeral's son **Krehlan is alive** but out of contact. [[Lore/The Blackstaff]] lists him as Blackstaff from 1400 and "later killed." Brodie decides.
  - **Khelben is dead** (decided by Brodie, 2026-10-19; vault notes updated). Part two makes it clear. Laeral's weak spot is her hope that **he cheated death**, and **Vajra Safahr** holds the Blackstaff title (by 1479 in Schend's novels; see [[NPCs/Laraelra Harsard]]). [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember]] says **Khelben helped engineer Dagult's 1489 ouster**. Ed says the **Masked Lords** ousted Dagult, nudged by Mystra.
  - **Khelben as active:** several vault notes treat Khelben as active in the 1480s–1500s: [[Lore/Sword Coast Reference]] ("Khelben Blackstaff's seat"), [[NPCs/Claugiyliamatar - Old Gnawbone]], [[NPCs/Indrina Lamsensettle]] and [[NPCs/Ophala Cheldarstorn]].
  - **The dates:** in the Laeral video Ed has **Dagult ousted in late 1488** and **Laeral in office from 1489**. In his Masked Lords video he says the Lords **first formally dismissed an Open Lord in 1489**, ousting Dagult (see [[Lore/Ed Greenwood - The Masked Lords of Waterdeep]]). So Ed's own videos split the same way the vault's sources do; [[Factions/Lords' Alliance]] already notes the split. The vault's 1489 matches the Masked Lords video.
  - **The witch titles:** "**Witch Queen of the North**" was a respectful title from her **Stornanter** reign. Her Crown of Horns years were when she was called the **"Wild Woman"** and **"Witch of the North."** The vault's Waterdeep note links the Witch-Queen title to the Crown.
- **Open question (already in the vault):** is Laeral still Open Lord in 1505? Ed's present is the late 1490s, when she is.
- **Who else is in Waterdeep (Ed's present):**
  - **Mirt is alive** and living in his mansion alongside her. Part one says Mirt was presumed dead when she moved in; part two has him down the hall.
  - **Elminster** is also in the city, close enough to summon through the Weave.
- **Ed's corruption lesson fits the campaign:** "Of course leaders are corrupt; they're human," and the choice between removing a corrupt ruler and risking civil war. That's the question at the heart of [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember|Dagult]] (Ed's version) and the Dance of the Revolving Lords that followed him (see [[Lore/Neverwinter History]]).
- **Laeral's dream of a safe kingdom** echoes her lost **Stornanter**, whose capital was **Port Llast**, down the coast from Neverwinter.
- **The gods pulling back:** Ed says the gods are **distancing themselves from mortal affairs**. [[Lore/Current Era]] says they've become **more active**. This is the same tension noted with Shar's dancing (see [[Lore/Ed Greenwood - Fanes of Shar]]).
- **Port Llast was Stornanter's capital**, and Laeral planned to refound Luskan. That's Sword Coast North history near Neverwinter (see [[Locations/Luskan]], [[Locations/Mirabar]]).
- **Her targets match the vault's threats:**
  - She hunts **casters who mind-control or deceive**, which describes House Cyl's mind flayer and its thralls ([[Factions/House Cyl]]);
  - and she opposes the **Zhentarim and Red Wizards** ([[NPCs/Manshoon]], [[Lore/Ed Greenwood - Szass Tam's Schemes]]).
- **The Crown of Horns** was an ancient Netherese artifact (see [[Lore/Ed Greenwood - Netheril]]).
- **Renaer and Laraelra** have Waterdeep and Blackstaff ties to her world ([[NPCs/Renaer Neverember]], [[NPCs/Laraelra Harsard]], [[Lore/The Blackstaff]]).
- **No movie lore.**
