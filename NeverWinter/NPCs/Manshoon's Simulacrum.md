# Manshoon's Simulacrum

**What it is:** a magical duplicate of [[NPCs/Manshoon]], made from snow and ice with a modified *simulacrum* spell. It commanded his Zhentarim in the field during *Dragon Heist*.
**This campaign:** it is **the one the party fought in 1492 DR**. It **survived**, fleeing the Vault of Dragons.
**The catch:** it **can never regain a spell slot**. Every slot it spent in 1492 is gone for good.

> **Canon vs. guess:**
> - **Canon:** its spell list, its lack of 6th-level and higher slots, and its full hit points come from *Dragon Heist* (via fan guides).
> - **Simulacrum rules:** these come from the *Player's Handbook* spell.
> - **My best guess, marked below:** what it spent in 1492, so what it has left, and its CR.

> - **Fire Bolt (cantrip).** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +11 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 22 (4d10) fire damage. An object it hits that isn't worn or carried catches fire.

## How It Operates Now (with four slots)
- **Avoids fights.** It has no misty step left to escape with, so it can't afford open combat. It works through minions and its intellect.
- **Fights with cantrips.** If cornered, it opens with fire bolt (22 damage a turn) and keeps its last slots in reserve.
- **Saves wall of force** (5th) for its one escape, and **counterspell** (3rd) for the one spell that would end it.
- **Uses greater invisibility** (4th) for one theft or one getaway.
- **Can call Manshoon once.** Its last 3rd-level slot could go on **sending**, but only if Manshoon is still around to answer.
- **Knows things.** With Int 23 and Arcana and History +11, it recognises magic items, spells, factions and famous people on sight.
- **Looks exactly as Manshoon did in 1492**, because it never ages.
- **In canon,** its room in Kolat Towers was freezing: anyone spending an hour there makes a DC 10 Con save or gains a level of exhaustion.

## Simulacrum Rules (*Player's Handbook*, summarised)
- **Creating one takes** 12 hours, snow or ice for a life-sized copy, some hair or nail clippings from the original, and **powdered ruby worth 1,500 gp**, which the spell consumes.
- **Hit points:** a normal simulacrum has half the original's hit point maximum. *Dragon Heist's* modified version has the **full** 126.
- **Obedience:** it obeys its creator's spoken commands. It can't level up or regain slots.
- **Ending:** it lasts until it drops to 0 hit points, or until the creator casts *simulacrum* again.
