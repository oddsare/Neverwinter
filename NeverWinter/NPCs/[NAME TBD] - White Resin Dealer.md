## What They Do

- Sells [[Items/White Resin|white resin]] to buyers in Neverwinter’s black market
- Operates discreetly — word of mouth, trusted contacts only
- Likely has a legitimate cover (dock worker, merchant, apothecary, tavernkeeper)
- Prices: **5–15 gp per dose** depending on supply, buyer relationship, and heat from the Guard

---

## What They Know

**At minimum:**
- The resin comes from somewhere south or inland — they receive it in shipments, they don’t make it
- It’s more addictive than anything else on the market
- Some buyers stop coming back. They don’t ask why.
- The Guard has been sniffing around the Docks. Business is getting riskier.

**Possibly (DM decides):**
- They know the resin is yuan-ti in origin — they were recruited deliberately
- They have been partially dosed themselves (Stage 1 symptoms, doesn’t know it)
- They report buyer names and locations upstream to a yuan-ti handler they’ve never met in person
- They are terrified and want out but don’t know how

## As a Quest Hook

**Tier 1 — First Contact:**
The party hears a rumour of a strange new substance in the Docks — white, resinous, sold quietly. Finding the dealer requires either street contacts, a Docks District connection, or a successful DC 14 Investigation or Charisma (Persuasion) check among the right crowds.

**Tier 1–2 — The Supply Chain:**
Tracing the dealer’s source leads out of Neverwinter. Shipments arrive by boat (moonless nights — connecting to [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|the Shadowy Force]]?) or overland by hidden caravan. At the end of the chain: a yuan-ti operation.

**Tier 2 — The Collector:**
The dealer notices that some buyers develop reptilian features and disappear. Yuan-ti agents have been following certain customers. If the party is investigating disappearances, the dealer’s buyer list is a map of victims.

## DM Notes

- The dealer is a **thread-end**, not a villain. They are the lowest visible link in a chain that goes much deeper.
- **Do not make them obviously sinister on first meeting.** They should seem like any other black market contact — nervous, pragmatic, profit-motivated.
- If the party threatens or arrests them, they can give up: the drop-point for shipments, a description of their handler’s go-between, and the names of two or three recent buyers.
- If the party befriends them, they become a recurring contact and an early-warning system for yuan-ti activity in the Docks.
- **Stage 1 tell:** if the dealer is already dosed (DM option), a perceptive party (DC 13 Perception) may notice the sibilant speech or occasional lip-flick before they know what it means.
