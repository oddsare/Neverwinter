# Unsung Heroes of the Realms

> Source: **Realms Lore** (YouTube), Ed Greenwood, *The Forgotten Realms' Forgotten Heroes*. Transcript added 2026-10-19.
> Ed offers these as **lesser-known heroes you can meet in the Realms "right now"**: heroes to call on when the famous ones are busy. His "now" is the **late 1490s DR**; the campaign is in 1505.

## Terrestra Vance, "the Lady Ghost" *(uncertain)*

**Female wizard and superb archer**

- **Spell arrows:** for years she worked with her mother **Valara** to devise **arrows that deliver arcane spells** to whatever or whoever they hit. Since Valara's death she has carried on alone. She also casts spells the normal way.
- **Netherese mantles:** she recently found **ancient Netherese mantle spells** that she wears as **invisible wards that move with her** in battle. Most **reflect or absorb particular kinds of spells or magical effects**.
- **Where she turns up:** unexpectedly, sometimes alone and sometimes with ever-changing companions, along the **Sword Coast** and the **Heartlands trade routes** between the Sword Coast and the Sea of Fallen Stars. She **aids travelers beset by brigands** or by orc, goblin, hobgoblin and gnoll raiders.
- **Her friendships:** she sometimes forms **intense friendships with women of any race** she aids. Years later she may drop in on them, or spy on them, to see whether they need more help, and then provides it. That might be fighting skill, money, or **putting them in touch with skilled people** they need.
- **Her nickname** comes from her habit of traveling in ***enhanced wraith form***: translucent, ghostly and faintly glowing.

### Spell: *Enhanced Wraith Form*
*7th-level transmutation*

- **Casting time:** 1 action
- **Range:** Self
- **Components:** V
- **Duration:** up to 7 hours
- **Available to:** wizards and sorcerers, **only if Terrestra teaches it to them**
- **Partly incorporeal:** the caster takes on a wraithlike form. They are **resistant to bludgeoning, piercing and slashing damage from nonmagical weapons** that lack silver-coated blades. They can use their **reaction** to become wholly incorporeal for a moment and gain **immunity to that attack's damage**.
- **Resistant to damage from spells.**
- **Walking through walls:** as an **action**, the caster can become fully incorporeal until the end of their turn. They can move through solid objects as difficult terrain and avoid all opportunity attacks. If their turn ends inside solid matter, they're ejected back the way they came to the nearest open space and take **3d4 force damage**.
- **Limits:** while in this form the caster **can't make weapon attacks** or cast **any spell, including cantrips, that needs verbal, somatic or material components**.
- **Unarmed strikes** deal **5d4 force damage** instead of their usual damage.

## Arggoth Halland, "the hero unlooked for" *(uncertain)*

**Calishite human male ranger**

- **Appearance:** very dark, almost gray skin; jet-black hair; steel-gray eyes; tall and lanky. **Three deep parallel scars on his right bicep** from a dragon's talons. A **family good-luck tattoo around his right eye**, shaped like a dark vertical spindle with teardrop ends.
- **What he does:** wanders the **Sword Coast**, and more and more the **Sea of Fallen Stars**, and has **slain many monsters and slavers**.
- **What drives him:** an **intense dislike of humans who enslave, or even just coerce, others**. The slaves he's freed and the people he's kept from slavery revere him as **"the hero unlooked for."** Tales of his deeds travel the trade routes and ports, growing in the telling.
- **What he wants:** he's a **loner looking for a life partner** he can trust and adventure with. **Race and gender don't matter** to him, only trust and shared adventuring interests and skills. So he **works more and more with adventurers he meets**, hoping to find one.
- **Background — the dragon hunts:**
  - Born in **rural northeastern Calimshan**. Always a good climber; as a youth he hunted **wyverns and perytons**.
  - He moved up to **blue dragons** when spawnings to the east sent **young wyrmlings into his home region** looking for lairs. He took part in **five successful dragon hunts**.
  - Then the **mother dragon came for vengeance**. He was **one of only two hunters who survived** the battle that followed. The mother dragon died, and a battered Arggoth dragged a badly wounded comrade down from the foothills to be healed.
  - **He still detests dragons**, two decades later.
- **His war with the Zhentarim:**
  - **Zhentarim agents tried to frame him** for their own smuggling in Sea of Fallen Stars ports. So he began **hunting Zhent spies and smugglers**, and Zhent mages began hunting him.
  - So far he has killed **almost two dozen Zhent wizards** and **more than three times as many of their bodyguards**.
  - Senior Zhent mages have ordered that **he be avoided** as too expensive to take down.
  - Instead, the Zhents now hire **local pirates** to handle the last stage of their smuggling: ship to shore, or shore to ship.

## Lurs Thult, "the Scaly Red Hope"

**Male kobold**, with the usual muted red, reptilian look

- **How he fights:**
  - Agile with superb balance, so he climbs, jumps and clings better than most foes.
  - **Fearless but not foolhardy:** he avoids fights to the death in favor of relocating to fight another day.
  - **Like most kobolds, he doesn't fight fair.** He uses **traps** often, especially **along a planned escape route**, and **flees without hesitation** rather than be cornered.
- **Career:**
  - He started as a **stealth spy for hire** for wealthy merchants, and later for **smuggling gangs, in Athkatla**.
  - He then became a **bandit preying on rich former clients**, and made a **huge fortune in 1482 DR** with a quick series of **gem heists**.
  - Set for life, he made **covert investments** for a steady income. Then he abruptly moved on to the **Sword Coast North, the Dales and Sembia**.
  - He can raid all of those areas because he **discovered ancient gates or portals** for fast travel across Faerûn.
- **Why many call him a hero:**
  - He **harasses wealthy merchants and nobles who use their power to cheat, dominate or coerce** others.
  - He **dislikes anyone becoming too powerful without officially ruling**.
  - He believes **official rulers have made themselves official targets** and may pay the price for their power.
- **Past deeds:**
  - He **spied on mercenaries and killed those he saw mistreating women, children, prisoners and noncombatants**. When he was too late to stop them, he struck at night and caught them alone. Whenever he could, he swooped in to defend the helpless.
  - Someone in **Sembia** dubbed him **"the Scaly Red Hope"** in those years. He roved and fought tirelessly, even **killing Netherese arcanists** who unleashed cruel spells on laborers or tried to enslave people.
- **These days:**
  - He still comes to the aid of people in trouble, more often in the **Dales, especially Daggerdale**, than anywhere else.
  - He saves his special attention for **the Zhentarim and the Red Wizards**.
  - He has begun collecting **magic items he can wear or carry that heal him**, so he now survives attacks that once would have laid him low.

## How this fits the vault (Brodie decides)
- **Lurs and the kobolds:** he's a **heroic kobold**, a contrast to **Kurtulmak's Chosen**, the underground kobold faction in [[Lore/3 Faction Rule]]. He also operates in the **Sword Coast North**.
- **Lurs and the nobles:** his view that **official rulers are official targets** would apply to [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer]]. His dislike of coercive nobles fits the city's noble houses, including [[Factions/House Cyl]].
- **Arggoth and dragons:** he **detests dragons**, and the campaign has good dragons in disguise ([[NPCs/Zundaerazylym - The Laughing Wyrm|Zundaerazylym]], and Murthra in [[Lore/Ed Greenwood - Secrets of Neverwinter]]). There would be friction if he found out.
- **Zhentarim and Red Wizards:** both have ties to Neverwinter in the vault (see [[NPCs/Manshoon]], Thay in [[_Neverwinter Handoff]]). Arggoth fights the Zhents; Lurs targets both.
- **No clashes** with the vault.
