# Manshoon

**Title:** Founder of the Zhentarim, former Lord of Zhentil Keep
**What the Waterdeep one is:** a **clone** of the original Manshoon (canon, 1492 DR)
**Base (1492 DR):** **Kolat Towers**, Southern Ward, Waterdeep, with an **extradimensional sanctum** reached through a teleportation circle
**Status (1505 DR):** unknown. Brodie to decide.
**Simulacrum:** [[NPCs/Manshoon's Simulacrum]], which **survived 1492 and is the one the party fought**

> Canon reference plus this campaign's decisions. Canon parts come from the Forgotten Realms Wiki and from guides to *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist*. Parts marked **This campaign** come from [[Campaign Decisions]].

---

## Canon Background
- **Founded the Zhentarim** in **1261 DR** as the power-hungry wizard-lord of **Zhentil Keep** on the Moonsea. He used it as a tool to spread his control across Faerûn.
- **Clones:** he has survived death many times through stored clones. During the **Manshoon Wars**, many of his clones woke at once and fought each other. Several versions of Manshoon have existed since.
- **The Waterdeep Manshoon:** a clone who survived the Spellplague and a duel with the mad mage **Halaster Blackcloak**. Halaster cut off his **left arm**, which could not be regrown by magic. He now uses a **metal prosthetic**, and Halaster kept the original arm and animated it.
- He hid in Waterdeep for years in Kolat Towers. The towers looked abandoned and haunted, but they were protected by a wall of force. He turned the dead wizard **Duhlark Kolat's** skull into a **flameskull**.

## His Plan (Dragon Heist, 1492 DR)
- **The gold:** take Dagult Neverember's hidden hoard, **500,000 gold dragons** in the Vault of Dragons.
- **The coup:** use the gold to **bribe and coerce the Masked Lords into making him Open Lord**, overthrowing Laeral.
- **The Watch:** replace the City Watch with Black Network mercenaries loyal only to him, and rule as Wizard-King.
- **Leverage:** he was already **blackmailing two Masked Lords**. Guides name them as **Corylus Thann** and **Jelenn Urmbrusk**.

## His People in Waterdeep (canon)
- **Manshoon's simulacrum:** runs his Zhents in the field. See [[NPCs/Manshoon's Simulacrum]].
- **Urstul Floxin:** an assassin who leads his Zhent cell and takes orders from the simulacrum. He caused the Gralhund Villa Bloodbath.
- **Vevette Blackwater:** a former pirate and favoured lieutenant.
- **Amath Sercent:** a priest of Bane, turning **Yellowspire** into a temple of Bane.
- **Agorn Fuoco.**
- **Kaevja Cynavern:** guards the sanctum entrance.
- **Apprentices:** Ered Payno, Havi Termock, Savara Firethorn.
- **Staff at Kolat Towers:** Manafret Cherryport (cook), Yorn the Terror (thug), and Havia Quickknife and Mookie Plush (halfling martial artists).
- **His ledger,** kept in his study, holds valuable secrets. In the book, Laeral and Vajra both offer rewards for it.

## This Campaign
- **1492 DR:** *Dragon Heist* **went Manshoon's way** (the winter branch).
- **The party fought his simulacrum, not him.** That's how the book runs: the simulacrum handles the field encounters, including Yellowspire, the Vault of Dragons and Kolat Towers. The real Manshoon stays in his sanctum and would rather hire the heroes than kill them.
- **The party won.** With Renaer, they recovered Dagult's hoard and **returned it to Waterdeep**. That ended Manshoon's bid to become Open Lord.
- **Manshoon himself was never fought.** He came out of 1492 with his full spells, his staff and his sanctum. What he lost was the gold, his plan, and the secrecy of Kolat Towers, plus his ledger and his blackmail if the party took the ledger.
- **The simulacrum survived 1492.** See its note. That means **Manshoon has not cast *simulacrum* again since**, because doing so would instantly destroy the old one.

## Canon After 1492
- **1496 DR:** Manshoon's **splinter sect of the Zhentarim** works to tighten its hold on the Sword Coast. In Phandalin, the Zhent agent Halia Thornton is secretly killed and replaced by a Manshoon loyalist, **Sharna Quirstiron**, who plots to undermine the town's leaders.

## Stat Block (canon summary)

> **Manshoon**
> *Medium humanoid (human), lawful evil*
>
> **Armor Class** 19 (robe of the archmagi, staff of power)
> **Hit Points** 126 (23d8 + 23)
> **Speed** 30 ft.
>
> | STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) | 23 (+6) | 15 (+2) | 16 (+3) |
>
> **Saving Throws** Str +2, Dex +4, Con +3, Int +13, Wis +9, Cha +5
> **Skills** Arcana +11, History +11
> **Senses** darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 12
> **Languages** Common, Draconic, Goblin, Infernal, Orc, Undercommon
> **Challenge** 13 (10,000 XP)
>
> **Special Equipment.** He carries a *robe of the archmagi* and a *staff of power*.
>
> **Magic Resistance.** He has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
>
> **Spellcasting.** He is an 18th-level spellcaster who uses Intelligence. *(By my calculation from his gear, spell save DC 21 and +15 to hit with spell attacks. Check against the book.)* Prepared wizard spells:
> - **Cantrips (at will):** fire bolt, light, mage hand, prestidigitation, shocking grasp
> - **1st level (4 slots):** detect magic, mage armor, magic missile, shield
> - **2nd level (3 slots):** detect thoughts, mirror image, misty step
> - **3rd level (3 slots):** counterspell, lightning bolt, sending
> - **4th level (3 slots):** fire shield, greater invisibility, polymorph
> - **5th level (3 slots):** Bigby's hand, scrying, wall of force
> - **6th level (1 slot):** flesh to stone, globe of invulnerability
> - **7th level (1 slot):** finger of death, simulacrum
> - **8th level (1 slot):** feeblemind, mind blank
> - **9th level (1 slot):** imprisonment, power word kill
>
> **Actions**
> - **Metal Fist.** An attack with his metal prosthetic arm. *(Numbers not confirmed.)*
> - **Staff of Power.** A melee attack with the staff, which counts as a +2 quarterstaff. On a hit he can spend 1 charge for an extra 1d6 force damage. *(Exact numbers not confirmed.)*

**Staff of power** (*Dungeon Master's Guide* rules):
- **Bonuses:** +2 to AC, saving throws and spell attack rolls.
- **Charges:** 20, regaining 2d8 + 4 each dawn.
- **Spells and charge costs:**
  - cone of cold, fireball (5th level), hold monster, lightning bolt (5th level) and wall of force: 5 charges each
  - globe of invulnerability: 6 charges
  - levitate: 2 charges
  - magic missile and ray of enfeeblement: 1 charge each
- **Retributive strike:** breaking the staff makes it explode.

**Robe of the archmagi:** +2 to spell save DC and spell attacks, advantage on saves against spells, and a base AC of 15 + Dex.

**How he fights** (from fan tactics guides):
- He casts mirror image and fire shield before a fight.
- He opens with a concentration spell: wall of force, hold monster, globe of invulnerability or Bigby's hand.
- He uses **feeblemind** on the most dangerous spellcaster and **power word kill** on anyone under 100 hit points.
- He uses shield and counterspell throughout, including counterspelling a counterspell.
- He **flees at about 73 hit points** (60%), using misty step and wall of force to reach his teleportation circle and escape to Yellowspire or another refuge.
