# Indrina Lamsensettle

**Title:** Actress / Aristocrat of Waterdeep
**Status (1525 DR):** Unknown — alive, hidden, silenced, or dead. DM's choice.

---

## Why she matters

Indrina is the canonical figure who **proved that [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember|Dagult Neverember]]'s Alagondar claim is fabricated.**

> **Canon (FR Wiki: Indrina Lamsensettle, Neverember):**
> She "commissioned genealogical research and found enough genealogical data left intact to prove that Dagult's claim of lineage was completely fabricated." She found "no blood ties to Lord Nasher Alagondar." She **feared Dagult would silence her over it.**

She holds — or held — the single most politically dangerous piece of evidence on the Sword Coast.

---

## What she has (or had)

A **genealogical dossier** disproving Dagult Neverember's claim of descent from Nasher Alagondar. The dossier:

- Demonstrates no blood tie between House Neverember and the Alagondar royal line.
- Likely names the alternate (real) lineage Vers Never's children (if Vers existed) actually produced — or proves Vers never existed at all.
- Cites surviving Waterdhavian and Neverwinter records Dagult missed when burning archives.
- May identify **surviving genuine Alagondar heirs** as a side effect of the research — if any could be traced.

**If the dossier still exists, anyone who possesses it can:**
- Politically destroy Dagult retroactively.
- Politically destroy [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer Neverember]] (same lineage).
- Identify (or rule out) competing claimants.
- Trade it for nearly anything Renaer would pay to bury.

## Background (canon snippets, supplemented)

- A **Waterdhavian actress and aristocrat** — both performer and noble. Implies wealth, social network, possibly stage-name fame.
- Commissioned the genealogical research at her own expense — implies she could afford serious archival work in multiple cities.
- Either had personal grievance with Dagult or moral objection to a fraudulent ruler.
- **Open question:** why did she investigate in the first place? Possibilities:
  - Personal grievance (Dagult wronged her family / friend)
  - Romantic resentment (rejected suitor, betrayed lover)
  - Civic conscience (couldn't stand the fraud)
  - **Patronage** (someone *hired* her to do it — possibly Khelben Blackstaff's apparatus, possibly a genuine Alagondar heir, possibly the Harpers)

The last option is the richest: **Indrina was a Harper agent or asset.** That would explain the resources, the access, and her successful survival of Dagult's wrath (the Harpers protect their own).

## Campaign hooks

### The party finds her

A Waterdhavian contact, an old letter, a Harper introduction. She's hidden in a working-class district — possibly under an assumed name, possibly even performing at a small theater. She is **terrified.** Convincing her to trust the party is the social challenge.

### The dossier hunt

The dossier may be:
- In her personal possession (high risk for her).
- In a Harper safe-house (their basement under the [[Lore/Neverwinter Sub-Locations|House of a Thousand Faces]] is a candidate).
- In a Waterdhavian vault (Khelben Blackstaff's apparatus, or Brandarth Hall's *private* archive).
- In multiple copies, distributed for survival.

### The leverage

Once obtained, the dossier is the campaign's most powerful single political document. **What the party does with it** is a defining choice:

- **Publish it** — destroys Renaer's legitimacy, throws Neverwinter into a succession crisis.
- **Hold it as leverage** — Renaer must do whatever the holders demand.
- **Destroy it** — protect Renaer's rule, but leave the Alagondar question permanently unresolved.
- **Use it to find a true heir** — the genealogical research likely identified surviving lineages. Track them down.
