# Laraelra Harsard

**Role (Brodie, 2026-10-18):** a **trusted friend and advisor** to [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]]. **Renaer brought her with him** to Neverwinter.
**Still the Blackstaff's Heir** (Brodie, 2026-10-18). The canon ties below are true in this campaign. See [[Lore/The Blackstaff]].

---

## Canon (Forgotten Realms Wiki, citing *Blackstaff Tower*, 2008 novel)
- A **human sorceress** with minor magical power.
- **Looks:** very thin and pale, with long black hair.
- **Personality:** an **idealist** who cares deeply about her city and its people. She championed underdogs, such as dwarf workers in her father's guild who faced discrimination. Independent, but **insecure** and afraid of her father's disapproval.
- **Family:** daughter of **Malaerigo Harsard**, guildmaster of Waterdeep's **Cellarers' & Plumbers' Guild**, so she knows the sewers and tunnels under a city in detail.
- **1479 DR:** while in the sewers with her bodyguard **Meloon Wardragon**, she heard screams coming from a house owned by Renaer. Together they rescued **Vajra Safahr**, the future Blackstaff. Framed for Vajra's "murder," the group fled Waterdeep, came back, and saw Vajra take the title.
- **Blackstaff's Heir:** Vajra chose her as Heir over **Eiruk Weskur**. That let her carry a Blackstaff and channel Vajra's power through it.
- **Founding member** of the hero group Renaer formed afterward, modeled on the Moonstars.

---

- **Other canon details:** goes by **Elra**; also used the alias **Ararna**; speaks **Dwarvish**.

- **Age:** canon gives no birth year or age. She was a young adult in 1479, so she'd be older by 1505.

- **Heir rules to keep in mind** (canon, see [[Lore/The Blackstaff]]): only she or Vajra can safely carry the staff; Vajra's power can be channeled through her when she touches it; if Vajra dies, the Heir must go to Blackstaff Tower in Waterdeep at once, or risk madness.

## Relationships

- **Meloon Wardragon**: her hired bodyguard in 1479; fellow founding member.
