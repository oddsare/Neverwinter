# Selûne and Shar — The Sister Goddesses

> **STATUS (updated 2026-05-15):** LOCKED as a parallel thread per user's "breathing world" design. The sisters' cosmic conflict expresses through their **clergies in Neverwinter** — Sharran cells (likely tied to the Shadowy Force on moonless nights) versus Selûnite presence (Moonstone Mask, Ophala Cheldarstorn). The goddesses themselves are weather, not antagonists; the clergy are the playable faces.

## Canon snapshot (Forgotten Realms)

- **Selûne** — goddess of the moon, light in darkness, navigators, good lycanthropes, mystery, the silvered path. Lawful good / chaotic good aligned faithful.
- **Shar** — goddess of darkness, secrets, loss, the unrevealed, the dark moon, forgetting. The Mistress of the Night. Neutral evil. One of the eldest deities.
- They are **twin sisters** in cosmology. Their conflict is the oldest in the Realms — predates the gods of magic, predates most pantheons. The original light-vs-dark cosmic war.
- Shar's clergy are masters of **secrets, blackmail, illusion, and erasure**. They cultivate cells inside cities. They corrupt nobles. They unmake memories. They are the canonical FR intrigue cult.

## Why this is the strongest hook flagged so far for a political intrigue spine

- **Shar IS political intrigue.** Of all FR deities, she is the one whose clergy actually does what intrigue campaigns are about: secrets, blackmail, hidden cells, manipulation of nobles, erasure of inconvenient truths. She is built for this campaign.
- **"The Shadowy Force" connection writes itself.** Disappearances happen on **moonless nights** — i.e. the **dark moon**, Shar's holy time. This isn't subtle. Shar's clergy *are* the Shadowy Force, or are sponsoring it.
- **Selûne is already in the vault.** The **Moonstone Mask** festhall in the Docks District is named for her. **Madame Ophala Cheldarstorn** — already established as enigmatic and an information broker — is almost certainly Selûne-aligned. That's a ready-made Selûnite ally for the party.
- **The Shadowy Force / Sister War overlay:** the campaign's surface mystery (vanishings) is the local symptom of the cosmic war reaching Neverwinter. Players don't realize this until tier 2.

## Possible integration angles

### A. Shar cult is the "Shadowy Force"
- A Sharran cell operates inside Neverwinter. They are abducting people on moonless nights — for ritual, sacrifice, or recruitment-by-amnesia.
- The cult is hidden inside a respectable institution: a charity, an orphanage, a memorial society, a temple of a *different* god as cover.
- Replaces or merges with the previously sketched "Kurtulmak cult hiding in plain sight" idea — Shar makes more sense for political intrigue than Kurtulmak does.

### B. Claugiyliamatar's ritual is Shar-blessed
- The "make me human while keeping my power" ritual requires Shar's favor — Shar specializes in unmaking and remaking identities.
- The price: Claugiyliamatar must surrender something to Shar — memories, a name, an heir, a piece of her hoard, or the souls of dragon-slayers.
- This puts the dragon and the goddess on the same side, with the Sharran cult as Claugiyliamatar's ritual-providers and political infrastructure.

### C. Selûne is the party's cosmic patron (without preaching)
- Selûnites are NOT preachy in FR canon — they are travelers, sailors, navigators, healers. They turn up when the party needs an ally and disappear when they don't.
- Madame Ophala / the Moonstone Mask becomes the party's tier-1 base of intelligence, supply, and rumor.
- One PC could be Selûne-touched without being a paladin — a moon-blessed traveler, a foundling, a were-creature in remission.

### D. The campaign's moral spine is the Sister War in microcosm
- Tier 1: investigate disappearances. Discover Sharran cult.
- Tier 2: cult is patronized by a noble house; Selûnite Moonstone Mask is the party's safe house; the war is in the city.
- Tier 3: Claugiyliamatar's ritual is the **Sharran endgame for Neverwinter** — if it completes, Shar gains a permanent power-asset in mortal form running the city.
- The final question: do you stop the dragon, do you stop the goddess, or do you stop the politicians who let both happen?
