# Campaign Decisions

Brodie's decisions that don't have their own note yet. Recorded 2026-10-12. Details to come.

## 1. Politics
- **Renaer and his council are the weaker faction.** Some council seats are owned by houses.
- **4 major noble houses**, each with vassal houses:
  - **3 bad**: out for personal gain.
  - **1 good**: stands with the people, currently weaker than the other 3.
- **Major crafting guilds** in the city.
- **One of the largest houses** is known for **crafting and using the powder**.
- **Another house** handles the **mining**.
- **No single family controls the powder.**

See [[Lore/The Powder]].

## 2. Shar and Selûne
- **Shar and Selûne are a major part** of the campaign.
- **A group that thinks the gods are overstepping.** It uses Shar's tactics openly and blames her for what it does.
- Still to decide (Brodie): the group's goals, how far along they are, what people have noticed, where the story goes.

Ties to [[Lore/Current Era]]: the gods have become more active in mortal affairs.

See [[NPCs/Selune and Shar - Sister Goddesses]].

## 3. Magic
- **Magic is abundant.** Reference: Critical Role campaign 4 (Aramán), where magic runs through institutions and the noble houses are a threat you can't just fight.
- **Heads of houses are around level 10–15.**
- **There may be no magic shops** as such.

## 5. Waterdeep: Dragon Heist (1492 DR)
Recorded 2026-10-17.
- In this world, *Waterdeep: Dragon Heist* (1492 DR, the Year of Three Ships Sailing) **went Manshoon's way**. That is the winter branch. In canon, the Manshoon in Waterdeep that year is a clone based in Kolat Towers.
- **The party succeeded.** They foiled Manshoon's scheme and recovered Dagult's hidden hoard (500,000 gold dragons) from the Vault of Dragons.
- **They fought Manshoon's simulacrum, not Manshoon.** That matches the book: the simulacrum handles the field encounters, and the real Manshoon stays in his sanctum. Manshoon himself came through 1492 untouched.
- **The simulacrum survived 1492.** It can never regain spell slots. My best guess is that it has 4 of its 18 left (see [[NPCs/Manshoon's Simulacrum]]). Because it survived, Manshoon has not cast *simulacrum* again since, since that would destroy it.
- **They returned the money to Waterdeep.** Per the book, Laeral lets the party keep a 50,000 gp finder's fee, and the rest goes back to the city.
- **Renaer was there.** In the winter branch, the vault is entered through the Brandath mausoleum, and only Brandath blood gets past its guardian, so Renaer went into the vault with the party.
- This covers only the hidden hoard. The money Dagult spent rebuilding Neverwinter is a separate matter.
- Still to decide (Brodie):
  - Where is the real Manshoon in 1525, and why hasn't he recast *simulacrum*?
  - Where is the simulacrum in 1525, and who does it obey?
  - How did Dagult react? This was 10 years before his 1502 disappearance.
  - Who was the party?
  - Did they take Manshoon's ledger, or get Jelenn Urmbrusk's favour?
