# Campaign Decisions

Brodie's decisions that don't have their own note yet. Recorded 2026-10-12. Details to come.

## 1. Politics
- **Renaer and his council are the weaker faction.** Some council seats are owned by houses.
- **4 major noble houses**, each with vassal houses:
  - **3 bad**: out for personal gain.
  - **1 good**: stands with the people, currently weaker than the other 3.
- **Major crafting guilds** in the city.
- **One of the largest houses** is known for **crafting and using the powder**.
- **Another house** handles the **mining**.
- **No single family controls the powder.**

See [[Lore/The Powder]].

## 2. Shar and Selûne
- **Shar and Selûne are a major part** of the campaign.
- **A group that thinks the gods are overstepping.** It uses Shar's tactics openly and blames her for what it does.
- Still to decide (Brodie): the group's goals, how far along they are, what people have noticed, where the story goes.

Ties to [[Lore/Current Era]]: the gods have become more active in mortal affairs.

See [[NPCs/Selune and Shar - Sister Goddesses]].

## 3. Magic
- **Magic is abundant.** Reference: Critical Role campaign 4 (Aramán), where magic runs through institutions and the noble houses are a threat you can't just fight.
- **Heads of houses are around level 10–15.**
- **There may be no magic shops** as such.
