## Recent History

### 1451 DR - The Cataclysm
**Location:** [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Protector's Enclave]]

A devastating cataclysm struck Neverwinter, causing massive destruction. The city has been undergoing restoration efforts in the decades since.

- Protector's Enclave faced significant damage
- Ongoing restoration under [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]]
- Lava that cooled in the riverbed and streets still holds Maegera's fire: the source of [[Lore/Dawnfire|Dawnfire]]

---

### ~1475 DR - Rise of the Emperor of the Seas
**Location:** [[Locations/Luskan|Luskan]]

The "Emperor of the Seas" begins his reign over Luskan. He has ruled for about **30 years with an iron grip** (Brodie, 2026-10-18).

- Title: Emperor of the Seas
- Status: Supreme ruler of Luskan
- Reign: ~1475 DR - Present (1505 DR)
- **Canon vs. this campaign:** canon describes Luskan around 1480 as having no real government, just gangs and the five Ships. **That's what outsiders saw.** In truth the Emperor has held the city since ~1475.

---

### 1486 DR - The Chasm Sealed; Dawnfire Discovered
**Location:** [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]]

Dagult seals the Chasm. The same year, the deposits of [[Lore/Dawnfire|Dawnfire]] are found during the clean-up of the city after the Cataclysm.

- Next came figuring out what it was and refining it
- Then making guns to be able to use it
- **Dagult and his mercenaries** see that Dawnfire could give them **military might**, enough to stay in power by force

### 1493 DR - Dagult Neverember Disappears
**The Beginning of Chaos**

Lord Dagult Neverember, ruler of Neverwinter, mysteriously disappears. This event triggers a decade of political instability.

- **What really happened (DM only):** Dawnfire weapons weren't coming fast enough for Dagult to hold power by force. He had known about the Lost Crown for a long time, so he gambled on it: he went into the [[Locations/Neverwinter/The Neverneath|Neverneath]] to claim it and got trapped. See [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember]].

- Father of [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer Neverember]]
- Circumstances of disappearance remain mysterious
- His absence creates a power vacuum
- Secret, only the DM knows this: Dagult disappeared mostly because of Dawnfire. Someone else wanted Dawnfire for themselves. They used Dagult's shady dealings to remove him. Who that person is has not been decided yet.

## The Dance of the Revolving Lords (1493-1503 DR)
**The Dark Period**

Following Dagult Neverember's disappearance, Neverwinter endures the reign of six failed rulers over the course of a decade. The city earns the sardonic nickname "The Dance of the Revolving Lords."

*See: [[Lore/Neverwinter History]] for detailed account*

### Characteristics of This Period:
- **Duration:** 10 years of instability
- **The six rulers' names:** to be decided
- **Rulers:** Six different failed leaders
- **Impact on City:**
  - [[Factions/City Guard|City Guard]] became demoralized and ineffective
  - Political turmoil and rapid succession of power
  - City reputation suffered
  - Trade and stability compromised
- **Guns perfected** for use with [[Lore/Dawnfire|Dawnfire]]

### Key Figures Affected:
- **[[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer Neverember]]** - Son of disappeared ruler, estranged during this period
- **[[NPCs/Ophala Cheldarstorn|Madam Ophala Cheldarstorn]]** - Operated [[Locations/Neverwinter/The Moonstone Mask|The Moonstone Mask]] through this chaos (exact timeline unclear)

## The 7th Ascension - Hope Returns

### 1503 DR - Lord Renaer Neverember Takes Power
**The Turning Point**

The turmoil finally subsides with the rise of Dagult's estranged son, [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer Neverember]], at **age 42**, young for a ruler. He becomes the 7th ruler to ascend following his father's disappearance.

**Renaer's Reforms:**
- Restored [[Factions/City Guard|City Guard]] effectiveness
- Improved training and morale
- Brought political stability
- Rebuilt trust with citizens
- Restored Neverwinter's reputation

**Key Achievements (1503-1505 DR):**
- 2 years of stable rule (as of 1505 DR). Not much time to get everything done.
- Pushback from influential people in the city and in the neighboring places the city protects
- Restoration of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Protector's Enclave]]
- Strengthened city defenses
- Renewed trade partnerships

## Current Era (1505 DR)
**Campaign Start Date**

*See: [[Lore/Current Era]] for detailed description*

### The State of the World
**Year:** 1505 DR (Dale Reckoning)
**Era Status:** Period of relative peace and magical resurgence
**Setting:** [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]], Sword Coast North, [[Lore/Faerun|Faerûn]]

### Current Situation in Neverwinter:
- **Ruler:** [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]] (2 years in power, age 44)
- **Political Status:** Stable, recovering from a decade of chaos
- **Population:** ~50,000, about twice its pre-eruption size
- **Magical Climate:** Resurgence of magic following Spellplague recovery
- **Tension Level:** Moderate - new threats emerging

### The Six Districts Today:
1. [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Protector's Enclave]] - Political and commercial heart
2. [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|The Docks District]] - Bustling trade hub with criminal underbelly
3. [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Tower District|Tower/River District]] - Historical and magical center
4. [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Bluelake District|Bluelake District]] - Wealthy residential area
5. [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Chasm District|The Chasm District]] - Dangerous, magically unstable
6. [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Neverdeath District|Neverdeath District]] - Ancient graveyard
