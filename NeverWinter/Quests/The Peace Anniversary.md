# The Peace Anniversary

> Two years ago, Renaer signed peace with the city that plotted to kill his father. Now they're coming back to celebrate.

> [!info] Where this note came from
> Written in May 2026 on another branch and brought in on 2026-10-24 (Brodie). Updated to **1505 DR**: the peace was signed in **1503** as Renaer's first act, so 1505 is its **second anniversary** (Brodie, 2026-10-24). Dagult went into the Neverneath in **1493**; the Dance ran **1493–1503**. The May session recorded the **real intent**, the **"not assassination, not sabotage"** rule and the **timing** as Brodie's locks. Anything marked **(May draft, keep?)** was suggested by Claude in May, not decided by Brodie.

## The premise

The **second anniversary** of the [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer Neverember]]–[[Locations/Luskan|Luskan]] peace falls in **1505 DR**, the campaign's opening year. [[Factions/Luskan|Luskan]] is sending a **diplomatic delegation** to Neverwinter to mark it:

- **The Emperor of the Seas' son** *(name not set)*: Luskan's public face of goodwill.
- **An [[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood|Arcane Brotherhood]] advisor** *(name not set)*: officially senior magical counsel; actually Luskan's real decision-maker on the trip.

**Surface intent:** a ceremonial renewal of the peace, gift exchange, a joint statement of friendship, processions in Protector's Enclave, a banquet at Castle Never.

**Real intent (locked, May):** a **political move**. The delegation is here to *weaken Renaer's position*: stir up a riot when the chance comes, seize a public moment that embarrasses or pressures him, and leave the city with **leverage** over him that Luskan can spend for years. **Not assassination, not magical sabotage.** Worse: the kind of political damage that gives Luskan a permanent hook in Neverwinter.

## The bitter history (Renaer's burden)

- **Before 1493:** Luskan was **plotting to assassinate [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember|Dagult]]**. He had specific intelligence on a planned hit, and it was one of the reasons he went into the Neverneath after the Lost Crown in **1493**. He hasn't been seen since.
- **1493–1503:** the Dance of the Revolving Lords. Six short-lived rulers fail one after another. Neverwinter is divided, distrustful and exhausted.
- **1503:** Renaer takes power as the 7th. **His very first act is to make peace with Luskan**, the city that was plotting against his father. In the May session's words: "fix what his dad started."

Why did he sign? Three readings, all in play:

1. **He doesn't know.** The Luskan plot intel went into the Neverneath with Dagult. Renaer made peace with what he thought was a hostile but uninvolved neighbor.
2. **He knows and chose anyway.** Stability mattered more than family. He inherited a broken city, and peace with Luskan bought room to rebuild.
3. **He suspects but can't prove it.** He took the peace, kept the suspicion close and has watched Luskan carefully for two years.

**Each reading reshapes the event.** If he doesn't know, the delegation are smiling enemies he treats as friends. If he knows, the anniversary is a public act he's choosing to perform. If he suspects, every gesture from Luskan is weighed for evidence.

*(Fits [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|his note]]: he knows nothing of his father's actual fate and doesn't want to know.)*

## The delegation

### The Emperor's son *(name not set)*
- The public diplomat. Young, well trained, charming if scripted.
- May or may not know the real plan. Could be a pawn his father is using.
- Someone the party might befriend before they realize what he's part of.

### The Brotherhood advisor *(name not set)*
- A senior [[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood|Arcane Brotherhood]] mage. The real operator.
- Officially: "magical counsel for the delegation's safety."
- Actually: planning the political move, studying Neverwinter's magical defenses, looking over the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Tower District|Tower District]] and the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Shard of the Moon|Shard of the Moon]] *(May draft, keep? The May note called the Brotherhood "Sharran-adjacent"; nothing else in the vault says so)*, and reporting back to Luskan's true rulers.

## Timing (locked, May)

**The party arrives in Neverwinter first.** Let them settle, meet [[NPCs/Soren Ironwake|Soren Ironwake]], drift into the [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|Shadowy Force]] investigation, and learn the city as it normally runs.

**Then the anniversary lands:** banners, processions, the delegation arriving, the city tense and proud at the same time. The party is there for whatever the delegation does.

### Ways in for the party (May draft, keep?)
- Hired by Renaer's people (or Soren) to **add security** during the visit, since the City Guard is stretched.
- Approached by someone who distrusts the Brotherhood (a Harper, a Selûnite from the Shard, a worried merchant) and **suspects the delegation is more than it seems.**
- **Befriend the son** at a banquet or by chance, which complicates everything when the move comes.
- A small Luskan informant (a sailor, a scribe, a junior aide) tries to **slip them a warning** before the move.
- Cross paths with [[Quests/The Foster King|Aldric]] at a salon during the festivities.

### The political move itself (May draft, keep?)
Pick or combine:
- **A staged provocation** in the Chasm District or the Docks: the delegation "happens" to witness a Neverwinter failure that Luskan can use.
- **A leaked document** or "discovered" evidence that makes Renaer look weak, complicit or fraudulent.
- **An apparent attack on the son**, fake or real, so Renaer either failed to protect a guest or looks involved.
- **A public bloodline challenge:** the Brotherhood advisor "reveals" something about the Neverember claim mid-toast at the banquet (see [[Factions/The Alagondar Bloodline]]).
- **A targeted seduction or recruitment:** the son befriends a key Neverwinter figure whom Luskan then owns.

The party's job is to **read the move before it lands, redirect it, or deal with the fallout.**

## Possible links to other plots (May draft, keep?)

- **[[NPCs/Dagult Neverember]]:** if the party finds Dagult alive in the Neverneath, **he knows the names of the original conspirators**, possibly the very families sending the delegation.
- **[[Quests/The Foster King]]:** if the Brotherhood advisor is one of the mages secretly teaching Aldric for Claug, the visit is also a quiet check-in on her asset.
- **[[Quests/The Shadowy Force]]:** if the disappearances tie to Luskan or the Brotherhood, the delegation may be working with people already in the city.
- **[[Locations/Neverwinter/Shard of the Moon]]:** the advisor's Tower District visit is a pretext to look over the Shard, a former Sharran site.
- **[[NPCs/Renaer Neverember]]:** the event tests his rule. Getting through it cleanly cements him; mishandling it hands Luskan leverage for years.

## DM notes

- **The anniversary is a stage, not a battle.** The advisor isn't going to throw fireballs. The danger is political, social and reputational. A fight during the visit should feel *out of place*, which makes it matter if one happens.
- **The son is the emotional hook.** If the players bond with him before the move lands, the betrayal hits harder.
- **Keep Renaer's three readings open** until the party actually pushes for the answer. Let their investigation decide which is true.
