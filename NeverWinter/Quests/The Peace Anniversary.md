# The Peace Anniversary

> Five years ago, Renaer signed peace with the city that probably killed his father. Now they're coming back to celebrate.

## The Premise

The **5-year anniversary** of the [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer Neverember]]–[[Locations/Luskan|Luskan]] peace lands in **1525 DR** — the campaign's opening year. [[Locations/Luskan|Luskan]] is sending a **diplomatic delegation** to Neverwinter to mark the occasion:

- **The Emperor of the Seas' son** — *[SON TBD]* — leading the delegation as Luskan's public face of goodwill.
- **An [[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood|Arcane Brotherhood]] advisor** — *[ADVISOR TBD]* — officially accompanying the son as senior magical counsel; actually Luskan's real decision-maker on the trip.

**Surface intent:** ceremonial renewal of the peace, gift exchange, joint statement of friendship, public processions in Protector's Enclave, banquet at Castle Never.

**Real intent:** a **political move**. The delegation is in Neverwinter to *destabilize Renaer's position* — incite an opportunistic riot, seize a public moment that embarrasses or pressures Renaer, and walk out of the city with **leverage** over him that Luskan can spend for years to come. Not assassination, not magical sabotage. Worse — the kind of political damage that gives Luskan a permanent hook in Neverwinter's neck.

## The Bitter History (Renaer's Burden)

The peace itself is morally fraught:

- **1502 DR:** [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember|Dagult Neverember]] disappears. Luskan had a confirmed assassination plot active against him at the time *(see Dagult file)*. **Luskan very likely had a hand in Dagult's vanishing.**
- **1502–1520 DR:** The Dance of the Revolving Lords. Six short-lived rulers fail in succession. Neverwinter is fractured, distrustful, exhausted.
- **1520 DR:** Renaer ascends as the 7th. **His very first act of reign is to make peace with Luskan** — the city that likely killed his father.

Why did Renaer sign? Three readings, all in play:

1. **He doesn't know.** The Luskan plot intel died with Dagult. Renaer signed peace with what he thought was a hostile-but-uninvolved neighbor.
2. **He knows and chose anyway.** Civic stability mattered more than family vengeance. He inherited a broken city; peace with Luskan bought him room to rebuild.
3. **He suspects but cannot prove.** He took the peace, kept the suspicion close, and has watched Luskan carefully for five years.

**Each reading reshapes the anniversary event.** If Renaer doesn't know, the delegation are smiling enemies he treats as friends. If he knows, the anniversary is a public lie he is choosing to perform. If he suspects, every gesture from the Luskan side is being weighed for evidence.

## Tier 1 Pacing

**The party arrives in Neverwinter first.** Let them settle, meet [[NPCs/Soren Ironwake|Soren Ironwake]], drift into the [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|Shadowy Force]] investigation, perhaps cross paths with [[Quests/The Foster King|Aldric Vance]] at a salon. They learn the city as it normally runs.

**Then the anniversary lands** — banners, processions, the delegation's arrival, the city tense and proud at the same time. The party is now present for whatever the delegation does.

### Hook angles for the party
- Hired by Renaer's people (or [[NPCs/Soren Ironwake|Soren Ironwake]]) to **augment security** during the visit, given the City Guard is stretched.
- Approached by a Brotherhood-skeptical insider (a Harper, a Selûnite from the Shard, a worried merchant) who **suspects the delegation is more than it appears.**
- Accidentally **befriend the son** at a banquet or chance meeting — a relationship that will complicate everything when the move comes.
- A small Luskan informant — a sailor, a scribe, a low-ranking aide in the delegation — tries to **slip them a warning** before the political move triggers.

### The political move itself (design space)
Pick or combine:
- **A staged provocation in the Chasm District or the Docks** — the delegation "happens" to witness a Neverwinter civic failure that Luskan can use diplomatically.
- **A leaked document or "discovered" piece of evidence** — something that makes Renaer look weak, complicit, or fraudulent.
- **An apparent attack on the son** — fake or real — that puts Renaer in the position of either failing to protect a guest or being implicated.
- **A public bloodline challenge** — the Brotherhood advisor "discovers" and announces something about the Neverember claim during the banquet, mid-toast.
- **A targeted seduction or recruitment** — the son befriends a key Neverwinter figure (Renaer's chancellor, an Alagondar sympathiser, a popular reformer) who Luskan then owns.

The party's job is to **read the move before it lands, redirect it, or own the fallout.**

## Connections to Other Plots

- **[[NPCs/Dagult Neverember]]:** the 1502 Luskan plot file lives here. If the party finds Dagult alive in the Neverneath later, **he will know names of the original conspirators** — possibly the very families now sending the delegation.
- **[[Quests/The Foster King]]:** if the Brotherhood advisor is one of the [[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood|Brotherhood mages secretly grooming Aldric]] on Claug's payroll, the delegation visit is **also a quiet check-in on her asset.** Plots fuse.
- **[[Quests/The Shadowy Force]]:** if the moonless-night disappearances are connected to Luskan-Brotherhood-Sharran activity, the delegation may be **coordinating with the cell already operating in Neverwinter.**
- **[[Locations/Neverwinter/Shard of the Moon]]:** the Brotherhood advisor visiting the Tower District is a natural pretext to **case the Shard** — a former Sharran site Luskan/Brotherhood/Shar would all be interested in.
- **[[NPCs/Renaer Neverember]]:** this event tests Renaer's reign. Surviving it cleanly cements his rule. Mishandling it gives Luskan leverage for years.

## DM Notes

- **The anniversary is a stage, not a battle.** The Brotherhood advisor is not going to throw fireballs. The danger is political, social, reputational. Combat encounters during the visit should feel *out of place* — and therefore very meaningful if one does occur.
- **The son is the heart hook.** If players bond with him before the move lands, the betrayal hits harder. Lean into it.
- **Renaer's three readings are a Schrödinger box.** Don't lock which one is true until the party actually pushes for the answer. Play it ambiguous; let the party's investigation collapse the waveform.
