# The Foster King

> A young man on a country estate. A loving mother who is not what she seems. A throne lost 54 years ago. A dragon who plays the long game.

> [!info] Where this note came from
> Written in May 2026 on another branch and brought in on 2026-10-24. Updated to **1505 DR**, with **Aldric 25** (Brodie, 2026-10-24). The Tier 1/2/3 ladder is now **early / middle / late**. Anything marked **(May draft, keep?)** was suggested by Claude in May, not decided by Brodie. The notes [[Factions/Renaer's Government]] and [[Factions/Claug's Network]] are still on the May branch and are reviewed separately.

## The premise

[[NPCs/Claugiyliamatar - Old Gnawbone|Claugiyliamatar]] **suspects** she has found a true [[Lore/The Alagondar Dynasty|Alagondar]] heir.

She has no proof. Her research, almost certainly from a stolen or secretly commissioned copy of [[NPCs/Indrina Lamsensettle|Indrina Lamsensettle]]'s dossier, points to a cadet line of the Alagondars that survived the 1451 eruption of Mount Hotenow. One descendant, a magically gifted orphan, was found and adopted by her around **1495 DR**, when he was about **15**. In **1505 DR** he is **25**: a mid-level wizard (5th to 7th level), **not yet a master**. He's grown, sure of himself, and building a name in Neverwinter society: salons, arcane circles, quiet patronage of younger mages, careful friendships with anyone who matters. He's old enough to be courted politically and swayed emotionally, but not yet powerful enough to craft Claug's ritual to become human for good.

His name in the world is **Aldric Vance** *(placeholder; Brodie will name him)*. He believes his adoptive mother is **Lady Maeve Vance**, a wealthy widowed minor noble with several properties to manage. The family estate where Aldric lives is on the outskirts of Neverwinter. Lady Maeve is **rarely there**; she travels constantly between her holdings. Aldric runs the estate as live-in steward and is happy with it: a city to charm, a quiet country house to come home to, and a mother whose love arrives by letter, gift and the occasional visit.

Lady Maeve Vance is Claugiyliamatar, wearing her *ring of chameleon power* and *alter self*. Aldric has no idea. He loves her completely. And this is the horror: **she genuinely loves him back.** The love is real. So is the plan.

**She watches him constantly.** Lady Maeve is rarely at the estate because Claug's real work happens in her dragon lair, including hours every day at her crystal ball, scrying her son. Aldric doesn't know. To him, Mother just has an uncanny sense of when to write, when to visit, when he's unwell. He finds it tender. **A sharp party will eventually notice her timing is *too* good.**

## What Claug wants: the double prize

If he proves to be the real heir, she gets two centuries of ambition in one stroke:

1. **The throne of Neverwinter, through him.** Raised kindly. When he's ready, his bloodline is revealed, he opens the bloodline-locked vaults, and the [[Lore/The Lost Crown and Hidden Alagondar Vaults|Lost Crown of Neverwinter]] is his. The Sons of Alagondar rally to him. The Neverember claim (which [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember|Dagult]] faked) collapses under [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer]]. Aldric becomes king, and rules with his beloved mother's quiet counsel.
2. **Her ritual to become human for good.** At 25 Aldric can't craft it yet. The crown can come first: his blood qualifies him, not his magic. Her plan is **throne first** (middle to late campaign), **ritual later** (after the campaign or near its end, once 10 to 20 more years of patient teaching make him a master). He'll craft it out of love for the woman who raised him.

She gets her ritual **and** a city.

## The uncertainty: this is the engine

**Claug only suspects.** The genealogy is suggestive but incomplete. She has spent ten years raising him on a guess.

So:
- He might not be Alagondar at all. The vaults might not open, and her ten years are wasted.
- He might be, and the proof is somewhere in Indrina's surviving notes.
- Someone else (the [[Factions/The Harpers|Harpers]], Renaer's people, a rival pretender) might prove or disprove it first.

**The party can be the ones who find out.**

## Three-Faction map (per [[Lore/3 Faction Rule]])

Applied to the succession:

| Faction | Side | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **[[Factions/Renaer's Government\|Renaer's Government]] + the Sons of Alagondar** | **Good** | Renaer knows the Neverember claim is suspect and would consider stepping aside for a true heir if it served Neverwinter. The Sons want restoration. Agreed on the city's good, split on how. |
| **Dagult loyalists + the people who helped fake his claim** | **Bad** | Mintarn mercenary survivors, the scribes who forged Dagult's lineage, the families who helped bury rival heirs. A real Alagondar proves the Neverember claim was always a fraud and exposes them. They'll kill Aldric to keep the lie alive. |
| **[[Factions/Claug's Network\|Claug's network]]** (Aldric, Lady Maeve, hidden agents) | **Ugly** (the tipping point) | They have the heir. Without them, no restoration. With them, Neverwinter becomes a dragon's protectorate by proxy. |

**How it tips:** the Good side can't restore the throne without a real heir. The heir exists, but he's Claug's foster son and would never knowingly betray her. The party has to broker the deal the Good side can't, or turn him.

### Factions inside the factions

**Inside [[Factions/Renaer's Government|Renaer's Government]] (Good):**
- Loyalists who believe in his reforms (purest good)
- [[NPCs/Soren Ironwake|Soren Ironwake]] and the [[Factions/City Guard|City Guard]]: the muscle that follows him
- Practical officials who'll switch sides if a real heir is proven (the Ugly within the Good)

**Inside the Sons of Alagondar:**
- Old loyalists who remember King Bann and want true blood (good)
- Opportunists who'd rule through any puppet heir (bad)
- Younger members who'd accept Renaer if he stepped aside cleanly for a real heir (ugly)

**Inside the Dagult loyalists (Bad):**
- True loyalists who still want Dagult back (rare, dangerous)
- Conspirators afraid of exposure if the Neverember claim breaks publicly (most of them; they bury, blackmail and kill)
- Mintarn mercenary leftovers and old enforcers for hire (the muscle; they work for whoever pays, but all of them gain from the lie holding)

**Inside [[Factions/Claug's Network|Claug's network]]:**
- Aldric himself: kind, devoted, unaware (good)
- Claug's hidden lieutenants who know the plan and back it (bad)
- Sharran ritualists and [[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood|Arcane Brotherhood]] mages she's hired to forge documents and train Aldric's magic (ugly) *(May draft, keep?)*

## How it unfolds

### Early: the charming young wizard

Aldric is already a known face in Neverwinter society. The party will probably meet him before they suspect anything. Possible ways in:

- They meet him at a salon, an arcane society evening or a noble's dinner. He's polite, generous, well read and keen to befriend adventurers. He might hire them for a small errand or buy them a round.
- A merchant's caravan to the Vance estate was attacked. Lady Vance is famously hard to find ("always traveling between her other properties"). Aldric handles it himself, pays well and asks the party to keep it quiet.
- An old Sons of Alagondar contact mentions whispers of a hidden heir. They don't know who. The party may already have met him.
- Someone close to Indrina (a Harper, an aging Waterdhavian aristocrat, maybe Indrina herself) brings the party a scrap of genealogy pointing to a cadet Alagondar line. The trail goes cold at "the Vance estate."

**Early goal:** make Aldric a likeable, familiar part of the party's life. Don't reveal Claug. Don't connect Aldric to the genealogy yet.

### Middle: discovery

- The party finally meets Lady Maeve at the Vance estate (she's dodged them until now). She's charming, gracious and generous. She feeds them, gives them minor magic items, and asks (kindly) what business they have with her boy.
- Aldric is a known friend or ally by now, more confident and more ambitious. He still defers to Mother on everything that matters, and the party may notice how odd that is for a grown man.
- Signs of what she is: she avoids direct sunlight in a particular way. The estate's hounds won't go near her. A servant disappears after seeing something they shouldn't. Aldric mentions "Mother's other house, the one in the deep forest, where the mushrooms glow."
- **Signs of the scrying:** her letters arrive with uncanny timing. She "just knew" he was ill last week. The party mentions a private plan to him, and a sealed letter from Mother answers it the next morning. Small wrongness, piling up.
- **The turn:** the party confirms Lady Maeve is Claug, or proves Aldric is a true Alagondar, or both, or neither.
- The Lost Crown vault opens for Aldric, or it doesn't. **Either way, the whole plot changes.**

### Late: the coronation crisis

- Aldric is a powerful wizard, close to but not yet a master. Beloved son. Thinks his mother is the wisest soul in Faerûn.
- The Sons of Alagondar (or Claug, through her agents) move to put him on the throne. Renaer is being pushed toward resigning, exile, defeat or assassination, depending on what the party has set up.
- **The party's big choice:**
  - **Let it happen.** Aldric becomes king. He's a good king. Neverwinter prospers. Claug rules from the shadows forever, and most of the city never knows.
  - **Tell Aldric about Claug.** He has to choose: his mother or his city. He might break and refuse the throne, side with her and become a tyrant, kill her, or be killed by her.
  - **Kill Claug before the coronation.** Aldric is orphaned a second time, becomes king, and hates the party for taking his mother.
  - **Discredit his claim.** The bloodline is denied or the genealogy buried. Renaer keeps the throne. Aldric is broken. Claug loses ten years of work and turns her rage on the party.
  - **Save Aldric from Claug without exposing her.** Get him away. He gives up the throne. Renaer keeps power. Claug hunts the party forever.

## Renaer's fate: up to the players

Per Brodie (May): Renaer's fate is whatever the players make of it. Possible endings:

- **Discredited and exiled:** Indrina's dossier goes public and Renaer is forced out.
- **Steps aside:** the party convinces him the Neverember claim is false and the city deserves a true heir.
- **Killed in a Sons of Alagondar coup,** which the party may or may not back.
- **Assassinated by Claug's agents** to clear the way for Aldric.
- **Kept in power:** the party buries the heir and the dossier and lets the lie stand for stability.
- **Becomes Aldric's regent or chancellor:** the party brokers a way for both to stay.

**Don't decide ahead of time.** Each stage builds toward whichever ending the party drives.

## Indrina Lamsensettle: the linchpin

See [[NPCs/Indrina Lamsensettle]] for the canon figure.

The whole plot depends on her genealogy dossier:

- **Claug got a copy or summary.** Maybe she commissioned Indrina decades ago under a false noble identity, maybe she stole it, maybe she intercepted a Harper courier carrying it.
- **The original still exists,** with Indrina, the Harpers or in a Waterdhavian vault.
- **The full dossier may name several possible heirs.** Aldric is one; Claug may not have the others. *(May draft recommended yes, for a rival-heir subplot. Keep?)*

If the party finds Indrina or her dossier, they learn the truth about Aldric's blood **and** the names of other possible claimants.

**Indrina's 1505 status** is still open (see the handoff). The May draft recommended: alive, in hiding, dossier intact, terrified.

## DM notes

- **Lady Maeve has to be genuinely loving on screen.** The horror is that the love is real. Claug has raised him for ten years (from 15 to 25) and does care for him. The tragedy is she'd still sacrifice him if the plan needed it.
- **Aldric is innocent.** Players will want to save him. Let them, but make the cost matter. What the party gives up to save him matters more than the rescue.
- **The "still at home" tell.** Aldric is 25 and has never lived anywhere but the family estate. He doesn't think it's strange: he's the steward, Mother is rarely there, and he's looking after family property. The off note is *how* he talks about her, with a child's unguarded warmth instead of a grown son's measured affection.
- **Don't reveal Claug too early.** Early: "who is this charming young wizard?" Middle: "wait, his mother is *what*?" Late: "what will the heir choose?"
- **Use the [[Lore/3 Faction Rule|3 Faction Rule]] inside each faction too.** The Sons of Alagondar, Renaer's court and Lady Maeve's own staff each split into good, bad and ugly. The party should feel lost in the political forest.
- **The Lost Crown vault test is the single most important scene of the middle campaign.** Set it up carefully and make the players earn it. If the vault opens for Aldric, the whole campaign changes shape.
