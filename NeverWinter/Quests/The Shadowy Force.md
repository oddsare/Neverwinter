## Quest Summary

Investigate the mysterious disappearances plaguing [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter's]] trade routes. Ships vanish beneath moonless skies, cargo disappears from harbors, and caravans report strange encounters.

---

## The Call to Adventure

### Official Notice

> **CALL TO ALL ABLE SOULS OF COURAGE AND RESOLVE!**
>
> As day turns to night, a shadowy force haunts our trade routes—ships vanish beneath moonless skies, cargo disappears from our harbors, and caravans traveling overland report strange encounters.
>
> The city guard calls upon any brave soul to take up this challenge. Riches, glory, or status await those willing to uncover the truth behind this shadowy force.
>
> If interested, speak to Guard Captain Soren Ironwake in the Dock Ward. He will direct you on your next steps.
>
> May Tymora be in your favor.

## The Mystery

### What's Disappearing?

#### Ships
- Vanish beneath **moonless skies**
- No wreckage found
- No survivors
- Simply disappear without a trace

#### Cargo
- Disappears from **harbor warehouses**
- Vanishes from secured locations overnight
- Guards report seeing nothing unusual
- No signs of forced entry

#### Caravans
- Report **strange encounters** on overland routes
- Some disappear entirely
- Survivors tell **conflicting stories**
- Encounters seem to happen at night
