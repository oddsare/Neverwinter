# Mithral Hall: Stronghold of Clan Battlehammer

**Also Spelled:** Mithril Hall
**Region:** Beneath Fourthpeak in the Frost Hills (part of the Spine of the World), formerly in Luruar (the Silver Marches)
**Size:** Large town. 10,000 in 1183 DR, 2,500 in 1357 DR, 4,991 in 1372 DR
**Government:** Monarchy
**Ruler (from 1486 DR):** Queen Dagnabbet Waybeard, the first queen of Mithral Hall
**Lords' Alliance influence:** Moderate. It brings military weight and mithral.

> Canon reference only, as of about 1486–1489 DR. The campaign is set in 1505 DR. See [[Factions/Lords' Alliance]].

---

## Exposition

Mithral Hall is one of the best-known dwarven strongholds in Faerûn. It began as a **mithral mine of Clan Battlehammer**, with veins of mithral as thick as a man's arm running through the mountain. It became a refuge for many dwarf families and some outsiders. Its main entrance lies in the valley of **Keeper's Dale**, behind two huge, heavily guarded granite doors.

To the south stands **Settlestone**, an ancient village of huge stone slabs leaned against one another. It is one of the few dwarven settlements built entirely above ground, and it serves as the Hall's public face and forward base. About 200 dwarf guards there control traffic and keep the area clear of monsters.

The Hall's history is full of loss and recovery:
- It was lost to the shadow dragon **Shimmergloom** and his duergar servants.
- **Bruenor Battlehammer** and his companions, Drizzt, Catti-brie, Wulfgar and Regis, retook it in **1356 DR**.
- It was **besieged by the drow** of Menzoberranzan in 1358 DR.
- Obould Many-Arrows' orc horde attacked in **1370 DR**.
- That war ended with the **Treaty of Garumn's Gorge** in 1372 DR.

## Who Rules

### Government
- **A monarchy.** Some kings abdicated and returned, so the list repeats names. In order:
  - Gandalug Battlehammer
  - Bruenor I
  - three unknown rulers
  - Garumn
  - Bangor
  - Bruenor II (1356–1362 DR)
  - Gandalug again (1362–1370 DR)
  - Bruenor II again (1370–1409 DR)
  - Banak Brawnanvil (1409–1445 DR)
  - Connerad Brawnanvil (1445–1486 DR)
  - **Dagnabbet (1486 DR onward)**
- **Settlestone** is run as Mithral Hall's outpost and checkpoint. Reghedmen barbarians from Icewind Dale under **Berkthgar** lived there for a few years from 1357 DR.

### The Ruler: Queen Dagnabbet Waybeard
- **Age:** not given. Dwarves live for centuries, so she could plausibly still rule in 1505 DR.
- **Known for:**
  - She is the **first queen** of Mithral Hall (from about 1486 DR).
  - She was **King Connerad's general** during **the Darkening (1484 DR)**, when Many-Arrows orcs threatened the Hall. She **turned back the first attack** with sharp tactics and prevented heavy losses.
  - She is the daughter of **Dagnabbit** and granddaughter of **Dagna Waybeard**, Bruenor's general.
  - She appears in *Rise of the King*, *Vengeance of the Iron Dwarf*, *Archmage*, *Relentless* and *Storm King's Thunder*.

## Laws & Justice
**Canon does not document Mithral Hall's internal laws.** It is a dwarven monarchy, so the ruler's word and clan custom are presumably the law, but no code or punishments are written down. The Hall's one well-documented legal agreement is a treaty:

### The Treaty of Garumn's Gorge (1372 DR)
- **Signed by:** King **Bruenor Battlehammer** and King **Obould Many-Arrows**, at Garumn's Gorge. King **Emerus Warcrown** of Citadel Felbarr also signed.
- **Terms:** Mithral Hall and the orc Kingdom of Many-Arrows **would not attack each other or interfere in each other's business**.
- **Lead-up:** it ended the war that began with Obould's 1370 DR invasion, after a rebellion by Karuck orc chieftains was crushed.
- **How long it lasted:** it was still in force in 1479 DR. Many-Arrows orcs then threatened Mithral Hall in **the Darkening (1484 DR)** during the War of the Silver Marches.

## People & Society
- **Races:** 81% shield dwarf, 7% svirfneblin (deep gnome), 5% gold dwarf, 4% human, 3% urdunnir dwarf, 1% other.
- **Languages:** Dwarvish and Common.
- **Faith:** the dwarven gods.

## Economy
- **It is a mine and a business.** Exports are **mithral, mithral goods and iron**.

## Relations
- **Dwarven allies:** close ties with Citadel Adbar, whose King Harbromm sent **2,000 dwarves** to resettle the Hall, and Citadel Felbarr, which trades caravans with it.
- **Silverymoon:** helped defend the Hall against the drow (1358 DR) and against Obould (Alustriel stepped in, 1372 DR).
- **Mirabar:** an antagonist. Mirabar tried to **sabotage the Hall's ore** in 1372 DR, and some of its dwarves defected to the Hall. See [[Locations/Mirabar]].
- **Famous allies:** Drizzt Do'Urden (who kept his own rooms near the entry hall), Catti-brie, Wulfgar, Regis and Dagna Waybeard.
- **Enemies:**
  - **Shimmergloom (Haerinvureem):** the shadow dragon, killed in 1356 DR when he and the king plunged burning into Garumn's Gorge.
  - **Clan Bukbukken:** duergar who served Shimmergloom.
  - **The Kingdom of Many-Arrows.**

## Recent History (canon)
- **1484 DR:** the Darkening. Dagnabbet repelled the orcs' first attack.
- **1486 DR:** Dagnabbet became queen.
- **1488 DR:** the dwarven states, Mithral Hall among them, **left the Silver Marches**, which caused the confederation to collapse.
- **c. 1489 DR:** Mithral Hall sat on the Lords' Alliance council.

## 1505 DR: Open Questions (Brodie)
