# Longsaddle: Home of the Harpells

**Region:** Savage Frontier, on an isolated stretch of the Long Road east of the Crags and west of the Evermoors
**Size:** Hamlet. About 130 in the hamlet and about 1,000–1,100 across the surrounding countryside (1358–1368 DR)
**Government:** Council of elders, with a Harpell as spokesman
**Ruler / Alliance seat (c. 1489 DR):** Spokesman Dowell Harpell
**Lords' Alliance influence:** Low politically, but its magic is far stronger than its size suggests

> Canon reference only, as of about 1489 DR. The campaign is set in 1525 DR. See [[Factions/Lords' Alliance]].

---

## Exposition

Longsaddle is a sleepy ranching hamlet on the Long Road, surrounded by grassland where cattle and sheep graze. On the map it shouldn't matter. Yet it holds a seat on the Lords' Alliance council, because it is home to the **Harpells**, a powerful and famously eccentric family of wizards who live in the magical **Ivy Mansion**.

Harpell spellcasting is often as dangerous as it is impressive. The family's odd ways have spread into the land around them: the forest near town is home to **"the Bidderdoos,"** werewolf descendants of **Bidderdoo Harpell**. The townsfolk fear them, but they also give the town some protection. For a traveller, Longsaddle can be a quiet inn stop or a very strange night, depending on which Harpell you meet.

The hamlet was founded in **1042 DR** by **Shardra Harpell**, an escaped slave from Calimshan.

## Who Rules

### Government
- **A council of elders** runs the hamlet, and in practice **the Harpell family** dominates it.
- **Spokesmen:** Ardanac Harpell (1358–1370 DR), then Dowell Harpell (1489 DR, representing Longsaddle on the Lords' Alliance council).

### The Ruler: Dowell Harpell
- **Age:** not given in canon.
- **Known for:**
  - He is spokesman of the Harpell family and of Longsaddle on the Alliance council (*Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*, 1489 DR).
  - He **took the Harpell name when he married Penelope Harpell**. They met before the 1480s DR, when she saved him from being **eaten during a fight with a hill giant king**. They love each other deeply and have an open relationship.
  - He is a **skilled enchanter** but a notably **poor evoker**.
  - He helped Penelope **teach magic to the reborn Catti-brie** (*The Companions*, *Night of the Hunter*).

---

## Laws & Justice
**Canon gives no legal code for Longsaddle.** It is ruled by elders and the Harpells. There is one recorded example of how the Harpells handle trouble:

- **The rabbit sentence (1376 DR):** two rival **Malarite** sects (worshippers of Malar, the god of the hunt) tried to build churches by a magically created lake and started fighting each other. The Harpells couldn't drive them out, so they **permanently transformed the most dangerous members into harmless rabbits.**

**Protection:**
- the Harpells' magic
- the Bidderdoo werewolves in the forest
- the **Blue Sigil** mercenary company, based at nearby **Mog's Keep**

## People & Society
- **Races:** humans.
- **Families (1368 DR):** Beliver, Cadrasz, Dostril, Emmert, Feldryn, Gosstal, **Harpell**, Irimarl, Kromlor, Mammlar, Ostever, Sharnshield, Silverhand-Arunsun-Maerdrym, Stormrider, Suldivver, Zelorrgosz, and Del Goldwhite.
- **Notable resident:** from 1479 to 1482 DR, the reincarnated **Catti-brie**, living as **Delly Curtie**, stayed at the Ivy Mansion to learn magic.

## Economy
- **Exports:** beef and mutton from the surrounding ranches.
- **Trade:** a stop on the Long Road for travellers and horse traders.

## Notable Places
- **Ivy Mansion:** the Harpells' magical home.
- **Inns and taverns:**
  - **Fuzzy Quarterstaff:** an inn the Harpells run out of the Ivy Mansion.
  - **The Gilded Horseshoe:** run by "Trappy" and his 16 daughters.
  - **The Gambling Golem:** a festhall for gamblers.
  - **The Horn and Hoof:** a rough tavern with steep prices.
  - **The Night Cloak:** a quieter festhall that rents rooms by the tenday or month.
- **Shops:**
  - **Bristle and Brettons:** clothing and costumes.
  - **The Leaping Hooves Trade Stables:** buys worn-out mounts and sells fresh ones.
  - **Jaster's Ring of Bells:** bell-smith Jaster Redshar.
  - **Nalathar's Fine Stirrups & Spurs:** riding gear sold across Faerûn.
  - **Ostever's Slaughterhouse:** nearly went out of business for serving goblin meat.
  - **Perfectone Mercantile:** an expensive guard company working with the Seven Suns Trading Coster.
  - **The Rolling Wheel:** travellers' supplies.
  - **Sixhorns Select Wares:** general goods.
- **Water:** the Hill Stream and Saddle Pond.

## Relations
- **Lords' Alliance:** a member, purely because of the Harpells.
