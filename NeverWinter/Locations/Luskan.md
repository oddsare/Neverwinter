## Society & Culture

### A Haven for Ruffians
Luskan is a place where **ruffians thrive**:
- Pirates and smugglers
- Criminals fleeing justice elsewhere
- Those seeking fortune outside the law
- Merchants dealing in illegal goods

### The Pirate's Dream
**Life on the open sea** is a dream come true to most who set their roots here.

**Why people come to Luskan:**
- Freedom from law and order
- Opportunity for riches through piracy
- All forms of trade allowed
- No questions asked about your past
- The thrill of the high seas

### Trade
**All forms of trade is allowed** in Luskan:
- Legal goods
- Stolen cargo
- Slaves (forbidden elsewhere)
- Dangerous magical items
- Contraband of all types

This makes Luskan both wealthy and dangerous.

## Quest Hooks

### The Shadowy Force
Could [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|the disappearing ships]] be Luskan's doing?

**Evidence For:**
- Luskan is a pirate city
- They have the ships and crews to raid trade routes
- All forms of trade allowed - including stolen goods
- Close enough to Neverwinter to strike

**Evidence Against:**
- Why the stealth? Pirates usually want credit
- [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|Ships vanishing without trace]] doesn't match pirate MO
- Moonless nights suggests magic, not normal piracy

**Arcane Brotherhood Angle:**
- Could the Brotherhood be using magic to make ships disappear?
- Testing new magical capabilities?
- Gathering resources for larger plot?

### Other Hooks

- Diplomatic mission to Luskan (dangerous!)
