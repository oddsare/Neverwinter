# Daggerford: The Stubborn Duchy

**Region:** Delimbiyr Vale, Sword Coast, about 150 miles south of Waterdeep on the Trade Way
**Size:** Small town. 300 inside the walls (1,000 counting the area) in 1347 DR, 891 in 1372 DR, about 1,200 in 1485 DR
**Government:** Oligarchy. A **Duke or Duchess** rules alongside the **Council of Guilds**.
**Ruler (from c. 1486 DR):** Duchess Morwen Daggerford, though see the warning below
**Lords' Alliance influence:** Low
**Coat of arms:** a silver dagger covered in blood, on dark blue

> Canon reference only, as of about 1485–1489 DR. The campaign is set in 1505 DR. See [[Factions/Lords' Alliance]].

---

## Exposition

Daggerford is a small town with a high opinion of itself. It is a walled farming community that some travellers dismiss as a stopover, but it has long wanted to grow into an **alternative to Waterdeep**. It is a relic of the old kingdom of **Delimbiyr** and too stubborn to fall, surviving war after war that swept the Sword Coast.

Most of its people live outside the walls in nearby hamlets, farms and estates, so the town streets are never crowded. In the 13th century the dwarves of **Clan Ironeater** rebuilt most of its forty-odd wooden buildings in stone. Even a century later the roads were still unpaved and several buildings looked ramshackle.

A modest **moat** surrounds the wall. For years it was the town's rubbish dump, a habit that only ended in the late 15th century. Three gates cross it: the **Farmer's Gate** (north), the **Caravan Gate** (west) and the **River Gate** (south). The **Ducal Castle** stands on a hill at the centre and is older than the town itself.

The town is a vital trade link. Here, goods on boats coming down the **Delimbiyr River** are moved onto caravans on the **Trade Way**, because the river is too shallow for ships to go further upstream. Prices are notoriously marked up. In Waterdeep, "gone to Daggerford" is slang for someone hiding out beyond the city walls.

## Who Rules

### Government
- **Dukes:** Tyndal "Daggerford" founded the town around **523 DR** as part of the **Kingdom of Phalorm**. Dukes of his line have ruled ever since, at least until the end of the Second Sundering.
- **The Council of Guilds:** created by a town charter from **Duke Conan** around **1280 DR**. It was meant to be anonymous, like Waterdeep's Masked Lords, but the town is too small for secrets and everyone knows the guildmasters sit on it. Each guild has a guildmaster, even if the guild is just one local representative, to make trade with other cities easier. Many guilds merged by the 15th century.
- **Succession:** traditionally the title passed to the **eldest male child**. This rule was **suspended in about 1486 DR**, and the title passed to Morwen.

### Dukes of Daggerford
- **Rugyar the Just**, the first Duke.
- **Edwin**, killed around 1270 DR defending the town from the devil Gorgomorz.
- **Conan**, Edwin's young son, who granted the charter.
- **Pryden** (mid 14th century), killed in the First Dragonspear War.
- **Pwyll "Greatshout"**, named for his magically booming voice. He was presumed dead in 1372 DR, and the wealthy merchant councillor **Matagar Bugo** took over as part of a **Zhentarim plot**. Bugo was burned to death when Gorgomorz invaded. Pwyll turned out to be alive, held hostage, and returned to rule.
- **Maldwyn**, before 1485 DR.
- **Morwen**, from about 1486 DR.

### The Ruler: Duchess Morwen Daggerford
- **Age:** about 40 around 1485 DR, so roughly 44 in 1489 DR and 60 in 1505 DR.
- **Known for:**
  - She was **Master of Arms** of the duchy and military commander of the castle.
  - She trains with the militia and regularly visits the shrine of **Tempus**. Officially she worships Lathander.
  - Many thought she would make a **better ruler than her brother Maldwyn** and blamed the inheritance law, but she was content with her position.
  - Gruff on the surface, she has a noble heart.
  - 5e stats: lawful good fighter.

> **Warning: the duchess may be a fake.** Around **1485 DR**, Duke Maldwyn took up with a woman named "Natyssa." She was really the **succubus Pencheska**, working for **Tarul Var of Thay**. Maldwyn was also possessed by the spirit of **Baazka**, the pit fiend behind the Second Dragonspear War. Pencheska **imprisoned the real Morwen in Cromm's Hold**, took her likeness and ruled as duchess. During the giant crisis she made a deal with the **Zhentarim** for the town's "safety." **As of 1489 DR, canon leaves open whether "Duchess Morwen" is the real Morwen or still the succubus.**

## Laws & Justice
**Canon does not give a written code for Daggerford.** What is known:
- **Rule:** the Duke rules with the Council of Guilds. Guild law and trade regulation carry real weight.
- **Militia service is compulsory.** Every citizen healthy enough to serve must do so, **regardless of gender or race**. Exempt are those too young or too old, pregnant women, and women raising young children. Recruits get basic training and simple weapons. Service is **three days a month**, mostly light duty unless the town is in danger. In 1368 DR the militia numbered only **50**, a tenth of the citizens.
- **Defenders:** the Duke's personal guard, plus a **Master of the Hunt** (Kelson Darktreader in the late 15th century).
- **Succession law:** the eldest-son rule was suspended in about 1486 DR.
- **Smuggling:** in 1372 DR the **Mariners' Alliance** guild, which serves the town's sailors and boatmen, was caught **smuggling weapons with the Zhentarim**.

## People & Society
- **Races:** dwarves, halflings and humans.
- **Faiths:** Amaunator, Chauntea, Lathander, Tempus and Tymora.
- **Notable residents (late 15th century):**
  - **Curran Corvalin:** a very wealthy halfling who keeps the Fairfortune Hall shrine.
  - **Kelson Darktreader:** Master of the Hunt, in service for well over a century.
  - **Filarion Filvendorson:** of the Floshin family, possibly once head of the town's thieves' guild.
  - **Darfin "Longwalker" Floshin:** an aged elf who guards secret magic in the Ardeep Forest.
  - **Jekk Ironfist:** a retired gladiator from Hillsfar.
  - **Sir Isteval:** a retired Cormyrean knight.

## Economy
- **Exports:** furs and clothing.
- **Imports:** green wood, from Andalor's Mill in Bowshot.
- **Trade:** the river-to-caravan transfer point, with steep prices. Otherwise largely self-sufficient.

## Relations
- **Lords' Alliance:** joined some time before the Second Sundering.
- **Waterdeep:** it imitates the big city but can't match it.
- **Thay and the Zhentarim:** both have meddled in the town (Matagar Bugo's coup in 1372, Pencheska in 1485–1489).

## Recent History (canon, c. 1485–1489 DR)
- The Pencheska plot described above.
- **1489 DR:** the "duchess" sent adventurers to find **Christos Tellum**, heir of Tellum's Hold, who had gone missing in the Dessarin Valley with a mysterious magic flask.
- The vampire **Dar Lavien** then unleashed undead on the town before the same adventurers killed him.
- The "duchess" used the adventurers to recover the **Ambergul Blade**, which ended up with the rakshasa **Nadir**. Through a slaad portal, Nadir used it to uncork the flask and release the ancient red dragon **Karrnsyrrl**, who badly damaged Daggerford and left with Nadir and Christos.

## 1505 DR: Open Questions (Brodie)
- Was Morwen ever rescued, or is the succubus still on the throne?
- Has Daggerford rebuilt after Karrnsyrrl's attack?
