# Amphail: Waterdeep's Horse Country

**Region:** Sword Coast North, three days' ride north of Waterdeep
**Size:** Village. About 850 people: 600 in the village and 250 on outlying farms
**Government:** Led by a **Lord Warder**
**Ruler (from c. 1485 DR):** Lord Warder Dauner Ilzimmer
**Lords' Alliance influence:** Low. It sits in Waterdeep's orbit.

> Canon reference only, as of about 1485–1489 DR. The campaign is set in 1525 DR. See [[Factions/Lords' Alliance]].

---

## Exposition

Amphail is a farming village three days' ride north of Waterdeep. It grows much of the city's food and breeds some of the finest horses on the Sword Coast: **Amphail grays**, glossy **black chargers**, and the showy **Amphail fancy**, a favourite of nobles. In the warmer months the whole village **smells of horse manure**.

The village is named after **Amphail the Just**, one of Waterdeep's early warlords, who had estates here. Locals say his ghost, the **"Ghost of Amphail,"** still patrols the streets.

Wealth here means horses, and the biggest breeders are **Waterdhavian noble families**. That ties this quiet village closely to the politics of the City of Splendors.

## Who Rules

### Government
- **The Lord Warder** leads the village. Holders on record: **Briiathor Alougarr** (1372 DR) and **Dauner Ilzimmer** (1485 DR).
- **Possible rotation (fan source, unconfirmed):** one fan wiki says the office rotates among three leading families, **Amcathra, Ilzimmer and Roaringhorn**, and changes at each Shieldmeet. The Forgotten Realms Wiki doesn't confirm this.
- **The landowners who breed horses are the local powers:**
  - **Roaringhorn** of Waterdeep, the largest breeder.
  - **Amcathra**, **Ilzimmer**, **Jhansczil** and **Tarm**.
  - Individual breeders **Rorth Baldasker**, **Ohm Oglyntyr** and **Elraghona Selember**.
  - The **Eagleshield** family doesn't breed horses but makes tack and runs a farm for sick animals.

### The Ruler: Dauner Ilzimmer
- **Age:** not given in canon.
- **Known for:**
  - He is a member of the **Ilzimmer** family and lord of Amphail some time after the War of the Silver Marches (*Storm King's Thunder*).
  - He is a **bombastic family man who loves horses more than people.**
  - He used to live in **Waterdeep**.
  - He is Chondathan, lawful neutral, with noble stats in 5e.
  - He speaks for Amphail on the Lords' Alliance council.

### The earlier Lord Warder: Briiathor Alougarr (c. 1372 DR)
- A muscular Tethyrian, loyal to Waterdeep's Open Lord **Piergeiron the Paladinson**.

## Laws & Justice
**Canon gives no legal code for Amphail.** What is known:
- **No militia.** The village relies on **Waterdeep's guards**, who patrol from **Rassalantar**.
- **Who rules:** the Lord Warder governs, and the horse-breeding families hold the money.

---

## People & Society
- **Races:** mostly human, with a few half-elves, dwarves, halflings and others. Citizens are called *Amphailan*.
- **Faith:** Chauntea.
- **Alignment:** neutral good.
- **Notable residents (c. 1372 DR):**
  - **Dlara:** an 8-foot-tall Chultan wrestler.
  - **Mother Gothal:** runs Mother Gothal's festhall.
  - **Krivvin Shamblestar:** owner of the Stag-Horned Flagon.

## Economy
- **Exports:** farm produce, mostly food for Waterdeep, and **fine horses**.
- **The Stag-Horned Flagon** makes two local drinks: a dense, dark **barley beer** popular with locals and visitors, and a **spicy purple mushroom wine**.

## Notable Places
- **Landmark:** Horse Pond.
- **Inns, festhalls and taverns:** Golaund Sester's, Hagala's Manyturrets, Mother Gothal's, The Stag-Horned Flagon, The Stone Stallion.
- **Shops:** Ammakyl Flowers and Foods, Blodhlar's Wares, Eagleshield Fine Horse Leathers, Elboar's Finest, Elraghona Selember's Ranch, Halana Shauluth's, Imbryl's Cloaks, Laughing Bandit, Maerlbar Eggs & Fresh Fowl, The Middens, Pelost Galathaer, Shrunedalar's Secrets, Ulvinhand Smithy.

## Relations
- **Waterdeep:** feeds it and depends on it for defence. Its breeders are Waterdhavian nobles.
- **Lords' Alliance:** a member with its own voice on the council.
