# Silverymoon: The Gem of the North

**Also Known As:** The Gem of the North, the Welcoming City, the Great Silver Hearth, the Shining Towers, Silvertowers, Homehaven (to the elves), Aerasumé (ancient Elven), "the Moon"
**Region:** Formerly Luruar (the Silver Marches), northwest Faerûn
**Size:** Metropolis, the largest city in the interior North
**Government:** Constitutional magocratic republic, meaning wizards rule under a constitution
**Ruler / Alliance seat (c. 1489 DR):** High Mage Taern "Thunderspell" Hornblade
**Lords' Alliance influence:** High

> Canon reference only, as of about 1489 DR. The campaign is set in 1525 DR. See [[Factions/Lords' Alliance]].

---

## Exposition

Silverymoon is a city of learning, music and magic, built on both banks of a bend in the River Rauvin, deep in the wild interior of the North. The older half, **Northbank**, is walled in a half circle with three gates. A broad Market runs from the wall down to the Docks, and the **High Palace** stands just inside the eastern wall. The two banks are joined by the **Moonbridge**, a span of pure magic that is invisible unless moonlight falls on it. The city's **mythal**, an ancient protective ward, was thought lost in the Spellplague. It had only gone dormant inside the Moonbridge, and it bloomed back to full strength when Mystra returned.

People call Silverymoon an echo of Myth Drannor. Under High Lady **Alustriel Silverhand**, humans, elves, dwarves and halflings lived together within its walls as nowhere else in the North. By 1357 DR it was the most influential city in the North after Waterdeep. It was the heart of the **Silver Marches** confederation until that confederation collapsed in 1488 DR.

The land was sacred to **Mielikki** and the unicorn goddess **Lurue** long before the city existed. It began in 384 DR as a rope-and-plank bridge across the shallows, called Silverymoon Ford.

## Who Rules

### Government
- **Constitutional magocratic republic.** The High Lady or High Mage sits at the head.
- **How Alustriel ruled:**
  - Legal disputes were heard each morning.
  - In the evening she announced new laws and decrees, met foreign delegations and oversaw policy debates.
  - If a debate ran too long, she had the arguments **written down, copied and handed out free to the citizens**.
  - The public argued the matter among themselves and handed in written opinions the next day.
  - She then shaped the final policy from those opinions and her own experience.
- **Council of the Marches:** Silverymoon held a seat as a Peer while the Silver Marches existed. Taern sat there and generally followed Alustriel's wishes.
- **Royal badge:** a silver star and crescent moon on royal blue.

### The Ruler: Taern "Thunderspell" Hornblade
- **Age:** born 1204 DR, which makes him about 285 in 1489 DR and 321 in 1525 DR. Sources conflict, so the wiki uses 1204 DR as a compromise. He has stayed healthy with **potions of longevity**.
- **Titles:** High Mage of Silverymoon, Lord Warder, Master Mage of the Spellguard, Seneschal of the High Palace. Disrespectful nickname: "Old Thunderspells."
- **Known for:**
  - He succeeded Alustriel as High Mage when she became High Lady.
  - He earned "Thunderspell" as a young man, showing off deadly battle spells in mage-fair duels.
  - He is a longtime **Harper**.
  - He founded the **Spellguard** in 1255 DR to protect Alustriel.
  - In 1361 DR he and the Spellguard held the southern walls against a dracolich.
  - He crafted cloaks of protection against chaos for the defenders against an orc army.
  - In the 1480s DR, Methrammar Aerasumé, leader of the Silver Marches, sent him to the Council of Waterdeep because of his diplomatic skill.
  - Stats (3e): lawful good, 18th-level evoker, CR 18. He has a rat familiar named Vesk.
  - His motto about Alustriel: *"Show me Alustriel's will, and I'll firmly label it the right way."*

## Laws & Justice
Canon gives **no written legal code** for Silverymoon. Here is what is known:
- **The Church of Cyric is outlawed.**
- Laws are made by decree from the ruler, shaped by the written public debate described above.
- **Defenders, from highest authority to lowest:**
  - **Knights in Silver:** the standing army, 500+ cavalry of humans and half-elves. They ride patrols 50 miles out in every direction, guard the walls and caravans, and escort important travellers. Their name comes from a ballad by the bard Mintiper Moonsilver (about 1349 DR).
  - **The Spellguard:** founded 1255 DR, led by the half-elf wizard Jorus Azuremantle. By 1372 DR it had 32 members, 7 of them Warders who oversee the rest. In the early Silver Marches years, a Spellguard mage always rode with Knight patrols.
  - **The Moon Garrison and the Silverwatch:** the city's everyday guards. They man the gates and walls, guard civic buildings and police the streets. They obey any Knight or Spellguard member without question, and the Knights take turns doing ordinary guard duty beside them.
  - **The High Guard:** only veteran Knights in Silver may join. They guard the High Palace.
  - **The Argent Legion:** the Silver Marches' combined army, based here while the confederation lasted. Its first High Marshal was Methrammar Aerasumé.
  - **The Sword-of-Coins:** an officer who hires mercenary bands for set jobs, such as clearing orcs from an area or destroying a troll lair.
- **Magical gear:** the city made many magical cloaks, all plain grey or brown, that fit any human-sized wearer:
  - *cloaks of reflection*, given to every Silverymoon messenger and ranger
  - *cloaks of the shield*
  - *cloaks of symbiotic protection*
  - *cloaks of survival*
  - *cloaks of stars*

## People & Society
- **Races:** 41% human, 29% elf, 12% half-elf, 10% shield dwarf, 5% lightfoot halfling, 2% gnome. Citizens are called *Silvaeren*.
- **Language:** Northern (Luruaran).
- **Values:** art, beauty and scholarship, music most of all. Magic shapes the city's culture.
- **Faiths:** Lathander, Oghma, Milil, Selûne, Helm, Mielikki, Shiallia, Silvanus, Tymora and Mystra. The city **also has a temple to Shar.** Cyric's church is outlawed. (Compare [[NPCs/Selune and Shar - Sister Goddesses]].)

## Economy
- **Industries:** the arts and scholarship, schools of music, magical study and forestry.
- **Exports:** Silverymoon Blue wine, books, paper, ink, herbs, glass and glassware, furs, timber and valuable ores.
- **Imports:** food (especially grain and livestock), armour, weapons, footwear and cloth.
- **Trade partners:** Sundabar is one of its three main partners and brings in the riches of Citadel Adbar. Caravans through Silverymoon Pass face bandits and orcs.
- **Currency:** the *eclipsed moon* (5 pp), *moon* (2 pp), *unicorn* (1 pp), and gold *dragons*, electrum *swords*, silver *shields* and copper *glints*.

## Relations
- **Lords' Alliance:** a core member. By 1357 DR it was second only to Waterdeep.
- **Harpers:** a Harper stronghold. Alustriel and Khelben openly admitted membership.
- **Goldenfields:** on good terms with the farming town.
- **Mithral Hall:** helped drive off a drow siege of Mithral Hall from Menzoberranzan in 1358 DR, and helped again against Obould's orcs in 1372 DR.
- **Enemies:**
  - the demons of Hellgate Keep (Grintharke)
  - King Graul's Ice Mountain orcs and the Kingdom of Many-Arrows
  - Luskan's **Arcane Brotherhood**, which came to see the Silver Marches as its main target
  - Zhentarim expansion into the North

## Recent History (canon)
- **1484–1485 DR:** the War of the Silver Marches.
- **1488 DR:** Luruar (the Silver Marches) dissolved after the dwarven states left. Silverymoon lost its regional bloc.
- **Vault link:** Ophala Cheldarstorn's Many-Starred Cloak is tied to Silverymoon. [[NPCs/Claugiyliamatar - Old Gnawbone|Claugiyliamatar]] spies on Alustriel and the city's rulers by scrying.
