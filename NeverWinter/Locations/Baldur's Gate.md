# Baldur's Gate: The City of Blood

**Also Known As:** The Gate, Gray Harbor, Loklee, the City of Blood, "halfway to everywhere"
**Region:** Sword Coast, Western Heartlands, on the River Chionthar
**Size:** Metropolis. About 125,000 in 1492 DR (120,000–140,000 in 1479 DR)
**Government:** Plutocracy, meaning the wealthy rule. Power sits with the **Council of Four** (the Grand Dukes) and the **Parliament of Peers**.
**Ruler (c. 1489 DR):** Grand Duke Ulder Ravengard, Marshal of the Flaming Fist
**Lords' Alliance influence:** High. It is the Alliance's southern anchor.

> Canon reference only, as of about 1489–1492 DR. *Baldur's Gate 3* (1492 DR) shook the city badly. The campaign is set in 1525 DR. See [[Factions/Lords' Alliance]].

---

## Exposition

Baldur's Gate is one of the largest and richest merchant cities on the Sword Coast, a crowded place of trade and opportunity. A Sembian saying goes: *"Whoever holds the Gate holds the goods."* Its **Gray Harbor** handles cargo to rival the ports of Calimshan. By the 15th century it had **outcompeted both Waterdeep and Amn** to become the greatest trading centre on the Sword Coast.

The city has three layers:
- **The Upper City:** wide, well-lit streets hung with plants, where the noble **patriars** live, guarded by the Watch.
- **The Lower City:** a crescent of tall, narrow buildings and packed alleys inside the walls, where trade and labour of every kind goes on.
- **The Outer City:** a sprawling, muddy shantytown outside the walls. It is taxed and nominally ruled, but barely governed.

Baldurians are proud that anyone can make a home here whatever their race, faith or past. Merchants, refugees, pirates and smugglers have all found a place. Even so, the city is remarkably clean and safe. Its people won't tolerate drunken debauchery, and the laws are enforced on the spot.

The Gate has always stayed neutral. It defends trade, stays out of other powers' wars, and is so well walled and garrisoned that few have ever seriously considered attacking it.

## Who Rules

### Government
- **The Council of Four:** created after the city's famous **tax revolt** against gate tolls. Grand Dukes are elected for life, or until they retire. After a coup attempt by Grand Duke Valarken, parliament briefly ruled through Grand Duke Portyr alone. The Council of Four was restored before 1479 DR.
- **The Parliament of Peers:** about 50 influential Baldurians. They elect the Dukes, act as the legislature and advise the Council.
- **Five deputies under the Council:**
  - **Harbormaster:** runs the Gray Harbor, sets tariffs on imports and keeps the shipping records.
  - **High Constable and Master of Walls:** commands the Watch Citadel.
  - **Master of Drains and Underways:** sewers, drains, waterways and aqueducts.
  - **Master of Cobbles:** roads, bridges and stonework.
  - **Purse Master:** collects taxes, invests city funds, pays officials and oversees the Bailiff of the Wide.
- **By tradition**, one Grand Duke is also Marshal of the **Flaming Fist**.

### The Ruler: Ulder Ravengard
- **Age:** canon gives no birth year. A fan wiki says **1440 DR**, which would make him about 49 in 1489 DR and 85 in 1525 DR. This is **unconfirmed**.
- **Titles:** Grand Duke, Marshal of the Flaming Fist. Formerly a *Blaze* (Fist officer) and Warden of the Wyrm's Rock fortress.
- **Known for:**
  - He was born poor in the Lower City and joined the Fist young, rising on fighting skill and brains.
  - He became Marshal in **1482 DR** when Grand Duke Abdel Adrian died, and was elected Grand Duke before 1488 DR despite protests in Parliament.
  - He **refused to give up command of the Fist**, which made him the most powerful person in the city.
  - He worships Torm. His wife is Francesca and his son is **Wyll Ravengard**.
  - He was the city's lead delegate to the Council of Waterdeep against the Cult of the Dragon, hoping a victory would prove Baldur's Gate the strongest city in Faerûn.
  - **1492 DR:** he went to Elturel on a diplomatic trip, and the city was dragged into **Avernus**. The trip was part of Thalamra Vanthampur's plot to become Grand Duke. The Fist fell apart without him.
  - Later he was imprisoned beneath the Gray Harbor, and **Enver Gortash** took his seat (*Baldur's Gate 3*).
  - 5e stats: lawful neutral, 11th-level fighter.

## Laws & Justice
- **The code:** strict and complex. It is a tangle of regulations, decrees and treatises that almost no one but the city's **barristers** and some patriars can follow.
- **Who it favours:** patriars, foreign diplomats, the Flaming Fist and the Watch. Ordinary citizens, and Outer City residents especially, get far less protection.
- **Consumer-protection laws:** the **Public Health Act** and the **Civilian Protection Order**. Breaches, usually found through customer complaints, are investigated by agents of the Council of Four. Anyone under investigation must not interfere and must not leave the city, on pain of contempt of the Council.
- **Enforcement:** anyone caught in the act is arrested and punished immediately by the Watch or the Fist. Anyone who obeys the law can walk the streets freely.
- **Punishments fit the crime:**
  - Violence or theft: **public whipping or removal of a finger.**
  - Breaking a contract: **forced labour.**
  - Disturbing the peace or public lewdness: **a night in the stocks.**
- **Licences:** inns and taverns inside the walls need a **hostelry licence** to operate.
- **Taxes:** tolls at nearly every gate, collected by a huge bureaucracy that is always at risk of corruption. This is ironic, since gate tolls caused the original revolt.
- **Banned groups:** the **Merchant's League** guild was banned by the Council of Four and now works in secret. The **Knights of the Shield** operate unofficially.
- **Emergency laws in 1482 DR (Murder in Baldur's Gate):** after Abdel Adrian's death, Parliament passed **sumptuary laws** requiring citizens to dress according to their station. The Upper City was locked down with a curfew, Ravengard held illegal tribunals, the free press (*Baldur's Mouth*) was shut down, and the city was put under **martial law**.
- **Who polices where:** the **Watch** enforces the patriars' laws in the Upper City. The **Flaming Fist** polices the rest of the city.

## People & Society
- **Races:** mostly human. No race is banned, but trolls, ogres and orcs are rare.
- **Faiths:** Gond, Tymora, Umberlee, Helm, Lathander, Ilmater, and cults of the Dead Three.
- **Festivals:** Highharvestide (autumn), **The Breaking** (spring, when the last ice leaves the harbour) and **Returning Day** (Balduran's homecoming).
- **Cobble parties:** neighbours gather on the street to tell stories, marked by special rose-red torches.

## Economy
- **Exports:** dyes, fish, lamp oil and trade coins.
- **Imports:** stone, historically from Mirabar by way of Luskan.
- **Trade bars:** the city mints silver **trade bars** (1 lb = 5 gp), sets their value and regulates their use.
- **Guilds:** sponsors nearly **90 guilds**. The **Iron Throne** controls the weapons and armour used by the Watch and the Fist.
- **Crime:** **the Guild**, led by **Nine-Fingers**, has dominated since the late 14th century, and almost every gang in the Lower and Outer City answers to it.

## Districts
- **Upper City:** Citadel Streets, Manorborn, the Temples District, the Wide. Landmarks include **High Hall** (palace of the Dukes), the Watch Citadel, the High House of Wonders (Gond) and the Silvershield Estate.
- **Lower City:** Seatower, Bloomridge, the Steeps, Heapside, Eastway, Brampton. Landmarks include Baldur's Mouth (news), the Gray Harbor (closed after sunset), the Seatower of Balduran, the Elfsong Tavern, the Blushing Mermaid (a hub of illicit business), Sorcerous Sundries and the Water Queen's House (Umberlee, the oldest temple).
- **Outer City:** Blackgate, Stonyeyes, Norchapel, Little Calimshan, Whitkeep, Sow's Foot, Twin Songs, Tumbledown, Rivington.
- **Wyrm's Crossing:** the bridge district.

## Defense
- **The Flaming Fist:** a mercenary company that serves as both the city's police and its army. It may hire out abroad as long as it never fights against the city. Many of its officers are retired adventurers.
- **Natural defences:** a sheltered inlet and the great wall paid for by Balduran.

## Relations
- **Lords' Alliance:** a member, though it has long been a **neutral power**.
- **Amn:** a long enmity that nearly became war during the 1360s iron crisis.
- **Other threats:** Luskan's pirates, and Waterdeep's merchants, who resent the Gate's growth.

## Recent History (canon)
- **1482 DR:** Grand Duke Abdel Adrian died fighting the last other Bhaalspawn. Chaos and martial law followed. The unrest turned out to be **Grand Duke Torlin Silvershield's plot to destroy the corrupt Parliament by blowing up stockpiled smokepowder.** (Compare [[Lore/The Powder]].)
- **1492 DR:** Elturel fell into Avernus while Ravengard was there.
- **1492 DR:** the **Cult of the Absolute** marched on the city and an illithid **netherbrain** burst from the caves beneath it, wrecking several districts, the Upper City worst. Heroes either destroyed it or took control of it (*Baldur's Gate 3*).
