# Yartar: City of the Waterbaron

**Region:** Dessarin Valley, Savage Frontier, where the Evermoor Way crosses the Surbrin River
**Size:** Small city. About 6,000 in 1366 DR, and more crowded since
**Government:** Barony ruled by a **Waterbaron**, elected for life
**Ruler (c. 1491 DR):** Waterbaron Nestra Ruthiol
**Lords' Alliance influence:** Moderate to low. It has wealth and connections, but crime holds it back.

> Canon reference only, as of about 1491–1492 DR. The campaign is set in 1525 DR. See [[Factions/Lords' Alliance]].

---

## Exposition

Yartar is a prosperous, fortified city that never sleeps. Rival caravans and loaded barges pass through its walls at every hour. A fortified bridge, wide enough for two wagons with room to spare, links the city to a citadel on the west bank, where the **Evermoor Way** (Triboar to Silverymoon) crosses the **Surbrin**. It is a natural crossroads, and **merchants, smugglers, mercenaries and spies** from all over the North meet here to do business.

By the late 15th century DR the city had run out of room. Old buildings were torn down for new ones up to four storeys tall.

Behind the honest trade lies a criminal underworld whose schemes have kept Yartar from becoming one of the truly influential city-states of Faerûn. The **Hand of Yartar**, a thieves' guild made up entirely of women, is the best-known gang. Its infighting is the main thing keeping it from real power.

## Who Rules

### Government
- **The Waterbaron** rules and is **elected for life**.
- **Past Waterbarons:**
  - **Alahar Khaumfros** inherited the office from his father. He turned out to be the leader of the **Kraken Society**, and four mind flayers killed him in the Waterbaron's Hall in about 1366 DR for mishandling Society funds.
  - **Belleethe Kheldorna** (1366 DR).
  - **Nestra Ruthiol** (around 1491 DR).
- **Seat:** the Waterbaron's Hall. The Waterbaron's barge is moored at the Shield Tower.

### The Ruler: Nestra Ruthiol
- **Age:** **in her fifties around 1491 DR**, according to *Princes of the Apocalypse*. That puts her at roughly **85–95 in 1525 DR**.
- **Known for:**
  - She is a Tethyrian noblewoman, **shrewd and farseeing**, and also **cunning and vindictive** (*Storm King's Thunder*). She is **lawful evil**.
  - She believes membership in the **Lords' Alliance is vital** to Yartar's survival and prosperity.
  - She knows the Harpers and the Zhentarim operate in the city. She lets them, and steps in only when her citizens' wellbeing is at risk.
  - She was accused of **murdering a former lover, Kaidrod Palyr**, after a public argument with his wife, Tiarshe. She was cleared, but some suspect the Hand of Yartar was involved.
  - She ruled during the **Elemental Evil** crisis of 1491 DR.
  - She has noble stats in 5e.

## Laws & Justice
**Canon does not document a legal code for Yartar.** What is known:
- **The Shields of Yartar:** a mounted fighting force of about **150 riders**, based in the **Shield Tower** on the west bank of the Surbrin. The tower has its own covered docks.
- **Policy on outside groups:** the Waterbaron tolerates the Harpers and the Zhentarim and intervenes only when the city is threatened.
- **Corruption:** in 1491 DR, **Lord Khaspere Drylund**, a member of the Kraken Society, was **bribing or blackmailing corrupt officials** to make himself the next Waterbaron.

## People & Society
- Citizens are called *Yartaran*. The city is a busy mix of townsfolk, travelling merchants and caravan crews.
- **Faith:** Tymora. The temple is the **Happy Hall of Fortuitous Happenstance**, which the Smiling Company adventurers supported with regular donations.

## Economy
- **Fishing:** crab, eel and other local seafood.
- **Barge-building:** the city's best-known industry.
- **Caravans:** a key stop between Silverymoon (an ally) and Waterdeep, with many businesses serving travellers.
- **Food:** imported, mainly through a **trade agreement with Goldenfields** for grain and produce.
- **Notable businesses:**
  - **Lionshield Coster:** a trading company (a lion's head on blue) based in Yartar, with offices in Triboar, Nightstone and Phandalin.
  - **Shops:** Dannath's Pickles, Nuts & Foods; Esklindrar's Maps, Books & Folios (huge numbers of treasure maps); Firelust Fabrics & Tailoring; Halassa's Waterwell & Fine Wines; Hasklar's Arms & Armor (guarded by ghosts); Winter Winds (clothing, run by two brothers from Baldur's Gate).

## Organizations
- **The Hand of Yartar:** an all-women thieves' guild, as large as any criminal group in the Sword Coast North but held back by infighting. It works with the Zhentarim when that suits it.
- **The Kraken Society:** a secret network tied to Luskan that has run Yartar from the shadows before.
- **The Smiling Company:** an adventuring company based here for nearly a decade.

## Relations
- **Lords' Alliance:** a member, with allies across the North.
- **Silverymoon:** an ally and trade partner.
- **Goldenfields:** linked by the food trade agreement.
- **Triboar:** a **historic rival**. The feud began when each town blamed the other for the disappearance of **the Lost Guide**, a caravaneer carrying a large amount of gold between them. It is mostly good-natured but sometimes turns violent.

## Recent History (canon)
- **1361 DR:** Waterbaron Alahar Khaumfros tried to provoke the Lords' Alliance, Waterdeep especially, into **war with Ruathym** for the Kraken Society and Luskan. Liriel Baenre, Fyodor and Caladorn Cassalanter foiled him.
- **c. 1366 DR:** mind flayers killed Khaumfros.
- **1491 DR:** the elemental cults abducted Yartar's poor and drunk to the Temple of Howling Hatred. The Hand stole a **devastation orb** from the vault of Triboar's Lord Protector and auctioned it.
- **1492 DR:** a mind-flayer **nautiloid** ship attacked, destroying one of the tallest buildings and snatching citizens. Three **githyanki riding red dragons** forced it to teleport away.

## 1525 DR: Open Questions (Brodie)
