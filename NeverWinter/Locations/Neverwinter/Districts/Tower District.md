# Tower District (River District)

**District Type:** Historic / Academic / Noble
**Also Known As:** The River District
**Notable Features:** Magical Academies, Noble Estates, Ancient Architecture, Neverwinter River
**Safety Level:** Medium to High - Generally safe but some areas affected by past conflicts
**Wealth Level:** Upper class

---

## Overview

The **Tower District**, also known as the **River District**, is a historic area of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]] featuring ancient architecture, magical academies, and noble estates along the Neverwinter River.

This district represents the intellectual and arcane heart of the city, where wizards study, nobles reside, and ancient history is preserved.

---

## Atmosphere

The Tower District is characterized by:
- **Ancient stone towers** reaching toward the sky
- **The flowing Neverwinter River** providing scenic beauty
- **Magical academies** where arcane arts are studied
- **Noble estates** with well-manicured grounds
- **Historical monuments** from Neverwinter's past
- An air of **scholarly pursuits** and **old money**
