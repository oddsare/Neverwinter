Many are unmarked with nameless souls that perished in one of Neverwinter's numerous tragedies and cataclysms. ## Overview

The **Neverdeath District** is [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter's]] ancient graveyard and burial ground, a sprawling necropolis filled with centuries-old tombs, mausoleums, and memorials to the city's departed.

This vast cemetery has served as the final resting place for Neverwinter's citizens for hundreds of years, and its oldest sections date back to the city's founding.

---

## Atmosphere

The Neverdeath District is characterized by:
- **Endless rows** of gravestones and monuments
- **Ancient mausoleums** of noble families
- **Weathered crypts** holding the remains of prominent citizens
- **Sacred grounds** blessed by various gods
- **Eerie silence** broken only by the wind
- **Morning mists** that shroud the tombstones
- An ever-present sense of **solemnity and death**

---

## Layout

Neverdeath is divided into **two major sections**:

### The Pauper's Section (Southwest)
The Pauper's section, to the **southwest**, contains rows upon rows of humble graves.

**Features:**
- Rows upon rows of **humble graves**
- Many are **unmarked** - nameless souls
- Victims of Neverwinter's numerous tragedies and cataclysms
- Simple burials for those without means
- Weathered and forgotten

**History:**
Those who perished in:
- The Spellplague
- Mount Hotenow eruption
- Political upheavals
- Other disasters

### The Noble Section
Contains more **elaborate mausoleums** where nobility and wealthy were laid to rest.

**Features:**
- Grand mausoleums
- Elaborate architecture and statuary
- The Neverember family crypt
- Other noble house tombs
- Well-maintained compared to the Pauper's section

---

## The Spellplague's Impact

The **Spellplague changed the landscape** of this area.

**Protective Measures:**
- **Walls were constructed** to protect from what is within
- Barriers to contain undead threats
- Sealed sections to prevent escape

**The Legend:**
Legends say **loved ones would spring back to life and attack their visitors**.

**Current Threat:**
- Undead still rise, especially in the oldest sections
- The walls contain the worst of the threat
- Visitors must remain vigilant

---

## Dangers

The Neverdeath District is not without its perils:

### Undead
- **Zombies and skeletons** occasionally rise, especially in the oldest sections
- **Ghosts** of those who cannot rest
- **Wights and wraiths** in the deepest crypts
- Rumors of **vampires** in certain mausoleums

### Grave Robbers
- **Thieves** seeking valuables buried with the dead
- **Necromancers** collecting components for dark magic
- **Cultists** performing forbidden rituals

### Natural Hazards
- **Unstable ground** over old graves
- **Dangerous crypts** with structural damage
- **Easy to get lost** among the endless tombstones

---

## Caretakers & Guardians

### Graveyard Keepers
Dedicated individuals who maintain the grounds and ensure proper burial rites.

### Temple Representatives
Priests and clerics who:
- Oversee burials
- Consecrate new graves
- Deal with undead threats
- Perform last rites

### City Guard Patrols
[[Factions/City Guard|City Guard]] patrols occasionally sweep through, especially after reports of undead activity.

---

## Why Anyone Goes There

Despite the dangers, people visit Neverdeath for:

- **Mourning loved ones** and tending graves
- **Burial ceremonies** for the recently deceased
- **Historical research** into old family lines
- **Hunting undead** for bounties or quests
- **Grave robbing** (illegal and dangerous)
- **Necromantic research** (forbidden)

As a place of death, Neverdeath has connections to:
