# Protector's Enclave District

**District Type:** City Center / Government District
**Notable Features:** Hall of Justice, Marketplaces, City Administration
**Safety Level:** High - Well-patrolled
**Wealth Level:** Mixed (Middle to Upper class)

---

## Overview

The **Protector's Enclave** is the heart of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]], serving as the primary hub for merchants, adventurers, and city officials.

This district represents the public face of the city, where business is conducted, justice is administered, and citizens gather.

---

## Notable Locations

### Castle Never (Castle Neverwinter)
Towering above Neverwinter, Castle Never symbolizes the city's strength and enduring legacy.

**Location:** Perched on cliffs where the Neverwinter River meets the Sea of Swords

**Key Features:**
- **The Three Bridges:** Castle Never connects key districts via three iconic bridges:
  - **Sleeping Dragon Bridge** - Links to [[Protectors Enclave|Protector's Enclave]]
  - **Winged Wyvern Bridge** - Links to [[Docks District|The Docks]]
  - **Dolphin Bridge** - Links to Blacklake District
- These bridges are vital arteries of the city's infrastructure

**History:**
- Built over a **millennium ago** (around 500 DR)
- Survived countless challenges, including the devastating cataclysm of **1451 DR**
- Ongoing restoration efforts under [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]]
- Lord Renaer's ascension in **1520 DR** brought hope for stability after years of political turmoil

**Current Status:**
- Central to the city's rejuvenation
- Whispers of unrest persist, but the castle stands strong
- **Enhanced Security** implemented to address recent threats:
  - Vigilant guard patrols
  - Enhanced checkpoints for safety
  - The dungeons (known as **"The Holes"**) serve as holding cells for the city's most dangerous criminals

### The Hall of Justice
The primary temple to **Tyr**, the god of law and justice, in Neverwinter.

**Architecture:**
- Grand stone walls
- Towering stone pillars
- High domed roof
- Impressive and intimidating structure

**Functions:**
- **Temple to Tyr** - Where the faithful worship the god of justice
- **Court of Low Justice** - The city's primary courthouse
- **Training Ground** - Drilling and training in disciplined weaponry for devout followers of Tyr

**Deity:** Tyr, god of law and justice

### The House of Knowledge
Both a library and the primary temple to **Oghma** in Neverwinter.

**Architecture:**
- Regarded as one of the most **impressive and beautiful** structures in the city
- Abundant windows
- Impressive arched roof

**Holdings:**
- **Vast libraries** containing many centuries of precious records and materials
- **Modern government documentation** - Currently serves this function as the city lacks a formal seat of government
- Historical archives
- Rare texts and scrolls

**Deity:** Oghma, god of knowledge

### Market Square
Protector's Enclave is home to an array of shops catering to every need and desire.

**What You Can Find:**
- **Masterful artisans** offering top-quality goods
- **Skilled merchants** selling rare treasures
- Some of the finest craftspeople in all of [[Lore/Faerun|Faerûn]]
- **Adventurer supplies** - Gear, equipment, and support for journeys

**Why It's Well-Stocked:**
Neverwinter relies heavily on **adventurers and mercenaries** to defend its borders, so the markets are well-equipped and welcoming to those seeking supplies.

**Activity Level:** Very High during daytime

### The Moonstone Mask (Nearby on Earthmote)
[[Locations/Neverwinter/The Moonstone Mask|The Moonstone Mask]] is a famous **festhall** located on a floating **Earthmote** near Protector's Enclave.

**Features:**
- Luxury establishment overlooking the Sea of Swords
- Owned by [[NPCs/Ophala Cheldarstorn|Madam Ophala Cheldarstorn]]
- High-class entertainment, fine dining, luxury accommodations
- Meeting place for nobles and wealthy merchants
- Strict discretion and excellent security
- Accessible via magical bridges or levitation

**Type:** Festhall (upscale tavern, entertainment venue, and social club)

**Clientele:** Nobles, wealthy merchants, those seeking discretion

The Protector's Enclave is characterized by:
- **Busy streets** filled with merchants, adventurers, and citizens
- **Well-maintained** buildings and infrastructure
- **Constant activity** during daylight hours
- **Heavy guard presence** ensuring order
