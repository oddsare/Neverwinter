# The Docks District

**District Type:** Waterfront / Trade District
**Notable Features:** Harbor, Warehouses, Taverns, Sailors' Quarter
**Safety Level:** Low to Medium - Rough area
**Wealth Level:** Lower to Middle class

---

## Overview

The **Docks District** is a rough-and-tumble waterfront area where ships come and go, featuring warehouses, taverns, and a mix of legitimate businesses alongside shadier establishments.

This is the lifeblood of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter's]] trade, where goods from across [[Lore/Faerun|Faerûn]] arrive by sea and are distributed throughout the city.

---

## Atmosphere

The Docks District is characterized by:
- **Salt air and sea spray**
- **Rough sailors and dock workers**
- **Taverns that never seem to close**
- **Warehouses packed with cargo**
- **Shady deals** in dark alleys
- **The creak of ships** and calls of gulls

---

## Current Crisis - The Shadowy Force

The Docks District is at the center of the [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|current crisis]]:

- **Ships vanish** beneath moonless skies
- **Cargo disappears** from harbors and warehouses
- Strange reports from sailors and merchants
- Trade routes are being disrupted

[[NPCs/Soren Ironwake|Guard Captain Soren Ironwake]] operates from this district and is coordinating the investigation.

---

## Notable Locations

### The Ports: The Jewel of the North
The bustling heart of Neverwinter's docks, alive with activity **day and night**.

**Characteristics:**
- Ships constantly arrive and depart
- Unloading cargo and taking on new goods in organized chaos
- **The docks never sleep** - embodying the lifeblood of trade and commerce
- Fuels the city's prosperity

**Current Threats:**
- [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|Ships vanish beneath moonless skies]]
- Cargo disappears mysteriously
- Dock workers must stay **vigilant** against pirate raids
- Different **pirate crews** attempt to raid passing ships entering and leaving the city

### The Swamp Docks (Northern Section)
A dangerous area of sunken, rotting docks currently under new construction.

**Location:** Northern section of the docks

**Dangers:**
- **Sunken docks** rotting away
- Home to different **criminals** seeking to avoid the law
- **Tribe of Sahuagin** (aquatic monsters) underneath the waters
- Construction workers face constant threats

**Status:** Under reconstruction, but dangerous

### The Driftwood Tavern
An upscale and expensive establishment that's almost more of a museum than a tavern.

**Location:** Northern docks area

**Characteristics:**
- One of **Neverwinter's oldest sites**
- One of the favorite spots for drinking locals
- Upscale clientele
- Expensive prices
- Historical significance

**Atmosphere:** More refined than typical dock taverns

### The Tarmalune Trade House
A mysterious trading hub from the city of Tarmalune.

**Location:** Southern docks - large warehouse complex

**Known Information:**
- **Not much is publicly known** about this establishment
- Trading hub from the distant city of **Tarmalune**
- Occupies a large warehouse complex
- Deals in **unusual wares** not normally seen in Neverwinter

**Mystery:** What exactly does this trade house deal in? Why the secrecy?

### Warehouses
Large storage facilities for goods waiting to be distributed throughout the city.

**Current Problem:** Some warehouses have reported cargo vanishing overnight - part of [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|the current crisis]].

### Guard Captain's Office
Where [[NPCs/Soren Ironwake|Guard Captain Soren Ironwake]] can be found coordinating patrols and investigations into the disappearances.
