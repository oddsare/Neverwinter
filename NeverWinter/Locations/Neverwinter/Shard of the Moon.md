# The Shard of the Moon

> [!info] Where this note came from
> Built on 2026-10-24 (Brodie) from the **canon history already in the vault** ([[Lore/Neverwinter Sub-Locations]], [[Lore/Pre-1502 Supporting Cast]], [[Lore/Neverwinter Historical Timeline (-10 to 1489 DR)]]) plus a **May 2026 draft**. Galaeron as head paladin comes from the May session's decisions. Anything marked **(May draft, keep?)** was suggested by Claude in May, not decided by Brodie.

**Type:** Temple of [[NPCs/Selune and Shar - Sister Goddesses|Selûne]] / floating tower
**Location:** above the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Tower District|Tower District]], [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]]
**Formerly:** a tower of **Moonshadow Hall** in Yhaunn, then the Shadovar's **Shard of Night**
**High Priestess:** **Lady Jasmine** (canon; led the 1479 cleansing), now older and likely still serving, **or her successor** *(not decided)*

**Access:** only trusted visitors, at the priestesses' discretion

## History (canon)

- **1379 DR:** the **Shadovar** (Netherese of the Shadowfell, servants of Shar) attack **Moonshadow Hall**, a temple of Selûne in the distant city of **Yhaunn**, and tear one of its towers free. They learn they can make it appear anywhere in the mortal world.
- **1385 DR (the Spellplague):** they float it above Neverwinter, using a portal inside it to travel between the city and **Xinlenal**. Renamed the **Shard of Night**, it looks black and casts no shadow by day.
- **1479 DR:** Selûne intervenes in mortal affairs by sending shards to Toril. **Lady Jasmine** leads Selûnite priestesses who, with adventurers' help, raid the tower and **purify it back to alabaster white.** It becomes the **Shard of the Moon**, and the priestesses have controlled it since.
- **1505 DR:** Selûnite custody almost certainly continues; the priesthood is an institution, not one person.

A Selûnite temple stolen by Shar's servants and taken back is the **Sister War in miniature, inside Neverwinter's walls.** Sharran clergy don't forget losing consecrated ground.

---

## Getting in

- **The moonlight stair (canon):** at night, when **three magical shards** are placed in or below the tower, **a stairway of solid moonlight** descends from its entrance.
- **Only trusted visitors** are admitted (canon). Strangers are politely turned away.
- **How trust is earned** *(May draft, keep?)*:
  - a vouch from a known Selûnite
  - a good deed for the temple (an errand, a recovered relic, exposing a Sharran cell)
  - a direct invitation from Galaeron or the High Priestess
- **House rules inside** *(May draft, keep?)*: no weapons drawn in the upper chambers, no shadow magic, no lies sworn under the moonlight glass.

### Benefit for trusted visitors (canon)
> Trusted visitors who take a **long rest** inside the Shard receive an **extra healing surge** that lasts until their next long rest.

*(The healing surge is a 4e rule. [[Lore/11 Places Every Town Needs]] already lists the Shard as "extra healing to visitors who rest there"; the 5e version is not set.)*

## The building

- **A floating tower** above the Tower District, held aloft by the Shadovar magic that moved it.
- **Levels** *(May draft, keep?)*: an open-moonlight sanctuary at the top, restricted floors below, and **sealed lower levels** rumored to date from the Shard of Night years.

## People

### High Priestess: Lady Jasmine, or her successor
Canon's Lady Jasmine led the 1479 cleansing and is older by 1505. Whether she still leads, or a successor does (name, race, age, personality), is Brodie's call. The High Priestess runs the tower, decides who is trusted and makes the call on which outsiders get in.

### Head Paladin: Galaeron *(spelling not set)*
Leads the tower's paladins and reports to the High Priestess. The public, martial face of the temple: handles threats, escorts important visitors and carries out the cleansing rites on the lower floors. Personality, race and backstory are Brodie's to write.

## Hooks (May draft, keep?)

- **The lower floors.** What's sealed below? A Sharran relic the Selûnites couldn't destroy? A Sharran prisoner from the old days? A door that should never be opened?
- **A Sharran plot to take it back.** Infiltrating the paladins, blackmailing the High Priestess, or an assault on a moonless night.
- **Galaeron's burden.** A public symbol of Selûne's victory who walks past sealed Sharran wards every day. Compromise, doubt or zealotry are all within reach.
- **Trust as currency.** The party earns entry (and the healing) by serving the temple.
- **A Selûnite ally network** of the Shard and traveling Selûnite navigators that the party can tap for information and refuge.

## DM notes

- **The flip is the story.** Shar stole it; Selûne took it back. Let the history come up as flavor early, then matter later.
- **Don't make the Selûnites preachy.** As the [[NPCs/Selune and Shar - Sister Goddesses|Selûne and Shar note]] says, Selûnites are travelers and quiet allies, not pulpit-thumpers. The Shard's priests should feel competent and watchful.
- **Use the gate as pacing.** Restricted entry gives the party something to earn early, and makes the welcome mean something.
