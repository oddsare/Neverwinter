## Overview

The **Shard of the Moon** is a **floating tower** in the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Tower District|Tower District]] of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]], currently held by the followers of [[NPCs/Selune and Shar - Sister Goddesses|Selûne]].

It was not always a temple of light. The tower was formerly called **"The Shard of Night"** and had a colored past before being reclaimed by Selûnites. What it was used for in those years — and what still lingers in its lower floors — is the tower's open secret.

---

## The Shar → Selûne Flip

The Shard's renaming from *Night* to *Moon* is **the Sister War in microcosm, inside Neverwinter's walls.** A site once held by Shar is now a Selûnite stronghold. This is significant because:

- It is a public, physical defeat of Shar inside the city
- Sharran clergy do not forget losses of consecrated ground — recovery is a long-term doctrinal goal
- The Selûnite priests holding it are aware of the history and treat the building accordingly: cleansed regularly, watched constantly, never fully trusted
- The tower itself may still carry residue — wards, hidden chambers, sealed levels, names carved where they shouldn't be

**This is a campaign hook engine.** A Sharran attempt to reclaim, infiltrate, or symbolically defile the Shard is a natural late tier-1 / tier-2 beat for any party investigating the [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|Shadowy Force]].

---

## Access

The priests of Selûne **allow only trusted visitors entrance** into the tower. Strangers are politely turned away at the bridge or lift. Entry is earned by:
- A vouching from a known Selûnite (Ophala Cheldarstorn, a traveling priest, a navigator-captain)
- Demonstrated good faith — completing an errand for the temple, restoring a lost relic, exposing a Sharran cell
- Direct invitation by Galaeron or the High Priestess

Once admitted, visitors are bound by Selûnite hospitality customs: no weapons drawn within the upper chambers, no working of shadow-magic, no lies sworn under the moonlight glass.

---

## Mechanical Benefit (Trusted Visitors)

> Trusted visitors who take a **long rest** inside the Shard of the Moon receive an **extra healing surge** that lasts until their next long rest.

A tangible blessing of the moon. Use sparingly — the perk is part of what makes earning trust here meaningful.

## The Building

- **Floating tower** — suspended above the Tower District. Mechanism is presumably magical (post-Spellplague earthmote? Selûnite ward? Sharran legacy magic re-purposed?). To be detailed.
- **Multiple levels** — public sanctuary at the top (open moonlight), restricted floors below, sealed lower levels rumored to date from the Shard of Night era.
- **Access bridge or lift** from a Tower District street to the tower's main entrance.

## Campaign Hooks

- **The lower floors.** What is sealed below? A Sharran relic the Selûnites couldn't destroy? A surviving Sharran prisoner from the original takeover? A door that should never be opened?
- **The Sharran reclamation plot.** A cell inside Neverwinter — possibly the same one tied to the [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|Shadowy Force]] — is working to take the Shard back. Methods could include infiltration of the paladin order, blackmail of the High Priestess, or a moonless-night assault.
- **Galaeron's burden.** As head paladin he stands between two worlds: a public symbol of Selûne's victory, and a man who has to walk past sealed Sharran wards every day. Compromise, doubt, or zealotry are all in reach.
- **Trust as currency.** The party wants the healing surge perk? They earn it by serving the temple. Quest-loop generator.
- **A Selûnite ally network.** The Shard, [[NPCs/Ophala Cheldarstorn|Ophala Cheldarstorn]], and traveling Selûnite navigators form a quiet network the party can plug into for tier 1-2 information and refuge.

---

## DM Notes

- **The flip is the story.** Don't waste it. The Shar-to-Selûne history should surface in tier 1 as flavor, become a plot driver in tier 2, and pay off in tier 3 (Sharran reclamation attempt during the [[Quests/The Foster King|coronation crisis]] is one option).
- **Don't make the Selûnites preachy.** Per the [[NPCs/Selune and Shar - Sister Goddesses|Selune/Shar file]], Selûnites in canon are travelers and quiet allies, not pulpit-thumpers. The Shard's priests should feel competent and watchful, not sermonising.
- **Use the gate as pacing.** Restricting access is a feature — it gives the party something to earn early, then makes the eventual welcome feel meaningful.
