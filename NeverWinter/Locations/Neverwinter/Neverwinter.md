# Neverwinter - The Jewel of the North

**Also Known As:** The Jewel of the North
**Location:** Sword Coast North, [[Lore/Faerun|Faerûn]]
**Current Ruler:** [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]] (1520 DR - Present)
**Population:** ~20,000 (estimate)

## Original Text (As Written)

This place is your guy's home, you have lived here your entire lives so you know it pretty well. If you guys wish to know each other or atleast rumors of each other that is completely good by me, ill let you guys discuss that.

The city is broken up into 6 districts

**Protectors Enclave District** - The Protector's Enclave is the heart of Neverwinter, featuring the Hall of Justice, bustling marketplaces, and serves as the primary hub for merchants, adventurers, and city officials.

**The Docks District** - The Docks District is a rough-and-tumble waterfront area where ships come and go, featuring warehouses, taverns, and a mix of legitimate businesses alongside shadier establishments frequented by sailors and merchants.

**Tower or River District** - The Tower District, also known as the River District, is a historic area of Neverwinter featuring ancient architecture, magical academies, and noble estates along the Neverwinter River.

**Bluelake District** - The Bluelake District is an upscale residential area centered around a beautiful lake, known for its high-end shops, elegant restaurants, and the homes of Neverwinter's wealthy citizens

**The Chasm District** - The Chasm District is a dangerous, partially destroyed area of the city created by a catastrophic event known as the Spellplague, featuring unstable magical energies and treacherous terrain that most citizens avoid.

**Neverdeath District** - The Neverdeath District is Neverwinter's ancient graveyard and burial ground, a sprawling necropolis filled with centuries-old tombs, mausoleums, and memorials to the city's departed.

## Overview

Your adventure begins in the sprawling city of **Neverwinter**, whether this is your first time here or you've lived here your whole life.

Nestled along the **Sword Coast North**, Neverwinter stands as a beacon of resilience and magic in the Forgotten Realms. Known as the **Jewel of the North**, this grand city rises from the ashes of past catastrophes.

Neverwinter has endured significant turmoil in recent decades. See [[Lore/Neverwinter History|Neverwinter - Recent History]] for the full story of the **"Dance of the Revolving Lords"** (1502-1520 DR) and the rise of [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]].

## The Six Districts

Neverwinter is divided into **six distinct districts**, each with its own character and purpose:

### 1. [[Districts/Protectors Enclave|Protector's Enclave District]]
The heart of Neverwinter, featuring the Hall of Justice, bustling marketplaces, and serving as the primary hub for merchants, adventurers, and city officials.

### 2. [[Districts/Docks District|The Docks District]]
A rough-and-tumble waterfront area where ships come and go, featuring warehouses, taverns, and a mix of legitimate businesses alongside shadier establishments frequented by sailors and merchants.

**Current Crisis:** [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|Ships and cargo are disappearing]] from this district.

### 3. [[Districts/Tower District|Tower/River District]]
A historic area featuring ancient architecture, magical academies, and noble estates along the Neverwinter River.

### 4. [[Districts/Bluelake District|Bluelake District]]
An upscale residential area centered around a beautiful lake, known for high-end shops, elegant restaurants, and the homes of Neverwinter's wealthy citizens.

### 5. [[Districts/Chasm District|The Chasm District]]
A dangerous, partially destroyed area created by the catastrophic **Spellplague**. Features unstable magical energies and treacherous terrain that most citizens avoid.

### 6. [[Districts/Neverdeath District|Neverdeath District]]
Neverwinter's ancient graveyard and burial ground, a sprawling necropolis filled with centuries-old tombs, mausoleums, and memorials to the city's departed.

## Current Threats

### The Shadowy Force

[[Quests/The Shadowy Force|A mysterious force]] haunts the trade routes:
- Ships vanish beneath moonless skies
- Cargo disappears from harbors
- Caravans traveling overland report strange encounters

[[NPCs/Soren Ironwake|Guard Captain Soren Ironwake]] in the [[Districts/Docks District|Docks District]] seeks brave souls to investigate.
