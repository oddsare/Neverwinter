## Original Text (As Written)

The Moonstone Mask is a famous tavern and festhall located in the city of Neverwinter. Known for its elegant atmosphere and mysterious allure, it sits atop a cliff overlooking the Sea of Swords.

Interior Description

The interior of the Moonstone Mask exudes luxury and sophistication. Crystal chandeliers illuminate the polished marble floors, while rich tapestries and ornate mirrors adorn the walls. The main hall features a sweeping staircase with gilded railings, leading to the upper floors.

The establishment is divided into several distinct areas:

- The main parlor features plush velvet seating arrangements and intimate alcoves for private conversations
- A grand ballroom with high, vaulted ceilings and floor-to-ceiling windows offering spectacular views of the Sea of Swords
- Multiple private dining rooms decorated with fine art and equipped with discrete servant entrances
- A well-appointed bar area featuring rare spirits and wines from across Faerûn

The upper floors contain luxurious guest rooms, each uniquely decorated with fine furniture, four-poster beds, and private balconies. The entire establishment maintains a perfect balance between opulence and taste, creating an atmosphere of refined elegance.

Notable Features

- Operated by the enigmatic Madam Ophala Cheldarstorn
- Popular meeting place for nobles and wealthy merchants
- Known for its high-class entertainment and discretion
- Features private rooms and a grand ballroom

History

The establishment survived multiple catastrophes that struck Neverwinter, including the Spellplague and the cataclysm that nearly destroyed the city. After each disaster, the Moonstone Mask was rebuilt, maintaining its reputation as one of the city's most prestigious venues.

Services

- Fine dining and exotic beverages
- Entertainment and musical performances
- Private meeting rooms for business dealings
- Luxury accommodations for wealthy guests

The Moonstone Mask maintains a strict code of conduct and is known for its excellent security, ensuring the safety and privacy of its patrons. this is in an Earthmote hovering near protectors enclave

## Overview

The **Moonstone Mask** is a famous **festhall** and tavern located in the city of [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]]. Known for its elegant atmosphere and mysterious allure, it sits on a **floating Earthmote** near [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Protector's Enclave]], overlooking the Sea of Swords.

### What is a Festhall?

A **festhall** is an upscale establishment that combines:
- Fine dining and drinking
- Entertainment (music, dancing, theater)
- Social gathering space for the wealthy
- Companionship and escort services
- Luxury accommodations
- Discretion and privacy

The Moonstone Mask is one of Neverwinter's most prestigious festhalls, catering to nobles, merchants, and those who can afford its services.

### Building — canon details

- **Five stories.** Ground floor is the dining room with a huge hearth and lanterns hanging from the grand staircase. Floors 2–4 are **magically soundproofed luxury rental rooms** (the soundproofing is part of the original construction). Fifth floor is the festhall proper.
- Defended by **twelve battle horrors** (canonical, 1366 DR — likely still in service or replaced)
- Owned and founded by **Ophala Cheldarstorn** in **~1363 DR**

### The Earthmote — full canon history

**Earthmotes** are floating islands or chunks of land suspended in the air by magical forces. Many were created during the [[Lore/Current Era|Spellplague]] (1385 DR), when the fabric of reality was torn and magic ran wild.

**The Moonstone Mask's Earthmote — chronology (FR canon):**
- **~1363 DR**: **Ophala Cheldarstorn** founds the Mask as a ground-based festhall. Rapidly becomes one of the most renowned festhalls of the North and Sword Coast.
- **1366–1374 DR**: Operating at peak. Ophala has 12 battle horrors guarding it.
- **1385 DR (Spellplague)**: *"The land around the inn broke free and started floating in the air, becoming an earthmote."* The Mask drifted free.
- **Post-1385 DR**: Owners commission **heavy chains "big enough to hold a giant"** plus a **floating, unstable bridge** for ground access from the docks.
- **~1451 DR (Mount Hotenow Cataclysm)**: *"The quake did not affect the earthmote, but the ropes holding it in place snapped, and it drifted aimlessly over the nearby ocean for months."* The Mask was eventually retrieved.
- **Post-1451 DR**: Mask "fell on hard times" — financially and structurally damaged.
- **Pre-1479 DR**: **Liset Cheldar** (presumed descendant of Ophala) approaches **Lord Dagult Neverember**. **Dagult personally sponsors the reopening.** This is a major canon plot tie — Dagult has direct, long-standing investment in the Mask.
- **~1479 DR**: Liset commissions the **magic portal located near the Tarmalune Auction House.** This becomes the standard means of access. The unstable bridge presumably remains as backup or relic.
- **By 1525 DR**: Ophala back in residence (and arguably the same woman who founded it — see her NPC file). Mask chained. Portal is the primary entry.

**The chains / tethering:**
- Heavy enough to hold a giant — physical AND magical anchoring
- Self-financed by Ophala / the Cheldar line
- **Have failed before** — snapped in 1451 DR during the Cataclysm. The Mask drifted to sea for months.
- This means the chains can fail again. **A campaign-level disaster waiting to happen.**

**The Cheldar lineage:**
- **Ophala (founder, ~1363 DR–present)** is the original. ~162+ years old by 1525 if she's the same woman.
- **Liset Cheldar (active ~1479 DR)** is canonically *"a presumed descendant of Ophala."* Filled in during Ophala's apparent absence after the 1451 Cataclysm.
- The line is **headed by Ophala**, with Cheldar descendants serving as deputies / stand-ins during her periods of withdrawal.

### Location: docks vs. Protector's Enclave (canon reconciliation)

Canon places the Earthmote in **Protector's Enclave**, connected to the **Neverwinter docks** by the original bridge and (after 1479) by the magic portal near the Tarmalune Auction House. So both location claims in earlier drafts were partially right:

- **Earthmote physically hovers over Protector's Enclave** (high-status district)
- **Connected to the working docks** by bridge and portal access
- **Tarmalune Auction House** is the ground-level entry point

This is canonically a *liminal* location — straddling the noble district and the working harbor. Perfect for an institution whose patrons span both worlds.

### The Tarmalune connection

The magic portal is canonically near the **Tarmalune Auction House** — note this is almost certainly the same **"Tarmalune Trade House"** referenced in the [[Campaign Hook]] as an establishment dealing in "unusual wares." 

**Built-in plot bridge:**
- Anyone using the Mask's portal has to go through Tarmalune ground.
- If the Trade House is shady (Campaign Hook implies it is), Ophala's patrons are subject to the Trade House's surveillance every time they enter.
- Or the Trade House is *Ophala's own* operation — a ground-level extension of her information network.

### Religious / divine affiliation — NOT Selûnite

Canon does **not** establish Selûnite, Sunite, or any specific divine devotion for the Mask. The **moonstone trim on the masks is decorative naming, not a clergy link.** Earlier speculation in these files about a Selûnite read was reach, not canon.

(Aesthetic similarities to Selûnite priestess imagery exist but are coincidental in canon. If the user wants to layer a Selûnite reading on top of canon, that's a homebrew choice — supported by visual but not by canon text.)

## History

### Survival Through Catastrophe

The establishment **survived multiple catastrophes** that struck [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]]:

- **The Spellplague** (likely when the Earthmote was created)
- **The cataclysm** that nearly destroyed the city
- Other disasters during [[Lore/Neverwinter History|the revolving lords]]

**After each disaster**, the Moonstone Mask was **rebuilt**, maintaining its reputation as one of the city's **most prestigious venues**.

### Enduring Legacy

The fact that it has been rebuilt multiple times shows:
- The wealth and determination of its owners
- Its importance to Neverwinter's social life

## Code of Conduct

The Moonstone Mask maintains a **strict code of conduct**:

- No violence on the premises
- Respect for all patrons and staff
- Discretion required (what happens here, stays here)
