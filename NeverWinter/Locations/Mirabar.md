# Mirabar: The City of Ore

**Region:** Sword Coast North, on the north bank of the River Mirar, below the Spine of the World
**Size:** Small city. About 23,000 through 1370 DR (4,100 of them dwarves in 1358 DR), then 10,307 in 1372 DR
**Government:** A hereditary **Marchion** as figurehead. Real power belongs to the elected **Council of Sparkling Stones**.
**Ruler (after 1485 DR):** Marchion Selin Ramur (spelled "Selin Raurym" in *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide*)
**Lords' Alliance influence:** Moderate. It is small in people but has great leverage through its ore.
**Badge:** a deep red double-bladed axe on black

> Canon reference only. Most detail comes from about 1367–1372 DR; the ruler is from about 1489 DR. The campaign is set in 1525 DR. See [[Factions/Lords' Alliance]].

---

## Exposition

Mirabar is a mining city of great wealth. As of 1368 DR it was **the richest city north of Waterdeep**. It stands on a knoll above the River Mirar, built of squat stone buildings and a few stone towers, all laid out for efficiency. Its walls are **extremely thick and sloped**. In winter the defenders pour water down them so they freeze slick. Open mines and heaps of rock surround the city, and guarded roads run to the major mines in the Spine of the World, which yield many metals and gems.

The city lives on two levels:
- **Below ground**, shield dwarves live and work beside their forges in the Undercity.
- **On the surface**, humans move the ore to market and defend the city against magical threats.

The two halves depend on each other, though they don't always get along. Mirabarrans are wary of outsiders, and of anyone from **Luskan** most of all. Even so, Luskan is the port that ships their goods south.

## Who Rules

### Government
- **The Marchion:** a hereditary ruler who issues edicts and, in this era, handles **only foreign policy**. A bodyguard unit called **the Hammers** protects the Marchion.
- **The Council of Sparkling Stones:** the real power. It is an **elected** assembly of dwarves and humans that meets **once a year** to set **production quotas** and to decide whether to **threaten clients with reduced output**. In about 1370 DR it was led by the dwarven cleric of Dumathoin, **Agrathan Hardhammer**.
- **The Sceptrana:** an elected office created in 1367 DR by the wizard **Shoudra Stargleam**, who ruled in coalition with the Marchion and the Council and controlled Mirabar's **trade agreements**.

### The Ruler: Selin Ramur
- **Age:** not given in canon.
- **Known for:**
  - He was Marchion some time after the War of the Silver Marches.
  - He is Mirabar's figurehead but a **key member of the Lords' Alliance**, because foreign policy is the one thing he controls.
  - If the *Sword Coast Adventurer's Guide* spelling "Raurym" is correct, he descends from the earlier **Marchion Elastul Raurym**.
  - He is Damaran, lawful neutral, with noble stats in 5e.

### The earlier Marchion: Elastul Raurym (c. 1368 DR)
- He saw **Mithral Hall as an enemy**.
- **The Torgar affair:** when the dwarf **Torgar Hammerstriker** befriended King Bruenor's caravan and later left for Mithral Hall, the Hammers secretly captured and imprisoned him. When the news leaked, the Undercity dwarves **rioted**, and the riot spread to the surface streets. Elastul had to free Torgar, and **400 dwarves left Mirabar** for Mithral Hall.

## Laws & Justice
- **The Axe of Mirabar:** the city's army, about 2,000 strong, about 500 of them dwarves. It defends the city and its mines and guards the deep entrances.
- **Virtually no crime.** The city employs professional thief-trackers known as the **"Shadow District"** to watch possible criminals. They also act as an intelligence service and report rumours from abroad.
- **Mapmaking is illegal**, so that enemies, and Luskan above all, can't get intelligence on the city.
- **Attacking a councillor or a councillor-elect is punished by death.** This stops anyone from wrecking a candidate's election.
- **Production quotas** set by the Council are the city's most important rules. Around 1,000 dwarves work the mines to meet them.

## People & Society
- **Races:** humans and shield dwarves. Citizens are called *Mirabarran*.
- **Faiths:** Chauntea, Tymora and the dwarven gods (the Morndinsamman).
- **House rivalries:** the mining houses once fought openly in the streets and mines. **The Harpers revealed this was a plot** by agents of Hellgate Keep, the Zhentarim and Luskan. After that the houses united against their enemies and began watching the mines for anything suspicious.

## Economy
- **Mining and metalwork.** Stone and metal are cut and worked by expert crafters.
- **Stone exports:** shipped by magic on floating barges to **Luskan**, at great cost, then sent south. Buildings in Amn, Baldur's Gate and Waterdeep use Mirabar stone.
- **Pottery:** brightly glazed pottery coloured with crushed minerals and gems.
- **Roads:** waste stone is crushed for roads, and the work goes on year round.
- **The Mirabar District in Luskan:** a whole district of Mirabarran trading companies, including **Anvilfist Banner**, **the Golden Hand** and **Thalorin's Manymetals**. Luskan once halved Mirabar's harbour fees as a "good faith" gesture.
- **Mining problems:** the richer deep veins are infested with monsters, and the city can't spare miners to explore them because the Axe is needed to guard the existing mines. The Marchion hired more than two dozen alchemists and metallurgists to create a metal stronger than adamantine. They produced a five-year plan.

## Relations
- **Lords' Alliance:** a key member.
- **Mithral Hall:** a rival. When Mithral Hall reopened, its better metal **took Mirabar's customers**. Publicly Mirabar welcomed its fellow dwarves. Privately, most cursed them. Council factions argued over working harder, digging deeper, **sabotaging Mithral Hall's charcoal** or seizing Battlehammer caravans, which would likely mean war. In 1372 DR Mirabar sent agents to sabotage Mithral Hall's ore, and some Mirabar dwarves defected in disgust.
- **Luskan:** a trade partner, but deeply distrusted.
- **Waterdeep:** a commercial rival (see [[Lore/Sword Coast Reference]]).
- **Fireshear:** in about 1357 DR, a triumvirate of merchants from Mirabar, Waterdeep and Neverwinter ran the mining town of Fireshear so that no single city could take control of it.

## Recent History (canon)
- **1367 DR:** Shoudra Stargleam became Sceptrana.
- **1369 DR:** Mirabar dwarves, with the crystal dragon **Saryndalaghlothtor**, wiped out the Kreeth goblin tribe in the Crags and founded the **House of the Axe**.
- **1370 DR:** King Bruenor's visit and the Torgar affair.
- **After 1485 DR:** Selin Ramur became Marchion.
