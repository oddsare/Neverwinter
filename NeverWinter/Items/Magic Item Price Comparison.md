# Magic Item Master Price List

**Every item priced by any of the three sources, in one table.** All prices in gp. Pick the price at the table when the item comes up. The default house approach is in [[Items/House Magic Item Prices]]; Rhexx's custom enchantments are in [[Items/Rhexx Enchantments Reference]].

**Sources**
- **SMP** — *Sane Magical Prices* PDF (Saidoro, with input from Giant in the Playground, r/DnDNext and EN World; 5e 2014). Default base. Prices non-combat and intrigue items high on purpose. Copy: Downloads folder.
- **Rhexx** — MrRhexx, *What They Don't Tell You About Magic Items* (PDF, 2022, 5e 2014). Enchanting/crafting system; some prices match SMP exactly. Copy: Downloads folder.
- **SMM** — [Sane Magic Market](https://sanemagicmarket.com/pricing) website (5e 2024 SRD). Separate newer project; community-voted. Snapshot taken **2026-10-11**; live prices drift.

**Quick read of the differences**
- **SMP** is usually the most expensive for utility, intrigue and defensive items.
- **Rhexx** sits between, but is cheap on a few strong items (Ring of Invisibility, Bag of Holding, Winged Boots, +1 weapon).
- **SMM** is the cheapest for most utility items and potions, but the most expensive for flight, invisibility, legendary items and spellcasting staffs.

**How to read the table:** "—" means that source doesn't price the item. Rarity is from SMP where it has one, otherwise SMM. A † marks items SMP refuses to price (see "Never for sale" in the house note).
