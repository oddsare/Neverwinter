# Arcane Acquisitions — d12 Magic Item Table

> [!warning] Supplemental Material
> Lifted and paraphrased from a fan-made guide (Jacob Johnston, v1.6) set in the **early 1490s DR** — roughly **30+ years before the campaign's 1525 DR start.** The shop and its proprietor are **homebrew** in the original guide; treat the *items* as the durable contribution. Reskin or rename the shop/proprietor freely.

## The Shop (Source PDF pp. 25–26)

**Arcane Acquisitions** sits in the **Chasm District**. Easy to find — its many forges and braziers emit bright purple, green, and blue glows (much to the irritation of neighbors). Approaching the shop, customers hear hammer-on-anvil, hissing alchemical stills, and the roar of magical flame.

**Proprietor:** **Flinar Ralolar**, an eccentric half-elf wizard of unknown age. Wears a robe and goggles, perpetual frown. Communicates telepathically with his goblin assistant **Gigz**, who handles mundane blacksmithing. Flinar is flat in demeanor but tries to be polite; his attempted smiles come across as awkward. He does not take commissions — only sells what he has already made. Some speculate the shop is really a research lab, and he sells items to clear bench space.

**Backstory hook:** Before coming to Neverwinter, Flinar was among those who travelled to **Wave Echo Cave** outside Phandalin to study the **Forge of Spells** once adventurers cleared it of hostile forces. (p. 25)

> [!note] Status by 1525 DR
> If you carry the shop forward 30+ years: Flinar is a half-elf, so plausibly still alive (maybe semi-retired); Gigz, a goblin, is likely long dead and replaced. Easiest swap: rename the proprietor, keep the items.

## DM Notes
- Every item except the **Ring of Echoes** is **Uncommon**; the Ring is **Rare** (despite its modest effect — it's a quirk of the original table on p. 26).
- All items require attunement.
- The two "1d4 unreplenishable charges" items (**Airy Leather Armor**, **Babel Earring**) are intentionally consumable — once spent, they become non-magical.
- Pricing isn't specified in the guide; use the DMG defaults for Uncommon / Rare magic items, or your table's house rules.
