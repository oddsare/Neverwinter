# House Magic Item Prices

The default prices for this campaign. **Brodie picks the final price item by item at the table**, using the master list: [[Items/Magic Item Price Comparison]] (every item, all three sources). Rhexx's custom enchantments: [[Items/Rhexx Enchantments Reference]].

## How prices work

1. **Base price list: the *Sane Magical Prices* PDF (SMP)** (decided 2026-10-11). Any item it prices, use its price.
2. **Rhexx's rules** for crafting, availability, selling and custom enchantments, plus his **poisons** and optional **mana potions** (SMP doesn't price those).
3. Items SMP doesn't price: use the [Sane Magic Market site](https://sanemagicmarket.com/pricing) price, or judge by a similar SMP item.
4. Possible overrides are listed at the bottom. **None are applied yet.**

SMP deliberately prices non-combat and intrigue items high. That suits this campaign: mind reading, disguise and hiding from divination stay rare and valuable.

## Never for sale (SMP's unpriced list)

These can't be bought in Neverwinter. They come only as treasure or plot:
- Anything that grants **wishes**: Efreeti Bottle, Iron Flask, Ring of Three Wishes, Luck Blade.
- **Chaos items:** Deck of Many Things, Wand of Wonder, Bag of Beans, Helm of Brilliance.
- **Economy breakers:** Bag of Tricks, Well of Many Worlds, Candle of Invocation.
- **Too powerful:** Ring of Djinni Summoning, Staff of the Magi.
- **Stat boosters:** manuals and tomes, Belt of Giant Strength, Potion of Giant Strength.
- **Plot devices:** Rod of Resurrection, Tome of the Stilled Tongue, Manual of Golems.
- **Cursed items.**

## Rules (from Rhexx, summarized)

### Crafting / enchanting
- Anyone can enchant an item if they have the gold. The gold stands for materials, help, research, or a divine bargain.
- Progress per day of work (8 hours, item within reach, at least 100 gp of materials):
  - **100 gp/day** normally
  - **200 gp/day** with Arcana proficiency
  - **300 gp/day** with Arcana expertise
- Several characters can work together, each adding their own daily amount.
- **Enchantment limits per item:** two minor enchantments, *or* one minor plus one major.
- Rhexx's custom enchantments (Bind Spell, Summon Monster, +X tiers and the rest) are in his PDF. Use his prices for those.
- **Consumables need tools:** potions need an herbalism kit, oils need alchemist's supplies, scrolls need Arcana plus the spell, and poisons need a poisoner's kit.

### Buying
- **Commission:** an enchanter works at the 300 gp/day rate; come back when it's done.
- **Off the shelf:** pay the item price. Finding a seller takes about a day.

### Selling
- Sell for **50%** of the item's value. Takes about a day.

### Availability by settlement size
- **Village** (under 1,000): almost nothing worth 100 gp or more.
- **Town** (1,000–6,000): up to about 5,000 gp.
- **City** (6,000+): almost anything.

## Neverwinter house rule (suggested)

Neverwinter (~20,000 people) counts as a city, so by Rhexx's rule almost anything is for sale. For an intrigue campaign, consider:
- **Common and uncommon:** shops all over the city.
- **Rare and above:** only from **specific sellers**, which makes each purchase a hook.

**Possible sellers:**
- [[Items/Arcane Acquisitions Shop Table|Arcane Acquisitions]] (Chasm District): quirky uncommon items, no commissions.
- **The Tarmalune Trade House** (Docks District): rare imports nobody else stocks. Who is it really selling for?
- **Luskan contacts / the Arcane Brotherhood:** dangerous items, for a price that isn't only gold.
- **Private commission** from a Tower District wizard: slow, expensive, and the wizard now knows what you own.
