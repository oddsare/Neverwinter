# White Resin

**Type:** Drug / Poison (ingested or dissolved under the tongue)
**Source:** Yuan-ti — refined from rare tropical herbs and yuan-ti halfblood or pureblood venom
**Street Price:** 10–25 gp per dose (high demand, difficult supply; price spikes when shipments are interrupted)
**Legal Status:** Illegal in Neverwinter. Possession and distribution are serious crimes under the [[Lore/Code Legal of Neverwinter|Code Legal]].
**Availability:** Black market only — see [[NPCs/[NAME TBD] - White Resin Dealer]]

---

## Appearance

A thick, milky-white resin, faintly luminescent in darkness — a barely visible inner glow that suggests something more than herbs went into it. Semi-solid at room temperature, it softens against skin heat into something almost liquid. Sold in small wax-sealed clay vials or folded into treated cloth. 

The smell is distinctive: sweetgrass, cold copper, and something faintly reptilian that users learn to crave.

---

## How It Is Used

- **Dissolved under the tongue** — the most common method. Takes effect in roughly 10 minutes.
- **Swallowed with wine** — slower onset (30 minutes), milder but longer-lasting effect.
- **Skin absorption** — rubbed into the inner wrist. Unpredictable dosing; favoured by experienced users managing their intake.

---

## Immediate Effects (per dose)

White resin works in two simultaneous waves:

### The Paralysis
- Within minutes the body becomes **completely still**. Not sleep — the user is **fully conscious** throughout.
- Muscles relax entirely: the user cannot stand, speak above a whisper, or lift a hand. They can breathe and swallow.
- **Duration:** 2d4 hours
- **Mechanical effect:** The character is *Incapacitated* and *Restrained* (cannot move or take actions) for the duration. They are aware of everything around them.
- The inability to move is not distressing. That is part of the drug's design.

### The Pleasure
- Simultaneous with the paralysis, a wave of **intense physical and emotional sensation** begins — described by users as every nerve in the body coming alive at once.
- The pleasure is **disproportionate to any physical cause.** Users lying still in a bare room report sensations they describe as transcendent, overwhelming, almost unbearable in their intensity.
- It has an **unholy quality** — something in the yuan-ti venom resonates with deep, primal instinct in ways that feel less than wholesome. Users sometimes describe hearing distant sibilant whispering, seeing serpentine shapes at the edges of vision, or feeling as though something is *observing* them with great interest during the experience.
- **Duration:** Matches the paralysis. The pleasure does not fade until the paralysis does.
- On waking: profound physical exhaustion, 2 levels of exhaustion, a powerful sense of loss as the sensation recedes.

### The Aftermath
- Users describe the return to normal sensation as **bereavement.** The mundane world feels flat, grey, and insufficient.
- This is the hook that creates addiction faster than almost any other substance on the market.
- Most users begin seeking another dose within hours of recovering mobility.

## Addiction

| Exposure | Save | Consequence on Failure |
|---|---|---|
| First dose | DC 10 Con save | Craving forms — RP only, no mechanical effect yet |
| Second dose | DC 12 Con save | Mild addiction: DC 12 Wis save each day without a dose or the character fixates on obtaining one |
| Third dose | Addiction established | DC 15 Wis save per day without a dose or the character will pursue the drug by *any* available means |
| Heavy use (3+/week) | Physiological | See Repeated Use below |

Withdrawal from established addiction: 48 hours of **2 levels of exhaustion**, fever, uncontrollable muscle tremors, and acute psychological distress (disadvantage on all Wisdom and Charisma checks).

**No amount of willpower removes physiological addiction.** A *Lesser Restoration* spell cures withdrawal symptoms for 24 hours but does not break the dependency. Only *Greater Restoration* or equivalent fully clears the physical addiction — psychological craving may linger regardless.

---

## Repeated Use

### 3+ doses over any 30-day period
The yuan-ti venom accumulates. Symptoms develop between doses:
- Skin feels **tight and dry** — users moisturise obsessively, dislike coarse fabrics
- **Sibilant speech** — the user begins drawing out S-sounds without noticing
- Frequent unconscious **tongue-flicking**, especially when tasting food or scenting the air
- **Cold aversion intensifies** — the user seeks warmth, dislikes cold water, sleeps with extra blankets
- Eyes develop a faint vertical pupil in bright light (DC 14 Perception to notice)

Reversible at this stage with *Lesser Restoration*.

### 6+ doses, or 3+ per week
- Fine **scaling** appears along the neck and inner forearms — initially mistaken for a skin condition
- Venom glands begin forming (bite attack: 1d4 + 1d6 poison)
- Eyes become fully serpentine — slit pupils, permanent without magical intervention
- Emotional affect flattens: calm, cool, detached. Less fear. Less joy. More patience.
- Addiction is now **physiological** — withdrawal has physical symptoms (see above)

Requires *Greater Restoration* or *Remove Curse* (DC 18) to halt at this stage.

## The Transformation Mechanic

### Trigger
Every time a character takes a dose of white resin, roll a **d20**.

- On a **natural 1** — or a flat **5% chance** at the DM's discretion — the yuan-ti venom triggers a deep cellular response. The character has begun the **Path of the Tainted**.
- This can also trigger on any dose taken during the heavy use stage, automatically.

### The Path of the Tainted

**Stage 1 — The Taint (Days 1–7)**
- Reptilian mannerisms intensify overnight
- Gains: poison resistance, darkvision 30 ft.
- Loses: normal emotional range — becomes detached, analytical
- The user does **not feel ill.** They feel *clarified, purposeful, elevated.* This is the danger.
- *Greater Restoration* or *Remove Curse* DC 15 halts and reverses at this stage

**Stage 2 — Serpent Blood (Days 7–30)**
- Visible scaling spreads. Forked tongue develops.
- Gains: full poison immunity, venomous bite (1d6 + DC 12 Con or poisoned 1 hour), darkvision 60 ft.
- Loses: ability to wear standard armour (scales interfere); hair falls out
- Alignment pulls toward Lawful Neutral regardless of previous alignment
- **The yuan-ti consider Stage 2 individuals their property.** Any yuan-ti encountered will attempt to claim them.
- Only *Wish*, divine intervention, or a yuan-ti pureblood performing a reversal ritual can halt at this stage.

**Stage 3 — The Unworthy (30+ days, or Constitution below 12 at Stage 2)**
- The venom finds the body insufficient. Instead of a Tainted One, the character becomes a **Broodguard (Histachii)**: hairless, near-mindless, reptilian, Intelligence drops to 4.
- Effectively a character death. *True Resurrection* equivalent within 24 hours of Stage 3 onset is the only recovery.
- A Stage 3 character cannot be played as a PC.

## Yuan-ti Purpose

The yuan-ti designed white resin deliberately:
- The **paralysis** makes users completely vulnerable while dosed — they can be observed, manipulated, or moved without resistance
- The **pleasure** ensures they return. Addiction is the point.
- The **transformation pipeline** converts addicted populations into agents, servants, or Broodguards
- The yuan-ti are cautious about over-distribution — too many Broodguards reduces useful slave capacity

The presence of white resin in Neverwinter means **yuan-ti have a deliberate interest in this city.** The dealer is the visible end of a supply chain that leads somewhere.

---

## DM Notes

- The **paralysis + full consciousness** combination is the core horror: users are *aware* of being helpless. The pleasure makes them not care — until it wears off and they think about what they allowed.
- The **unholy quality** of the pleasure is intentional — the yuan-ti venom carries a faint resonance with their serpent gods (Merrshaulk, Sseth). Users who are sensitive to the divine or arcane may, with extended use, begin to unconsciously pray toward yuan-ti deities without knowing what they are doing.
- The **5% / nat 1 trigger** is a slow-burn horror element. Stage 1 is survivable and reversible — but a player may not know that at first, and Stage 1 *feels like a gift*.
- Yuan-ti agents in Neverwinter are monitoring known users. The dealer reports buyer identities upstream.
- The **Shadowy Force** connection: some disappearances on moonless nights are Stage 2 individuals being quietly collected by yuan-ti agents before they become visible.
- Tracing the supply chain leads *out* of Neverwinter toward an active yuan-ti operation in the region.
