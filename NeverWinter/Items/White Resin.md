# White Resin

**Type:** Drug / Poison (ingested)
**Source:** Yuan-ti — made from rare tropical herbs + yuan-ti halfblood or pureblood venom
**Street Price:** 5–15 gp per dose (varies by supply; scarcity drives price up)
**Legal Status:** Illegal in Neverwinter. Possession is a crime under the [[Lore/Code Legal of Neverwinter|Code Legal]].
**Availability:** Black market only — see [[NPCs/[NAME TBD] - White Resin Dealer]]

---

## Appearance

A thick, milky-white resin with a faint herbal sweetness. Typically sold wrapped in dark cloth or sealed in a small clay vial. At room temperature it is semi-solid; it softens slightly when held in a warm hand. Some users describe the smell as "green and cold, like forest rain and copper."

---

## Effects

### Immediate (per dose)
- Deep **euphoric stupor** lasting 1d4 hours
- Sensation of warmth spreading from the throat downward
- Heightened senses — colours are vivid, sounds feel distant, touch is muted
- **No pain** for the duration (disadvantage on Constitution checks during stupor)
- On waking: mild nausea, lingering calm, 1 level of exhaustion

### With Repeated Use (3+ doses over any 30-day period)
The yuan-ti venom component begins to accumulate in the user's blood. Symptoms appear between doses:
- Skin feels **tight and dry** — users moisturise obsessively
- **Sibilant speech** develops — the user begins drawing out S sounds without noticing
- Frequent unconscious **tongue-flicking** when tasting the air
- **Cold sensitivity increases** — the user craves warmth
- Eyes may develop a faint vertical pupil visible in bright light (DC 14 Perception to notice)

These symptoms are the early signs of **Tainted One** progression. At this stage they are reversible with a *lesser restoration* spell or equivalent.

### With Heavy Use (6+ doses, or 3+ doses per week)
- Reptilian features become more pronounced: **fine scaling** along the neck and inner forearms
- Venom glands begin developing (bite attack: 1d4 piercing + 1d6 poison, no venom save yet)
- Eyes fully serpentine — slit pupils are permanent without magical intervention
- Behaviour shifts: the user becomes cold-blooded in temperament, emotionally flat, drawn to warmth
- Addiction is now **physiological** — skipping a dose causes 2 levels of exhaustion, fever, and muscle cramps for 48 hours

At this stage, *greater restoration* or a *remove curse* (DC 18 caster check) is required to halt progression.

## The Transformation Mechanic

### Trigger
Every time a character takes a dose of white resin, roll a **d20**.

- On a **1 (natural 1)** — or if the DM calls for a flat **5% chance check** — the yuan-ti venom triggers a deep cellular response.
- The character has begun the **Path of the Tainted**.

This can also trigger on any dose taken while the character already has reptilian symptoms (heavy use stage), at the DM's discretion.

### The Path of the Tainted (Transformation Stages)

Once triggered, the transformation progresses through three stages unless halted by magic:

**Stage 1 — The Taint (Days 1–7)**
- Reptilian mannerisms intensify overnight — sibilant speech, tongue-flicking, cold preference
- User gains: *Poison Immunity* (resistance only at this stage), **darkvision 30 ft.** (or existing darkvision +30 ft.)
- User loses: normal emotional range — becomes detached, calculating, slow to feel fear or joy
- *Greater Restoration* or *Remove Curse* (DC 15) halts and reverses at this stage
- The user **does not feel ill.** They feel *clarified*. This is the danger.

**Stage 2 — Serpent Blood (Days 7–30)**
- Visible scales spread across shoulders, arms, and jaw
- Forked tongue fully develops
- Gains: **Poison Immunity** (full), venomous bite (1d6 + save DC 12 Con or poisoned 1 hour), **darkvision 60 ft.**
- Loses: ability to wear non-custom armour (scales interfere); hair falls out
- The character's alignment pulls toward **Lawful Neutral** regardless of previous alignment — they begin to value order, hierarchy, and cold logic
- Only *Wish*, a divine intervention, or a yuan-ti pureblood willing to perform a **reversal ritual** can halt at this stage
- The yuan-ti consider Stage 2 characters **property**. Any yuan-ti they encounter will try to claim them.

**Stage 3 — The Unworthy (30+ days, or if constitution is insufficient)**
- If the yuan-ti venom finds the body **unworthy** — Constitution score below 12 at Stage 2, or failed saving throws during the process — the character does not become a Tainted One.
- Instead they become a **Broodguard (Histachii)**: hairless, near-mindless, reptilian. Skin mottled grey-green. Functional intelligence drops to 4. They serve yuan-ti instinctively.
- This is effectively a character death unless divine magic (a *True Resurrection* equivalent) is used within 24 hours of Stage 3 onset.
- A Stage 3 character can no longer be played as a PC.

**Worthy vs. Unworthy:** The yuan-ti believe only those with strong enough blood become Tainted Ones. Weak-blooded individuals become Broodguards. From the yuan-ti's perspective, both outcomes are useful.

---

## Addiction

- **First dose:** DC 10 Constitution save or craving forms (no mechanical effect yet, but the DM may ask for roleplay)
- **Third dose:** Addiction is established. DC 14 Wisdom save each day without a dose or the character seeks out the drug by any means available.
- **Heavy use:** Physiological addiction (see above). Withdrawal has physical consequences.

---

## Yuan-ti Purpose

The yuan-ti introduce white resin into humanoid populations deliberately:
- **Control tool:** Addicted populations are compliant and manageable
- **Recruitment pipeline:** Stage 1–2 individuals who are "worthy" can be conscripted as agents or servants
- **Sabotage:** A city whose elite are quietly transforming is a city that can be taken from within
- The yuan-ti are **reluctant to overuse it** as heavy Broodguard creation reduces useful slave populations

In Neverwinter, the presence of white resin on the black market means **yuan-ti have an active interest in the city.** Where the resin goes, the yuan-ti watch.

## DM Notes

- The 5% / nat 1 trigger is designed to be a **slow-burn horror element**, not an immediate death sentence. Stage 1 is survivable and reversible — but the player may not know that at first.
- The fact that Stage 1 **feels good** (clarified, emotionally calm, cold and focused) is the real trap. A player character might *want* to stay at Stage 1 once they realise the benefits.
- **Yuan-ti agents in Neverwinter** will be monitoring known users. The dealer is likely reporting buyer identities upstream.
- The drug connects to the [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|Shadowy Force]] investigation: some of the "disappearances" may be Stage 2 individuals being collected by yuan-ti agents.
- If the party traces the supply chain, it leads out of Neverwinter — toward a yuan-ti operation somewhere in the region.
