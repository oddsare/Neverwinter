# Rhexx Enchantments — Quick Reference

An index of the custom enchantments in MrRhexx's *What They Don't Tell You About Magic Items* PDF (Brodie's copy: Downloads folder). **Names, costs, page numbers and a one-line summary only.** Read the PDF page for the full rules text. Prices in gp.

Related: [[Items/Magic Item Price Comparison]] · [[Items/House Magic Item Prices]]

## Core rules (summary, p. 4–5)
- Enchant progress is 100 gp/day (200 with Arcana proficiency, 300 with expertise). Hired enchanters work at 300/day.
- Each item holds **2 minor** enchantments, **or 1 minor + 1 major**.
- You can upgrade a tiered enchantment by paying the difference.
- Swapping an enchantment requires **Disenchant** first.
- Object enchantments can be **tattoos** instead: one per arm, same limits.
- Selling: 50% of the enchantment value plus the base item.

## Special — Bind Spell (major, attune, p. 13)

Put spells in an item to cast once each per dawn. Cost is per spell, by level:

| Cantrip | 1st | 2nd | 3rd | 4th | 5th | 6th | 7th | 8th | 9th |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 100 | 250 | 500 | 1,000 | 4,000 | 9,000 | 20,000 | 30,000 | 45,000 | 80,000 |

Add-ons raise the spell's level for pricing (never above 9th): Maximize +3, Quicken +1, Transmute element +1, Reach +1, Extend +1.

## Special — Summon Monster (major, attune, p. 14)

Summon creatures of one chosen type once per dawn for up to an hour. Cost by tier:

## Intrigue note

**False Detection (300 gp)** makes the wearer immune to divination and scrying, for less than a potion of healing costs in some lists. SMP prices the closest standard item, the Amulet of Proof against Detection and Location, at **20,000**. In a campaign built on secrets and scrying ([[NPCs/Claugiyliamatar - Old Gnawbone|Gnawbone's crystal balls]], [[NPCs/Ophala Cheldarstorn|Ophala's searches]]), decide this price before players find it.
