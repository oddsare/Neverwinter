## What This Faction Is

Renaer's government is not the throne, the bloodline, or House Neverember as a dynasty. It is **the people Renaer has gathered around him** to actually run a city the right way after ten years of failed lords. Includes:

- **Renaer himself** — the conscience of the regime. A follower of Tymora and **Tymora's Chosen**, whether he knows it or not.
- **[[NPCs/Laraelra Harsard|Laraelra Harsard]]** — his trusted friend and advisor, brought with him to Neverwinter; the Blackstaff's Heir
- **[[NPCs/Soren Ironwake]]** — Guard Captain, his most trusted lieutenant
- **[[Factions/City Guard]]** — restored and disciplined under Renaer's funding
- **Reformist nobles** — houses that backed his ascension and want stability
- **Civil servants** — the bureaucracy that survived the Dance and now serves him
- **[[Factions/The Harpers|The Harpers]]** — Renaer is **still a Harper**, and the Harpers led him to Neverwinter (Brodie, 2026-10-18)
- **Quiet allies** *(May draft, keep?)* — possibly [[NPCs/Ophala Cheldarstorn]], possibly Selûnite contacts from the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Shard of the Moon|Shard of the Moon]]

---

## What This Faction Wants

- **Stability.** No more revolving lords. No more civil war.
- **A new course.** Change some of his father's policies — lower the heavy noble taxes, dismantle the Mintarn mercenary garrison, rebuild trust with the guilds.
- **Legitimacy.** Renaer **knows the Neverember bloodline claim is suspect**. He doesn't want to die for it — but he wants to govern well enough that citizens don't care.
- **Survival.** The regime is two years old and fragile. Any major shock — a real Alagondar heir surfacing, the Luskan delegation succeeding, Dagult returning alive — could topple it.

---

## Renaer's Defining Tension

Renaer is GOOD but also a Neverember. If a real [[Quests/The Foster King|Alagondar heir]] surfaces, the morally correct move is to step aside. The civically correct move (right now) may not be the same. **He has built two years of stability on the assumption that the throne question is closed.** Reopening it is dangerous for Neverwinter, not just for him.

This is the wedge the party can use — or the wedge the party may have to defend Renaer against.
