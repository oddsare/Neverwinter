# Renaer's Government

> Renaer inherited a city beggared by his father's greed, fractured by twenty years of failed lords, and skeptical of the Neverember name. Five years in, he is the most honest ruler Neverwinter has had in a generation. He is also constantly one bad week away from losing it all.

**Type:** Civic government
**Seat:** Castle Never, [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Protector's Enclave]]
**Head of State:** [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]] — 7th ascension after the Dance of the Revolving Lords
**Alignment:** **GOOD** — actively trying to do right by the citizens of Neverwinter
**Status:** Active and stable for 5 years; under pressure

---

## What This Faction Is

Renaer's government is not the throne, the bloodline, or House Neverember as a dynasty. It is **the people Renaer has gathered around him** to actually run a city the right way after twenty years of misrule. Includes:

- **Renaer himself** — the conscience of the regime
- **[[NPCs/Soren Ironwake]]** — Guard Captain, his most trusted lieutenant
- **[[Factions/City Guard]]** — restored and disciplined under Renaer's funding
- **Reformist nobles** — houses that backed his ascension and want stability
- **Civil servants** — the bureaucracy that survived the Dance and now serves him
- **Quiet allies** — possibly [[NPCs/Ophala Cheldarstorn]], possibly Selûnite contacts from the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Shard of the Moon|Shard of the Moon]], possibly the [[Factions/The Harpers|Harpers]] (informally)

---

## What This Faction Wants

- **Stability.** No more revolving lords. No more civil war.
- **Restoration.** Undo Dagult's damage — lower the noble taxes, dismantle the Mintarn mercenary garrison, rebuild trust with the guilds.
- **Legitimacy.** Renaer **knows the Neverember bloodline claim is suspect**. He doesn't want to die for it — but he wants to govern well enough that citizens don't care.
- **Survival.** The regime is five years old and fragile. Any major shock — a real Alagondar heir surfacing, the Luskan delegation succeeding, Dagult returning alive — could topple it.

---

## Renaer's Defining Tension

Renaer is GOOD but also a Neverember. If a real [[Quests/The Foster King|Alagondar heir]] surfaces, the morally correct move is to step aside. The civically correct move (right now) may not be the same. **He has built five years of stability on the assumption that the throne question is closed.** Reopening it is dangerous for Neverwinter, not just for him.

This is the wedge the party can use — or the wedge the party may have to defend Renaer against.
