# House Crownrood (Cormyr)

**Type:** Minor noble house of Cormyr
**Seat:** **Roodhills**, a much-enlarged rural estate on farms northeast of **Kallamarn**. The family also keeps a home in **Suzail**, west of the Royal Palace (Castle Obarskyr).
**Ennobled:** **1324 DR**, by King **Rhigaerd Obarskyr II**
**Blazon:** a black silhouette of a spiked crown with five equal points, each a sword blade three times as tall as the crown is wide, on a white field
**Motto:** "Finding New Ways"

> Source: **Realms Lore** (YouTube), Ed Greenwood. Transcript added 2026-10-19. Summarized in my own words.
> Ed's "present" is the **late 1490s DR**; ages for 1505 are worked out below.

## Why this house exists: the "Readily Deployables"
- Cormyr has **about two dozen wealthy but quiet, low-profile minor noble houses**. Ed suggests DMs think of them as the **"Readily Deployables."**
- Little published lore exists about them, so **you can make them what your story needs**. They have to stay low-profile, though: no important official posts and no political prominence, or we'd have heard of them already.
- A house that's only a name is almost as much work to use as a famous one, so Ed fleshed this one out as **a noble house to play with**.
- **On the name:** in the Realms, **"rood"** doesn't mean a crucifix or a measure of land. It means **"of the land"** or **"keeper of the rules."**

## How they were ennobled
- King Rhigaerd raised them for "loyal and exemplary services to the Crown." That really meant two things:
  - **Feeding the poor:** they sponsored **food and clothing drives** for the poor of Suzail and its surroundings during the **lean harvests of 1322–1323 DR**.
  - **Keeping the nobles loyal:** they **quietly settled disputes among nobles** so they'd back the Obarskyrs, rather than plotting treason with **Sembian interests**.
    - Those Sembians wanted to **"buy in" to much of Cormyr east of the Wyvernflow** and secretly control it.
    - Their plan was to ship its harvests east to sell in Sembia while Cormyreans starved and food prices soared.
- **Inheritance:** like every ennoblement from that reign onward, it passes to the **eldest surviving legitimate heir, regardless of gender**.

## The generations

### 1. Alaskus, first Lord Crownrood
- **His father:** **Ralaraskus**, an infamous carouser who fathered dozens of illegitimate children.
- **Alaskus** was one of only two legitimate sons. His brother **Nevren** died young in a fall from a horse.
- **His character:** unlike his father, **lawful, bookish and respectable**. Over time he became a **shrewd judge of character** and a good actor: **a born diplomat**.
- **The king's envoy:** King Rhigaerd came to trust him and used him often as an **unofficial, unpaid "Crown envoy and advocate among the nobles."** Alaskus was happy to do it.
- **His wife, Lady Pelarla:**
  - fussy but private, devoted to **cleanliness, order and a good reputation**;
  - well-read, fond of philosophy, and designed her own clothes;
  - **designed the family blazon**, which the heralds accepted on first sight;
  - outlived Alaskus and stayed sharp to her deathbed.
- **Their four children**, eldest to youngest: **Borlays** ("BORE-laze"), **Taneera** ("Tah-NEER-ah"), **Sarlys** ("SAR-lees") and **Anamaetha** ("An-ah-MAY-thah").
- **The two youngest daughters:** **Sarlys and Anamaetha** were slender, lively, sharp-tongued and clever. Both grew wealthy through their own work and investments, and neither married.
  - **Sarlys** preferred women to men. She **joined the Harpers**, slipping away from Cormyr for adventures, and collected trophies and drinking friends.
  - **Anamaetha** preferred her spellbooks to anyone. She became a **minor mage** whom the **War Wizards watched closely** but never caught at anything except casting spells for her friends for free. She ended her days happily reading racy chapbooks.

### 2. Borlays, second Lord Crownrood
- **His character:** a noted **rider and hunter** at royal hunts, though only a so-so archer. Well liked, but quiet rather than the life of the party. **Unambitious and generous.**
- **Moving to the country:** he traded the family's modest Suzail holdings (three ordinary houses, two of them rented out) for **one better home west of the Royal Palace** and **farms northeast of Kallamarn**. He moved there and happily lived as "a country lord."
- **His wife, Alaethe** ("Al-LAYTH"): a quiet, kind, always sickly gardener and scholar. She raised a son, **Valrikh**, and **died in childbirth** trying to give Borlays a daughter.
- **Taneera runs the house:** after Alaethe's death, Borlays's eldest sister **Taneera** became chatelaine. The family says she ran the household "with an iron fist and a sharp steel tongue and mind."
  - **Her shrewd crop choices and her sales of food and herbs into Sembian markets made the family wealthy.** They've never lacked money since.
  - She never married. Rumors said she was a "close" friend of **Duke Bhereu Ammaeth**, and that she privately hosted **King Azoun IV** a few times.
  - She lived to almost **100**, gaunt but never slowing down.

### 3. Valrikh, third Lord Crownrood
- **His character:** moody, sarcastic, handsome and **hard to like**. He burned through the family fortune with **gambling and bad investments**.
- **His crowd:** a fast set of young noble poseurs devoted to pranks, vandalism, hunting, riding and womanizing.
- **His marriage:** Taneera all but forced him to **marry one of his conquests**, the timid, easily led **Malaerra Rallyhorn** ("Mah-LAIR-ah"), a fourth daughter, and to produce an heir and a spare.
- **His death:** he **trusted the wrong smuggler** to make him rich quick. He was found **drowned in Marsember's harbor**, paralytic poison in his veins, with **three daggers driven hilt-deep between his fingers**.
- **His sons:** **Melder** (a Rallyhorn family name) and his younger brother **Alaskus**. Malaerra did her best to raise them.

### 4. Melder: schemer and traitor
- **His character:** bright, with **iron self-control**, and an ever-better actor.
- **His brother's "accident":** he fooled his mother about who caused the "accident" with a heavy chair on Roodhills' grand staircase that killed his brother **Alaskus**.
- **His mother:** Malaerra was dead by **1476 DR**. Some former servants suspect Melder had a hand in it.
- **His ambition:** handsome but **mistrusted** by most of Cormyr's nobles, and looked down on at Court as an "impoverished country noble." He set out to **quietly get rich and become an important behind-the-scenes noble**.
- **His marriage:** to raise his standing he married **Ralavauntra Emmarask** (born **1457 DR**), of a senior noble house trusted across the realm as fair and loyal. They had three children in three years.
- **His schemes:** he left them at Roodhills with hired tutors and moved to **Suzail**. There he invested in **every shady venture out of Marsember, Westgate, Teziir and Sembia** that looked likely to succeed without drawing the Crown's or the War Wizards' wrath.
- **His fall:**
  - He had good judgment, and was an accomplished schemer and lawbreaker already toying with **small, sly treasons**. That's how he appears in Ed's novel ***Bury Elminster Deep***, even **before Manshoon took over his mind**.
  - **Manshoon mind-controlled him into rash mischief.** His **fellow traitors in Suzail** killed him, and made his body vanish, before he could expose them to the Crown.

### 5. Ralavauntra, Dowager Lady Crownrood (born 1457; about 48 in 1505)
- **Raising the girls alone:** a wise, quick-witted, **dark-haired spitfire with great connections**. She raised their three daughters alone through the later 1480s.
- **Turning to the War Wizards:** she suspected her husband's dealings. So she **quietly went to the War Wizards** to make her loyalty clear and ask their help in raising her daughters "right," in good standing with the Crown.
  - They leaped at the access. As a result, **the Crownroods are now among the most watched noble houses in Cormyr, and also among the most trusted**, and the likeliest to be **secretly helped**, sometimes without noticing.
- **Today:**
  - She's feeling her age and slowing down, devoted to making her daughters women she can be proud of.
  - She's turning **Roodhills** into a vast stretch of farms and woodlots where **tenants live very well** in new or refurbished cottages, and the house **sponsors local crafters and shops**.
  - That has made her **the best-loved Crownrood ever**, though Taneera was arguably more publicly respected.
- **Her character:** quiet and kind rather than pushy or manipulative, and **far more patient than most nobles**.
- **She is still the real day-to-day head of the family.**

## The three daughters (the current generation)

### Tasheera, Lady Crownrood (born 1477; about 28 in 1505)
- **Officially head of the house**, but she has no intention of a power struggle with the mother she loves and takes guidance from almost daily.
- **She wants to put off being a "respectable, staid matriarch" for as long as possible.** For now she's **trying her hand at everything**, to learn what she's best at and what to hire others for.
- **What she's found so far:**
  - **good at:** **figures and bookkeeping**, and pretty good at **spotting trends and foreseeing market shifts**;
  - **hates:** dancing, empty chatter, and especially catty gossip.
- **Her mind:** innately practical. She quickly sees what needs doing, how things work, and **whether there's deception going on**, or something she's not seeing.
- **Appearance:** tall and slender, almost mannish in build, with a strikingly beautiful face, long glossy raven-black hair, fierce brows, deep blue eyes and a lilting voice.
- **Talents:** a **superb singer and mimic**. She can play an imperious, utterly self-assured senior noble while **hiding any fear**.
- **Danger thrills her.** She isn't reckless, but she **won't flee or back away from trouble**. She wants to "be and see," not hear about exciting things later.
- **She's looking for her road ahead**, trying out professions and crafts, possibly including **adventuring**.
- **Weapons:** competent, not outstanding, and she takes no joy in it.
- **Clothes are tools to her.** She'll dress alluringly at a revel to reach certain people, or as a mature matriarch at Court. If someone suggests a fast ride, she'll swap gowns for riding leathers on the spot, audience or not.
- **Secrets she doesn't know about herself:**
  - **She has the Gift**: she can cast, wield and craft **arcane magic**. It showed up after she was tested.
  - **A wild talent she's starting to experiment with:** she can **project a thought into nearby minds**, conveying her presence and a wish. For example: go away, come here, or bring me a rope, food or a key.
  - She's beginning to **sense hostility** in anyone whose eyes she can look into, apparently without them noticing.
  - **Doppelgangers sense her growing powers and avoid her**, as a threat they don't understand.

### Pelarla (born 1478; about 27 in 1505)
- Named after the first Lady Crownrood.
- **An acrobatic, accomplished actress** with long curly hair. She can play the dignified or haughty noble to perfection, but would rather **master the longsword and daggers** and learn **trading and investment**.
- **She's looking for a lifelong companion she can trust, husband or wife.**
  - They needn't be noble; she doesn't like what she's seen of her realm's available nobles.
  - They must share her ambition: an interesting life with enough coin never to go hungry, and new ventures whenever they come up.
- **She loves Roodhills and Cormyr**, but if life leads her far away, she'll go. She wants **"the new"** and a life of her own making.
- She finds the noble social whirl tiresome, though she can act as if she loves it.
- **She's always very interested in a far-traveled adventurer or trader** who takes her seriously enough to talk candidly about distant places.
- **She knows she lacks the Gift.** One of her deepest secrets is that she's **collecting magic items she can wear or carry** to make up for it.
- **Skills:** an **expert climber** and a very good seamstress, especially at repairs.

### Glaraema (born 1479; about 26 in 1505)
- **A darkly beautiful, whimsical, impish flirt.** She wants adventure, and **a rich noble husband** who'll support her without expecting her to be faithful or to run his household (and she won't expect fidelity from him).
- **Her goal:** to see the Realms, have adventures, take lovers and try everything, rather than settle into routine. In her words, she wants to **do things and have colorful tales to tell**. She plans to write them up as **chapbooks** in old age, noble reputation be damned.
- **Her manner:** little modesty, finds most things funny rather than insulting, and will try almost anything once.
  - She once built a wardrobe of very daring clothes to provoke her family. She gave it up when she found none of them cared, and has since outgrown that kind of teasing.
- **Adventuring:** she'll eagerly join or help adventurers.
  - **Skills so far:** an expert rider, acrobatic climber and superb writer, and not much else yet.
  - **Unknown to her, she also has the Gift**: she can cast, wield and craft arcane magic.

## Realms Speak: Jergal
- Pronounced **"JER-gul"** by most (some say "YER-gul").
- **The Lord of the End of Everything**: the **original god of death**, before the Dead Three. Ed created him in **1966**.
- *Baldur's Gate 3* players know him as **Withers**, one of his many aliases.
- **He uses the form of a spellweaver**, a monster Ed created for *Dragon* magazine, and some think he's the **last spellweaver**.
- **He has been manipulating mind flayers**, is **much older than you think**, and likes to **control the world from behind the scenes**.

## How this fits the vault (Brodie decides)
- **A model for Neverwinter's nobles:** canon names almost no living noble houses in Neverwinter. Ed's **"Readily Deployables"** idea, a quiet minor house with full family lore, is a template Brodie could use. Nothing here decides any Neverwinter house.
- **Manshoon** mind-controlled **Melder** (in *Bury Elminster Deep*). The vault already has Manshoon and his simulacrum (see [[NPCs/Manshoon]], [[NPCs/Manshoon's Simulacrum]]).
- **Cormyr and the War Wizards:**
  - Volo's friend **Nanthe' Ryddorn** is a War Wizard ([[NPCs/Volothamp Geddarm (Volo)]]);
  - **Laeral** wants Cormyr in the Lords' Alliance ([[NPCs/Laeral Silverhand]]);
  - the Truesilvers and "Ayesunder" appear in [[Lore/Ed Greenwood - Elves]].
- **Doppelgangers avoid Tasheera**, and Laeral hates shapeshifters who prey on others.
- **The three daughters** are ready-made adventuring NPCs, aged 26–28 in 1505.
- **Jergal manipulating mind flayers** adds to the vault's flayers: House Cyl's Iscahill, Volo's Ixruu, and Ioulaum as an elder brain.
- **No clashes** with the vault, and no movie lore.
