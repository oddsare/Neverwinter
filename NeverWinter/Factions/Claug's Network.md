# Claug's Network

> [!info] Where this note came from
> Written in May 2026 on another branch and brought in on 2026-10-24 (Brodie). Updated to **1505 DR**: Aldric is 28 and was raised since 9, and Claug now lairs near Neverwinter ([[Campaign Decisions]] §14). Tier wording is replaced with plain timing. Anything marked **(May draft, keep?)** was suggested by Claude in May, not decided by Brodie.

> The dragon has been planning for two centuries. She has agents in three cities, a son she is preparing to put on a throne, and a ritual she wants performed on herself. The network is the quiet machinery that makes all of that work.

**Type:** Covert influence operation
**Head:** [[NPCs/Claugiyliamatar - Old Gnawbone|Claugiyliamatar]] — green dragon, "Old Gnawbone"
**Public Face:** Lady Maeve Vance (Claug's *alter self* persona)
**Alignment:** **UGLY** — not aligned good, not straightforwardly evil; serves the dragon's plan, which is patient, manipulative, and personal
**Status:** Active and undetected by Neverwinter's government (as of 1505 DR)

---

## Components

### The Heir Asset
- **[[Quests/The Foster King|Aldric Vance]]** — 28-year-old mid-level wizard, found by Claug in 1486 at age 9 and raised since then to be a usable Alagondar heir
- The **Vance estate** on Neverwinter's outskirts — Aldric's home, the network's public-facing real estate
- Aldric's growing **public network in Neverwinter society** — salons, arcane circles, patrons

### Surveillance & Control
- **Crystal ball scrying** — Claug watches Aldric daily from her new lair near Neverwinter (under the city, maybe in the Neverwinter Wood), which she moved to partly to be close to him ([[Campaign Decisions]] §14)
- **Lady Maeve persona** — Ring of Chameleon Power + *alter self* polymorph; visits in person as needed
- **Aldric's complete devotion** — the deepest control, soft and unbreakable

### Hidden Lieutenants
- Trusted agents who know the plan and execute it — names and structure TBD
- They handle: noble bribes, document forgeries (bloodline "evidence"), surveillance, intimidation, the occasional silenced witness

### Contracted Specialists (May draft, keep?)
- **Sharran ritualists** — hired to help groom Aldric's identity and (eventually) assist Claug's permanent-human ritual
- **[[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood]] mages** — secretly tutoring Aldric in advanced magic, on Claug's payroll. They believe they're playing her; they aren't.
- **Possibly:** the [[NPCs/Voaraghamanthar - Two Black Brothers]] (Twin Crowns of Miramaran could shield Aldric from Harper diviners)

### Indrina's Dossier (intelligence asset)
- Claug obtained a copy or summary of [[NPCs/Indrina Lamsensettle]]'s genealogy work — the source of her belief that Aldric is a true Alagondar
- The full dossier is still out there. Recovering or destroying it is on Claug's medium-term list.

## The Plan

1. **Throne first (mid-late campaign):** install Aldric on the Neverwinter throne via the Sons of Alagondar + Lost Crown reveal + Renaer maneuvered out
2. **Ritual second (after the campaign or near its end):** once Aldric grows into master-level magic, he crafts Claug's permanent-human ritual out of love for the woman who raised him
3. **Outcome:** Claug walks Neverwinter as a permanent human, Aldric rules with her counsel, the city is a dragon's protectorate by proxy

See [[Quests/The Foster King]] for the full plan.

---

## The Network's Vulnerabilities

- **Claug is one person.** She cannot be in two places at once; the lair, the estate, and the city all need her attention. Scrying covers a lot but not everything.
- **The Sharran ritualists are not loyal.** They serve Shar, not Claug. If Shar decides Aldric is more useful as a sacrifice than a king, the ritualists will turn.
- **The Brotherhood mages are not loyal.** They serve Luskan and themselves. The [[Quests/The Peace Anniversary|delegation visit]] may be a quiet check-in OR a quiet betrayal. *(May draft, keep?)*
- **Aldric is not yet a master.** The ritual is years off. Anything that kills him, exposes him, or turns him against her destroys two centuries of work.
- **Lady Maeve's persona has cracks.** Hounds don't approach her. Servants disappear after seeing things. Scrying timing is too perfect. A perceptive party can catch the off-notes.
