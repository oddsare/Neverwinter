# The Alagondar Bloodline

> A bloodline is not a faction. Until it is. A name on a forgotten family tree is just paper, until one of those names walks into a room and a vault opens for them. Then the paper becomes a kingdom.

> [!info] Where this note came from
> Written in May 2026 on another branch and brought in on 2026-10-24 (Brodie). Updated to **1505 DR**: Aldric is 28, Renaer has ruled 2 years, and tier wording is gone. **Known heirs and claimants** and **The plot so far** gather what other notes in the vault already say, with links back to them. Anything marked **(May draft, keep?)** was suggested by Claude in May, not decided by Brodie.

**Type:** Bloodline / royal claim / dormant restoration faction
**Founder:** the [[Lore/The Alagondar Dynasty|Alagondar Dynasty]]. [[Lore/The Alagondar Dynasty|King Bann Alagondar]] ruled until the 1451 eruption, when the royal family died.
**Status:** Dormant. No public heir. One secret candidate ([[Quests/The Foster King|Aldric]]) is being raised by Claugiyliamatar, more than a dozen hidden kin may live quietly in the city, and Indrina's dossier may name others.
**Alignment:** Neutral in itself, but it's Neverwinter's most legitimate path back to a true king.

---

## What this faction is

The Alagondar Bloodline isn't an organization. It's a **dormant political force** built on three things:

1. **Surviving descendants** of the royal line (see *Known heirs and claimants* below).
2. **Bloodline-locked artifacts:** the [[Lore/The Lost Crown and Hidden Alagondar Vaults|Lost Crown of Neverwinter]] and the hidden vaults, all keyed to true Alagondar blood.
3. **The Sons of Alagondar:** the movement waiting to rally to a real heir.

When a true Alagondar walks into a vault that opens for them, the bloodline **becomes a faction in a single moment.** Until then it is potential energy.

## Known heirs and claimants

### Possible true heirs

**Aldric Vance** *(placeholder name)*: see [[Quests/The Foster King]]
- 28 in 1505. Found and adopted by [[NPCs/Claugiyliamatar - Old Gnawbone|Claugiyliamatar]] (as "Lady Maeve Vance") in 1486, at 9.
- A mid-level wizard, known in Neverwinter society.
- Claug **suspects** he's Alagondar. Not proven.

**Ed Greenwood's hidden kin**: see [[Lore/Ed Greenwood - Secrets of Neverwinter]]
- **Probably Alagondar** (Brodie, 2026-10-22; [[Campaign Decisions]] §7). The video's captions said Neverember, but they hide from Dagult, who hunted Alagondar heirs.
- **More than a dozen relatives live quietly in Neverwinter** as crafters and traders. Dagult may not have known about them, **but every one of them knows their place in the family tree.**
- Three named:
  - **Wylandra Blaythloak:** makes and sells slab bread at her shop on Himmer Lane, where [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Protector's Enclave]] meets the [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Tower District|River (Towers) District]]. Recently widowed (Belaerak). Her six grown children live with her and work in the shop.
  - **Unthra Rorivan** *(first name uncertain)*: adventurer and guide to the Savage North. Plays an uncouth bumpkin but is a shrewd judge of people who notices everything. Survives danger with a knack rumored to come from inherited family magic items. Probably in her 30s (age given for the video's present).
  - **Rundrigo Blackserpent** *(uncertain)*: rich trader, investor and master caster, working on portable water clocks. At least six Blackserpent-owned ships sail the Sword Coast, with the family name kept off them.
- **Still open:** whether "Alagondar" is final, and how they tie to Renaer and the Lost Crown.

**Unknown heirs in Indrina's dossier**: see [[NPCs/Indrina Lamsensettle]]
- Her research may have identified surviving true heirs as a side effect, and ruled out or confirmed other claimants.
- Claug has a copy or summary that pointed her to Aldric. The full dossier (with Indrina, the Harpers or in a Waterdhavian vault) may list others.

**Heirs Dagult hunted**: see [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember]] and [[Locations/Neverwinter/The Neverneath]]
- Dagult **sought the death of rival Alagondar claimants** for 26 years (canon: he "silently disposes of rivals").
- He likely identified at least one true heir before he vanished in 1493. Who they were, and whether any are still alive, is something only he knows.

**Prisoners of the Two Black Brothers**: see [[NPCs/Voaraghamanthar - Two Black Brothers]]
- The brothers' prisoners may include true heirs (the note leaves this open).

### False claimants

**Dagult Neverember**: see [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember]]
- Claimed descent from **Vers Never**, a supposed bastard son of Nasher Alagondar. **Canon: the claim is fraudulent.** Indrina's research found no blood tie.
- Paid scribes to forge supporting books and burned books that cast doubt.
- Can't open the vaults or wear the Crown. Trapped in the Neverneath since 1493.

**Renaer Neverember**: see [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember]]
- Dagult's son, so he has the same problem. Not Alagondar.
- He styles himself "Lord," not "King."

**Seldra Tylmarande**: see [[Lore/Pre-1502 Supporting Cast]]
- In **1479 DR** she was given a **replica of the Crown**, disguised herself as a man and used it to claim the throne. The plot was defeated.
- Her fate by 1505 is uncertain.

## How a true heir is proven

1. **Bloodline-locked vaults open for them.** The [[Lore/The Lost Crown and Hidden Alagondar Vaults|Lost Crown vault]] is the canon test.
2. **Indrina's dossier** confirms the line in writing.
3. **The Crown itself.** Canon: any non-Alagondar who wears it is **destroyed by a burst of cold and heat.** That's why Dagult never put it on.
4. **Sons of Alagondar elders:** old loyalists who remember Bann's line and may recognize the family in someone.

> **A catch** (from [[Locations/Neverwinter/The Neverneath]]): Halueth Never predates the Alagondars by over a thousand years. If the Neverneath's Inner Vault checks for *Halueth's* line, even a true Alagondar might not pass.

---

## How the Neverember claim is disproven

- Indrina's research already showed [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember|Dagult]] has no Alagondar blood (canon).
- Renaer inherits the same problem.
- The vaults won't open for any Neverember. They might for Aldric, or for one of the hidden kin.

## Who wants the bloodline restored

- **The Sons of Alagondar:** the organized movement (below).
- **[[Factions/Claug's Network|Claug's network]]:** wants Aldric specifically, for her own ends (see [[Quests/The Foster King]]).
- **Some of [[Factions/Renaer's Government|Renaer's reformers]]:** if a real heir would govern well, they'd consider stepping aside.
- **Old Neverwinter loyalists** among the common people. About 1 in 3 people in the city come from pre-1451 families ([[Campaign Decisions]] §8).

## Who wants the bloodline buried

- **Dagult loyalists:** Mintarn mercenary survivors and the scribes who forged his lineage. A real Alagondar proves Dagult was always a fraud and exposes them as accomplices.
- **[[Factions/Luskan|Luskan]] and the [[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood|Arcane Brotherhood]]:** they prefer Neverwinter divided and unstable, and a legitimate king ends that leverage. *(May draft, keep?)*
- **Renaer himself** *(complicated)*: he doesn't want to die for the throne, but reopening the bloodline question threatens his two years of stable rule.

## The Sons of Alagondar

The organized restoration movement. They split by the [[Lore/3 Faction Rule|Three-Faction Rule]]:

- **Old loyalists** who remember King Bann: true believers who want true blood (good)
- **Opportunists** who'd rule through any puppet heir (bad)
- **Younger members** who'd accept Renaer if he stepped aside cleanly for a real heir (ugly; they swing on legitimacy)

They are weak, divided and have **no heir** to rally around in public. They're looking. If a real one appears, they unite within a season.

They were part of the rising opposition that pushed Dagult into the Neverneath in 1493 (see [[Locations/Neverwinter/The Neverneath]]).

## The plot so far

What the vault already has in motion around the bloodline:

- **1451:** the royal family dies in the eruption. Survivors hide the Alagondar treasures in bloodline-locked vaults across the city ([[Lore/The Lost Crown and Hidden Alagondar Vaults]]).
- **1467:** Dagult proclaims himself an Alagondar heir and starts "New Neverwinter." For the next 26 years he hunts rival claimants ([[NPCs/Dagult Neverember]]).
- **Indrina Lamsensettle** proves his claim is fake, and her research may name real heirs ([[NPCs/Indrina Lamsensettle]]).
- **1479:** Seldra Tylmarande's fake-crown plot fails ([[Lore/Pre-1502 Supporting Cast]]).
- **1486:** Claugiyliamatar finds Aldric, aged 9, and starts raising him as "Lady Maeve" ([[Quests/The Foster King]]).
- **1493:** under pressure from the Sons of Alagondar and others, Dagult goes into the Neverneath for the Lost Crown and is trapped. He takes the names of the heirs he was hunting with him ([[Locations/Neverwinter/The Neverneath]]).
- **1493–1503:** the Dance of the Revolving Lords, 6 rulers in 10 years.
- **1503:** Renaer takes power, with the same fake claim as his father.
- **1505 (campaign start):**
  - Aldric, 28, is a rising young wizard in Neverwinter society. Claug suspects but can't prove he's an heir.
  - More than a dozen hidden kin live quietly in the city, each knowing their place in the family tree.
  - The Sons of Alagondar have no heir and are looking.
  - The Lost Crown is still in the Neverneath, with Dagult.

**The big turn,** whenever it comes: a vault opens for someone. Who, and who sees it, decides who the bloodline belongs to.
