# The Alagondar Bloodline

> A bloodline is not a faction. Until it is. A name on a forgotten genealogical chart is just paper — until one of those names walks into a room and a vault opens for him. Then the paper becomes a kingdom.

**Type:** Bloodline / dynastic claim / dormant restoration faction
**Founder:** [[Lore/The Alagondar Dynasty|King Bann Alagondar]] — last true ruler of pre-1451 Neverwinter
**Status:** Dormant — no public candidate; one secret candidate ([[Quests/The Foster King|Aldric Vance]]) being groomed; possibly more unknown candidates
**Alignment:** Inherently neutral — but represents Neverwinter's most legitimate path to restoration

---

## What This Faction Is

The Alagondar Bloodline is not an organization. It is a **dormant political force** built on three things:

1. **Surviving descendants** of Bann Alagondar — at least one (Aldric, per Claug's research), possibly others (per [[NPCs/Indrina Lamsensettle]]'s dossier)
2. **Bloodline-locked artifacts** — the [[Lore/The Lost Crown and Hidden Alagondar Vaults|Lost Crown of Neverwinter]] and the hidden vaults, all keyed to true Alagondar blood
3. **The Sons of Alagondar** — the organization waiting to rally to a real heir

When a true Alagondar walks into a vault that opens for them, the bloodline **becomes a faction in a single moment.** Until then it is potential energy.

- **Suspected** Alagondar by Claug. Not yet proven.

- Claug only has Aldric. The full dossier (still with Indrina or in a Waterdhavian vault) may list others.
- A **rival heir surfacing** is a tier 2-3 plot detonator

## Proof Mechanisms

How is a real Alagondar proven, in-fiction?

1. **Bloodline-locked vaults open for them** — the [[Lore/The Lost Crown and Hidden Alagondar Vaults|Lost Crown vault]] is the canonical test
2. **Indrina's dossier** confirms genealogy in writing
3. **The Crown itself** — wearing it kills false claimants ([[NPCs/Dagult Neverember|Dagult]] would have died if he'd tried); fits a true Alagondar
4. **Sons of Alagondar elders** — old loyalists who remember Bann's line and can recognize bearing or family resonance

---

## Disproof Mechanisms

What proves the Neverember claim FALSE — and by contrast, the search for the real bloodline urgent?

- Indrina's research already confirmed [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember|Dagult]] has no Alagondar blood (canon)
- Renaer inherits the same problem — same fraudulent claim
- The vaults will not open for any Neverember. They might, for an Aldric.

---

## Who Wants the Bloodline Restored

- **The Sons of Alagondar** — the political organization (sub-faction below)
- **[[Factions/Claug's Network]]** — wants Aldric specifically, for her own ends
- **Some [[Factions/Renaer's Government|Renaer-aligned reformers]]** — if a real heir would govern well, they'd consider stepping aside
- **Old Neverwinter loyalists** in the common population

## Who Wants the Bloodline Suppressed

- **Dagult-loyalist remnants** — Mintarn mercenary survivors, forged-genealogy scribes; a real Alagondar = Dagult was ALWAYS a fraud, which exposes them as accomplices
- **[[Factions/Luskan]] / [[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood]]** — prefer Neverwinter divided and unstable; a legitimate king ends the leverage
- **Renaer himself** *(complicated)* — does not want to die for the throne, but reopening the bloodline question threatens his five years of stable rule

---

## Sub-faction: The Sons of Alagondar

The Sons are the organized restoration movement. They split internally per the [[Lore/3 Faction Rule|3-Faction Rule]]:

- **Old loyalists** who remember King Bann — pure ideologues, want true blood (good)
- **Opportunists** looking to rule through any puppet heir (bad)
- **Younger members** who'd accept Renaer if he abdicated cleanly to a real heir (ugly — swing on legitimacy)

They are weak, fractured, and have **no heir** to rally around publicly. They are looking. If a real one appears, they consolidate within a season.
