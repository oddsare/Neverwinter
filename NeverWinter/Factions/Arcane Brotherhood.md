# The Arcane Brotherhood

**Type:** Wizarding Cabal
**Base:** [[Locations/Luskan|Luskan - The City of Sails]]
**Alignment:** Evil (likely)
**Status:** Active
**True Rulers:** Of Luskan

## Overview

The **true power** in [[Locations/Luskan|Luskan]] resides with the **Arcane Brotherhood**.

This powerful magical organization controls the City of Sails from the shadows, allowing the Emperor of the Seas and the Five High Captains to rule publicly while they pull the strings behind the scenes.

## Threat to Neverwinter

### Why They're Dangerous
The combination of:
- Pirate fleets (through Luskan's High Captains)
- Powerful wizards
- Wealth from all forms of trade
- No moral restrictions

This makes the Arcane Brotherhood a **significant threat** to [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]].
