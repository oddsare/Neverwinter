# The Harpers

**Type:** Secret Society / Information Network
**Allegiance:** Good, Balance, Freedom
**Symbol:** A silver harp and crescent moon
**Scope:** Continent-wide across [[Lore/Faerun|Faerûn]]
**Status:** Active

---

## Overview

**The Harpers** are a semi-secret organization dedicated to promoting good, preserving history, maintaining balance between civilization and nature, and opposing the abuse of power.

They work from the shadows, gathering information, recruiting allies, and intervening when necessary to protect the common folk and the natural world.

---

## Philosophy & Goals

### Core Beliefs
- **Promote good** and oppose evil
- **Preserve historical knowledge** and lore
- **Maintain balance** between civilization and nature
- **Oppose tyranny** and the abuse of power
- **Protect the innocent** and common folk
- **Work from the shadows** rather than seeking glory

### Methods
- **Information gathering** - Spies and informants across Faerûn
- **Subtle intervention** - Acting through agents and allies
- **Recruitment** - Identifying and enlisting capable individuals
- **Preservation** - Protecting ancient sites and knowledge
- **Opposition** - Undermining tyrants and evil organizations

## Membership

### Who Are the Harpers?

Harper agents come from all walks of life:
- **Bards** - Traditional Harper members
- **Rogues** - Information gatherers and infiltrators
- **Rangers** - Defenders of nature
- **Wizards** - Scholars and arcane specialists
- **Anyone** dedicated to their cause

### Recognition

- Members carry a **silver harp pin** (usually hidden)
- Use specific **code phrases** and signals
- Operate with great secrecy

---

## Known Allies

The Harpers work with:
- **Good-aligned governments** (like [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer's]] rule)
- **Temples of good deities**
- **Druid circles**
- **Other secret societies** opposing evil

---

## Known Enemies

The Harpers oppose:
- **Tyrants and despots**
- **Evil cults** (especially Zhentarim)
- **Those who abuse magic**
- **Threats to historical knowledge**
- **Destroyers of nature**

## DM Notes

### The Harpers and the Laughing Wyrm
**Certain Harpers know** about [[NPCs/Zundaerazylym - The Laughing Wyrm|Zundaerazylym]]'s work: her secret guild (the Soft Claws), her dragon-feeding Longbite, her healing Soak, and her Never Tokens. They could move against her or step in to seize what she's made. **She only suspects this.** This gives the Harpers a gray edge in Neverwinter, and since [[NPCs/Ophala Cheldarstorn|Ophala]] is Harper-adjacent and the dragon's best friend, it can split loyalties.
