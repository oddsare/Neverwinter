# Neverwinter City Guard

**Type:** Military / Law Enforcement
**Allegiance:** [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]], [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember]]
**Headquarters:** [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Protectors Enclave|Protector's Enclave]] (Castle Never)
**Status:** Active

---

## Overview

The **Neverwinter City Guard** is the primary law enforcement and military force protecting [[Locations/Neverwinter/Neverwinter|Neverwinter]].

They maintain order in the city, protect citizens, enforce laws, and defend against external threats.

---

## History

### Strengthened Under Lord Renaer

One of [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Lord Renaer Neverember's]] key accomplishments during his [[Lore/Neverwinter History|five-year reign]] has been **strengthening the City Guard**.

After the chaos of the "Dance of the Revolving Lords" (1502-1520 DR), the Guard had become demoralized and ineffective. Lord Renaer has worked to:
- Improve training and discipline
- Increase patrols
- Root out corruption
- Restore public trust

## Current Operations

### The Shadowy Force Investigation
The Guard is currently stretched thin dealing with [[Quests/The Shadowy Force|mysterious disappearances]]:

- Ships vanishing from the harbor
- Cargo disappearing from warehouses
- Strange encounters on trade routes

[[NPCs/Soren Ironwake|Guard Captain Soren Ironwake]] has been authorized to recruit **adventurers** to assist with the investigation.

### Other Duties
- Maintaining order in the districts
- Patrolling for criminals
- Dealing with [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Docks District|pirate raids on the docks]]
- Monitoring the dangerous [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Chasm District|Chasm District]]
- Protecting against [[Locations/Neverwinter/Districts/Neverdeath District|undead from Neverdeath]]

---

## Relationship with Adventurers

Neverwinter **relies heavily on adventurers and mercenaries** to defend its borders, so the City Guard is generally:
- **Cooperative** with competent adventurers
- **Willing to hire** outside help for difficult problems
- **Supportive** of those who help the city

However, they still enforce laws and won't tolerate criminal behavior.
