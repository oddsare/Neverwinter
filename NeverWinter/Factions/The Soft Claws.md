# The Soft Claws

**Type:** Secret guild that passes as a mercenary company
**Allegiance:** Good (covertly)
**Symbol:** a smooth four-pointed silver-blue star (the Nevertoken itself)
**Meeting ground:** [[Locations/Neverwinter/The Moonstone Mask|The Moonstone Mask]] (secret)
**Scope:** Sword Coast North; historically as far east as Suzail and as far south as Baldur's Gate
**True leader:** [[NPCs/Zundaerazylym - The Laughing Wyrm|Zundaerazylym]], an adult steel dragon living as a human seamstress (unknown to most members)

## Overview

The **Soft Claws** look like a reliable, well-connected mercenary outfit working out of Neverwinter. In truth they are the hands of an old steel dragon's long plan to shift the balance of power on the Sword Coast, quietly and over centuries, without anyone knowing who is pulling the strings.

Most members think they work for a well-paying, unusually well-informed employer. A small inner circle may know more.

---

## Purpose: the long plan

1. **Steer evil dragons** into lairing near orc warrens or on horde routes.
2. **Prepare desirable lairs** in orc territory to suit a particular dragon: the right cave depth, water, defensible chokepoints, nearby prey.
3. **Plant rumors** through trader networks about the new lair to draw the target dragon in.
4. **Feed information to rival dragons** so they compete for the same orc-territory lairs.
5. **Spy and fetch:** watch dragon and orc movements, gather alchemy ingredients, and quietly remove obstacles.

If it works, evil dragons and orc hordes wear each other down over decades. Settlements are safer, and good dragons can one day step forward as protectors instead of threats.

## Membership

### Scale
About **60 agents**, working fairly independently. In canon (1374 DR) they were humans, halflings, elves and half-elves: traders, woodcarvers and adventurers. Membership has turned over many times since then. What carries on is the mission and the tokens, not the people.

### Known members (canon, 1374 DR)
- **Bruldin Oldturret**
- **Tamaer Shiversword**
- **Jhavaerra Erbrand**
- **Iorl Skyndul**

*1505 DR members: not decided.*

### Who gets recruited
- People who **travel widely**: traders, guides, caravan masters, river pilots.
- People with **useful crafts**: miners, tunnelers, woodcarvers, builders (for lair work).
- **Adventurers** who take unusual jobs without too many questions.
- People who are **trustworthy and discreet**. She watches them by crystal ball before anyone approaches them.

### What members know
Most members know:
- They work for a well-connected employer (or, when she's away, a lieutenant she names).
- The work is unusual: scouting caves, spreading particular rumors, fetching particular items.
- They're paid well and treated fairly, and loyalty is remembered for years.

Most members do **not** know:
- Their employer is a dragon.
- The work is part of a plan centuries long.
- Their tokens are tied to her.

## The Nevertoken

Every member carries one of **Zundaerazylym's Nevertokens**.

### Appearance
A smooth, glossy, **silver-blue metal star** with four blunted points, about **1 inch thick and 4 inches across**. Cool to the touch. It doesn't tarnish.

### Origin
She received a cache of **more than 100 tokens** in **1320 DR** from **Ensible Mritavvalan**, the dying last of that family. Who the Mritavvalans were, and why they had the tokens, is unknown.

### Properties (homebrew, May draft, keep?)
- **One-way message:** once a day, the bearer can send one word or a short phrase into her mind, wherever she is. She can answer or not.
- **Recognition:** any member who sees the token knows the bearer belongs.
- **Warms near dragons:** within 30 ft of any dragon, the token grows faintly warm. Members learn to read it as a warning without knowing why.

### As a plot object
A token found on a body, bought from a curio dealer or lifted from a contact is distinctive and magical, and it leads back to the Moonstone Mask if someone follows it. She has handed tokens out for about 185 years, so some are surely lost, stolen or sitting in ruins.

## Joining

She could recruit the party early, after a job they handled well, presenting the Soft Claws as an independent outfit doing unusual work for good pay. Who really leads them comes out later, once the party has earned her trust and is close enough to the truth that hiding it no longer helps.

## DM notes

- The Soft Claws are how the party can **touch her plan before they know she exists.**
- **Tokens as breadcrumbs:** one is a mystery; two or three from different places make a pattern.
- **Not obviously good.** They plant false rumors and steer dragons without the dragons knowing. The ends are good; the methods aren't always clean.
- **The risk is real:** dragons usually realize they were played, too late to move their hoard, and then hunt whoever played them.
