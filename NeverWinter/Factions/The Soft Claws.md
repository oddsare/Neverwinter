# The Soft Claws

**Type:** Secret Mercenary Company
**Allegiance:** Good (covertly)
**Symbol:** A smooth four-pointed silver-blue star (the nevertoken itself)
**Headquarters:** [[Locations/Neverwinter/The Moonstone Mask|The Moonstone Mask]], Neverwinter
**Scope:** Sword Coast North, operating as far east as Suzail and as far south as Baldur’s Gate
**True Leader:** [[NPCs/Zundaerazylym - The Laughing Wyrm|Zundaerazylym]] (the ancient steel dragon — unknown to most members)
**Status:** Active *(canon 1374 DR; 151 years of history by campaign start)*

---

## Overview

The **Soft Claws** appear to be a reliable, well-connected mercenary company operating out of Neverwinter. In truth they are the covert arm of an ancient steel dragon’s long-term plan to reshape the balance of power on the Sword Coast — quietly, over centuries, without anyone knowing who is pulling the strings.

Most members simply believe they work for a well-paying, unusually well-informed employer. A small inner circle may know more. Only one person knows everything: **Amundra Nelaerdra**, the jolly laundress at the Moonstone Mask, who is Zundaerazylym in disguise.

---

## Purpose — The Grand Plan

The Soft Claws exist to execute Zundaerazylym’s long-game manipulation of dragonkind:

1. **Relocate evil dragons** — scout, scout, scout, then engineer circumstances that make an evil dragon choose to move its lair into orc-heavy territory.
2. **Create desirable lairs** — physically prepare sites in orc territories to be attractive to a specific dragon’s tastes: right cave depth, water access, defensible chokepoints, proximity to prey.
3. **Plant rumors** — spread word through trader networks about the new lair’s quality to draw the target dragon toward it.
4. **Plant intelligence near existing dragons** — feed information to rival dragons that causes them to compete for the same orc-territory real estate.
5. **Spy and fetch** — gather intelligence on dragon movements, acquire specific items or components Zundaerazylym needs, and quietly remove obstacles.

The effect, if it works: evil dragons and orc hordes tear each other apart over decades. Humanoid settlements are safer. Good dragons can eventually step forward as protectors rather than threats.

## Membership

### Scale
In the 14th century (canon period), the Soft Claws numbered **more than sixty members**: humans, halflings, elves, and half-elves drawn from traders, woodcarvers, and adventurers.

By 1525 DR — 151 years later — membership has turned over many times. The organization is multigenerational. The continuity is the mission and the tokens, not the individual people.

### Known Historical Members *(1374 DR era)*
- **Bruldin Oldturret**
- **Tamaer Shiversword**
- **Jhavaerra Erbrand**
- **Iorl Skyndul**

*1525 DR members: to be developed as the campaign introduces them.*

### Who Gets Recruited
- People with **wide travel ranges** — traders, guides, caravan masters, river pilots.
- People with **specific craft skills** — miners, tunnelers, woodcarvers, architects (for lair preparation work).
- **Adventurers** willing to take unusual jobs without asking too many questions.
- People who are **trustworthy and discreet** — Zundaerazylym vets them through crystal ball observation before approach.

### What Members Know
Most members know:
- They work for a well-connected employer called Amundra (or, in her absence, a designated lieutenant).
- The work is unusual — scouting caves, spreading specific rumors, acquiring specific items.
- They are paid generously and treated fairly.
- The organization has a long memory and rewards loyalty across years.

Most members do **not** know:
- Their employer is a dragon.
- The mission is part of a centuries-long plan.
- Their tokens are tied to a specific ancient wyrm.

## The Nevertoken

Every Soft Claws member carries a **Zundaerazylym’s Nevertoken**.

### Appearance
A smooth, glossy, **silver-blue metal star** with four blunted points. Approximately **1 inch thick and 4 inches across**. Cool to the touch. Does not tarnish.

### Origin
Zundaerazylym received a cache of **more than one hundred tokens** in **1320 DR** from **Ensible Mritavvalan**, the dying last member of their family. The full history of what the Mritavvalan family were and why they possessed them is unknown — a good open question.

### Properties *(Homebrew — DM’s discretion)*
- **One-way mental signal:** Once per day, the bearer can send a single word or short phrase to Zundaerazylym’s mind. She hears it wherever she is. She may choose to respond or not.
- **Member recognition:** Any Soft Claws member who sees the token knows the bearer is in the organization. No verbal confirmation needed.
- **Warm to the touch when near danger:** Within 30 feet of a dragon (any alignment), the token grows faintly warm. Members learn to read this as a general threat-signal; they do not know why.

### As a Plot Object
A nevertoken found on a body, purchased from a curio dealer, or lifted from a contact is an excellent Tier 1 hook. It’s distinctive, magical, and trails back to the Moonstone Mask and Amundra if investigated.

- **Historical reach:** As far east as Suzail, as far south as Baldur’s Gate

### Current Operations *(1525 DR — DM to decide)*
Possible active threads:
- **Monitoring Claugiyliamatar’s network** — Zundaerazylym knows Old Gnawbone is moving into her city and has tasked agents to map the criminal network.
- **Tracking missing dwarven artificers** — connecting to the Kurtulmak’s Chosen thread; unusual disappearances along trade routes look like Soft Claws intelligence work.
- **Lair preparation in the Sword Mountains** — an ongoing effort to steer an evil dragon northward, away from settled lands.
- **Information blackout** — someone is interfering with the Soft Claws’ intelligence network. Agents are going quiet. Zundaerazylym needs new eyes.

## Joining the Soft Claws

The party could be recruited by the Soft Claws as early as Tier 1 — Amundra approaches them after a job they handled well. They would be presented as an independent mercenary outfit doing unusual work for good pay.

The reveal of the true leadership is a **Tier 2 milestone**, after the party has earned Amundra’s trust and stumbled close enough to the truth that concealing it becomes counterproductive.

## DM Notes

- The Soft Claws are the **operational layer** of the Zundaerazylym storyline. They give the party ways to engage with her plan before they know she exists.
- **Nevertokens as breadcrumbs.** Encountering one is a natural Tier 1 mystery hook. Collecting two or three from different sources makes the pattern unmistakable.
- **Don’t make them obviously good.** The Soft Claws take morally grey jobs. Planting false rumors. Engineering a dragon’s territorial movement without the dragon’s knowledge. The ends are good; the methods are not always clean. This is intentional — it mirrors Zundaerazylym’s own moral complexity.
- **The Arcane Brotherhood thread.** If the Brotherhood suspects a covert pro-Neverwinter network exists, they may task agents to find and dismantle it. The Soft Claws and the Brotherhood could be running counter-operations against each other throughout the campaign.
- **The token cache.** Zundaerazylym has over 100 tokens from 1320 DR. After 200 years of distributing them, how many are out in the world? In wrong hands? Lost in ruins? This is a rich vein of random encounters and plot hooks.
