## The Fey Pact — Structural Notes

Fey pacts in Faerûn canon typically involve:

- **Something gained:** beauty, talent, wealth, luck, magical ability, longevity, a child's gifts, social influence
- **Something owed:** service, secrets, a name, periodic tribute, a future child, a specific deed in a specific season, a piece of memory
- **Something forgotten:** the archfey often takes a *thing the family forgets they ever had* — a sibling's name, an ancestor's grave, a song, a recipe, a color
- **A hidden trigger:** the deal usually has a clause that has not yet been called in — and the day it is called in changes everything

The fey **do not break their word**. They also do not interpret it the way mortals do. Every fey deal is a slow trap.

## Open Questions to Lock

- **What is the family's name?** Neverwinter-flavored, ideally with pre-Spellplague aristocratic roots.
- **Which archfey?** This shapes the whole faction:
  - **Queen of Air and Darkness** (Unseelie) — secrets, ambition, dark glamour, compatible with Sharran themes
  - **Titania** (Seelie) — beauty, growth, light court politics, more sympathetic but with a sharp edge
  - **Oberon** — wild, romantic, prefers grand gestures
  - **Hyrsam** (the Prince of Fools) — chaos, music, satyrs, festival-coded
  - **The Prince of Frost** — cold, vengeful, courtly, very dangerous
  - Other — invent
- **What did the family gain?** Beauty? Luck? A specific magical aptitude across the bloodline? A long-lived matriarch?
- **What did they pay?** Past payments AND a not-yet-called-in clause.
- **When was the deal made?** Several generations back, or recent (one generation)?
- **Does the current head know?** Could be:
  - *Fully aware and managing it* — every generation is briefed
  - *Half-aware* — knows there's a "family blessing," doesn't know the terms
  - *Unaware* — the deal is a secret only one ancestor knew, and the family is unknowingly fulfilling it
- **What is the not-yet-called-in clause?** The plot detonator.
