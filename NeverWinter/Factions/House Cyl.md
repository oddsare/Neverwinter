# House Cyl

**Pronounced:** "sill", as in windowsill *(spelled C-Y-L)*
**Type:** Noble house (shipping, warehouses, ships)
**Seat:** a castle-mansion in Neverwinter
**Secret:** in thrall to mind flayers for generations
**Origin:** came to Neverwinter long ago from **Halruaa**

> Source: **Realms Lore** (YouTube), with **Ed Greenwood**; Elminster supplies "the truth." Transcript added 2026-10-19.
> Summarized from the video's auto-captions. Many names were garbled and are marked *(uncertain)*; check them against the video.
> Ed's "present" in the video is the **late 1490s DR**. The ages below are worked out for the campaign's **1505 DR**.

## Who they are
- **Wealthy shippers, dockside warehouse owners and shipowners** for six generations.
- They started as **would-be nobles** and were finally **given local noble status and titles around 1346 DR**.
- **Never popular in Neverwinter**, not the way House Neverember is. They're darkly handsome and they **sneer at everyone**.

## The tentacles
- **The local legend:** if you end up alone with a grown-up Cyl who doesn't like you, their **tentacles come out**. Tellers disagree about where from: ears, mouth, nose, armpits or worse.
- **Elminster's version (the truth):**
  - Certain Cyls can shoot **four purplish-black tentacles, glistening with slime and saliva, from inside their mouths**.
  - The tentacles reach **up to 7 ft** and are **about as thick as a thumb**.
  - They stab into a victim's **ears, nose and mouth**, stinging and injecting a toxin that leaves them **stunned (the stunned condition) for a few minutes**.
  - That's long enough to slit a throat, rob someone, hold them under in a pond, or push them out a window or off a balcony.
  - **The sting is all a Cyl can do to you directly.**

## The mind flayers
- Some Cyls may carry **illithid tadpoles in their brains**, making them **thralls of the mind flayers** who live unseen in the **darker secret rooms of the family mansion**.
- The thralls:
  - attack people their master wants harmed;
  - steal things for it;
  - **kidnap unlucky people and bring them to the mind flayer** for worse.
- The house's **power and property come from this thralldom**. Guided by the flayers, the Cyls rose from **struggling sea merchants to nobility**.

### The flayers themselves
- **Iscahill** *(uncertain)*, the **real head of House Cyl**.
  - Unusually **patient and easygoing** for a mind flayer.
  - **Happy to let House Neverember get the publicity, the anger and the danger of being a target**, while House Cyl lurks in the background and benefits from the Neverembers' policies.
  - Its plans are best learned from Iscahill itself. **Elminster neither knows nor cares.** He ranks Iscahill as less troublesome than **Szass Tam and Vecna**, who in turn rank well below **Larloch** and others.
  - **If Iscahill ever does anything bold, House Cyl will be its mind-controlled human agents.** Watch them for signs of that boldness.
- Two **younger, lesser mind flayers**, the heir and the spare: the only survivors of Iscahill's last spawning. The family calls them **"the Tormentors."**
  - **Exlaiaw** *(uncertain)*: a bit of a sneak; whimsical and lazy, but ambitious.
  - **Kathan** *(uncertain)*: a perfect servant or assistant.

### High Lady Meava *(uncertain)*: matriarch
- **Coldly haughty, raven-haired beauty.** She holds grudges and **always gets more than even**.
- Spends her days eating sweets, drinking **gallons of green wine and mint wine** *(type uncertain)*, and spending the family fortune on gown after new gown.
- **Several pet snakes nestle in her bodice** and rear up for a look around at the right moments during feasts and revels.
- She is the daughter of **Basos**. She is the eldest of his third and last trio of children, by his final wife **Alera** *(uncertain)*, and the only one of the three still alive.
- She married **Gallen Hond** *(uncertain)*, a successful merchant fleet owner from **Baldur's Gate**. They had two sons. **He drowned at sea in 1491 DR.**

### High Lord Faralthos *(uncertain)*: elder son
- **Resents being controlled** by Iscahill, by "the Tormentors" and by his mother, but **sees no way out** from under their quiet tyranny.
- In **1496 DR** he married **Tasa Emrand** *(uncertain)* of **Athkatla**, heiress to a **huge banking and city real-estate fortune**.
- Their two sons are **Scalos** (b. 1497) and **Almert** (b. 1498) *(uncertain)*.
- He hasn't decided what to do about **Lord Valit** (see *Behirs* below).

### Lord Belanther *(uncertain)*: younger son
- The usual **nasty, conniving, bored and resentful younger brother**.
- When he isn't **backing shady thieving and smuggling ventures in Neverwinter**, he's **womanizing his way up and down the Sword Coast port cities**.
- He knows the unscrupulous adventurers and Luskan's darker cabals *(captions garbled)*.

### Earlier generations
- **Lord Mander and Lady Norara Cyl** *(uncertain)*: died in the **Spellplague (1385 DR)** when the roof of a new mansion wing, built with magical help, collapsed on them.
- **Basos Cyl** (1348–1469 DR): their son, and Meava's father.
  - Strong-willed and creative. All his life he **quietly tried to break free of the mind flayers** by collecting enough magic items and potions to defy them. **He failed**, but the potions he secretly drank gave him an unusually long life.
  - He outlived **four wives** and the **betrayals of two sets of his own children**, whom he had **killed by Luskan assassins** *(captions unclear)*.
  - He died of **winter chill fever in 1469 DR**, aged 121.
- **Anali Cyl**: Basos's younger sister (he was older by a year), and **the only Cyl most of the Realms has heard of**.
  - Her parents planned to **marry her into Waterdhavian nobility** and send her to safety as the war began. She **defied them**, took the heirloom falchion ***Decision*** and left home for good to **lead Neverwinter's forces against Luskan in 1372 DR**.
  - She's remembered as a famous general. Her official title was **Neverblade**: one of three, and the only woman. The naval Neverblade was lost at sea and the other land commander fell in battle. **Anali survived the war.**
  - Afterward she went to **Waterdeep** to meet the husband chosen for her. Elminster says she didn't think much of him. She soon left for points east, toward the **Sea of Fallen Stars**, with *Decision* on her hip, and **vanishes from history**.

## Behirs (the Halruaan custom)
- The house came from **Halruaa** and kept one of that land's customs: they **secretly breed behirs** *(captions say "beir"; the horns, talons, teeth and scales point to behirs)*.
- The breeding happens in a **cavern network under the foothills northeast of Neverwinter**, rising toward **Mount Hotenow**. The caves also hold **treasure, food and a safe house** guarded by behirs.
- Behir breeding is becoming a lost art in Halruaa, but it's being **kept alive near Neverwinter**.
- **Hidden income:** they sell **behir horns, talons, teeth and scales in Waterdeep** through **Lord Valit** *(uncertain)*, who **passes on less than half** of what he makes. Faralthos hasn't decided how to deal with him. Perhaps the Tormentors could teach Valit a lesson.

## How this fits the vault (Brodie decides)
- **This replaces the older canon line** that no Cyls were left in Neverwinter by the late 1400s, which came from NWN2. Ed has the house **thriving in Neverwinter in the 1490s**.
- **House Neverember taking the heat:** in Ed's present that's Dagult. In 1505, [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer]] leads Neverwinter.
- **Halruaa:** [[NPCs/Zundaerazylym - The Laughing Wyrm|Zundaerazylym]] spent about 30 years protecting the last survivors of a Halruaan noble family, and her note asks which family. Whether House Cyl is connected is open.
- **Mind flayers in canon:** the 4e book has a mind flayer colony in Gauntlgrym controlled by the Abolethic Sovereignty (see [[Lore/Campaign_Guide/Chapter_3_Factions_and_Foes]]). Whether Iscahill has any tie to them is open.
- What Iscahill is actually after is **not decided**.
