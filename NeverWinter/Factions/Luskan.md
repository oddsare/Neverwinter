# Luskan (Political Faction)

> The City of Sails has no laws and many masters. The faction that walks under Luskan's banner is whichever Ship is currently in favor, and behind them all, the Arcane Brotherhood.

> [!info] Where this note came from
> Written in May 2026 on another branch and brought in on 2026-10-24 (Brodie). Updated to **1505 DR** (Dagult gone in 1493, the Dance 1493–1503), with Brodie's Luskan decisions from [[Locations/Luskan]] and the [[TIMELINE]] added. This is the **political** view; the city itself is in [[Locations/Luskan]]. Anything marked **(May draft, keep?)** was suggested by Claude in May, not decided by Brodie.

**Type:** City-state political coalition
**Seat:** [[Locations/Luskan|Luskan]], north of Neverwinter on the Sword Coast
**Public ruler:** the Emperor of the Seas *(identity unknown)*. Has ruled with an iron grip **since about 1475 DR**, roughly 30 years (Brodie, 2026-10-18).
**True power:** [[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood|the Arcane Brotherhood]]
**Also behind the Ships:** **Jarlaxle and Bregan D'aerthe**, the drow mercenary band, as in canon
**Public faces:** the five High Captains (Ships Taerl, Baram, Kurth, Suljack, Rethnor)
**Alignment:** Self-interested and opportunistic. Not good or evil as an institution; criminal at scale.

---

## Structure

### The Emperor of the Seas
Supreme ruler since about 1475. Identity still a mystery. Canon describes Luskan around 1480 as having no real government, just gangs and the five Ships. **That's what outsiders saw.** In truth the Emperor has held the city the whole time (see [[TIMELINE]]).

*(May draft, keep?)* He has a **son** *(name not set)* who leads the anniversary delegation to Neverwinter. See [[Quests/The Peace Anniversary]].

### The five High Captains
Each commands a "Ship": a political organization with several vessels and crews, not a single boat.
- **Ship Taerl**: High Captain not set
- **Ship Baram**: High Captain not set
- **Ship Kurth**: canon captain **Beniago Kurth**, secretly a Bregan D'aerthe drow (see [[Locations/Luskan]])
- **Ship Suljack**: High Captain not set
- **Ship Rethnor**: High Captain not set

### The Arcane Brotherhood (true power)
The wizards who let the Emperor and Captains rule in public while pulling the strings. **They lied** (Brodie, 2026-10-18): in canon they returned in 1486 promising to stay out of politics, but in this campaign they're always working behind the scenes. Full write-up: [[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood]].

### Bregan D'aerthe
Jarlaxle's drow mercenaries are still behind the Ships, as in canon.

**Open (Brodie):** who answers to whom among the Emperor, the Brotherhood and Bregan D'aerthe.

## Relationship with Neverwinter

- **Before 1493:** Luskan was **plotting to assassinate [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember|Dagult]]**. He had specific intelligence on a planned hit, and it was **one of the reasons he went into the Neverneath** after the Lost Crown in 1493. The plot may still operate in 1505 through successors.
- **1493–1503 (the Dance of the Revolving Lords):** *(May draft, keep?)* Luskan exploits Neverwinter's instability, raids trade routes and props up some of the failed lords for leverage.
- **Peace with Renaer:** *(May draft, keep?)* [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer]] signs peace with Luskan as his first act of rule, and the peace holds. **The year isn't set.** If it was his first act, it's 1503 and the peace is 2 years old in 1505. To be decided with [[Quests/The Peace Anniversary]].
- **The anniversary:** *(May draft, keep?)* Luskan sends a delegation to mark the peace. On the surface it's a celebration; really it's a **political move** against Renaer. See [[Quests/The Peace Anniversary]].

## Goals

- Keep Luskan independent, and keep its "all trade allowed" status
- Keep Neverwinter weak enough never to threaten Luskan
- Get leverage over Renaer's government before it becomes too stable
- Advance the Brotherhood's magical research and whatever deeper plans it has
- Profit from the [[Lore/Current Era|magical resurgence]] before rivals do

---

## Threats

- **Naval:** the five Ships' fleets, a direct threat to Neverwinter shipping
- **Magical:** the Brotherhood, a direct threat to Neverwinter's defenses
- **Political:** delegations, spies and informants in Neverwinter
- **Lawlessness:** it can do what lawful cities can't: bribery, smuggling, hired assassins, slave markets

## Factions inside the faction (per [[Lore/3 Faction Rule]])

| Group | Stance | Role |
|---|---|---|
| The Emperor and loyalists | Stable autocracy | Keep the throne and the trade |
| Ambitious High Captains | Want more | Each Ship pushes for advantage over the others |

*(May draft, keep?)* The anniversary delegation shows all three: the Emperor uses his son for image, a Captain may use the trip to spy on rivals, and the Brotherhood advisor is the real operator.
