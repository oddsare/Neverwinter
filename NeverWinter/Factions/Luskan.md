# Luskan (Political Faction)

> The City of Sails has no laws and many masters. The faction that walks under Luskan's banner is whichever Ship is currently in favor — and behind them all, the Arcane Brotherhood.

**Type:** City-state political coalition
**Seat:** [[Locations/Luskan|Luskan]] — north of Neverwinter on the Sword Coast
**Public Ruler:** The Emperor of the Seas *(identity TBD; ~30 years in power)*
**True Power:** [[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood|The Arcane Brotherhood]]
**Public Faces:** The 5 High Captains (Ships Taerl, Baram, Kurth, Suljack, Rethnor)
**Alignment:** Self-interested and opportunistic — not aligned good or evil as an institution; individually criminal at scale.

---

## Structure

### The Emperor of the Seas
Supreme ruler, ~30 years on the throne. Identity still mysterious. Sends his **son** *(name TBD)* as the public face of the 1525 anniversary delegation to Neverwinter — see [[Quests/The Peace Anniversary]].

### The Five High Captains
Each commands a "Ship" — a political organization with multiple vessels and crews:
- **Ship Taerl** — High Captain TBD
- **Ship Baram** — High Captain TBD
- **Ship Kurth** — High Captain TBD
- **Ship Suljack** — High Captain TBD
- **Ship Rethnor** — High Captain TBD

### The Arcane Brotherhood (true power)
The wizarding cabal that allows the Emperor and Captains to rule publicly while pulling strings. See [[Factions/Arcane Brotherhood]] for the full file.

---

## Relationship With Neverwinter

- **Before 1502 DR:** Active assassination plots against [[NPCs/Dagult Neverember|Dagult]] — Luskan very likely had a hand in his disappearance.
- **1502–1520 DR (Dance of the Revolving Lords):** Luskan exploits Neverwinter's instability, raids trade routes, and props up some of the failed lords for leverage.
- **1520 DR:** [[NPCs/Renaer Neverember|Renaer]] signs peace with Luskan as his first act of reign. The peace holds.
- **1525 DR — 5-year anniversary:** Luskan sends a delegation. Surface intent is celebration; real intent is a **political move** against Renaer. See [[Quests/The Peace Anniversary]].

---

## Goals

- Maintain Luskan's autonomy and "all-trade-allowed" status
- Keep Neverwinter weak enough to never threaten Luskan
- Extract leverage over Renaer's government before he becomes too stable
- Advance Brotherhood magical research / unknown deeper plans
- Profit from the [[Lore/Current Era|magical resurgence]] before rivals do

---

## Threat Profile

- **Naval power:** five Ships' fleets — direct military threat to Neverwinter shipping
- **Magical power:** the Brotherhood — direct threat to Neverwinter's defenses
- **Political power:** the delegation strategy + spies and informants in Neverwinter — soft power
- **Lawlessness:** can do things sovereign cities cannot — bribery, smuggling, hired assassinations, slavery markets

---

## Internal Tensions (per [[Lore/3 Faction Rule]])

| Sub-faction | Stance | Role |
|---|---|---|
| The Emperor + loyalists | Stable autocracy | Keep the throne and the trade |
| Ambitious High Captains | Want more | Each Ship pushes for advantage over the others |
| The Arcane Brotherhood | Deeper plans | Use the political layer as cover for magical agenda |

The 1525 delegation reflects all three: the Emperor uses his son to project image, a Captain may be using the trip to spy on rivals, and the Brotherhood advisor is the actual operator.
