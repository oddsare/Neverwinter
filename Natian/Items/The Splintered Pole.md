# The Splintered Pole

A heavy **wooden pole** dropped by the construct ("burb dude") in [[12 - The Splintered Pole]].

- Powerful and magical, but it **felt like a murder weapon**. Very old and very important.
- It rejected Kira, as if bound to something else.
- **Kira destroyed it.** Now it's just splintered, fragile, ordinary wood.
- Destroying it triggered [[Kira's Visions#The Pole|visions]] of [[Stark Woltoff]] and the [[Shrieking Void]].
