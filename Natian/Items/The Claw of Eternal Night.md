# The Claw of Eternal Night

A **black pillar of rock** in [[Midway]], one of [[Eternal Night]]'s claws. It's the **Pact Claw**, a sign of respect between [[Shimmerlight]] and [[Dragofell]].

- Feels magnetic and draws the eye. The land around it isn't withered (Arcana 10).
- Touching it gave Kira [[Kira's Visions#The Claw|a vision of Eternal Night's death]] and unlocked an ability.
- [[Rimeblood]]: only people with a strong presence get visions from it.
