# Kira's Visions

## The Fog Dream
([[03 - Session 3]]) A dizzying haze. She snaps to her senses in **black fog with eyes all around**, watching. No sound. She smells fire, hears a hiss, and drops fall around her. She stays. **Her job is not to kill it.**

## The Claw
([[04 - The Claw of Eternal Night]]) She touches [[The Claw of Eternal Night]] and **becomes Eternal Night**, huge, on a mountain peak among acid-ruined land.
- The screams of battle die out until only the dragon and **one bloodied, bruised soldier** remain. He fights with **bare fists** and has done serious damage, including tearing one wing apart.
- She charges, snaps him up in her jaws, and swallows him. Then a sharp pain in the neck. Her head tilts and her throat closes. Her bile melts anything, but **he's holding on inside.**
- A **snap** echoes out. No pain as the body collapses. The swallowed man falls out, barely more than flesh and bone, but with a **cocky, smug look** in his dying eyes. **Both die on the peak.** (He's [[Syndrige Contorur]]?)

## The Pole
([[12 - The Splintered Pole]]) After destroying [[The Splintered Pole]]:
1. **A luxurious home** by a fireplace. She's in a struggling body holding an **ancient leather tome** with human hands. The right hand has acid burns, the left is intact but greenish-grey. The body jolts, blood covers the tome, and **acid spreads up and overtakes the left arm.** It falls onto the tome, **laughing uncontrollably.** ([[Stark Woltoff]])
2. **Deep in heavy fog.** She can't see the body but feels many wounds, **rage, and biding time.** The rage fades, there's shifting and cracking, and a voice in the fog: ***"Kira, I didn't lose you..."*** ([[Shrieking Void]])

Also: a flash of dodging through alleyways, warmth in her hands, and a memory of **jabbing out with a weapon as the Shrieking Void screeched.**
