# The Eternal Night War

How **[[Eternal Night]]** was slain, as learned in [[04 - The Claw of Eternal Night]].

- Eternal Night came **from distant lands**, made her presence known, and kept slaves.
- Her era is what caused **[[Phonisha]]** to form.
- **[[Phonisha]] and [[Yoton]] united, with the giants' help,** and fought her at the peak now called **[[Dragofell]]**.
- **The [[Phoenix Feathers|Phoenix Feather]]:** a lesser-known, powerful hero blessed by the Phoenix. He held the power less than half a year before being slain leading the charge.
- **[[Syndrige Contorur]]:** the goliath of Yoton. He fought bare-handed, died in the battle, and **Eternal Night fell at his hands.**
- "The Phoenix took down the Night" really means it did so **with the help of others.**
- **[[Kira's Visions#The Claw|Kira's vision]]** shows the end: one bare-fisted soldier tore a wing, was swallowed, and broke the dragon's neck from inside.
