# Dragon and Phoenix Balance

A research paper from **[[Gem of the Times]]**, found in [[11 - The Balance Research]].

## Claims
- [[Minor NPCs#Groll|Groll]] mentions **Ashcoat** and notes that dragons and dragonkin **resist outside alteration** but must keep an **internal balance**.
- Some believe **dragons balance the scales of a region**: they're drawn to great evil to offset it so it doesn't overflow.
- Dragon and Phoenix are seen as bad and good. If they weren't balanced, one would overpower the other. Mostly unprovable, but some theories line up.
- After [[Eternal Night]] was killed, her influence never again shaped battles to the same degree.
- With Eternal Night gone but the [[Shrieking Void]] still here, **dragonkin must practice balance themselves.** In times of strife they're good, and in peace they become more sinful. Maybe subconscious, which might be why [[Dragofell]] loves peaceful times.
- The Phoenix has been seen in Natian, but Eternal Night's destruction outweighed the Phoenix's gifts. Eventually they evened out, "as they always do."

## Conjecture
- If fate brought Eternal Night here, **why destroy her opposite?** Instead she waited for the Phoenix to grow. There was a window of peace as long as nobody went looking for a fight.
- **She bore the burden of a child.** Why would she enter hostile territory with a child?
- The paper ends by setting out to learn **whether the child is real.**
