# Kira Blackbrook

Halfling Draconic Sorcerer, played by Womp. From [[Mossy Rock]].

## Appearance
Curly reddish-brown hair, small horns on top of her head, a freckled face. Her clothes are tattered but look like they once belonged to someone important (red with gold accents). She wears a **black bracelet covered in scales**.

## Personality
"A little dumb and selfish." **Knows how to sew.**

## Backstory
- Her family were merchants. While carrying goods, they took a shortcut through [[Cragg's Braids]] and ran into the black dragon the [[Shrieking Void]].
- The dragon asked whether she knew who it was. She said no, and **it spared her for that reason.**
- It came to treat her as a living trophy or pet and protected her a little. It **threatened to kill her family** if she ever left and didn't come back.
- She met the dragon at **30** and died at **40**, in **752**. She may have been killed when an adventuring party came to kill the dragon, and buried in [[Restful Grove]].
- She hates [[Dragofell]].
- She only half-remembers [[Phonisha]]. The tales sound like folk tales, but she knows it was a real place.

## Developments
- **[[04 - The Claw of Eternal Night|The Claw]]:** touching the Claw gave her [[Kira's Visions#The Claw|a vision of Eternal Night's death]]. She got 1 point of DM inspiration, and her magic was enhanced: **"you have met the conditions to unlock an ability."**
- **6/9 session:** [[Victoria]] believes Kira has a part to play but calls her a "grey area" and can't tell whether she is good or evil. Victoria sensed a creature with a similar aura near [[Mossy Rock]], "not something to be trifled with."
- **[[12 - The Splintered Pole]]:** destroying the pole gave her visions and the Shrieking Void's voice: *"Kira, I didn't lose you..."*

## Her NPC ideas (offered to the DM)
- [[Emberblood]]: Dragonborn Paladin guarding the temple in the dragon's ribs
- [[Tiv]]: Gnome artificer and potion seller from Mossy Rock
