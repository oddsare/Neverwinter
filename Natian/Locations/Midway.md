A town home to **[[The Claw of Eternal Night]]**, the Pact Claw marking respect between [[Shimmerlight]] and [[Dragofell]].

- Bandit trouble. The guards botched it and a guard died.
- Merchants with shipments of food, Dragofell steel, tools, and lumber were taken (4 shipments, 7 people).
