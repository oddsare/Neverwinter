# Phonisha

An ancient orc nation that has **fallen**. Kira's memories of it are faint and the tales sound like folk tales, but it was real.

- **Formed because of the era of [[Eternal Night]].** It united with [[Yoton]] and the giants to defeat her.
- When it fell, **its people lost themselves and became like the creatures** we fought in [[Restful Grove]].
- [[Minor NPCs#Phinika|Phinika]] and an orc man came from here.
