# Dragofell

A settlement **built in the corpse of [[Eternal Night]]**, on the peak where she was slain.

- Seat of the **[[Nightblood Temple]]** (a temple inside the dragon's ribs?).
- Exports **steel**.
- Allied with [[Shimmerlight]] (see [[The Claw of Eternal Night]]).
- Founded by the hero who slew Eternal Night (per [[Gem of the Times]]).
- Thought to love it when times are peaceful (see [[Dragon and Phoenix Balance]]).
- [[Kira Blackbrook|Kira]] hates it.
