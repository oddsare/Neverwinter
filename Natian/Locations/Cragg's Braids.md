# Cragg's Braids

Mountains or highlands **shrouded in endless fog.** Lair of the **[[Shrieking Void]]**, and where [[Kira Blackbrook|Kira]] was held captive.

- Rumors of the Void are now spread across the Braids rather than tied to one spot.
- "The last of the aspect cultivates itself in the Braids. Boiling in the fog." ([[12 - The Splintered Pole]])
