# Restful Grove

The graveyard and tombs where the party **woke from death.**

- Stone burial niches. Tombs shifted, charred, and covered in soot.
- Each grave is marked with name, passage, and age at death.
- Notable graves (from [[02 - Session 2]]): the pet grave (Lavender, Bud, Miss Sass), Gentle Landry, the Whiler family, the Winterbloom graves with the blue daffodil crown, and **Nerabee Winterbloom's freshly dug grave**, plus "Razor the First."
- Kira may have been buried here after the party that hunted the [[Shrieking Void]] killed her.
