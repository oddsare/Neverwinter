# Session 1: Waking in Restful Grove

> [!tldr] Summary
> The party wakes in a tomb in [[Restful Grove]] in "a different Naiten," decades after their deaths. They fight their way out alongside an orc woman and a man from [[Phonisha]].

## Log
- We wake by torchlight in stone burial niches dug into the walls. The tombs around us are shifted, charred, and covered in soot.
- Above are two niches with two large creatures waking up, an **orc and a human**. People are coughing all around, and someone is trying to break out of a tomb.
- Each tomb is marked: **name, passage, age at death.** The inscriptions say something like "freed from your shackles into the afterlife."
- We meet **[[Minor NPCs#Phinika|Phinika]]**, an orc woman, and an orc man from this place.
- **[[Birmak|Breman (Birmak?)]] has seen these creatures before.**
- When [[Phonisha]] (the orcs' homeland) fell, its people **lost themselves and became like the creatures we just fought.**
