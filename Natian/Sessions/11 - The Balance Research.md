# The Balance Research

> [!tldr] Summary
> Kira finds a [[Gem of the Times]] research paper by **Groll** arguing that dragons and the Phoenix keep each other in balance, and that [[Eternal Night]] may have had a child. Full notes: [[Dragon and Phoenix Balance]].

## Log
- Some **dragon worship is out of control**, almost a compulsion.
- We found many documents on the dragons, including the **[[Shrieking Void]]** and **[[Eternal Night]]**.
- One stood out: a **[[Gem of the Times]]** research paper citing several sources. One name Kira recognizes: **[[Minor NPCs#Groll|Groll]]**, who mentions **Ashcoat**.
- Main points are in [[Dragon and Phoenix Balance]]. The paper ends on one question: **was Eternal Night carrying a child, and does that child exist?**
