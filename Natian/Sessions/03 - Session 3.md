## The library: a book on Eternal Night
A non-fiction book published by **[[Gem of the Times]]** (dragonborn). It isn't about the [[Shrieking Void]]. It's about **[[Eternal Night]]**, another black dragon.
- Her acid **carved out the river of [[Mossy Rock]].**
- She is the ancient dragon whose **corpse [[Dragofell]] was built in.**
- She was **slain by a great hero several decades ago**, and that hero founded Dragofell (?).
- She could hollow out a lair in under a day and leap from peak to peak. She was big enough to **block out the sun** and cast a huge shadow, which is how she got her name.
- One passage says **Eternal Night had an offspring.** Some speculate the Shrieking Void is related.
