# The Claw of Eternal Night

> [!tldr] Summary
> In [[Midway]] a guard has died over a bandit problem. Kira is drawn to [[The Claw of Eternal Night]], touches it, and relives Eternal Night's death. Afterwards she learns about [[The Eternal Night War|the war]] and the [[Phoenix Feathers]].

## Previously
- Met with the merchants.
- Saw the **crazy evil dragon-cult lady** ([[Rimeblood]]?).
- Now it's noon in town.

## Log
- With **[[Rimeblood|Rynblood]]**, the "dragon lady."
- **Bandit trouble involving the guard.** The guards messed up, someone said "Brigins" (?), and **a guard died.**
- Everyone examines the body. Kira keeps looking out the window at a **black pillar of rock**, which makes her uneasy.
- **[[Minor NPCs#Henry|Henry]]:** "The rock is the **Pact Claw**, a sign of respect between [[Shimmerlight]] and [[Dragofell]]." It is the **[[The Claw of Eternal Night|Claw of Eternal Night]]**.
- Arcana 10: if it were bad juju the land around it would wither, but Kira's eyes are still drawn to it.
- **Stolen merchant goods:** 150 days of food, steel from Dragofell, tools for 20 men, 2 lumber shipments. Four shipments, 7 people taken.
- Up close the Claw feels magnetic. **[[Minor NPCs#Jade River|Jade River]]** steps out and says "little friend isn't supposed to be back there." Kira hears nothing.
- **Kira touches the Claw** and relives Eternal Night's death. See [[Kira's Visions#The Claw]].
- She is shaken awake by a **scaled hand on her shoulder**, first indignant, then curious. (Dragofell lore isn't her strength.)
- **The Phoenix was Eternal Night's enemy.**
- Kira gets **1 point of DM inspiration.** As her body shakes, her magic grows stronger: **"you have met the conditions to unlock an ability."**

## Lore learned
See [[The Eternal Night War]]. Short version: Eternal Night came from distant lands. [[Phonisha]] and [[Yoton]] united, with the giants' help, to fight her at the peak now called [[Dragofell]]. A [[Phoenix Feathers|Phoenix Feather]] and the goliath **[[Syndrige Contorur]]** brought her down. Ask [[Rimeblood|Rynblood]] to learn more, at the **[[Minor Locations#Church of the Traveler|Church of the Traveler]]**.

## Between sessions: chat with Chace (DM) about [[Rimeblood]]
Womp's plan: wait until things calm down, ask about the Claw, apologize, explain the vision to earn trust, then steer toward the Shrieking Void through old rumors.

Chace's answers:
- She would be **eager to talk about the Claw** and the glory of Eternal Night.
- Touching it: **apprehension.** Getting a vision: she would **study Kira** and remark that despite Kira's size and lack of draconic features she has a great presence. **Only people with a strong presence get visions from Eternal Night.**
- Not fully convinced, she would still **invite Kira to [[Dragofell]] to meet the [[Nightblood Temple]]'s more influential members.**
- **Shrieking Void:** she confirms there are theories of another dragon in the Braids, possibly **of Eternal Night's blood.** The temple has never hunted for it, so as not to suggest to the dragon that they control it. They would welcome it if it ever made itself known.
- Some members and adventurers broke off to search for it. When she was young, **two half-dragons** went to find out whether the myths were true. **They survived, but never returned to Dragofell.**
- **Is it still alive?** The rumors haven't stopped. They're just spread across the Braids now rather than tied to one place. Probably still alive.
