# Session 2: The Graveyard

> [!tldr] Summary
> A fight, then a search of the [[Restful Grove]] graves. We find tiny bottled stories, a crate of rare [[Phoenix Fire Vineyards Wine]], and a lead on a reward in [[Shimmerlight]].

## Log
- **Fight** (no details recorded).
- **Pet grave** surrounded by lavender:
  - *Lavender*, 23 (paw print, a cat)
  - *Bud*, 4 (bigger paw print, a dog)
  - *Miss Sass*, age unknown (a feather, a bird?)
- Several **empty tomes**.
- **A set of 3 small bottles** labeled "Tiki, clear gem, 143." It's unclear whether Tiki was a halfling. They hold small readable stories:
  - **Hintry** (unknown writer) spent a week drunk ("drunk bucky") on mushrooms.
  - A group of **butterfly riders** took a cardinal. One fell, but was caught in time.
  - Met a friend, **[[Minor NPCs#Meric Sunderblade|Meric Sunderblade]]**.
  - Went into the **[[Minor Locations#Fluttering Forest|Fluttering Forest]]** and found spiderwebs. Nobody saw what made them, but people were walking.
- Epitaph: *"Death is just one step in the story."* **Gentle Landry** (312).
- **A crate of 9 bottles** of brew, labeled with a "cander" (?) logo.
- **Rose graves**, each rose with a name:
  - Matthew Whiler, 30
  - Rachel Whiler, 30
  - Sammy Whiler, 8 (fresh roses in a pot)
- **Crown of blue daffodils** with the line: *"I will always be there to catch you when you fall, so fly high."*
  - Priney Winterbloom, 134
  - **Nerabee Winterbloom**, under 1, a **freshly dug grave**
- A grave with two short engravings: *"Quiet, please"* and **"Razor the First," 89.**
- Labels that were professionally made about 70 years ago and have since worn off: **[[Phoenix Fire Vineyards Wine|Phoenix Fire Vineyards]]**. We think we've seen this bottle before. Back in our day one sold for **200 gp**.
