# The Splintered Pole

> [!tldr] Summary
> A wooden construct ambushes us ("intruder detected, eliminate") and someone drops to death saves. Kira destroys the [[The Splintered Pole|magic pole]] it dropped and sees visions of [[Stark Woltoff]]'s death and the [[Shrieking Void]], which says her name. We pull a huge dragon tooth from a body and get five chilling answers from the dead.

## Log
- The room fills with red light and smells of copper and blood. Wood creaks. **"Intruder detected." "Eliminate."**
- **Brodie** got **1 success on a death saving throw** (who was down?).
- The **wooden pole** the "burb dude" (construct?) dropped is heavy. Kira gets a flash of memory, there and gone.
- Vision: Kira dodging through alleyways, then back. **Warmth fills her hands.**
- The pole is powerful and magical but **feels like a murder weapon.** Something rejects her, as if it's bound to something else. It feels very old and very important.
- **Kira destroys the pole.** See [[Kira's Visions#The Pole]] for the two visions, ending with the fog and the voice: ***"Kira, I didn't lose you..."***
- Afterward the pole is just splintered, fragile, ordinary wood.

## Stark Woltoff and the tooth
- **[[Stark Woltoff]]**, a past [[Phoenix Feathers|Phoenix Feather]].
- Visions of the past land on his **mangled, scarred hands.** Kira gets a brief flash of her own memory, of **jabbing out with a weapon** and **the Shrieking Void's screech**, and stumbles back.
- **A tooth is removed from this man**, as big as a hand, broken off inside him.
- **Acid burns:** one side is worse than the other. The **right is worst**, the **left more surface-level and fresher.** In the vision his whole body melted. Here the effect is similar but the vision's version was more powerful.

## The five answers
These look like *Speak with Dead* answers, probably from [[Birmak]] (?). The questions weren't recorded.

1. "To preoccupy myself, to destroy the last remaining knowledge of the person who remembered."
2. "I was taken, and morphed, warped into what I am."
3. **"Lead the dragon here, let it inspire conflict, let there be survivors. Let malice cultivate."**
4. **"The last of the aspect cultivates itself in the Braids. Boiling in the fog."**
5. "They were taken as well... underwent their own separate changes... each falling before me."

> [!question] Theories
> - "The last of the aspect... in the Braids" sounds like the [[Shrieking Void]] in [[Cragg's Braids]].
> - "They were taken as well... separate changes" could be the **two half-dragons** [[Rimeblood]] mentioned, who never returned.
> - "Let malice cultivate" fits the [[Dragon and Phoenix Balance|balance theory]]: someone is deliberately feeding conflict.
