# Nightblood Temple

The **[[Eternal Night]]** worshippers of **[[Dragofell]]**.

- Members: [[Rimeblood]]. [[Emberblood]] (player-proposed) guards the temple in the dragon's ribs.
- They believe **only people with a strong presence get visions** from Eternal Night's remains.
- They've never sought out the [[Shrieking Void]], to avoid suggesting they control it, but would welcome it.
- Some members broke off to find it, including the two half-dragons.
- Kira may get an invitation to meet the leaders.
- "Some dragon worship is out of control, almost a compulsion."
