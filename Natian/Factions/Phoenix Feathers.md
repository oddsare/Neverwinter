# Phoenix Feathers

The title for heroes **blessed by the Phoenix**, the enemy of [[Eternal Night]].

- The first major Phoenix Feather held the power **less than half a year** before he was slain leading the charge on Eternal Night. "Only one Phoenix Feather is alive"(?) at a time.
- **Hofery** (half goliath, half human) became one.
- **[[Stark Woltoff]]**, a past Phoenix Feather.
- **[[Victoria]]** was chosen by the Phoenix.
- [[Eldrid]] was deafened by a vision of the Phoenix.
- See [[Dragon and Phoenix Balance]].
