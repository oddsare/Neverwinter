# The Shrieking Void

A **black dragon** living in the endless fog of **[[Cragg's Braids]]**. It held [[Kira Blackbrook]] captive for ten years.

## Known
- **Its wings were clipped** by a wizard who wanted to control it.
- It **collects the skeletons** of those it has killed.
- It spared Kira because she didn't know who it was. It kept her as a living trophy or pet and **threatened to kill her family** if she left and didn't return.
- It may be **[[Eternal Night]]'s offspring** ([[Gem of the Times]] book, [[Rimeblood]], [[Dragon and Phoenix Balance|Groll's paper]]).
- The [[Nightblood Temple]] has never hunted it. **Two half-dragons** went looking for it; they survived but never came back.
- **Recent rumors:** it's been seen **all over the mountain** and moves faster than before. A few years ago its location could be pinned down. Not anymore. Things have been "mad" for about a month.
- [[Victoria]] passed a creature with an aura like Kira's near [[Mossy Rock]], "not something to be trifled with."
- In Kira's vision after she destroyed the pole: deep in the fog, heavily wounded, full of rage, **biding its time.** It said ***"Kira, I didn't lose you..."***
- Kira remembers stabbing out with a weapon as the Void screeched. A hand-sized dragon tooth was pulled from [[Stark Woltoff]]'s body.
