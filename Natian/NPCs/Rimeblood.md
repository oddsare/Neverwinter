# Rimeblood

The "dragon lady" of the **[[Nightblood Temple]]**, met around [[Midway]]. Possibly the "crazy evil dragon-cult lady."

- Eager to talk about the glory of [[Eternal Night]].
- Believes **only people with a strong presence get visions** from Eternal Night. If she believes Kira's vision, she'll **invite Kira to [[Dragofell]]** to meet the temple's leaders.
- Confirmed rumors of another dragon in the Braids that may share Eternal Night's blood. When she was young, **two half-dragons** went looking for it and never returned.
- A source for more Eternal Night lore. She was at the **[[Minor Locations#Church of the Traveler|Church of the Traveler]]** (?).
- See [[04 - The Claw of Eternal Night]].
