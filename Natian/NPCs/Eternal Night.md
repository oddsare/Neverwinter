# Eternal Night

An **ancient black dragon** (she) who came **from distant lands.** Her corpse is the foundation of **[[Dragofell]]**.

- Her acid **carved out the river of [[Mossy Rock]]**.
- She could hollow out a lair in under a day and leap from peak to peak. Her size could **block out the sun**, which is where the name comes from.
- She kept slaves.
- Her era is what caused **[[Phonisha]]** to form.
- She was slain at the peak now called Dragofell by a **[[Phoenix Feathers|Phoenix Feather]]** and **[[Syndrige Contorur]]**. See [[The Eternal Night War]].
- Her last moments are recorded in [[Kira's Visions#The Claw]].
- **She may have had a child.** See [[Shrieking Void]] and [[Dragon and Phoenix Balance]].
- Relics: [[The Claw of Eternal Night]].
