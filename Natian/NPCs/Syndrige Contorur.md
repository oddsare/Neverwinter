# Syndrige Contorur

A hero from **[[Yoton]]**, a giant-blooded goliath who could **throw boulders with his bare hands** and **never touched a weapon.**

- He fought in [[The Eternal Night War]]. He was slain in the battle, and **[[Eternal Night]] fell at his hands.**
- He wasn't a good man. He often abused the power he was given, but in those times he commanded a certain respect.
- **The Phoenix "took down the night" mostly through him and others.**
- His son **Hofery** (half goliath, half human) became a [[Phoenix Feathers|Phoenix Feather]]. The notes can also be read as Hofery being the first Phoenix Feather's son.
