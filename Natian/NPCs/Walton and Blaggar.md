# Walton and Blaggar

**Human twins** with big black beards and plate mail. They **came out of the crypts** and seemed alive and well.

- They **turned the bandits** and gave them **power and coin.** The power lets its user take out detractors but **eats their life over time.**
- **Captured merchants were killed in the coffins.**
- The bandit leader hates them and wants revenge.
- They left over a week before the March 31 session:
  - **Walton** rode to **[[Shimmerlight]]**.
  - **Blaggar** took his **5 best guards to the mountain** to visit "an old friend." The [[Shrieking Void]]?
